This follow-up study had researchers rebuild and "role-play" with reconstructions of historical electronic sound instruments, then reflected on what gets changed, lost, or reinterpreted when you try to recreate old electronic gear using modern parts and knowledge. It received a Highly Commended paper award at the NIME 2025 conference (the main academic venue for new musical instrument design research).
