Researchers invited musicians and designers to play with old, historical electronic instruments held in KMH's own archives and at EMS (Stockholm's electronic music studio). By studying how people react to and reinterpret these forgotten machines, the paper argues that looking backward at old, sometimes failed, instrument designs can inspire and inform how new musical instruments should be built today.
