KMH (Kungl. Musikhögskolan) is Sweden's state-funded conservatory in Stockholm. It does not have its own computational research lab, but runs a joint "Music and Technology" doctoral program with KTH's Sound and Music Computing group, and hosts artistic researchers who study historical electronic instruments and machine improvisation.
