Part of the Division of Applied Acoustics at Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg. The group focuses on 3D/spatial sound capture and reproduction, sound field synthesis, and the psychoacoustics of spatial hearing — the audio-engineering side of Sound and Music Computing rather than the music-composition side.
