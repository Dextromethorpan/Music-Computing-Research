A deliberately math-free, beginner-friendly explanation of ambisonics — a technique for recording and playing back sound so that it feels like it is coming from all around the listener in three dimensions, rather than just from left and right speakers. Ambisonics is the technology behind much of today's 360-degree video sound and virtual-reality audio, and this paper is written specifically to make the underlying idea approachable to newcomers before introducing any of the mathematics.
