Auralization means simulating what a space would actually sound like before it physically exists — for example, hearing what a concert hall will sound like while it is still just a blueprint. This open-source toolbox provides the mathematical models needed to simulate how sound reflects, echoes, and travels inside a virtual room, so architects and acousticians can predict and improve a room's sound before it's built.
