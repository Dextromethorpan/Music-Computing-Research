A free, open-source software toolkit that lets researchers take recordings made with special spherical microphone arrays (many microphones arranged on a ball shape) and analyze exactly how sound was moving through a room — where it came from, how it reflected, and how it would sound from any listening position. It's a practical research tool used to study and visualize 3D sound fields rather than a musical instrument or composition tool itself.
