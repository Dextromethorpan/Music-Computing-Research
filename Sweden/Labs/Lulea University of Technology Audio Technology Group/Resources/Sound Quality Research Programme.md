This research programme studies how to measure, in an objective and repeatable way, whether audio "sounds good" — combining acoustics, electronics, and psychology to build listening tests and technical measurements rather than relying only on personal opinion. The work applies to recorded music, loudspeakers, and other sound-producing equipment, aiming to give engineers reliable ways to judge and improve audio quality.
