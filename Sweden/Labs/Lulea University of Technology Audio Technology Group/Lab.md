Part of Luleå University of Technology's Department of Civil, Environmental and Natural Resources Engineering. This group studies audio production and sound quality evaluation — how to scientifically measure whether audio "sounds good" — alongside a separate, less music-specific engineering acoustics group working on building acoustics and noise.
