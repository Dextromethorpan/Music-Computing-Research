The Integra Framework is an open-source software system built to help composers and sound engineers organize and reuse the many small audio-processing building blocks ("modules," similar to plugins) used in live electronic music performances. Before tools like this, laptop-based concert setups were often fragile, one-off, and hard for anyone but the original creator to reproduce; Integra aimed to make such performances easier to build, share, and preserve.
