Part of the Division of Signals and Systems at Uppsala University's Department of Electrical Engineering. This group researches how to make speakers and headphones sound more accurate: room and loudspeaker correction (fixing how a room's shape distorts sound), sound field synthesis (controlling sound with many speakers at once), binaural audio (making headphones sound like real 3D space), and active noise control (using speakers to cancel unwanted noise, e.g. in cars). It is academically the same lineage that produced the room-correction company Dirac Research, though Dirac is tracked separately as a company.
