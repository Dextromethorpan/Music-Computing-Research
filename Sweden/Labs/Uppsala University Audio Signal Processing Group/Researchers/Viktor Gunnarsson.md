Viktor Gunnarsson completed his PhD at Uppsala University's Signals and Systems division in September 2024, researching binaural audio: how to make headphones convincingly recreate real 3D sound. His work covered new HRTF (head-related transfer function) measurement methods and fixes for tonal coloration in spatial audio rendering. He has since moved into industry at Dirac Research, the Uppsala-founded room-correction company.
