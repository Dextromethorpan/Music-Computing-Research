Anders Ahlén is a Professor of Signal Processing at Uppsala University's Division of Signals and Systems, where he has long led research on audio systems, room and loudspeaker correction, and sound field control. He co-authored foundational research (with Lars-Johan Brännmark of Dirac Research) on multichannel room correction with focus control — work that connects this academic group directly to Dirac's commercial room-correction product.
