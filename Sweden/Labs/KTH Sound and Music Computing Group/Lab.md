Part of the Division of Media Technology and Interaction Design at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm (historically linked to the Department of Speech, Music and Hearing). This is the strongest and most active Sound and Music Computing research group in Sweden, working on expressive music performance, sonification (turning data or movement into sound), robot sound design, and accessible music technology.
