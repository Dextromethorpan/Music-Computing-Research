This research turns the movement of Swedish folk dancers into sound in real time — the dancers' steps and body motion are tracked by sensors, and the data is converted into audible tones and rhythms as they dance. The goal is accessibility: someone who cannot see the dance, or wants a new way to study it, can instead "hear" the structure and timing of the dance pattern. It also connects to Swedish folk music research, since the polska (a traditional dance/tune type) has a distinctive, hard-to-notate rhythmic feel that sound can sometimes capture better than sheet music.
