This doctoral thesis (a joint project between KTH and the KMH Royal College of Music) looks at how to design web-based, browser-run music tools — like interactive instruments or sound apps that work directly in a web browser without extra software — so that they are usable by people with disabilities. It studies technical standards (such as Web Audio, the technology browsers use to generate and process sound) and proposes guidelines so future browser-based music tools are inclusive by default rather than as an afterthought.
