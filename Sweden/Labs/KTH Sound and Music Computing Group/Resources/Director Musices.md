Director Musices is a computer program built at KTH that takes a plain musical score (the notes on a page) and makes it sound like a real musician is playing it, rather than a flat, robotic rendering. It does this by automatically adjusting small details of timing, loudness, and articulation the way an experienced human performer naturally would — for example, slightly slowing down at the end of a phrase or leaning into an emotionally important note. It grew out of decades of research at KTH into what makes musical performance sound "expressive" rather than mechanical, and it remains a long-running research and teaching tool for the group.
