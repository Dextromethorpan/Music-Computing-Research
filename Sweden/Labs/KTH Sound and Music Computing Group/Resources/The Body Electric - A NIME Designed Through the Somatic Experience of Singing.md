This paper describes a newly built electronic musical instrument (a "New Interface for Musical Expression," or NIME) designed around the physical sensations a singer feels in their own body while singing — like vibration in the chest or throat — rather than around a keyboard or touchscreen. It's an example of designing instruments from the performer's bodily experience outward, aiming for an instrument that feels natural to a singer's own physical intuition instead of forcing them to learn an unrelated interface.
