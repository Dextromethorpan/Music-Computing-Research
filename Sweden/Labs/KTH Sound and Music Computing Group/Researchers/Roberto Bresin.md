Roberto Bresin leads KTH's Sound and Music Computing group and has spent decades researching how computers can play or respond to music expressively — capturing the small human touches (timing, dynamics, emotion) that make a performance feel alive rather than mechanical. His more recent work extends this into sonification (turning data and movement into sound) and designing sounds for social robots so people find them easier to understand and trust.
