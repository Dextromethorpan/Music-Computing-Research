Net Insight is a Stockholm-based, publicly traded (Nasdaq Stockholm) media networking company. Its "Nimbra" product line moves live audio and video for broadcasters and sports leagues over IP networks with very low delay, using and helping shape industry standards like SMPTE ST 2110 and the JPEG XS video/audio compression format — the kind of protocol- and codec-level infrastructure work the vault's broadened company criteria specifically covers, even without academic-style published papers.
