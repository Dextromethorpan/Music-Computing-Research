When a broadcaster sends live audio or video over two separate network paths at once (for backup), a receiver normally has to briefly pause or glitch when switching from a failed path to the working one. This patent describes a way to combine two live streams so the switch-over happens invisibly and inaudibly, with no dropped frames or clicks, by buffering and stitching together data from whichever path is currently healthy. This kind of "hitless failover" is core to how Net Insight's Nimbra network keeps live sports and news broadcasts running smoothly even when part of the internet or a private network briefly breaks.
