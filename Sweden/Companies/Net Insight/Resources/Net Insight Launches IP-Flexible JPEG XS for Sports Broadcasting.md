This announcement describes Net Insight's Nimbra hardware being upgraded, via software, to support several official broadcast industry standards at once: SDI (the older cable-based video/audio format), SMPTE ST 2110 and ST 2022-6 (standards for sending uncompressed video/audio over IP networks), and the JPEG XS video compression standard (used when full uncompressed transport is too costly). It also references SMPTE RP 2129, a standard for keeping IP media networks secure. This shows the kind of active, hands-on standards-based engineering work — not academic papers, but real deployed infrastructure built to industry specifications — that the vault's company criteria are meant to capture for protocol and codec companies.
