This technical blog post explains lossless and visually-lossless compression — squeezing a live video and audio signal down to a fraction of its original size for transport over a network, without a viewer or listener being able to tell anything was compressed at all. It compares older approaches like MPEG-4 with JPEG 2000 and explains why broadcasters need compression that is both extremely fast (real time, not just efficient) and mathematically predictable, since dropped or corrupted frames are not acceptable in live sports and news production.
