Per Lindgren is Net Insight's Group CTO and a founding member of the company, with a PhD in Telecommunications from KTH Royal Institute of Technology and more than twenty patents to his name. He leads the company's technical direction for the Nimbra media transport network and its network time-synchronization products, which keep audio and video streams precisely timed across broadcast and mobile networks.
