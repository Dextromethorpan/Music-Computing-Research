Hans Sjöstrand leads Product Management for network synchronization at Net Insight, work he joined in 2021. Earlier in his career at Ericsson he was a technical leader on the MPLS networking project and co-authored MPLS specifications. At Net Insight he focuses on precise network timing for media transport and 5G networks, and has spoken publicly at industry events (Netnod, WSTS) about synchronization over shared and wholesale telecom networks.
