Dirac Research is a digital signal processing company founded in 2001 in Uppsala by PhD students from Uppsala University's Signals and Systems group. It builds "Dirac Live," room and speaker correction technology licensed to car makers (BMW, Volvo, Bentley) and audio brands, based on real academic DSP research and a public technical track record.
