This article explains "room correction" — technology that fixes the way a room's shape and speaker placement distort sound before it reaches your ears. Older correction methods only fixed the sound at one "sweet spot" listening position; Dirac's spatial approach instead gets multiple speakers working together so the correction improves sound quality throughout the whole room, not just in one chair. It was also republished as a technical article in the trade magazine audioXpress.
