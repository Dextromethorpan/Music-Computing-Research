This paper describes IVAS, the newest phone and video-call audio standard, which — unlike older call audio that is flat mono or stereo — supports spatial (3D) sound, so you can hear roughly where each speaker on a call is positioned, or experience richer, more natural-sounding group calls and virtual meetings. Ericsson was one of the companies that helped develop and standardize this technology as part of the 3GPP group (the international body that sets mobile-network technical standards).
