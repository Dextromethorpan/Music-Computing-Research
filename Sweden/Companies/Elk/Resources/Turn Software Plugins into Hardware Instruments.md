In this Audio Developer Conference talk, Elk's CTO explains how the company's real-time audio operating system lets manufacturers take an existing software audio plugin (a piece of software that processes or generates sound inside a music program) and turn it into a standalone piece of hardware, like a guitar pedal or a synthesizer, without having to rebuild the sound-processing logic from scratch.
