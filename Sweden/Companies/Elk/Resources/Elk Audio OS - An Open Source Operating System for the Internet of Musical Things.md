This peer-reviewed journal article describes Elk Audio OS, a piece of software (an operating system, like the software layer under apps on a phone) built specifically so musical instruments and audio devices can process sound with almost no delay. A musician playing an instrument built on this software doesn't notice any lag between pressing a key and hearing the sound — which matters a lot for anything from smart guitars to instruments that connect and jam together over a network, an idea the paper calls the "Internet of Musical Things."
