Elk is a Stockholm-based company that grew out of collaboration with KTH Royal Institute of Technology. It builds Elk Audio OS, an open-source, ultra-low-latency operating system for musical instruments and audio hardware, and has a genuine peer-reviewed research track record alongside its product work.
