Kilohearts AB is a small, self-owned audio plugin studio based in Linköping, Sweden, founded in 2012. It makes DAW (digital audio workstation) plugins for music production and sound design — synths and effects such as Phase Plant, Multipass, and Snap Heap. It's included here not for published research (it has no papers, patents, or technical blog) but for a genuinely novel, well-documented technical approach: its "Snapin" architecture, a modular system that lets any effect or modulator be freely combined, chained in series or parallel, and modulated by any other module — effectively turning audio effects into interchangeable building blocks shared across all of Kilohearts' products.
