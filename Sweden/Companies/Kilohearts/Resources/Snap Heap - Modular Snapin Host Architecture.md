Snap Heap is an audio effects plugin built around a modular architecture: instead of one fixed effect, it gives you up to seven parallel "lanes," each of which can hold a chain of small effect modules called Snapins (things like distortion, chorus, or frequency shifting). Lanes can be wired in series (one after another) or in parallel (processed side by side and mixed back together), so a musician can build a completely custom effects chain rather than being stuck with a preset signal path. The same Snapin modules also work standalone in any DAW, or nested inside Kilohearts' other host plugins (Multipass, Phase Plant), so one small library of building blocks gets reused everywhere — this reuse-across-products design is the specific technical idea that makes it more than "just another effects plugin."
