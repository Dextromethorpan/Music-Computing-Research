Anders Stenberg is a co-founder and one of three owner-employees of Kilohearts, based in Linköping, Sweden. He holds a Master of Science in Engineering from Linköping University and previously worked at Mode17, Powerchallenge AB, Ageia Technologies, and Meqon Research AB before co-founding Kilohearts in 2012. He is a key developer behind Phase Plant and the shared Snapin/modulation architecture used across Kilohearts' product line.
