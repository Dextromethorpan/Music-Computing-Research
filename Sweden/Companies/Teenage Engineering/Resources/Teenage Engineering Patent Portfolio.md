Teenage Engineering holds more than two dozen granted US patents covering the engineering behind its synthesizers and sample-based instruments — for example, mechanisms for an "interactive music player" that lets a user mix and layer multiple tracks live using simple hardware controls. This is real invention activity showing the company's in-house engineering work, though it is filed as patents rather than published as academic papers.
