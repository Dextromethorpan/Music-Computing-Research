This paper describes how to make AI text-to-speech systems — software that reads text out loud in a synthetic voice, like the "AI DJ" feature in the Spotify app — run faster and cheaper without losing voice quality. It uses a technique called knowledge distillation, where a smaller, quicker AI model is trained to mimic the behavior of a larger, slower one, so the smaller model can be deployed more cheaply while sounding almost as good.
