This blog post explains how Spotify's researchers can take a finished, fully mixed song and split it back apart into its individual instruments — isolating just the vocals, just the drums, just the bass, and so on. This process, called source separation, helps Spotify's systems "understand" what's actually happening musically inside a track (for example, whether a song is vocal-heavy or instrumental-heavy), which can then feed into search, recommendation, or remixing features.
