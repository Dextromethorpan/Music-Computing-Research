LLARK is a chatbot-style AI model that can listen to a piece of music and then answer questions about it in plain English — for example, describing its genre, mood, or instrumentation, or comparing it to another track. It combines a language model (the kind of AI behind chatbots) with audio understanding, so it can reason about sound the way a text-based chatbot reasons about words. This is a step toward AI systems that can talk intelligently about what music actually sounds like, not just what it's labeled as.
