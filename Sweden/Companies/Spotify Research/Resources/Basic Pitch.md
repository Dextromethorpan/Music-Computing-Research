Basic Pitch is a free, open-source tool that listens to an audio recording — say, someone playing guitar or piano — and automatically converts what it hears into note data (in MIDI format, a standard way computers represent musical notes). This is called automatic music transcription: instead of a musician writing down what they played by ear, the software does it for them. Basic Pitch works across many instruments and even multiple notes played at once (polyphony), which is technically much harder than transcribing a single melody line.
