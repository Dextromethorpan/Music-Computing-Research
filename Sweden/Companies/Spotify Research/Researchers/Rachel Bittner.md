Rachel Bittner works on music transcription (converting audio into notes) and source separation (splitting a mixed song into its individual instruments), and is one of the creators of Spotify's Basic Pitch tool. Her broader research area is "machine listening" — teaching computers to extract structured, useful information out of raw audio.
