Inma Hernáez Rioja is a professor at the University of the Basque Country and heads Aholab. Her research covers text-to-speech synthesis, emotional/expressive speech, and speech databases for Basque, and she has been a driving force behind the lab's long-running AhoTTS system and its various speech corpora projects.
