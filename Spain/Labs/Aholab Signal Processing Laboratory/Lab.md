Aholab is the Signal Processing Laboratory of the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU) in Bilbao, part of the Basque Center for Language Technology (HiTZ). It focuses on text-to-speech (TTS) and speech/speaker recognition, primarily for Basque and Spanish, and is best known for its AhoTTS synthesizer.
