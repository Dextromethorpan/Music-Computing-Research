Bizkaifon is a large collection of speech and video recordings documenting the many local Western dialects of Basque, spoken across the villages of Bizkaia. Most recordings are transcribed into Standard Basque and are searchable online, preserving traditional songs, sayings, and legends that might otherwise disappear as regional dialects fade. Beyond its cultural preservation value, this kind of labeled, dialect-diverse speech data is also valuable raw material for training speech recognition and synthesis systems that need to handle real regional variation, not just a single "standard" accent.
