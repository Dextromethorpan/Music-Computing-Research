Building a good text-to-speech voice requires a carefully recorded database of many spoken sentences from the same speaker, covering the full range of sounds in a language. This paper, presented at the LREC language-resources conference, describes the design and recording of several such speech databases created by Aholab specifically for Basque, aimed at producing high-quality synthetic voices as well as supporting related tasks like voice conversion. It reflects the practical, unglamorous groundwork — careful recording, labeling, and organizing of speech data — that underlies working TTS systems like AhoTTS.
