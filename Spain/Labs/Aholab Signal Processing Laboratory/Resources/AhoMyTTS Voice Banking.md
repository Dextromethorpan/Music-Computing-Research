AhoMyTTS is a web platform that lets ordinary people (not just researchers) create their own personalized synthetic voice with minimal technical effort — a process known as "voice banking." A common real-world use for this is helping people who are losing the ability to speak, for example due to a degenerative illness like ALS, to record their voice in advance so a text-to-speech system can keep "speaking" in their own voice later. The project frames volunteering a voice as a donation that also helps expand Aholab's speech data resources.
