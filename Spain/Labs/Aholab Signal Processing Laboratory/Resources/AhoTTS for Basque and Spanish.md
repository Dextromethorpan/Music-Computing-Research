AhoTTS is Aholab's text-to-speech system that reads written text out loud in Basque and Spanish, two languages that historically had far less commercial voice-assistant support than English. It is available as open-source code, a downloadable desktop tool, and free mobile apps (Android and iOS), and its synthetic voices are used by Basque-language media outlets and dictionary websites. Because Basque is a minority language with relatively few speakers compared to major world languages, having a solid, actively maintained open TTS system for it is significant for keeping the language usable in modern technology.
