AHOcoder is a speech "vocoder" — a tool that breaks down a recorded voice into a compact set of numbers describing its pitch, spectral shape, and other properties, and can then rebuild an audio waveform from those numbers. This kind of encode-decode pipeline is a building block used inside statistical and machine-learning speech synthesis systems: instead of directly generating raw audio, a model can generate the simpler vocoder parameters and let AHOcoder turn them back into sound. It has been used as a component inside Aholab's own TTS research and shared with the wider speech-processing research community.
