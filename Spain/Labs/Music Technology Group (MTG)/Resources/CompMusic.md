CompMusic was a major MTG research project (funded by a European Research Council grant) that applied computational analysis to non-Western musical traditions, including Hindustani and Carnatic music from India, Turkish-Ottoman music, Arab-Andalusian music, and Beijing opera. Rather than assuming that computer tools built for Western pop and classical music work everywhere, the project built new datasets, analysis methods, and search tools tailored to the actual structure of each tradition — for example, how melody and rhythm concepts differ across these cultures. The project's datasets and tools are still used today for research in what is now called computational musicology, and it pushed the wider MIR field to think harder about cultural bias in its methods.
