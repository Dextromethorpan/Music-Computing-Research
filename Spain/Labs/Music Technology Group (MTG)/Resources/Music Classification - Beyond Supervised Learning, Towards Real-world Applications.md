This is an open, freely available online book/tutorial written by MTG-affiliated researchers that explains how machine learning is used to automatically classify music — for example, tagging a song's genre, mood, or instrumentation. It walks through the standard "supervised learning" approach (where a model learns from lots of manually labeled examples) and then covers newer, more data-efficient approaches such as self-supervised learning, where a model learns useful patterns from unlabeled audio before being fine-tuned on a smaller labeled dataset. It is aimed at bridging academic research and the practical challenges of building real music classification systems, and is widely used as a teaching resource in the field.
