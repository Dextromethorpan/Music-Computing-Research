Neural singing synthesizers are AI systems that can generate a realistic singing voice from written notes and lyrics, similar to how text-to-speech systems generate spoken voice. Normally, training one of these systems to sound like a specific singer requires many hours of that singer's recordings. This paper shows a method that needs far less data: it starts from a voice model trained on many different singers, then "adapts" it to a new target singer using only a small amount of that person's singing, a technique often called voice cloning. This kind of work underpins commercial products like Vocaloid-style singing synthesizers and grew out of MTG's long research line on singing voice technology, which also produced the Voctro Labs spin-off company.
