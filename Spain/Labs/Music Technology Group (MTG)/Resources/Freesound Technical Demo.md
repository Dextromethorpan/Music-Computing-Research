Freesound is a large, free online collection of sound recordings (footsteps, rain, instrument notes, machine noises, and more) that anyone can upload and download for use in music, film, games, or research. This paper describes the technology behind the platform: how uploaded sounds are automatically analyzed and tagged so people can search for the sound they need, and how the site uses collaborative features (ratings, comments, packs of related sounds) to keep the collection organized and useful. Freesound has become a standard resource used across the sound and music computing research community, both as a source of training data and as a real product with hundreds of thousands of users.
