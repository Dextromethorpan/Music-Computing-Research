Emilia Gómez is a researcher associated with MTG whose work spans music information retrieval and, notably, the responsible and trustworthy use of AI — including in music technology. She has been a co-author on MTG's core audio-analysis research (including the Essentia library) and has worked on questions of algorithmic bias and ethics in music AI systems.
