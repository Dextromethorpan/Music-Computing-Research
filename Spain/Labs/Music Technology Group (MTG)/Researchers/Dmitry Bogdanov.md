Dmitry Bogdanov is a senior researcher and the lead developer of Essentia, MTG's widely used open-source library for analyzing audio and extracting musical descriptors (tempo, key, mood, and more). His work focuses on large-scale audio analysis and semantic annotation of music collections using signal processing and machine learning.
