Jordi Bonada has spent many years at MTG researching singing voice synthesis, analysis, and voice transformation — technology that lets software generate or modify a sung vocal performance. In 2011 he co-founded Voctro Labs, a spin-off company built on this research (later acquired by Voicemod), and he has continued to publish on neural approaches to singing synthesis together with colleagues like Merlijn Blaauw.
