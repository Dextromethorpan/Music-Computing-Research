Xavier Serra founded MTG in 1994 and has led it ever since, building it into one of the world's leading Sound and Music Computing research groups. He holds a PhD in Computer Music from Stanford and has worked across audio analysis/synthesis, music information retrieval, and — more recently — the ethical and cultural implications of AI applied to music, including how computational tools handle music from non-Western traditions. He also coordinates UPF's Master in Sound and Music Computing.
