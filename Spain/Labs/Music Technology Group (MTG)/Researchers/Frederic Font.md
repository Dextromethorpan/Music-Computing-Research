Frederic Font leads the Freesound project and all Freesound-related research at MTG, and is also affiliated with Phonos, MTG's associated music technology research center. His research sits in Machine Listening and Music Information Retrieval, with a focus on making large collections of sound recordings easier to search, describe, and reuse in creative and scientific work.
