The Music Technology Group (MTG) is a research group at Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF) in Barcelona, founded in 1994 by Xavier Serra. It is one of the world's leading research groups in Sound and Music Computing, working on how computers can analyze, understand, and generate sound and music — spanning signal processing, machine learning, music perception, and computational musicology.
