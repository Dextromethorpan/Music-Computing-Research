Isabel Barbancho is a professor at the University of Málaga whose research covers musical acoustics, digital signal processing, and machine learning applied to music. She has led several projects on polyphonic music transcription, optical music recognition (reading sheet music from scanned images), and music information retrieval, and is a co-author on much of ATIC's core singing-transcription and score-recognition research.
