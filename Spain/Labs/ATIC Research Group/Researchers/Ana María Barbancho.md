Ana María Barbancho is an associate professor at the University of Málaga working within ATIC on optical music recognition (getting software to read printed or handwritten sheet music) and singing-voice analysis. She has co-authored research on recognizing music notation without damaging the notes during processing, including work on historical notation styles used in old manuscripts.
