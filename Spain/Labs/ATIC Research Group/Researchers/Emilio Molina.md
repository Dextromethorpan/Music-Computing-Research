Emilio Molina completed his PhD at the University of Málaga in 2017 on "Singing Information Processing: Techniques and Applications," done within ATIC. His work focuses on automatically transcribing and scoring sung performances — figuring out the pitch and timing of a human voice from a recording — and on turning that analysis into interactive applications like voice-controlled instruments.
