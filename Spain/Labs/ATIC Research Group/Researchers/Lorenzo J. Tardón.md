Lorenzo J. Tardón leads the ATIC Research Group at the University of Málaga, where he has worked in the Department of Communications Engineering since 1999. His research spans audio and music signal processing, including singing voice transcription, optical music recognition, and music information retrieval, and he was a driving force behind ATIC hosting the SMC 2019 conference.
