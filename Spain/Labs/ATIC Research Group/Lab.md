ATIC (Aplicación de las Tecnologías de la Información y Comunicaciones) is a research group at the ETSI Telecomunicación, University of Málaga, founded in 2007. It works on audio and music signal processing, music information retrieval, machine learning, musical acoustics, optical music recognition, and EEG (brainwave) signal processing related to music; it organized the SMC 2019 international Sound and Music Computing Conference.
