Presented at ISMIR (the main international conference for music information retrieval research), this paper tackles a problem that sounds boring but is actually crucial: how do you fairly measure whether one singing-transcription system is better than another? Different research teams had been using inconsistent ways of scoring transcription accuracy, making results hard to compare. ATIC proposed a standardized evaluation framework — a common, agreed-upon way to measure how close an automatically transcribed melody is to the ground truth — so future singing-transcription research could be benchmarked on equal footing.
