SMC (Sound and Music Computing) is one of the field's main annual international conferences, bringing together musicians, engineers, and computer scientists working on sound and music technology. ATIC organized and hosted the 16th edition in Málaga in 2019, themed "Music and Interaction" — covering topics from music information retrieval and sonification to new musical interfaces and serious games. Hosting a conference like this is a strong signal of a group's standing in the field, since it involves reviewing and curating the year's international research submissions.
