VOICE2TUBA is a system that lets someone "play" a virtual musical instrument, like a tuba, just by humming or singing into a microphone — the system analyzes the pitch and timing of the voice in real time and drives a synthesized instrument sound that matches. This means people without instrumental training can still create expressive, instrument-like performances using only their voice as the controller. The work builds on ATIC's earlier research into pitch tracking and singing transcription, repurposing those analysis techniques for a real-time, interactive music-creation tool rather than only for offline scoring.
