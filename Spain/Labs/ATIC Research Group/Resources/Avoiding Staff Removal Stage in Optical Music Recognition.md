Optical Music Recognition (OMR) is the sheet-music equivalent of OCR (text scanning) — software that looks at a photo or scan of a printed musical score and figures out what notes are written on it. Most OMR systems first try to erase the five horizontal staff lines from the image, because they visually clutter the note symbols, but this "staff removal" step often accidentally damages the notes themselves. This paper, focused on scores written in an older notation style called white mensural notation (used in centuries-old manuscripts), proposes a method that recognizes the notes without needing to remove the staff lines first, improving recognition of historical scores that are important for musicology and archive digitization.
