Sfëar is Eurecat's own immersive 3D audio technology, first shown publicly at the Sónar music and technology festival in Barcelona. It packages software plugins for producing "binaural" audio (3D sound designed for headphones that makes sounds seem to come from specific points in space around the listener) together with a head-tracking device, so that the sound field actually rotates and stays fixed in space as the listener turns their head — similar to how real-world sound behaves. It grew out of the team's earlier work founding the 3D-audio spin-off ImmSound (later acquired by Dolby), and has been applied in music production, live performances, and even 3D-audio restaurant installations.
