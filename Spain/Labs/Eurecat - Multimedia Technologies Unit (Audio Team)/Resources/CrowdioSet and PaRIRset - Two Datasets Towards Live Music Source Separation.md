"Music source separation" is the task of taking a mixed recording (say, a live concert) and splitting it back apart into its individual ingredients — vocals, drums, crowd noise. This paper introduces two new datasets to help train AI models to do that specifically for live concert recordings, which are much messier than studio recordings. CrowdioSet contains real crowd-noise recordings and synthetic sing-alongs, while PaRIRset contains "room impulse responses" (recordings that capture how a specific room or venue colors sound) taken with a microphone array across 40 real concert venues with PA systems. Together, training on these datasets improves source separation quality on real live recordings compared to only using studio data.
