Eurecat is an applied research center (Centre Tecnològic de Catalunya) based in Barcelona. Within its Multimedia Technologies Unit, the Audio Team specializes in spatial audio, immersive listening, perceptual audio enhancement, and intelligent audio analysis and generation — building on decades of experience as pioneers of 3D audio (the team's earlier spin-off ImmSound was later acquired by Dolby).
