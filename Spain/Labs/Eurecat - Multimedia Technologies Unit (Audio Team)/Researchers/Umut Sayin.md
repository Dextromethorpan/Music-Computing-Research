Umut Sayin leads the Audio Group within Eurecat's Multimedia Technologies Unit. He holds an MSc in Acoustics from Aalborg University and a PhD in Acoustics from La Salle, and his work centers on binaural audio (3D sound designed for headphones), virtual sound environments, and hearing-aid research, including HRTF (a mathematical model of how the shape of a person's head and ears change sound) measurement and prototyping.
