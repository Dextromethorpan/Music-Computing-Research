Rafael Redondo Tejedor directs Eurecat's Multimedia Technologies Unit, which covers the Audio, Image, and Visualization teams. He holds a PhD in Computer Vision and a Master's in Sonology, and his own research has spanned biological models of human vision, image compression, and — more recently — deep learning applied to multimodal generative AI, including sound-to-video generation.
