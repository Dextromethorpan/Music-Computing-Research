Timothy Schmele is a senior researcher at Eurecat focused on spatial audio and sound synthesis, and is also pursuing a doctorate at the University of Music in Karlsruhe on the compositional possibilities of spatialized electronic music. He developed "Spatial Matrix Synthesis," a way of generating new sounds directly from spatial-movement mathematics, and has also studied how the position of a sound source in space affects listeners' emotional responses.
