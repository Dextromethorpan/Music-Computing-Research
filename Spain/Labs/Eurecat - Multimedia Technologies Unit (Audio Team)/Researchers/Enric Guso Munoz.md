Enric Gusó Muñoz is a PhD student at Eurecat, jointly connected with the Music Technology Group at Universitat Pompeu Fabra. His research focuses on music and speech source separation — teaching AI models to split a mixed audio recording back into its individual sound sources — including work on evaluation metrics for separation quality, hearing-aid speech enhancement, and building datasets for live-concert source separation.
