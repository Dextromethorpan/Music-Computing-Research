AffectiveLab (part of the Advanced Interfaces research group) is a research team within I3A, the Aragón Institute for Engineering Research at Universidad de Zaragoza. Its work focuses on Music Information Retrieval, musical acoustics, and using Artificial Intelligence (especially deep learning) to analyze, transcribe, and generate music.
