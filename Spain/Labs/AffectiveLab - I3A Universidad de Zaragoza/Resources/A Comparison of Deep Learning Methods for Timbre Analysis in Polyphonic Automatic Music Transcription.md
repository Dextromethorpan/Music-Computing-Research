"Automatic music transcription" is the task of getting a computer to listen to a recording and write out which notes were played, similar to a musician transcribing a song by ear. This is much harder when several instruments play at once ("polyphonic" music), because the computer must also figure out which instrument ("timbre," the tonal color that lets you tell a violin from a trumpet) played each note. This paper compares several deep-learning (a type of AI that learns patterns from large amounts of data) approaches for recognizing instrument timbre within transcription systems, helping identify which techniques work best for separating and labeling instruments in a mix.
