"Exer-games" are video games that get people moving, sometimes played together with others (collaborative exer-games). This paper describes a system of music services that automatically picks or generates music to influence how players feel emotionally, aiming to boost motivation and physical performance during group exercise. The music services connect to IoT (Internet of Things — everyday devices like fitness trackers connected to the internet) and cloud-computing resources so that a game can react to players in real time. It's an example of AffectiveLab's broader interest in linking music, emotion, and interactive technology.
