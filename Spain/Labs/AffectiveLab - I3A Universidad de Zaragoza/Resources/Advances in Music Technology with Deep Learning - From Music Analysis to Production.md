This is the PhD thesis of Carlos Hernández Oliván, completed at Universidad de Zaragoza under the supervision of Jose R. Beltrán. It brings together several years of work on using deep learning (AI systems that learn from data) across the whole chain of music technology: analyzing existing music (like finding its structure or transcribing its notes), generating new music, and even assisting music production. It includes the musicaiz software toolkit and the graph-based structure-analysis methods as part of its contributions, making it a good single entry point into the lab's overall research direction.
