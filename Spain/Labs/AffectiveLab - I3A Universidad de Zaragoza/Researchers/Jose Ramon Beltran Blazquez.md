Jose Ramón Beltrán is a professor of Electronic Engineering and Communications at Universidad de Zaragoza and a researcher at I3A (Aragón Institute for Engineering Research). His research builds machine-learning systems that analyze, process, and generate musical signals, covering topics like automatic music transcription, symbolic music structure analysis, and AI-based music composition. He also does science outreach, regularly appearing on the Aragon Radio science program "Ágora."
