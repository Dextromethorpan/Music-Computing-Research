GTM (Grupo de Tecnologías Multimedia / Multimedia Technologies Group) is a research group at the School of Telecommunication Engineering, Universidade de Vigo, part of the AtlanTTic research center. It has over fifteen years of experience in speech technology — text-to-speech (TTS) and automatic speech recognition (ASR) for Galician and Spanish — as well as voice transformation, speaker verification/biometrics, acoustics, and image/video processing.
