Cotovía is a bilingual text-to-speech (TTS) system — software that reads written text out loud — built specifically for Galician and Spanish, two languages that had far less commercial speech-technology support than English. It works using a technique called unit-selection: instead of generating a voice completely from scratch, it stitches together short fragments of pre-recorded real speech, picking the best-matching fragments and pitch/rhythm pattern for the sentence being read. It ships with several ready-made voices (for example "Iago," a Galician male voice, and "Sabela," a Galician female voice) and remains one of the main open resources for Galician-language speech synthesis, with community plugins that let it work in modern voice-assistant software.
