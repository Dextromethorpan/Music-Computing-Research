Speaker diarisation is the task of automatically answering "who spoke when?" in an audio recording with multiple people talking — for example, splitting a meeting recording into segments by speaker without knowing in advance who's in the room. This paper describes GTM's system built for the Albayzin 2016 evaluation, a Spanish-language benchmark competition where different research teams' systems are tested and compared on the same recordings. It reflects GTM's broader expertise in analyzing and identifying speakers from audio, which connects to their voice-technology and speaker-verification research.
