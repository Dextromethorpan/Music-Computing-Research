José Luis Alba Castro is a full professor and senior researcher at AtlanTTic who heads GTM's Vision Laboratory. While much of his recent work is on computer vision and sign-language recognition (building datasets and recognition tools for Spanish Sign Language), he sits within GTM's wider multimedia signal processing research alongside the group's speech and audio work.
