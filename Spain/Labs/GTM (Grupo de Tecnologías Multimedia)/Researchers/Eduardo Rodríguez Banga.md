Eduardo Rodríguez Banga was a key developer of Cotovía, GTM's open text-to-speech system for Galician and Spanish, and has worked extensively on unit-selection speech synthesis — including how to score and select the best recorded speech fragments to join into natural-sounding sentences. His more recent research covers cross-lingual speaker adaptation and speaker de-identification for speech synthesis (making a synthetic voice sound like a different, unidentifiable speaker).
