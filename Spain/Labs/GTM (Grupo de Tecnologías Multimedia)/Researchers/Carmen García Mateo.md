Carmen García Mateo is a professor at the University of Vigo whose research covers speech processing, automatic speech recognition, and voice biometrics — including speaker verification and speaker diarisation (figuring out who spoke when in a recording with multiple people). She has led GTM's participation in national speech-technology benchmark evaluations such as Albayzin.
