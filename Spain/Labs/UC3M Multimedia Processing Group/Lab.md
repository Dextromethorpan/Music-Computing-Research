The Group of Multimedia Processing (GPM) is part of the Department of Signal Theory and Communications at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M). Its broad focus is speech, audio, image, and video processing — most of its output is about general speech recognition, video coding, and computer vision rather than music specifically, but it has published a genuine handful of papers on audio-signal-processing topics that overlap with music computing, such as speech/music discrimination and acoustic event classification, so this entry stays intentionally thin.
