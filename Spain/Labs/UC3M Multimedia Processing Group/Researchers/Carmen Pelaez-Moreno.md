Carmen Peláez-Moreno is a professor at UC3M's Department of Signal Theory and Communications, working on robust speech recognition, acoustic saliency (models of what makes a sound stand out perceptually), and information-theoretic tools for evaluating machine-learning systems. She frequently co-authors with Ascensión Gallardo-Antolín on audio and speech signal-processing research.
