Ascensión Gallardo-Antolín is an Associate Professor at UC3M's Department of Signal Theory and Communications. Her research centers on automatic speech recognition, audio classification and segmentation, multimedia information retrieval, and models of auditory/visual "salience" (what stands out and grabs attention in a signal). Within this broader speech-processing work, she has published specifically on distinguishing speech from music and song in audio recordings, and on acoustic event and species classification.
