Broadcast audio (like a radio or TV news program) usually mixes several kinds of content back to back or on top of each other: clean speech, speech with background noise, music, and speech playing over music. This paper describes a system, built jointly by UC3M and Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM) for a national evaluation challenge (Albayzín 2010), that automatically labels which of these categories each moment of a broadcast audio file belongs to. It uses Hidden Markov Models (a classic statistical technique for modeling sequences that change over time) and compares a simple one-step approach against a more layered ("hierarchical") one that uses different measurements for different categories.
