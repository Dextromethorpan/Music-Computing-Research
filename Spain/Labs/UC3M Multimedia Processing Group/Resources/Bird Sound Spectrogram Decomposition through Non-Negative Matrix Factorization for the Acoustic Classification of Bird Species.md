This paper is about automatically recognizing which bird species is making a sound in a recording, using a technique called Non-Negative Matrix Factorization (NMF) — a mathematical method that breaks a "spectrogram" (a visual/numeric picture of how the loudness of different frequencies changes over time) down into a small number of simpler recurring building-block patterns. It's included here because this same feature-extraction technique — breaking a sound down into a small number of recurring spectral patterns — is widely used in music computing for tasks like source separation and instrument recognition; here the group applies it instead to bioacoustics (the study of sounds made by animals).
