SPAT (Signal Processing & Acoustic Technology Group) is a research team within the School of Engineering (ETSE) at the Universitat de València, led by Maximo Cobos. It designs signal processing and machine learning methods for audio, acoustics, and multimedia technology, with particular strength in spatial audio, sound source localization and separation, acoustic event detection, room acoustics, and microphone arrays.
