ISLA-ALAMO was a Spanish government-funded research project (2019-2022) exploring how networks of distributed microphones, combined with machine learning, can automatically build a high-level description of a "soundscape" — identifying, locating, and tracking the sound sources that make it up (for example, recognizing that a particular sound is a car, a bird, or a conversation, and roughly where it is). The goal was to use that automatically extracted information to recreate soundscapes with interactive, context-aware features, rather than just recording and replaying raw audio.
