This was an industry-funded project where SPAT developed signal processing and machine learning software for a company (Visualfy) to automatically detect the presence of specific acoustic events — like a smoke alarm, a doorbell, or breaking glass — happening inside a home. It's an example of the group's research being applied directly to a commercial product, in this case supporting assistive/accessible smart-home technology rather than staying purely academic.
