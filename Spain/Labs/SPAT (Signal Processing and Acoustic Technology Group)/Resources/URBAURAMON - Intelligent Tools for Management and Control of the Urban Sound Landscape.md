URBAURAMON was a Spanish-government-funded project (2017-2020) that built platforms and protocols for monitoring and understanding the "urban soundscape" — the overall mix of noise and sound in a city — using an Internet of Things approach, with networks of connected sensors placed around urban areas. Beyond noise monitoring, the project also looked at protecting and studying "sound heritage": historically or culturally important sounds and acoustic spaces worth documenting and preserving.
