Francesc J. Ferri is a full professor at the Universitat de València whose core expertise is in pattern recognition, machine learning, and computer vision. Within SPAT he co-leads projects (such as ISLA-ALAMO) that apply machine learning to distributed-microphone soundscape analysis, contributing the machine learning and classification side of the group's audio localization and monitoring work.
