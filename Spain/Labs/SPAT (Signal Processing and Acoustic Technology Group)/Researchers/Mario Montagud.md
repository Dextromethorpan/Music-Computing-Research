Mario Montagud is a postdoctoral researcher affiliated with SPAT and also Research Director of the Media & Internet Unit at the i2CAT Foundation in Barcelona. His research spans interactive and immersive media (including virtual reality), accessibility, human-computer interaction, and quality of experience, building on an earlier PhD focused on synchronization and quality of service for networked media.
