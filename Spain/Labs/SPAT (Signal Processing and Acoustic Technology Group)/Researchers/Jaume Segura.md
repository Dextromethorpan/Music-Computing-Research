Jaume Segura is an associate professor at the Universitat de València with a background in physics and prior work on intelligent transportation systems. Within SPAT he co-led the URBAURAMON project on monitoring and managing urban soundscapes using Internet-of-Things sensor networks, and is a member of the Spanish and European acoustics societies.
