This paper describes a joint Vicomtech / Universidad de Granada submission to ASVspoof5, a major international competition for detecting fake or spoofed speech. It tackles two connected problems at once: telling a fake voice clip from a real one ("deepfake detection"), and "spoofing-aware speaker verification" (SASV) — confirming a voice really belongs to the claimed speaker while also being resistant to attackers who use a cloned or replayed voice to impersonate someone. Combining both defenses in one system is important for building voice-based security and authentication tools that can't be easily fooled by AI-generated speech.
