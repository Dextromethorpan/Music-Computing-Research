"Voice biometrics" means recognizing or confirming who is speaking based on the unique characteristics of their voice, similar to a fingerprint. This paper, presented at the IberSPEECH conference (a major Spanish/Portuguese-language speech-technology conference), describes Vicomtech's entry to the Spoofing-Aware Speaker Verification (SASV) Challenge — a competition that tests whether a voice-identity system can both correctly recognize a genuine speaker and reject an impostor who is using a synthetic or replayed copy of that speaker's voice.
