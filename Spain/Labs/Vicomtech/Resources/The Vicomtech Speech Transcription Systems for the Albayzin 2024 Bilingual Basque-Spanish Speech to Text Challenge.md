"Speech to text" (also called speech transcription) means automatically converting spoken audio into written words. This paper describes the systems Vicomtech built for a challenge that specifically tests transcription of speech that mixes two languages together — Basque and Spanish, both spoken in Vicomtech's home region — which is much harder for AI transcription systems than single-language speech because the system must recognize which language is being spoken from moment to moment as well as get the words right.
