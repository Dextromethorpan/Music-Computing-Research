Vicomtech is an applied, non-profit research center based in San Sebastián / Donostia (Basque Country), part of the Basque Research and Technology Alliance. Its Speech & Dialogue Technologies group works on speech recognition, transcription, speaker verification, and — closely tied to music/audio computing — detecting AI-generated ("deepfake") audio and voice.
