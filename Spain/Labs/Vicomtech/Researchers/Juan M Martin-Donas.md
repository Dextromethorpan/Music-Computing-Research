Juan Manuel Martín-Doñas is a researcher at Vicomtech whose work focuses on speech enhancement (cleaning up noisy speech), voice biometrics, and detecting AI-generated ("deepfake") audio and spoofed speaker-verification attacks. He is a lead author on several of Vicomtech's entries to international audio-deepfake-detection and anti-spoofing challenges.
