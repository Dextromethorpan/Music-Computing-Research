Aitor Álvarez Muniain is a Senior Researcher at Vicomtech with a PhD in Computer Science, working within the Speech & Dialogue Technologies group. His research covers automatic speech transcription and subtitling (including Basque-Spanish bilingual speech), speech emotion recognition, and audio deepfake / spoofing detection.
