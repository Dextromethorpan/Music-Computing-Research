Joaquín Mora is a researcher at the Universidad de Sevilla who has worked alongside José-Miguel Díaz-Báñez on the COFLA project since its early stages, contributing to the design of the flamenco audio corpus and to studies that combine musicological knowledge of flamenco singing styles with computational analysis of recordings.
