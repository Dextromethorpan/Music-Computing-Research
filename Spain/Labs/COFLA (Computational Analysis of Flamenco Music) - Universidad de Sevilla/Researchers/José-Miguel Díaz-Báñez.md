José-Miguel Díaz-Báñez is a professor at the Universidad de Sevilla who coordinates the COFLA project, which applies mathematics and computer science to the study of flamenco music. He works on turning recorded flamenco singing into data that computers can analyze — for example, tracking how a singer's melody moves — and has spent close to a decade building tools and datasets for this niche research area, which he sometimes calls the "Computational Theory of Flamenco."
