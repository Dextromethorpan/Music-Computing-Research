COFLA is a research group coordinated from the Department of Applied Mathematics II at the Universidad de Sevilla, working with collaborators at Universitat Pompeu Fabra's Music Technology Group. It applies computational and mathematical tools — audio analysis, pattern recognition, geometry — to study flamenco music, an oral-tradition genre with almost no written scores, building datasets and analysis tools that let computers "listen to" and describe flamenco singing.
