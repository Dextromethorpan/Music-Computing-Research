A sound level meter is a handheld or fixed instrument that measures how loud a sound is, usually in decibels, and a "spectrum analyser" breaks that sound down further into how much energy is present at each frequency (bass, midrange, treble, and so on) — useful for understanding not just how loud something is, but what it's made of. The SC250 is CESVA's class-1 (high-precision) instrument combining both functions, extendable from a basic sound level meter into a full spectrum analyser. This product page is an example of CESVA's patented, in-house-engineered measurement hardware, the kind of calibrated tool used in building acoustics, environmental noise studies, and other real-world sound measurement work that underpins acoustics research.
