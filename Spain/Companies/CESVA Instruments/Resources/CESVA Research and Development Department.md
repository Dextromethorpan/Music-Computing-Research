CESVA is a Barcelona company that has been designing and manufacturing its own acoustic measurement instruments — sound level meters, calibrators, vibration sensors — since 1969. This page describes its in-house R&D department, staffed by engineers across acoustics, mechanics, electronics, and software, which develops and patents CESVA's instrument designs rather than buying components from outside vendors and rebranding them. While CESVA's instruments are aimed at things like environmental noise measurement and building acoustics rather than music-making itself, this kind of precise, patented measurement hardware is part of the infrastructure that acoustics and audio researchers rely on to measure sound accurately in real spaces.
