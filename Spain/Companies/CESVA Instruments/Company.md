CESVA Instruments is a Barcelona-based manufacturer of acoustic measurement equipment, founded in 1969, that designs and builds its own sound level meters, calibrators, and noise-monitoring sensors. It qualifies via traditional R&D: it runs its own in-house Research & Development department staffed by acoustics, electronics, and software engineers, and holds multiple patents on its acoustic instrumentation — the kind of specialized measurement infrastructure that underlies acoustic and audio research more broadly (its instruments are used, among other things, to measure and calibrate sound in the physical spaces music technology researchers study).
