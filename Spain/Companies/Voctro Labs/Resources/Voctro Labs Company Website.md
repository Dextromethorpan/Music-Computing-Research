Voctro Labs' own company website describes it as a "creative voice technology" company based in Barcelona, founded in 2011 by four researchers formerly at the Music Technology Group of Universitat Pompeu Fabra (MTG-UPF). It summarizes the company's work across singing voice synthesis (making a computer sing in a chosen voice), voice conversion/transformation (changing how a voice sounds), and the company's long-running collaboration with Yamaha's R&D division on VOCALOID, the singing-voice-synthesizer software behind virtual singer characters such as Hatsune Miku, and later Yamaha-Voctro co-created virtual singers Bruno, Clara, and MAIKA. The site states that the founders are named inventors on more than 50 patents accumulated over their careers (most from the pre-Voctro Labs era working with Yamaha), reflecting how much of the underlying technology predates the company itself and was carried into it by its founding researchers.
