Holly+ is a digital clone of American musician Holly Herndon's singing voice, built using Voctro Labs' voice-cloning and singing-synthesis technology. Anyone can upload a recording of themselves singing or speaking, and the system converts it to sound like it was performed by Holly Herndon — a process called voice conversion. A song made with Holly+ became the first AI-voice track released commercially on Spotify, drawing wide press attention to what AI-generated vocals could sound like when built with consent from, and in collaboration with, the original singer. The project became one of Voctro Labs' best-known demonstrations of its research, and its underlying tech later fed into Voicemod's AI voice products after Voicemod acquired Voctro Labs in December 2022.
