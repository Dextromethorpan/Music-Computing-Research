This is Voicemod's own press release announcing that it acquired Voctro Labs in December 2022. It explains that Voctro Labs' four co-founders (Jordi Bonada, Jordi Janer, Merlijn Blaauw, Oscar Mayor) joined Voicemod's team immediately and took charge of its R&D department, and that Voctro Labs' core singing- and voice-AI technology would be folded into Voicemod's real-time voice-changing products (used mostly by gamers and streamers) as well as new "text-to-song" features. The release is useful background for understanding how a small, research-heavy spin-off company (Voctro Labs, itself a spin-off of Barcelona's Music Technology Group at Universitat Pompeu Fabra) became part of a larger commercial voice-tech company while keeping its research team intact.
