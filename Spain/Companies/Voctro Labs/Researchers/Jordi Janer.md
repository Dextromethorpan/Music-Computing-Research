Jordi Janer is a co-founder of Voctro Labs and served as the company's Director. Like the other founders, he came from the Music Technology Group at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, where his research included performance-driven control for sample-based singing voice synthesis — techniques for letting a live performance (rather than only a written score) drive how a synthesized singing voice behaves. After Voicemod's 2022 acquisition of Voctro Labs, he moved into a Technical Product Manager role at Voicemod.
