Merlijn Blaauw is a co-founder of Voctro Labs and its Principal Scientist, specializing in designing and building the software and machine-learning algorithms behind audio synthesis. He has a background in music-technology software development (having worked at companies like IK Multimedia and Sonic Timeworks before joining MTG-UPF) and, more recently, focuses on applying deep learning to singing-voice synthesis and voice cloning — the technology later used in Voctro Labs' Holly+ project with musician Holly Herndon. His university email (merlijn.blaauw@upf.edu) appears as a corresponding-author contact on his published research papers.
