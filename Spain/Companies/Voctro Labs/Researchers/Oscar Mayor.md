Oscar Mayor is a co-founder and was the Chief Technology Officer of Voctro Labs, overseeing the engineering behind the company's voice and singing-synthesis products, including the Voiceful cloud API/SDK. Before Voctro Labs, he was part of the Music Technology Group research team at Universitat Pompeu Fabra working on voice and audio technologies. He joined Voicemod's R&D leadership after the December 2022 acquisition of Voctro Labs.
