Jordi Bonada is one of the four co-founders of Voctro Labs and, before that, a longtime researcher at the Music Technology Group (MTG) of Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona. He works on making computers sing convincingly: separating a voice into pitch and timbre so a performance can be retuned to any melody, and using neural networks (including WaveNet-style models) to generate natural-sounding singing and speech from small amounts of training data. He has been a named inventor on singing-voice-synthesis patents dating back to Yamaha's original VOCALOID research, and continued this line of work as co-founder of Voctro Labs and later at Voicemod after its 2022 acquisition of Voctro Labs. His university email address (jordi.bonada@upf.edu) appears as a corresponding-author contact on his published research papers.
