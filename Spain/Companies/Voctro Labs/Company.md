Voctro Labs, S.L. is a Barcelona-based voice-technology company focused on singing voice synthesis (making a computer sing) and voice conversion/transformation (changing how a voice sounds), founded in 2011 as a spin-off of the Music Technology Group (MTG) at Universitat Pompeu Fabra. It was acquired by voice-AI company Voicemod in December 2022, with its founders taking over Voicemod's R&D department.
