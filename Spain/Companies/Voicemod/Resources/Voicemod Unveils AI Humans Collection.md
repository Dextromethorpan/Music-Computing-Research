This June 2023 press release describes Voicemod's "AI Humans" collection: 20 human-sounding AI voice characters (plus a randomizer), trained on recordings from professional voice actors. The pitch is that these voices are more natural and expressive than earlier robotic-sounding filters, because the system carries over changes in the speaker's real pitch, tone, and volume into the converted voice in real time, rather than just applying a fixed effect. Users can also adjust pitch to change the perceived age or gender of the resulting voice. The release frames this as part of a broader move toward "voice avatars" as a counterpart to visual avatars in online spaces.
