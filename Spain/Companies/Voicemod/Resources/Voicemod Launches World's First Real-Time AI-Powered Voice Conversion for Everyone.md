This June 2022 press release announced Voicemod's first AI-powered "speech-to-speech" voice conversion engine, which the company describes as a world first for real-time, consumer-facing use. Unlike older, simpler voice filters (which just shift pitch or add robotic effects), this system tries to build a genuinely new-sounding voice while still carrying over the emotion, accent, and pacing of the person actually speaking. It works live, with low enough delay to be used while talking in games and chat apps like Discord, Zoom, and Google Meet, rather than only being usable on pre-recorded audio.
