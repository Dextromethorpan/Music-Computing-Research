In September 2022, Voicemod announced it had received a public research grant from the Valencian regional government's PIDI-CV program (a Spanish public funding scheme for small/medium company R&D) to run a project called REVORM — short for "Transformación ética y responsable de la voz" ("ethical and responsible transformation of voice"). The project's stated goal is to research three safety-related technologies for AI voice conversion: audio watermarking (embedding a hidden, detectable signal in AI-generated audio so it can be identified later), deepfake detection (spotting audio that has been artificially generated or altered), and automatic moderation (automatically flagging misuse of the voice-changing technology). This is Voicemod's clearest public commitment to researching the ethics and safety side of its own voice-changing product, not just the voice technology itself.
