Voicemod is a Valencia-based company that makes real-time AI voice-changing software (used mostly by gamers and streamers on platforms like Discord and Twitch), and it runs an internal R&D team — largely built from its December 2022 acquisition of Barcelona voice-tech spin-off Voctro Labs — that researches low-latency AI voice conversion, voice ethics/safety technology such as audio watermarking and deepfake detection, and on-device (NPU) acceleration for its voice models.
