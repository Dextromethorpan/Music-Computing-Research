Jordi Janer co-founded Voctro Labs (a voice/audio AI spin-off of Barcelona's Music Technology Group) in 2011 and, after Voicemod's December 2022 acquisition of Voctro Labs, now leads Voicemod's R&D department. He has over 20 years of research experience in audio and voice processing, including source separation, voice signal analysis, and — most recently at Voicemod — real-time voice conversion systems designed to run efficiently on-device.
