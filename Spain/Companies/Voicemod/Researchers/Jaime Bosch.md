Jaime Bosch co-founded Voicemod in Valencia in 2014 with his brothers Fernando (CTO) and Juan (CPO), and leads the company as CEO. He speaks publicly about Voicemod's push into AI-powered voice conversion — pushing for both highly realistic-sounding voices and very low delay, so the technology can be used live in games, streams, and calls rather than only on pre-recorded audio.
