Pritish Chandna leads AI voice-processing and generative-modelling research at Voicemod. He holds a PhD from Universitat Pompeu Fabra on using neural networks to isolate singing voices from full songs, and at Voicemod his work has focused on making real-time voice conversion systems more data-efficient and better able to run on constrained hardware such as on-device chips.
