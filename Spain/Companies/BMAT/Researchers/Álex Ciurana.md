Álex Ciurana is a research engineer at BMAT Licensing S.L. who has co-authored the company's core audio fingerprinting research, including the BAF broadcast-monitoring dataset paper and a 2025 study on using music source separation (splitting a mix back into isolated parts like vocals or music) to improve TV broadcast monitoring.
