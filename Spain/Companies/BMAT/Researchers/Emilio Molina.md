Emilio Molina is a research scientist at BMAT who appears as an author or supervisor across most of the company's published research, spanning audio fingerprinting for broadcast monitoring, setlist identification at live concerts, and estimating how audible background music is in TV broadcasts. He also supervises industrial PhD projects run jointly between BMAT and the Music Technology Group at Universitat Pompeu Fabra.
