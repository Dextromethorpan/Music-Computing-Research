Guillem Cortès Sebastià is a Research Engineer at BMAT working on music identification and metadata reconciliation, focused on making audio fingerprinting (recognizing recordings from short, noisy clips) more robust — for example when broadcast audio is sped up, slowed down, or mixed with background noise. He completed an industrial PhD on audio fingerprinting jointly with the Music Technology Group at Universitat Pompeu Fabra and did a research internship at Deezer Research, and he is a maintainer of the open-source Mirdata/Soundata libraries used across the music-computing research community.
