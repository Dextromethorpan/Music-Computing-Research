At a live concert, the actual performance of a song is rarely identical to its studio recording — the tempo, key, arrangement, or even the singer's phrasing can all shift. This ICASSP 2021 paper studies "setlist identification": automatically figuring out which songs were played at a live show, and exactly when, purely from an audio recording of the concert. The authors treat this as a "version retrieval" problem — the same computational family used for finding cover songs (different performances of the same underlying composition) — and compare three such systems to see which handles live-performance changes best. They also release a new dataset of nearly 100 hours of annotated concert recordings, with timestamps for when each song starts and ends, to support further research on this industrially useful but previously under-studied task.
