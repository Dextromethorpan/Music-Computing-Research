BMAT (Barcelona Music and Audio Technologies) is a Barcelona-based music-tech company that started as a research spin-off of the Music Technology Group (MTG) at Universitat Pompeu Fabra. It builds broadcast monitoring and music-identification technology (audio fingerprinting) used by rights holders, publishers, and broadcasters to track how and where music is played, and its in-house R&D team continues to publish research on fingerprinting robustness, live-event identification, background-music audibility, and auditing AI-generated music.
