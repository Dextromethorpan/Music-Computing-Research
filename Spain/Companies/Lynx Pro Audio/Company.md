Lynx Pro Audio is a professional loudspeaker and amplifier manufacturer based in Picassent, Valencia. It qualifies via the documented product/architectural innovation path: its in-house DSPB-FL digital signal processing architecture and its Rainbow 3D electroacoustic simulation software are genuinely novel, well-documented engineering work developed entirely by Lynx's own R&D department, rather than licensed from a third party — this is what distinguishes it from a company simply reselling generic pro-audio hardware.
