DSPB-FL is the digital "brain" that Lynx Pro Audio builds into its self-powered speaker systems, and the company designed the whole thing itself rather than buying an off-the-shelf chip solution. In plain terms: digital signal processing (DSP) is the computer chip inside a speaker that decides how loud each frequency should be, how to split the sound between different drivers (woofer, tweeter, etc.), and how to keep everything in time with itself. This documentation explains DSPB-FL's technical specs — very high-precision audio conversion, filters that avoid smearing the timing of different frequencies relative to each other ("linear-phase"), and the ability to store multiple ready-made setups for different venues. The page is useful as an example of a mid-size pro-audio manufacturer documenting genuinely in-house engineering work, rather than reselling a third-party processing chip under its own brand.
