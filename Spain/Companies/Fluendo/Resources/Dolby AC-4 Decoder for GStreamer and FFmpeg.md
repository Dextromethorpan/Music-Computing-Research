Dolby AC-4 is a modern audio codec (a way of compressing and packaging sound) that is becoming a mandatory standard for next-generation TV broadcasting in places like the US, EU, Brazil, and South Korea — it supports immersive "Atmos" surround sound, automatic dialogue enhancement (making speech clearer without changing the rest of the mix), and metadata that lets broadcasters personalize what viewers hear. Fluendo built the only Dolby-certified software decoder for AC-4 that works inside the open-source GStreamer and FFmpeg frameworks, meaning other companies can legally decode AC-4 audio in their own products without needing specialized, expensive hardware chips. The technical documentation lists real engineering details — supported speaker layouts up to 5.1.4, real-time extraction of loudness and dialogue metadata, and on-the-fly reconfiguration — showing this is a fully implemented, shipping piece of audio infrastructure rather than a marketing claim.
