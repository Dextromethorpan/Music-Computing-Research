Fluendo is a Barcelona-based multimedia technology company founded in 2004, best known as a long-time core contributor to GStreamer (an open-source framework used to build audio and video software) and as a licensed provider of patented audio and video codecs, including a certified Dolby AC-4 decoder. It qualifies via the specialized codec/protocol infrastructure path: rather than publishing academic papers, Fluendo's technical depth shows up in maintaining and extending a widely used open multimedia framework and in shipping the only Dolby-certified AC-4 audio decoding path for GStreamer and FFmpeg.
