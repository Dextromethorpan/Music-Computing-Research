LSP is a CNRS research unit (UMR 8248) at the École Normale Supérieure in Paris, part of PSL University. Its Audition team studies how humans perceive sound and music — including questions like why some musical intervals sound "right" across very different cultures — combining behavioral experiments, brain recordings, and computer modeling.
