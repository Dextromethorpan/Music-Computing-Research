Daniel Pressnitzer did his PhD at IRCAM on musical consonance and dissonance before working in the UK on the neuroscience of hearing. He now leads the Audition team at LSP (ENS-PSL), where he studies how the brain organizes sound into meaningful patterns — including how humans perceive melody, rhythm, and pitch, and why some of these abilities seem to be shared across very different cultures.
