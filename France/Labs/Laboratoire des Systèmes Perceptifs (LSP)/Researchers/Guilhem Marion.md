Guilhem Marion studies why different people can feel different emotions while listening to the exact same piece of music. His work looks at how culture and individual experience shape "melodic expectation" — the sense of which note is likely to come next in a tune — using behavioral tests and brain recordings (EEG, MEG) to trace how these expectations are encoded in the brain.
