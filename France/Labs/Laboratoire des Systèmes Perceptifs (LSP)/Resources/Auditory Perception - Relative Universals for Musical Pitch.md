This short article discusses a big study that asked people from many different cultures, including some with little exposure to Western music, to sing back short melodies they heard. The surprising finding: while the exact "in-tune" pitches people used varied a lot between cultures, the size of the steps between notes (the intervals) followed similar simple patterns everywhere. This suggests something about how the human ear and brain organize musical pitch is shared across humanity, even though musical scales themselves differ. Pressnitzer, who leads the Audition team at LSP, wrote this piece to explain what the results mean for how we understand hearing and music.
