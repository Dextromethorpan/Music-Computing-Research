PRISM (Perception, Représentations, Image, Son, Musique) is a joint CNRS / Aix-Marseille Université / French Ministry of Culture laboratory in Marseille, formed in 2017 by merging researchers from acoustics, cognitive science, musicology, and the arts. It studies how humans perceive and create sound, image, and music, and builds tools (synthesis, sonification, spatial audio) grounded in that understanding of perception.
