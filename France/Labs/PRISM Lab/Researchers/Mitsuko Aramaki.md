Mitsuko Aramaki leads PRISM's "Perceptual Engineering" research theme, which studies sound-generating processes and the perception they induce, and designs tools for sound and music creation as well as perceptually grounded control models. She has worked on projects spanning industrial applications, virtual/augmented sound perception, and the therapeutic use of sound and music (MusiDance).
