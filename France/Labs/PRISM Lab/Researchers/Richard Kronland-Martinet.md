Richard Kronland-Martinet founded and led PRISM's predecessor research group on sound modeling, synthesis, and control at the Laboratory of Mechanics and Acoustics before co-founding PRISM itself in 2017. His research covers sound synthesis, audio signal processing, wavelets, and immersive/virtual sound environments (his current focus is PRISM's "Immersive Virtual Worlds" theme), and he founded the long-running CMMR conference series.
