Sølvi Ystad directs PRISM and researches how the brain identifies meaningful structure in sound: which acoustic features let a listener recognize what caused a sound, and how those features can be turned into intuitive controls for sound synthesis. Her broader interests include sound and multimodal (sound plus touch or movement) modeling, sonification, and non-verbal communication through sound. She trained as an electronics engineer in Norway before completing a PhD in acoustics in Marseille and a postdoc at Stanford.
