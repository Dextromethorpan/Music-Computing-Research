MusiDance studies how sound and music can help people who are in situations of dependency (for example, due to aging or illness), aiming toward applications in public health and wellbeing. Because the topic is broad, the project deliberately combines several angles: acoustics (what physically makes a sound therapeutic), musicology and history, behavioral and neuropsychological science (how the brain and body respond), and hands-on artistic practice. It reflects PRISM's general approach of pairing perceptual science with real-world, human-centered applications.
