PHYSIS was a French national research project focused on generating realistic "diegetic" sounds (sounds that belong to a scene, like footsteps, engines, or collisions) for interactive virtual worlds, video games, and augmented reality. Rather than relying purely on physics simulation (which is accurate but slow) or purely on playing back recordings (which is fast but inflexible), the project modeled sounds using descriptors tied to what listeners actually perceive, so that a designer could control a sound with meaningful, semantic controls such as "make it sound heavier" or "make it sound more metallic" while keeping real-time performance.
