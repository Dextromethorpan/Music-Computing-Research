CMMR (Computer Music Multidisciplinary Research) is an international academic conference series created in 2003 by researchers who later founded PRISM. Unlike conferences that focus on just one angle of computer music, CMMR deliberately brings together people studying how sound is produced acoustically, how it is perceived by listeners, and how it can be synthesized and processed by computer, publishing peer-reviewed proceedings with Springer each year. Editions have been hosted across Europe, Asia, and South America, and several were organized directly by PRISM (or its predecessor lab, LMA) in Marseille.
