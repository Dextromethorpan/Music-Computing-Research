SoniMove investigates "sonification": using carefully designed sounds, rather than pictures or text, to help people control their movements in real time. The project studies how sound feedback can guide functional gestures for new car dashboards and driver-assistance interfaces, and how it can help train precise, expert movements in sports and music practice. The underlying idea is that well-designed sound can communicate information (how fast, how far, how accurate) faster and more intuitively than a visual display, especially when someone's eyes are busy with another task.
