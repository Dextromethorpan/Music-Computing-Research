Guido is a text-based way of describing musical scores: instead of drawing notes with a mouse, you type a compact sequence of characters representing pitches, durations, and other markings, and software renders it as a normal-looking music score. This makes scores easy to store, search, generate automatically, and share as plain text, similar in spirit to how HTML describes a web page. GRAME provides both the notation format and browser-based editors and viewers for it.
