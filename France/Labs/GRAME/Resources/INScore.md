INScore is a software environment for creating live, interactive music scores that can mix traditional notation with graphics, text, and images, and update on screen in real time as a piece is performed or generated by a computer. It lets composers design augmented scores where visual elements react to what a performer or algorithm is doing, useful for contemporary music, mixed instrumental/electronic pieces, and music education.
