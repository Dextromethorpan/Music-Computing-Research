Romain Michon has been part of the core Faust development team at GRAME since 2008, splitting his time with a research and teaching role at Stanford's CCRMA (Center for Computer Research in Music and Acoustics). His research interests include embedded real-time audio systems, running audio DSP on FPGA hardware, physical modeling of musical instruments, and new interfaces for musical expression.
