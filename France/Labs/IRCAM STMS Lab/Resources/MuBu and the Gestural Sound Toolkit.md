MuBu is a set of building blocks for the Max/MSP visual programming environment that lets artists record, store, and analyze both sound and movement data (like sensor or motion-capture streams) together in the same container. On top of it, the Gestural Sound Toolkit provides ready-made pieces for training a computer to recognize specific gestures (a wave, a tap, a swipe) and to trigger or shape sound in response, without needing a background in machine learning. It underpins many of the interactive installations and digital instruments built by IRCAM's ISMM (Sound Music Movement Interaction) team, led by Frédéric Bevilacqua.
