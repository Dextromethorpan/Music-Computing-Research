ISiS is software developed at IRCAM that turns written lyrics and a musical score into a synthetic singing voice. It works by modeling a voice as separate ingredients (pitch, timbre, vibrato, breathiness) that can each be controlled independently, then recombining them into a convincing sung performance. Later versions add deep-learning components for tasks like separating a singer's voice from a mix, or automatically estimating the pitch of a singing recording. It grew out of a series of French national research projects (ChaNTeR, then ARS) led by IRCAM's Analysis/Synthesis team.
