This paper, presented at the NIME (New Interfaces for Musical Expression) conference, introduces a new dataset of electric guitar recordings covering many different playing techniques (palm mutes, harmonics, slides, etc.) captured from six different microphones and pickup setups. The authors trained a neural network to recognize which technique is being played, then packaged it as "ipt~", a plug-in for the Max/MSP software that can identify the playing technique live, as a guitarist performs. This kind of tool could let an instrument automatically change effects or trigger different sounds depending on how it is being played.
