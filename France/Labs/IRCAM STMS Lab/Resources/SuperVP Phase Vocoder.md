SuperVP is a software engine, developed at IRCAM since 2000, that can stretch a sound in time or shift its pitch without the usual robotic side effects. It does this using a technique called a "phase vocoder", which breaks a sound into many narrow frequency bands, keeps track of exactly how each band's energy evolves over time, and then reassembles a modified version. SuperVP is used inside well-known IRCAM tools such as AudioSculpt and OpenMusic, and its real-time version is used by musicians directly inside Max/MSP. It remains one of the reference engines worldwide for high-quality audio time-stretching and pitch-shifting.
