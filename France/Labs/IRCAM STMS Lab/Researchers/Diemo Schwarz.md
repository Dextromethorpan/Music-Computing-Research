Diemo Schwarz is a researcher and creative programmer at IRCAM best known for creating CataRT, a system for exploring and playing large sound databases in real time by their sonic qualities rather than by file name. His work sits at the crossroads of sound analysis/synthesis and gestural control, and he also performs live using his own CataRT-based digital instrument. He has been an artist-in-residence at Aix-Marseille Université's IMERA institute.
