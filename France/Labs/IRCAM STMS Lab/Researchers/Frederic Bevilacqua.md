Frédéric Bevilacqua leads IRCAM's Sound Music Movement Interaction team, which studies how body movement and gesture can be captured and turned into musical control. His work spans digital musical instruments, motion-capture-based performance systems, and sound-guided physical rehabilitation. He joined IRCAM in 2003 after a background in physics and biomedical optics, and coordinated several French national research projects on gesture and interactive sound learning.
