Axel Roebel leads IRCAM's Analysis/Synthesis team, which develops algorithms for analyzing and transforming speech and music signals. He created SuperVP, a widely used phase-vocoder engine, and more recently has focused on deep-learning approaches to voice and music processing, including neural vocoders and singing voice synthesis and conversion. He has supervised many PhD students working on voice transformation, drum synthesis, and expressive speech synthesis with neural networks.
