STMS (Sciences et Technologies de la Musique et du Son) is a joint research lab of IRCAM, CNRS, Sorbonne Université and the French Ministry of Culture, based in Paris. It is one of the largest and longest-running centers in the world combining musical creation with research into sound analysis, synthesis, gesture, and music representation.
