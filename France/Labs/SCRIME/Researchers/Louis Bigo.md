Louis Bigo is a computer scientist and current head of SCRIME. His research uses mathematical and machine-learning models to help with computer-assisted music analysis, composition, and performance, including spatial (geometric) representations of chords and pitch relationships (his HexaChord software) and, as coordinator of the TABASCO project, computational tools for guitar tablature. He completed his PhD at IRCAM before working at the University of Lille's Algomus team.
