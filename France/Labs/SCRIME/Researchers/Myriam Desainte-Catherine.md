Myriam Desainte-Catherine co-founded SCRIME in 2000 and has led its "Sound and Music Modeling" research theme within LaBRI. Her research covers interactive and formal models for musical composition and performance, particularly systems (like Iscore) that let composers define flexible, rule-based timing relationships between musical events rather than a single fixed sequence. She has supervised 16 doctoral theses and authored more than one hundred publications in music computing.
