TABASCO is a French national research project building computer tools to help people compose and write guitar music in tablature (the notation style guitarists use, showing which string and fret to play rather than standard notes on a staff). The team analyzes large collections of existing tablature to find statistical patterns in how guitarists write music, and uses machine learning to automatically identify musical structure (like which notes carry the rhythm) directly from tablature. It is a partnership between academic researchers and the company behind Guitar Pro, a popular tablature-editing program.
