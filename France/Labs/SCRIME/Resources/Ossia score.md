Ossia score is free, open-source software for building and performing "interactive scores": timelines where sounds, lighting, video, and other media are triggered not just at fixed points in time but according to rules and conditions (for example, "play this sound only after the performer moves past this point"). It grew out of SCRIME's long-running research into interactive scores (the earlier Iscore project) and is used by composers and media artists to choreograph complex multimedia performances that can adapt live rather than following a rigid, pre-set timeline.
