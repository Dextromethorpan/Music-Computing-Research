This SCRIME research line builds "meta-instruments": augmented versions of real instruments (including a wind-instrument variant) and devices like the "Métapiano" that let a performer's normal playing gestures also control live electronics and sound processing. The goal is to expand what a performer can expressively do with a familiar instrument without forcing them to learn a completely new interface, blending traditional instrumental technique with real-time computer music.
