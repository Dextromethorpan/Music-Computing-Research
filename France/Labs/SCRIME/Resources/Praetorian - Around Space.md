Praetorian is part of SCRIME's sound spatialization research, which studies how to place and move sounds around a listener in three dimensions, for example using SCRIME's dome of 25 loudspeakers arranged around and above the audience. This particular project, "Around Space," explores spatial sound composition and playback techniques for immersive, multi-speaker listening environments, connecting acoustic research with electroacoustic composition practice.
