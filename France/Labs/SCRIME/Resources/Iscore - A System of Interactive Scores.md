This paper presents Iscore, a system developed at SCRIME/LaBRI for designing and performing "interactive scores" for electronic and mixed music. Instead of a score being a fixed sequence played back exactly the same way every time, Iscore lets a composer specify flexible timing relationships between musical events (for example, "event B must happen sometime after A starts, but before A ends") so a live performer or improviser can shape exactly when things unfold within those rules. This research direction, led by Myriam Desainte-Catherine, later evolved into the modern open-source Ossia score software.
