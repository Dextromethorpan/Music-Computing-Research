SCRIME (Studio de Création et de Recherche en Informatique et Musiques Expérimentales) is a research and creation studio in Bordeaux, founded in 2000 and hosted within the LaBRI computer science laboratory at the University of Bordeaux. It brings together computer scientists and electroacoustic composers to work on topics such as interactive scores, sound spatialization, and augmented musical instruments ("meta-instruments").
