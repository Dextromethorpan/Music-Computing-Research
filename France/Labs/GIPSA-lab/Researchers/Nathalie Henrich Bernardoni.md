Nathalie Henrich Bernardoni studies the human voice from many angles at once: how the vocal folds and larynx physically move, how singers use different vocal techniques across musical traditions worldwide (including Bulgarian singing, Sardinian polyphonic singing, Mongolian khoomii, Persian tahrir, yodeling, and beatboxing), and how voice signals can be measured, analyzed, and synthesized. Her research bridges physics, phonetics, and musicology to understand and model the singing and speaking voice.
