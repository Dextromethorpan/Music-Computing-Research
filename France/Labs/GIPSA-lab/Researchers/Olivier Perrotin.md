Olivier Perrotin is a CNRS researcher at GIPSA-lab working on human-computer interaction and voice signal processing. Much of his work is about controlling singing and speech synthesis in real time through hand gestures (as in the Cantor Digitalis singing instrument) and about separating a voice signal into its underlying vocal-tract and glottis components so that voice quality can be modified directly. He also works on evaluating text-to-speech systems, including organizing the Blizzard Challenge, an international benchmark competition for speech synthesis.
