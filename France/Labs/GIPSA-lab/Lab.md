GIPSA-lab (Grenoble Images Parole Signal Automatique) is a joint CNRS / Grenoble INP / Université Grenoble Alpes research unit. Within its Speech and Cognition division, researchers work on singing-voice analysis and synthesis, gesture-controlled digital musical instruments, and voice-quality modification — publishing at NIME, Interspeech, ICASSP and in speech/audio journals.
