Whispering has no vocal-fold vibration (no "voicing"), which is why it sounds breathy and toneless compared to normal speech. This Interspeech 2022 paper tackles "whisper-to-speech conversion" — automatically turning a whispered recording into normal-sounding voiced speech — by improving how the system decides which parts of the whispered signal should become voiced sounds in the output. It does this by classifying phonemes (the small speech-sound units that make up words) and analyzing the shape of the sound's frequency spectrum. Better voicing decisions make the converted speech sound more natural, which has applications for people with voice disorders who can only whisper and for silent-speech communication devices.
