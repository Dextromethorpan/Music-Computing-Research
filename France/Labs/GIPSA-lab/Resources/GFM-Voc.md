GFM-Voc is a real-time tool that can change the quality of someone's voice as they speak — for example making it sound softer and more relaxed, or louder and more tense — without needing any training data and without hurting the audio quality. It works by splitting the voice signal into two separate ingredients: the "glottis" part (the buzzing sound made by the vocal folds vibrating) and the "vocal tract" part (how the mouth and throat shape that buzz into vowels and consonants), then letting the user adjust each ingredient independently through a simple on-screen control panel. This kind of independent control is new compared to older voice-modification tools, which mixed the two ingredients together. Possible uses include making synthetic speech sound more expressive, studying how speakers react when they hear their own voice altered, and speech therapy.
