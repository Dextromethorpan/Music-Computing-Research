Jean-Loïc Le Carrou studies how musical instruments physically vibrate and produce sound — particularly the concert harp, electric guitars, and historical keyboard instruments like the clavichord — and how the musician playing the instrument changes its behavior. His work combines vibration measurement, mathematical/physical modeling, and acoustics to understand instrument design and the interaction between a player and their instrument. He co-heads the Lutheries-Acoustique-Musique (LAM) team at the Institut Jean Le Rond d'Alembert.
