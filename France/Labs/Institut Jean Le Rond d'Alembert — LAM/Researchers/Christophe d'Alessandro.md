Christophe d'Alessandro is a CNRS director of research whose work spans singing voice synthesis, gestural (hand-based) control of digital musical instruments, and — more recently — physical modeling of historical keyboard instruments such as the clavichord. He co-created the Cantor Digitalis singing synthesizer (originally developed while he was at LIMSI, now continued in collaboration with colleagues at GIPSA-lab) and now works within the Lutheries-Acoustique-Musique (LAM) team at the Institut Jean Le Rond d'Alembert.
