Guitarists often talk about how long a note "sustains" (rings out) before fading away, and solid-body electric guitars (like a Stratocaster or Les Paul, which have no hollow resonating chamber) vary a lot in this respect depending on details like the wood used and how the instrument is built. This paper, published in the Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, develops a way to predict how long a note will ring out ("decay time") from physical measurements of the guitar, connecting a subjective, musically important quality to measurable engineering properties of the instrument.
