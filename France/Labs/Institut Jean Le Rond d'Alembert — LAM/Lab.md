The Lutheries-Acoustique-Musique (LAM) team at the Institut Jean Le Rond d'Alembert (Sorbonne Université / CNRS) is one of France's leading musical acoustics research groups, studying how musical instruments and the singing voice physically produce sound, how musicians interact with their instruments, and how these can be simulated or synthesized. It publishes extensively in the Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, Acta Acustica, and at music acoustics conferences (ISMA, Forum Acusticum, SMAC).
