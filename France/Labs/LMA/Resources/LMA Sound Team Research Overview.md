This overview page describes the four research themes of LMA's dedicated "Sound" team: controlling and synthesizing acoustic fields, understanding the self-sustaining oscillations that make wind and bowed instruments produce sound, designing and improving the sound quality of musical instruments (including an industrial partnership with clarinet/saxophone maker Buffet-Crampon), and studying the mechanisms of human auditory perception and hearing. Together these themes connect the physics of how instruments and voices make sound to how humans actually hear and judge it.
