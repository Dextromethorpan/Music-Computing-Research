Wind and bowed-string instruments (clarinets, saxophones, violins) only produce a clean, sustained note within a certain range of blowing pressure, bow speed, and similar playing effort - outside that range you get squeaks, silence, or unstable noise. This paper published in Acta Acustica uses statistical methods to map out, for physics-based instrument models, exactly which combinations of playing parameters actually produce a stable, "playable" note. This kind of map is valuable both for understanding real instruments and for tuning digital physical-model synthesizers so they behave realistically across their full playable range.
