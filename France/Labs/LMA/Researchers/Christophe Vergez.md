Christophe Vergez studies how musical instruments physically work, with a particular focus on the nonlinear behavior of wind instruments such as clarinets and saxophones - how a reed and an air column interact to create and sustain a musical note. His work combines mathematical modeling of these self-sustained oscillations with practical questions about instrument playability and design.
