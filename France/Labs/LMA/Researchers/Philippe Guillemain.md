Philippe Guillemain works on the physics and real-time digital synthesis of wind instruments, translating physical models of reed instruments like the clarinet into working sound-synthesis algorithms. His research sits at the intersection of musical acoustics and signal processing, contributing to LMA's broader work on instrument design and sound quality.
