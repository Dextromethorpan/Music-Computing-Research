Jean Kergomard is a long-standing figure in musical acoustics at LMA, known for foundational work on the physics of wind instruments, including how reeds, air columns, and tone holes interact to produce and shape musical sound. His work on the nonlinear modes of clarinet-like instruments underpins later research into physically accurate digital synthesis of wind instruments.
