LMA (Laboratoire de Mécanique et d'Acoustique) is a CNRS joint research unit in Marseille (UMR 7031, with Aix-Marseille Université and Centrale Méditerranée) covering acoustics and solid mechanics broadly. Its dedicated "Sound" team does genuine music-computing-relevant research: physically modeling how musical instruments (especially wind instruments) actually produce sound, and building real-time digital synthesizers based on those physical models.
