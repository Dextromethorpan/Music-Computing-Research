Slim Essid coordinates the ADASP (Audio Data Analysis and Signal Processing) group at Télécom Paris. His research covers machine learning and AI for analyzing data that changes over time, with applications spanning machine listening and music content analysis, human behavior and emotion analysis, and joint audio-visual (multimodal) data analysis.
