Geoffroy Peeters is a full professor in the S2A team whose research focuses on using signal processing and machine learning (including deep learning) to automatically analyze audio, with a strong emphasis on music: describing timbre, detecting beats and rhythm, estimating chords and keys, recognizing cover versions of songs, and automatically tagging audio. Before joining Télécom Paris he led music information retrieval research at IRCAM. He served as president of the ISMIR (International Society for Music Information Retrieval) board for 2022–2023 and has co-chaired major conferences in the field, including ISMIR 2018 and DAFx 2011.
