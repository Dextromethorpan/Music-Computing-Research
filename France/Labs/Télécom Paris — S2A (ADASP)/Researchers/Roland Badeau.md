Roland Badeau is a full professor in the S2A team whose research is on the statistical modeling of signals that change over time, applied to audio and music: separating mixed sound sources, removing noise, removing echo/reverberation, detecting multiple simultaneous pitches, and automatic music transcription. He is a co-author of over 30 journal papers and holds several patents in this area.
