Gaël Richard is a full professor at Télécom Paris who has spent his career on speech and audio signal processing — teaching computers to separate mixed sounds into their sources, analyze music, and understand audio scenes. He leads much of the ADASP group's work and won a major European Research Council grant (ERC) in 2022 for a project on "AI for sound," and he is a Fellow of the IEEE. He is also co-scientific director of the Hi! PARIS interdisciplinary AI research center.
