The Audio Data Analysis and Signal Processing (ADASP) group is part of the S2A (Signal, Statistics and Learning) team within the LTCI laboratory at Télécom Paris, Institut Polytechnique de Paris. It is one of France's most active academic groups in music information retrieval and audio machine learning, publishing regularly at ISMIR, ICASSP, DCASE, Interspeech and DAFx, and running a joint industry lab (LISTEN) on machine listening.
