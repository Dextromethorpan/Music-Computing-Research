Ambisonics is a technique for capturing and reproducing sound in full 3D space (rather than just left-right stereo), which is used for immersive/spatial music recordings and virtual-reality audio. To capture 3D sound accurately, engineers place microphones at very specific points around a sphere, and choosing good point layouts ("grids") matters for audio quality. This paper, published in the Journal of the Audio Engineering Society, presents a 50-point arrangement (a "Lebedev grid," a mathematically efficient way of spacing points on a sphere) and shows how to apply it to Ambisonics microphone arrays and sound-field processing, improving the accuracy of 3D sound capture and playback.
