Brass instruments like trumpets and horns spread sound differently in different directions — this pattern is called "directivity," and it changes depending on what's around the instrument's bell, such as an enclosure or cabinet in a loudspeaker horn design. This 2024 paper, published in the Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, develops a mathematical method ("multimodal formulation," meaning it describes the sound field as a combination of a set of basic vibration/wave patterns) to predict how a horn's directivity changes when it is mounted inside a finite (bounded) enclosure. The work is relevant both to designing brass-instrument-like horns for loudspeakers and to understanding the acoustics of wind instruments more generally.
