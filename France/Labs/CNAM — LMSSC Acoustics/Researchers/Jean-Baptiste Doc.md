Jean-Baptiste Doc studies how sound is produced and radiated by wind and brass instruments — for example how a trumpet or horn's bell shapes the directional pattern of the sound it emits, and how to accurately simulate the acoustic behavior of pipes and waveguides with complex shapes. His research supports both the scientific understanding of wind-instrument acoustics and practical instrument/loudspeaker-horn design.
