Christophe Langrenne is a research engineer at CNAM's Laboratoire de Mécanique des Structures et des Systèmes Couplés (LMSSC), working on 3D sound-field capture and reproduction (Ambisonics), acoustic imaging, and the mathematics of sound radiation from complex shapes. His work on microphone-array geometries and room-reflection cancellation underpins immersive and spatial-audio technology used in music and sound recording.
