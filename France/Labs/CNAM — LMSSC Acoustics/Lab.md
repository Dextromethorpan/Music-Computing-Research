The Laboratoire de Mécanique des Structures et des Systèmes Couplés (LMSSC) at CNAM (Conservatoire National des Arts et Métiers) includes an acoustics group whose "musical acoustics" and 3D sound-field research covers Ambisonics (immersive spatial audio capture/reproduction), acoustic imaging, and the physics of wind and brass instrument sound production and radiation.
