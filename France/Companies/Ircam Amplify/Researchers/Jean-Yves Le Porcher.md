Jean-Yves Le Porcher has worked as a designer for over 35 years and leads Ircam Amplify's "Sound Experience" branch, which designs custom sound (rather than purely music) for brands, retail spaces, events, and venues — for example, deciding what a store or product should sound like. His work combines psychoacoustics (the study of how humans perceive sound) with sound design and technology, applying IRCAM's audio research to commercial and multisensory design projects rather than to music recordings themselves.
