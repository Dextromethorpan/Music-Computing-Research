Frederic Amadu is Ircam Amplify's CTO, overseeing the engineering behind its audio products. He trained as an engineer and spent 17 years at Arkamys, a company specializing in embedded audio signal processing (audio software built directly into devices like cars or phones rather than run on a server), where he led innovation and later served as CTO; he is credited as an inventor on more than a dozen patents in that field. At Ircam Amplify, he works on connecting IRCAM's academic sound research to real, everyday audio products, as he described in a 2023 conference talk.
