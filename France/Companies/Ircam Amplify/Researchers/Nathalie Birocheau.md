Nathalie Birocheau co-founded Ircam Amplify in 2020 to commercialize IRCAM's audio research and has led the company since. She holds an engineering degree and a Master's in signal processing from Polytechnique Montréal, and has overseen the development of the company's audio AI products including spatial-audio conversion and AI-generated-music detection tools.
