Ircam Amplify is a Paris-based company founded in 2020 as a commercial spin-off of IRCAM (the Institute for Music/Acoustic Research and Coordination), created to turn decades of IRCAM's audio research into industrial products and licensable technology for the music industry — including tools for spatial audio conversion, AI-generated music detection, and audio quality analysis.
