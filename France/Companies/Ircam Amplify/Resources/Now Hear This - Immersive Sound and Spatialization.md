This blog post from Ircam Amplify explains, in accessible terms, what "immersive sound" and "sound spatialization" actually mean and why the underlying science is decades old rather than a brand-new invention — it traces the idea back to IRCAM's long history of acoustics research. It's a useful plain-language companion piece to the company's Stereo-to-Spatial product, aimed at helping non-technical readers (labels, artists) understand what they're buying when they license spatial-audio conversion.
