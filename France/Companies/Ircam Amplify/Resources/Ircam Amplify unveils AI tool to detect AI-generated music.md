This trade-press article covers the public launch of Ircam Amplify's AI Music Detector and includes a direct technical quote from Romain Simiand, the company's Chief Product Officer, explaining how it was built: the team used their experience in "Music Information Retrieval" (MIR — the field of getting computers to automatically analyze and describe music, e.g. its genre, tempo, or in this case its origin) combined with a "deep neural network," a type of AI model loosely inspired by how brain cells connect, trained on both human-made and AI-generated tracks. The article explains that the tool can identify not just whether a track is AI-made, but which specific AI system likely generated it, because the training was specific to each model's telltale patterns. It situates the launch in the wider industry context of roughly 170 million AI-generated tracks estimated to have been uploaded to streaming platforms in 2023, and how competitors were building similar tools around the same time.
