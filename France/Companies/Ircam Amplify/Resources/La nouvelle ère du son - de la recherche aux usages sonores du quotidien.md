This is a conference talk (in French, title translates to "The new era of sound: from research to everyday sound uses") given at the Tech.Rocks Summit 2023 by Frederic Amadu, Ircam Amplify's Chief Technology Officer. Before joining Ircam Amplify, Amadu spent 17 years at Arkamys, a company specializing in embedded audio signal processing, where he holds credit for more than a dozen patents. The talk's stated theme is how to better connect academic research with real-world sound products — explaining, for a business/tech audience, how Ircam Amplify tries to turn IRCAM's lab research into technology people actually use day to day (such as the spatial audio and AI detection tools described elsewhere in this vault). It is useful mainly as evidence of a genuine, patent-experienced technical leader bridging research and product at the company, rather than as a deep technical paper itself.
