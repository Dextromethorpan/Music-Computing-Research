The AI Music Detector is a tool that analyzes the raw sound wave of a track and returns a probability score for whether it was generated by AI rather than performed and recorded by humans. It looks for subtle acoustic artifacts — small technical imperfections in the audio — that current AI music generators tend to leave behind, even when the track sounds convincing to human ears. Ircam Amplify filed patents around this detection method in December 2024, and positions the tool as one of the more accurate third-party AI-music detectors available, aimed at record labels and distributors who need to label or verify catalog content.
