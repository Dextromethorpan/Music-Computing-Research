Spleeter is a free, open-source tool built by Deezer Research that takes a normal song file and splits it into separate tracks (called "stems") — for example, just the vocals, just the drums, just the bass, and everything else. This process is called "source separation": pulling apart the different instruments and voices that were mixed together into one audio file. Spleeter does this quickly using a type of neural network trained on many songs where the individual stems were already known. It became very popular because it works well and runs fast even on an ordinary computer, and it is used for things like making karaoke tracks, remixing, or preparing data for other music research. The accompanying paper was published in the Journal of Open Source Software in 2020.
