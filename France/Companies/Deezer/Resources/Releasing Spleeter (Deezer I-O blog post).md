This is the engineering blog post (on Deezer's "Deezer I/O" tech blog) where Deezer's R&D team announced the public release of Spleeter, their music source-separation tool. In plain language, it explains why Deezer built a tool that can automatically split a song into separate instrument/vocal tracks, what it can be used for outside of Deezer (research, remixing, education), and why the team chose to open-source it instead of keeping it private. It's a good beginner-friendly companion to the more technical Spleeter paper, written for a general engineering audience rather than for MIR researchers.
