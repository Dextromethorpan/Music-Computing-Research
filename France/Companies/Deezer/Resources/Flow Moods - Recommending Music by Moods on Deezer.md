This paper describes how Deezer's "Flow" personalized radio feature was extended to let users pick a mood (like "energetic" or "chill") and get a stream of songs matching that feeling. A recommender system is software that predicts what a user will want next based on data about their past listening and about the songs themselves; here the challenge was combining a listener's usual taste with a mood filter without the results feeling random or repetitive. It was presented at RecSys, the leading conference on recommender systems.
