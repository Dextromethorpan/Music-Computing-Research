As AI tools that generate full songs (vocals, instruments and all) have become widespread, streaming platforms need a way to tell whether a track was made by a human artist or generated by AI. This paper presents Deezer's system for spotting AI-generated songs by analyzing subtle audio patterns that current music-generation models tend to leave behind, and discusses why this is a genuinely hard and moving-target problem, since generation models keep improving and detectors have to keep up. Deezer has used this kind of research to start labeling AI-generated tracks on its platform.
