Deezer is a French music streaming service (based in Paris) with an internal Deezer Research team of scientists and engineers who study how people listen to and interact with music. They publish regularly at top audio and information-retrieval conferences (ISMIR, ICASSP, WASPAA, RecSys, ACL) and release open-source tools, with a focus on music analysis, music information retrieval (MIR), natural language understanding for music, and recommendation.
