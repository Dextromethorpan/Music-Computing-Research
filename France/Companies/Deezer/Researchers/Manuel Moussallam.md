Manuel Moussallam joined Deezer in 2014, moved into the R&D team in 2015, and has led it since 2016 (Deezer's research page currently lists both him and Romain Hennequin as Head of Research). His background is in audio signal processing and machine learning for music, and he has co-authored work on source separation (including Spleeter), music recommendation, and — more recently — detecting AI-generated music, an effort press coverage describes his team spending years building.
