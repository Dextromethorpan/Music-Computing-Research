Gabriel Meseguer-Brocal is a research scientist at Deezer specializing in machine learning and signal processing for sound and music, with a PhD earned in 2020 and postdoctoral work at CNRS (France's national research center) before joining Deezer. His recent work focuses on self-supervised learning (training models without needing manually labeled data) applied to tasks like music tagging, key/tonality estimation, and detecting AI-generated music.
