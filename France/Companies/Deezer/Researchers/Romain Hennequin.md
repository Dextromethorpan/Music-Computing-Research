Romain Hennequin has led Deezer's research team since joining the R&D group in 2015, after a PhD in signal processing from Télécom Paris. He has worked across many areas of music AI over the years — separating instruments and voices out of songs (source separation), understanding what a song is about from its audio or lyrics, recommending music, and, more recently, detecting AI-generated music and lyrics. He is a co-author of Spleeter and is listed on the majority of Deezer Research's recent published papers.
