Darius Afchar is a researcher at Deezer (listed on the Deezer Research site as a PhD Student) working on making machine learning models easier to understand ("interpretability"), on music recommender systems, and on detecting AI-generated music. He is a co-author of the Fourier-based AI-music detection paper and several earlier papers on explainability in recommendation.
