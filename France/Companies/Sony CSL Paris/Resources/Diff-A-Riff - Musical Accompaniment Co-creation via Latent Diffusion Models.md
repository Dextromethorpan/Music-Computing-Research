Diff-A-Riff is an AI tool that generates a new instrument part (like a bassline, guitar riff, or drum pattern) that fits an existing piece of music you already have. You can guide what it creates either by giving it an audio example to imitate, a short text description (like "warm analog bass"), or both. Under the hood it uses a "diffusion model" — a type of AI that starts from random noise and gradually shapes it into something structured, the same general approach used by popular AI image generators, here applied to audio. It produces high-quality studio-ready stereo audio quickly, aiming to give musicians a fast way to sketch out new accompaniment ideas without needing to play every instrument themselves.
