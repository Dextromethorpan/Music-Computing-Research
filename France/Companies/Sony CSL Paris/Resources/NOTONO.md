NOTONO is a free, open-source web tool for designing sounds by "painting" them. Instead of turning knobs on a synthesizer, you see a picture of the sound (its frequencies over time) and can select and repaint parts of that picture — for example, adding a "stringy" texture at the start of a sound or a bright, trumpet-like quality near the end — and an AI model regenerates the audio to match. This technique is called "inpainting": the same idea used in photo-editing AI that fills in a missing or edited part of an image, applied here to sound instead of pictures. You can then play the resulting sound like an instrument from a MIDI keyboard. It builds on an earlier related interface called NONOTO, published as an academic paper at the ICCC 2019 conference.
