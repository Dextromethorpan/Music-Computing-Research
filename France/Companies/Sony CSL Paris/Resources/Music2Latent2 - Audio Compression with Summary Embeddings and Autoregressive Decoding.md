When AI models generate or process music, working directly with raw audio (which has tens of thousands of numbers per second) is slow and expensive. Researchers get around this by first "compressing" audio into a much smaller, simplified representation — called a "latent" or "embedding" — that an AI model can work with faster, then decoding it back into full audio. Music2Latent2 is a new method for doing this compression and decoding for music, designed to shrink audio down further while still reconstructing high-quality sound, using techniques borrowed from both autoregressive models (which generate things step by step, like predicting the next word in a sentence) and consistency models (a fast type of generative AI). This kind of audio codec is a foundational building block for other AI music tools, including generation and compression systems used across the field.
