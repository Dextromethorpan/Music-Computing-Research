Cyran Aouameur is a research engineer on the Music Team at Sony CSL Paris, with a background in acoustics and signal processing from IRCAM's ATIAM program. He joined the team in 2018 and has worked on generative models for drum sound synthesis (VAEs, a type of generative AI), the NOTONO sound-painting interface, and more recently on latent diffusion approaches for generating musical accompaniment.
