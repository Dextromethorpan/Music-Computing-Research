Stefan Lattner leads the current Music Team at Sony CSL Paris. His research covers generative AI for music production, "music information retrieval" (getting a computer to automatically understand things about a piece of music, like its key or rhythm), and "representation learning" (teaching an AI a useful, compact way to describe music internally). Before Sony CSL, he worked on modeling musical structure and computational pitch perception, and was previously lead developer at Re-Compose on a music re-harmonization tool called Liquid Notes.
