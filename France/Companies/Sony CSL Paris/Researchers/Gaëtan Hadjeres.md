Gaëtan Hadjeres joined Sony CSL Paris in 2014 as a PhD student (supervised by François Pachet and Frank Nielsen), created DeepBach, and became a permanent member of the Music Team after finishing his PhD in 2018. He works on "generative models" (AI that creates new content, like music) and "self-supervised learning" (training AI without needing humans to label the data), with a particular focus on making these tools interactive and playable by real musicians rather than just automatic.
