Emmanuel Deruty is a researcher, musician and music producer at Sony CSL Paris who recently completed a PhD at Télécom Paris. His work sits at the crossroads of music production practice and AI research — for example studying how pitch is actually used in contemporary popular music (beyond the standard 12-note tuning system) and writing about AI-assisted music production for a general audience via the team's Medium blog.
