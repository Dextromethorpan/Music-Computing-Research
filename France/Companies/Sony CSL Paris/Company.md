Sony Computer Science Laboratories (Sony CSL) Paris is one of Sony's corporate research labs; its Music Team builds AI models and tools for music production, working directly with musicians. Historically famous for Flow Machines and DeepBach (AI music generation under François Pachet, who left for Spotify in 2017), the team today — led by Stefan Lattner — publishes actively at venues like ISMIR and ICASSP on topics such as pitch estimation, audio compression, and AI-assisted music co-creation.
