Ubisoft is a French video game publisher headquartered in Paris. La Forge is its academic R&D program, which pairs university researchers with in-house engineers on prototypes for future games — including a dedicated speech-and-sound machine learning team (work spans La Forge's global sites, including Ubisoft Bordeaux in France) that publishes on text-to-speech, voice conversion, and audio generation for game characters, plus patented spatial-audio technology.
