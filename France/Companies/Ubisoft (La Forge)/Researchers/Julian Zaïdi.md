Julian Zaïdi is a researcher at Ubisoft La Forge who works on expressive text-to-speech systems for video game dialogue, including cross-speaker style transfer and handling tricky pronunciation cases in French. He is the lead author of Daft-Exprt and of La Forge's entry to the international Blizzard speech-synthesis challenge.
