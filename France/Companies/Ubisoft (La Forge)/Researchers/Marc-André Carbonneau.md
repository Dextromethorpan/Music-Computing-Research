Marc-André Carbonneau led Ubisoft La Forge's team of researchers working on speech and sound machine learning, focused on making game character voices sound more expressive and natural through text-to-speech, voice conversion, and audio generation research. He co-authored several of the team's core papers, including Daft-Exprt and EDMSound. As of 2025 he has moved to Epic Games as a senior research scientist.
