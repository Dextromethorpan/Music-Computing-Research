The Blizzard Challenge is an annual public competition where research teams submit text-to-speech (AI systems that read text aloud) systems to be judged on how natural and correct they sound. This paper describes Ubisoft La Forge's entry, which focused especially on French, including tricky cases called "homographs" — words spelled the same but pronounced differently depending on meaning (like the English word "read"). Their system combined a model that plans out the melody and rhythm of speech with a separate model, called HiFi-GAN, that turns that plan into an actual audio waveform.
