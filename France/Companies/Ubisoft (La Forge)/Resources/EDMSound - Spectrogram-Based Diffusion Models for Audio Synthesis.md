A "diffusion model" is a type of AI that generates new content (in this case, sound) by starting from pure random noise and gradually "cleaning it up" into something structured, step by step — the same family of technique behind popular AI image generators, applied here to audio. This paper applies diffusion models to a picture-like representation of sound called a spectrogram (a graph of which frequencies are present over time) to generate high-quality music and sound effects efficiently, aiming for good sound quality without the very slow generation speed diffusion models often have.
