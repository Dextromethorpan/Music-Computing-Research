Emmanuel Favreau joined GRM in 1994 and has been one of the two full-time developers behind GRM Tools ever since, converting the institute's decades of experimental sound-transformation research into real-time software plugins used by composers and sound designers worldwide. He studied at ENST Paris (Télécom Paris) and has led the software side of GRM Tools' evolution from early hardware-based processing to today's spectral-processing plugin bundles.
