In this interview, GRM's technical director Emmanuel Favreau explains how GRM Tools grew out of decades of in-house sound-processing research at the Groupe de Recherches Musicales, starting from custom hardware used by composers in the 1970s-80s and evolving into the real-time software plugins sold today. It's a beginner-friendly, first-person account of how a small full-time team turned experimental studio techniques for reshaping sound into commercial software tools still used by composers and sound designers worldwide.
