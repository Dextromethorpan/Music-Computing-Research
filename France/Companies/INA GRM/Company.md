INA GRM (Groupe de Recherches Musicales, part of France's National Audiovisual Institute) is a Paris-based institute that has produced electroacoustic music research since the 1950s and sells its own line of audio plugins, GRM Tools. Included via path 3 (documented product innovation): GRM Tools' Spectral Transform plugins implement specific, named real-time spectral-processing techniques — such as "Contrast" (regrouping a sound's frequency components by amplitude strength) and "FreqWarp" (freely reassigning frequency components to new positions) — developed and documented over decades by GRM's own engineers rather than published as academic papers.
