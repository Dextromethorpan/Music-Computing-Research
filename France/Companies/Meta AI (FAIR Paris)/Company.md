FAIR (Fundamental AI Research) is Meta's AI research lab, and its Paris office is where the AudioCraft project — including the MusicGen and EnCodec models — was built by researchers based in France. The team publishes open-source, peer-reviewed research on generating and compressing music and audio with deep learning.
