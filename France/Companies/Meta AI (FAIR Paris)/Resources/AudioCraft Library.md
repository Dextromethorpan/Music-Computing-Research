AudioCraft is the open-source software toolkit released by Meta AI that bundles together their audio-generation research, including MusicGen (text-to-music), AudioGen (text-to-sound-effect generation), and EnCodec (audio compression), plus newer additions like watermarking tools to help identify AI-generated audio. It gives other researchers and developers a single, ready-to-use codebase to train or run these models themselves, which is a large part of why MusicGen became widely used and studied outside of Meta.
