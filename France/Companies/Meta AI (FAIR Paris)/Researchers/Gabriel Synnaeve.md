Gabriel Synnaeve is a Paris-based research scientist at Meta AI (FAIR) who co-authored the MusicGen and AudioCraft papers on generating music and audio with AI. His broader research includes machine learning applied to games, code generation, and language, and he previously worked on speech-acquisition modeling at École Normale Supérieure in Paris.
