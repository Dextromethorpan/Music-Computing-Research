L-Acoustics is a French audio hardware and spatial-audio company (Marcoussis, near Paris) best known for L-ISA, its immersive/spatial sound system used at concerts, theme parks, and museums. Included via path 2 (specialized spatial-audio company): L-ISA is backed by real published science — L-Acoustics engineers regularly present peer-reviewed research at Audio Engineering Society (AES) conferences on topics like how listeners perceive spatial sound and musical "groove" in large immersive venues.
