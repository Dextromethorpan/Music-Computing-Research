Etienne Corteel holds a PhD in acoustics and signal processing (completed partly at IRCAM) and has worked in 3D audio research for companies including Studer and sonic emotion before joining L-Acoustics in 2017. He has authored more than 60 publications and 10 patents on spatial and immersive audio, and now leads L-Acoustics' scientific and educational outreach, including its AES conference research on immersive sound and how audiences perceive it.
