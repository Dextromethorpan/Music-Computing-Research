L-ISA Immersive is L-Acoustics' spatial audio system, which lets a sound engineer place individual sounds (a voice, an instrument) anywhere in a 3D space around the audience, rather than just left-right like normal stereo. It works by combining a specific layout of loudspeakers (front, side, and overhead), a dedicated audio processor that calculates how to render each "sound object" in 3D, and software tools for designing and mixing the immersive scene. This official technology page explains, in plain terms, how the system builds a complete surrounding soundscape for concerts, museum exhibits, and other live events.
