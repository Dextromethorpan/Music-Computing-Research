At a huge concert, sound from different loudspeakers reaches different parts of the audience at slightly different times, because sound has to travel through the air. This AES (Audio Engineering Society) research paper, presented by L-Acoustics scientist Etienne Corteel, studies how those tiny timing differences affect whether a live audience still perceives the music's "groove" (its rhythmic feel) the same way across a large venue. The findings were turned into practical guidelines for how to mix and design large-scale sound systems so the rhythmic feel of the music stays intact no matter where you stand.
