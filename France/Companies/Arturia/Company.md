Arturia is a music software and hardware company founded in 1999 in Grenoble, France, best known for its virtual and analog synthesizers. Beyond selling instruments, Arturia has a real in-house DSP (digital signal processing) engineering team that develops and publishes explanations of two proprietary sound-modeling technologies — True Analog Emulation (TAE) and physical modeling — the second of which was originally developed in partnership with IRCAM, France's national institute for music and acoustics research.
