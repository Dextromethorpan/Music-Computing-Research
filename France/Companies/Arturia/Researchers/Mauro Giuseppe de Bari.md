Mauro Giuseppe de Bari is a DSP (digital signal processing) engineer at Arturia who moved from Italy to join the company's Grenoble headquarters. Per his bio on Arturia's official team page, his work involves applying mathematical tools to design and improve audio algorithms, and at the time of writing he was working on improving filter algorithms (filters are the part of a synthesizer that shapes which frequencies of a sound are heard).
