Kevin Arcas holds a PhD and works at Arturia as a DSP (digital signal processing) engineer. Per his bio on Arturia's official team page, he worked on modeling classic hardware audio effects units as software, including the Flanger BL-20, the Chorus Dimension-D, the Chorus JUN-6, and the Bus FORCE — all effects that shape or thicken a sound (flangers and choruses create a shifting, swirling texture by mixing a signal with slightly delayed copies of itself). His work is focused on making faithful digital recreations of iconic hardware effects gear.
