Fanny Roche holds a PhD and works at Arturia as a DSP (digital signal processing) and machine learning engineer. According to her bio on Arturia's official team page, she worked on the Bus FORCE plugin, specifically on a "saturation" module (a way of adding pleasant distortion/grain to a sound) modeled after a hardware unit called the Overstayer. Her focus is finding efficient ways to recreate realistic analog-style distortion in software while keeping the amount of computer processing power required as low as possible.
