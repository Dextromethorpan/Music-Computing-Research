Alexandre Bellot co-founded Expressive E, a Paris-based company built by engineers who wanted electronic instruments to feel as expressive to play as acoustic ones. As CEO, he has spoken publicly about the multi-year engineering effort behind Osmose's Augmented Keyboard Action mechanism and the company's broader mission of designing new, more expressive ways to play electronic music.
