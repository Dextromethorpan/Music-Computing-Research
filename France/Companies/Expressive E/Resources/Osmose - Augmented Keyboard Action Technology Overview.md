Osmose is a synthesizer keyboard where every single key can sense much more than just "pressed" or "not pressed." Its patented keybed, called Augmented Keyboard Action (A.K.A.), measures three things at once for each finger: how hard you press down, whether you push further into a soft second stage below the normal key travel, and whether you nudge the key slightly left or right. All of this is turned into continuous expression data sent separately for every note being played (a standard called MPE), so a single chord can have each note swell, bend, or change tone independently — much closer to how a violin or voice can shape a single note than a normal piano-style keyboard.
