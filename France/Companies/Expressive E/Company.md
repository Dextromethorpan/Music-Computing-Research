Expressive E is a Paris-based instrument maker best known for Osmose, a synthesizer keyboard. Included via path 3 (documented product innovation without formal publication): each of Osmose's 49 keys uses a patented sensing mechanism called Augmented Keyboard Action (A.K.A.) that reads three simultaneous dimensions of a finger's movement — downward pressure, a deeper "aftertouch" push, and side-to-side motion — and sends all of it as continuous per-note expression data (MIDI Polyphonic Expression, or MPE), a specific and well-documented mechanical/software design not found in ordinary keyboards.
