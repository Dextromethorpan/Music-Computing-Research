Metronaut is a consumer mobile app (iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch) that puts Antescofo's score-following research into the hands of everyday musicians as a practice tool. The app listens to a musician playing through their device's microphone, follows along in the sheet music in real time using "intelligent machine listening," and can play synchronized backing tracks, adjust playback tempo to match the player, or give feedback — effectively acting like a patient practice partner. It has been an Apple App Store Editor's Choice and is used by hundreds of thousands of musicians, making it a real-world commercial application of academic score-following research.
