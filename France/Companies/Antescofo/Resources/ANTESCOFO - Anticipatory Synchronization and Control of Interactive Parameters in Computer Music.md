This is the founding research paper behind the technology that later became the company Antescofo. It introduces "score following": a computer system that listens to a live musician playing and figures out, moment by moment, exactly where they are in the written musical score — even if the performer speeds up, slows down, or makes small mistakes. The system described here doesn't just track the performer, it also predicts ("anticipates") what will happen a fraction of a second ahead, so it can trigger electronic sounds or lighting cues in perfect sync with a live human performance, which is essential for contemporary concert music that mixes acoustic instruments with computer-generated parts.
