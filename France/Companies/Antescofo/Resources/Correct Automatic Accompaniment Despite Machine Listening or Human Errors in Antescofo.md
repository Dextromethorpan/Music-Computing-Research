"Automatic accompaniment" is when a computer plays a musical part alongside a live human performer, adjusting its timing in real time to stay together with them, similar to how a human accompanist follows a soloist. This paper tackles a practical problem: what should the system do when either its own listening makes a mistake, or the human musician themselves plays a wrong note or skips a passage? The authors design the Antescofo system so that small errors on either side don't throw the whole performance out of sync, making it robust enough for use in real, imperfect live concerts rather than only in controlled lab conditions.
