Antescofo is a Paris-based startup founded in 2016 by Arshia Cont to commercialize "score following" technology developed over seven years of research at IRCAM — software that listens to a live musician and tracks their exact position in written sheet music in real time. That underlying research is now sold to consumers through the company's Metronaut mobile app, a practice companion for musicians.
