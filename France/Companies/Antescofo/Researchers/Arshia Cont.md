Arshia Cont created the Antescofo score-following technology in 2007 while a researcher at IRCAM, combining real-time machine listening with a programming language for synchronizing electronics to live musicians. He is the author of 50+ academic papers on computer music and holds a PhD (awarded the SPECIF Gilles Kahn prize) from Université Pierre et Marie Curie. In 2016 he co-founded Antescofo SAS to bring this research to musicians directly through the Metronaut app.
