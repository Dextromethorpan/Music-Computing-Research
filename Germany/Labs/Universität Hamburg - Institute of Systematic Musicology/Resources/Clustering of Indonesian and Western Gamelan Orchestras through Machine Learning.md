Gamelan is a traditional Indonesian ensemble music style built around tuned metal percussion, which has also been adopted and imitated by some Western ensembles. The authors recorded several Indonesian and Western gamelan groups and fed acoustic features — like tuning and "articulation" (how sharply notes are struck) — into a machine-learning clustering algorithm called a Self-Organizing Map, which automatically groups similar-sounding recordings together. The algorithm clearly separated Western from Indonesian ensembles based on performance style (Western groups played with less variation and a calmer character), even though tuning system alone did not separate them. This is an example of using computational, data-driven methods to study cross-cultural music performance rather than just instrument physics.
