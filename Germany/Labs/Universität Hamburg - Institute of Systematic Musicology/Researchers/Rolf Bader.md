Bader leads the institute's entire research program spanning musical acoustics, computational modeling, psychoacoustics, and ethnomusicology. He is the driving author or co-author of nearly all the resources gathered from this group, and has authored and edited multiple books including the "Springer Handbook of Systematic Musicology." His ongoing work covers metamaterial instruments, physical modeling, the Impulse Pattern Formulation, and computational archiving of ethnomusicological recordings.
