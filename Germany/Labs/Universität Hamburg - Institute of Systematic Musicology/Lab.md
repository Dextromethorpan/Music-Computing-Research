The Institute of Systematic Musicology (Institut für Systematische Musikwissenschaft) at Universität Hamburg, led by Prof. Rolf Bader, studies music through physics and computation: the acoustics of musical instruments, computational models of how instruments vibrate and produce sound, and how humans perceive music across cultures.
