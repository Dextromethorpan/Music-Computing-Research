Nori Jacoby led the Computational Auditory Perception research group at the Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics from 2018 to 2025. He builds computational models and runs large-scale online experiments to study how people from different cultures perceive musical rhythm and pitch, and co-authored the institute's ISMIR 2021 paper on cross-cultural mood perception in pop songs.
