Owen Green is a researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics working on the project "Sonic-Social Genre: Towards Multimodal Computational Music Genre Modelling" since 2023. His background is in electroacoustic composition and machine listening for live electronic performance, and he has published extensively at NIME, DAFx, and in the Computer Music Journal on tools for computer-assisted sound and music analysis.
