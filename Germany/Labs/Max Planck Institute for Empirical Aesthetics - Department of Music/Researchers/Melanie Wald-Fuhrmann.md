Melanie Wald-Fuhrmann is a musicologist who directs the Department of Music at the Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics in Frankfurt. She studies how people experience music aesthetically, combining historical musicology with empirical and computational methods, and is a co-author on the institute's 2024 ISMIR paper critically reviewing music genre recognition research.
