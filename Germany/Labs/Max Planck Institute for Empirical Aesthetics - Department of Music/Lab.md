The Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics (MPIEA) in Frankfurt am Main studies why and how people create, perform, and experience art, with music as its central focus. Within its Department of Music, researchers combine musicology and psychology with computational and machine-learning methods to study things like automatic music genre classification, cross-cultural music perception, and machine listening for live electronic performance.
