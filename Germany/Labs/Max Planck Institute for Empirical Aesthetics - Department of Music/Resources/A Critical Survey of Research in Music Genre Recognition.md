"Music genre recognition" (teaching a computer to guess a song's genre, like rock or jazz, just from the audio) has been a popular research task for decades. This paper looks back at 560 research papers on the topic published between 2013 and 2022 and asks a bigger question: does this kind of research actually engage with what genre really means to musicians and listeners, or does it just treat genre as a simple label to predict? The authors find that most of this research does not seriously grapple with criticism of the task itself, or with how musicologists and sociologists actually think about genre. It was presented at ISMIR 2024, the leading academic conference on music information retrieval (using computers to organize, search, and understand music).
