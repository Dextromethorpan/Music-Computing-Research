Peter Birkholz works at the intersection of speech technology, phonetics, and machine learning. He created and maintains VocalTractLab, a physical simulation of the vocal tract used to synthesize speech and singing, and also researches "silent speech recognition" — detecting what someone is saying from articulatory movement alone, without any audible sound.
