Wolfgang Klippel is an honorary professor at the institute who extends its teaching in electroacoustics — the design and measurement of devices like loudspeakers that convert electrical signals into sound. He is also the founder of Klippel GmbH, a well-known company that builds measurement systems for testing loudspeaker quality and distortion, work that is widely used across the professional audio and music-technology industry.
