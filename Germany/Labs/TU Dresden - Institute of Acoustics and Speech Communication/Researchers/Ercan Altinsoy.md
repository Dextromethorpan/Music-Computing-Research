Ercan Altinsoy researches how auditory and haptic (touch/vibration) perception interact — for instance how what we feel through an instrument or device changes how we judge what we hear. His chair's teaching and research span physical acoustics, electroacoustics, and room acoustics, and includes musician-focused perceptual studies such as listening/playing tests with violinists.
