VocalTractLab is a free software tool that simulates the human vocal tract — the throat, mouth, and tongue shapes that turn air from the lungs into speech and singing sounds — as a 3D physical model, rather than just imitating a recorded voice. You can move virtual articulators (tongue, jaw, lips) and hear the resulting synthetic voice, which makes it useful both as a research tool for understanding how speech and singing sounds are physically produced and as a teaching tool for phonetics. It is one of the most developed "articulatory synthesis" systems in the world and is maintained by this lab.
