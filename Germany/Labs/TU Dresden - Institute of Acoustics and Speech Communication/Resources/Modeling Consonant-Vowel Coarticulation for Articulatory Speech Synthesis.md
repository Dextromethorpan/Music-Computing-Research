When people talk, the mouth doesn't fully finish shaping one sound before starting the next — a consonant and the vowel next to it blend into each other, a phenomenon called "coarticulation." This paper describes a computational model of coarticulation for articulatory speech synthesis (the technique behind VocalTractLab), letting a computer simulate more natural-sounding, connected speech rather than a robotic string of separately produced sounds. It's a foundational paper behind the lab's ongoing articulatory synthesis work.
