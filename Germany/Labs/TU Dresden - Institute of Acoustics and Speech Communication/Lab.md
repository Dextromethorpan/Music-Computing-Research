The Institut für Akustik und Sprachkommunikation (IAS) at TU Dresden's Faculty of Electrical and Computer Engineering is made up of two chairs: Acoustics and Haptics (Prof. Ercan Altinsoy), covering electroacoustics, room acoustics, and how hearing and touch perception combine; and Speech Technology and Cognitive Systems (Prof. Peter Birkholz), covering speech technology, phonetics, and machine learning, including one of the most advanced systems for simulating how the human vocal tract physically produces speech and singing sounds.
