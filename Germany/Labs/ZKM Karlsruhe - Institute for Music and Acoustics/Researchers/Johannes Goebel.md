Johannes Goebel wrote the 1992 essay that defined what ZKM's Institute for Music and Acoustics would become, while the ZKM building itself was still under construction: a place combining artistic composition, acoustic/psychoacoustic research, and the development of new instruments. He later went on to direct EMPAC (the Experimental Media and Performing Arts Center) at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in the United States.
