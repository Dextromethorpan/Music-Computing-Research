Ludger Brümmer is a composer and professor of composition who has led ZKM's music-and-technology research department continuously since 2003 — first as head of the Institute for Music and Acoustics, then, since the 2017 reorganization, as director of the ZKM Hertzlab. His own music uses the computer both as a compositional tool and as a means of directly generating electronic sound.
