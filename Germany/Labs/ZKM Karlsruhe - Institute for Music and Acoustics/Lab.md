The Institute for Music and Acoustics (IMA) was a research and production department of the ZKM | Center for Art and Media Karlsruhe, active from 1990 to 2017, focused on electroacoustic and computer music: live electronics, acousmatic (loudspeaker-based) music, spatial/3D sound, and psychoacoustics, produced together with invited guest composers. In 2017 it merged with the Institute for Visual Media to form the ZKM | Hertzlab, which continues artistic-technological research (including spatial sound and software development) in an extended, more transdisciplinary form.
