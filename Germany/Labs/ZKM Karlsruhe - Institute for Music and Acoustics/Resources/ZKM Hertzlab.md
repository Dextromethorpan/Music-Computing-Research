Since 2017, the ZKM Hertzlab has been the direct successor to the Institute for Music and Acoustics, formed by merging it with the former Institute for Visual Media. It continues the older institute's work in electronic/computer music, spatial sound, and software development (like Zirkonium), while broadening into a wider "artistic research" platform on themes such as AI and machine learning in art, and residencies for artists working with new technology. Named after Heinrich Hertz, who proved electromagnetic waves exist during experiments at the University of Karlsruhe, it functions as ZKM's current research-and-production department for technology-driven art, including music technology.
