An overview of ZKM's Institute for Music and Acoustics, which ran from 1990 to 2017 as a combined workspace, production studio, and performance venue for electronic and computer music. Guest composers were invited to develop new works on-site, exploring "acousmatic" music (composed for loudspeakers rather than live instruments), live electronics, spatial/3D sound, sound art, and the connection between sound and body movement. Two of its lasting developments were the Sound Dome (a large multi-speaker performance space in the ZKM building) and Zirkonium (spatial-sound control software). In 2017 the institute's work was folded into the newly formed ZKM Hertzlab.
