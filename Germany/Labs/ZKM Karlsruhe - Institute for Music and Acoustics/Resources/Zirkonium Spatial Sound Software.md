Zirkonium is free software, developed at ZKM starting in 2006 and still updated today, that lets composers control where sound "moves" in a room full of loudspeakers — for example making a sound circle around the audience or drift from one side of a hall to another. It was built to drive ZKM's Sound Dome, a large installation with many individually controllable speakers, and it uses spatialization techniques (including VBAP and higher-order ambisonics-style rendering) to calculate exactly what each speaker should play so a listener perceives sound coming from a specific point or moving path in 3D space. An iPad app (ZirkPad) lets composers control it live during a performance.
