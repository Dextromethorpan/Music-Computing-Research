Grollmisch works on deep learning for audio, including semi-supervised, few-shot, and transfer learning approaches, as well as anomaly detection in sound and general audio signal processing. Before his PhD at TU Ilmenau on few-shot and semi-supervised audio classification, he co-founded the spin-off Songquito, an interactive music-education software company. He is a frequent co-author of IDMT's music genre classification and SoundsLike research.
