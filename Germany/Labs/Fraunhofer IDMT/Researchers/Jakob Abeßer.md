Abeßer develops AI and deep-learning methods for analyzing audio and music, spanning acoustic scene analysis (like urban and traffic soundscapes), Music Information Retrieval (transcription, jazz solo analysis, performance assessment), and multimodal analysis that combines audio with text or images. He has been a prolific co-author with IDMT's Semantic Music Technologies group since around 2008, working on projects from bass-guitar transcription to industrial sound classification.
