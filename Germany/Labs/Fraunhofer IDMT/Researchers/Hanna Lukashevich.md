Lukashevich leads the group at Fraunhofer IDMT responsible for the semantic analysis, search, and recommendation of music and audio data. Her work covers automatic music genre and mood classification, studying how reliable machine-learning confidence scores actually are, and metadata-driven music search products like SoundsLike. She has published on music classification and applied Music Information Retrieval projects at IDMT since around 2009.
