SoundsLike is a commercial AI toolkit built at Fraunhofer IDMT that automatically listens to a music archive and adds "metadata" — extra descriptive information like genre, mood, tempo, key, or which instruments are playing — to every track. It then lets someone search that archive either by typing tags or by playing a sample song to find similar-sounding tracks, using "similarity search": the software converts each song into a numeric fingerprint and finds other songs whose fingerprints are close together. It's aimed at broadcasters, production-music libraries, and archives with too many songs to tag by hand, and packages years of IDMT's genre and mood classification research into a licensable product.
