This is the original research paper behind IDMT's SoundsLike product. It explains the underlying machine-learning approach for automatically labeling large collections of music with descriptive tags — genre, mood, instrumentation — and for recommending similar tracks, without needing a human to listen to and tag every song by hand. It's a good "bridge" resource connecting IDMT's academic Music Information Retrieval research to the commercial metadata product they later built and continue to license.
