Guitar "tablature" is a simplified notation showing which string and fret to press, rather than standard musical notes. This DAFx (Digital Audio Effects conference) paper describes a system that listens to a recording of an electric guitar and works out both which notes were played and which string-and-fret combination was most likely used, since the exact same note can be played in several different places on a guitar. It combines signal-processing techniques (like detecting pitch and string-resonance characteristics) with music-theory rules about how guitarists typically play. This early IDMT work fed into the institute's later music-education and transcription products.
