This resource is not about music, but uses the same "listen and classify sound" technology IDMT builds for music metadata, applied instead to street noise. The team recorded thousands of short audio clips of vehicles passing — cars, trucks, motorcycles, buses — under different microphones, weather conditions, and locations, then released the collection publicly so other researchers can train and test "sound event detection" systems (software that recognizes what kind of sound is happening) for smart-city traffic monitoring. It shows how IDMT's core machine-listening technology, developed originally for music, extends naturally to environmental and industrial sound.
