Fraunhofer IDMT (Institute for Digital Media Technology) in Ilmenau, Germany, is a Fraunhofer applied-research institute. This entry covers its Semantic Music Technologies group, led by Hanna Lukashevich, which focuses on automatically extracting metadata (genre, mood, instrumentation, tempo) from music and audio, and building content-based search and recommendation tools.
