This paper introduces a standardized "test room" — a real physical space that was carefully measured and documented — that any researcher can use to check whether their room-acoustics simulation software is accurate, like a reference ruler for measuring simulation quality. It comes with a freely usable dataset of real acoustic measurements (echoes and reflections recorded in that room), so different labs worldwide can compare their results on the same benchmark instead of each testing on a different room. This kind of shared benchmark helps the whole field agree on what "good" virtual acoustics actually looks like.
