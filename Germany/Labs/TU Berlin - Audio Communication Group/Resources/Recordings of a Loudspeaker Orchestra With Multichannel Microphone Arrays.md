Spatial audio is sound reproduction that tries to recreate a 3D sense of where sounds come from — surround sound, ambisonics, or binaural (headphone-based) 3D audio are all examples. To test how well different spatial audio techniques actually work, the researchers built a "loudspeaker orchestra" — many loudspeakers arranged like musicians in an orchestra — and recorded it using several microphone arrays (clusters of microphones capturing spatial information) at once. This produces a shared, richly-documented recording that other researchers can use to fairly compare competing spatial audio rendering methods against each other.
