An "anechoic" recording is made in a room specially built to have no echoes at all, so every instrument is captured completely "dry," without any room sound mixed in. The group recorded a full symphony orchestra performing Beethoven's Symphony No. 8, with every instrument or section on its own separate microphone channel, inside such an echo-free space. This kind of dry, multi-channel recording is extremely valuable for room-acoustics and spatial-audio research: scientists can artificially add any virtual room's acoustics to the dry recording afterward, letting them test and compare different concert hall designs or spatial audio systems using real, complex orchestral music instead of simple test tones.
