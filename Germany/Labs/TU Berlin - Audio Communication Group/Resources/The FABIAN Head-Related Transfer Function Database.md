A "head-related transfer function" (HRTF) describes how one specific person's head, ears, and torso shape sound before it reaches their eardrums — and this shaping is exactly what lets us tell where a sound is coming from. FABIAN is a detailed, high-resolution, freely downloadable database of these HRTF measurements, recorded using a specially built dummy head with a movable torso and head, capturing sound arriving from nearly every direction around it. It's a widely used building block for creating realistic binaural (headphone-based) 3D audio and virtual acoustic reality systems.
