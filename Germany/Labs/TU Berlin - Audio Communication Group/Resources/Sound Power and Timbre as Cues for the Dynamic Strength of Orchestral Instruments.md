When a musician plays "loud" (forte) versus "soft" (piano), the sound doesn't just get louder or quieter — its "timbre" (tone color, how bright, harsh, or mellow it sounds) changes too. The researchers recorded 40 orchestral instruments playing single notes at their loudest and softest across their full pitch range inside an anechoic (echo-free) room, then measured which acoustic features — loudness, brightness, and others — best signal that a note is being played dynamically loud or soft for each instrument. This helps explain, scientifically, how listeners and musicians perceive musical dynamics, directly relevant to music performance research.
