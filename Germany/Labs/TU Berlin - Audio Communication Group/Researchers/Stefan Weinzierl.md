Weinzierl has led the Audio Communication Group at TU Berlin since 2004, directing research into virtual acoustics (binaural technology, sound field synthesis), room acoustics, audio technology, auditory perception, and musical acoustics. Trained as both a physicist and a Tonmeister (sound engineer/producer), his PhD reconstructed the original performance rooms of Beethoven's concerts in Vienna. He also consults professionally on real concert hall acoustics projects.
