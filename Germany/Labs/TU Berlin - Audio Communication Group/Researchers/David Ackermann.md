Ackermann's PhD thesis (supervised by Stefan Weinzierl) describes and implements how to model the way dynamic sound sources radiate sound for use in virtual acoustic reality. He works on multichannel and anechoic orchestra recordings and on spatial audio evaluation methods, contributing to the group's benchmarking of room-acoustic simulation.
