Böhm's PhD thesis (supervised by Stefan Weinzierl) focuses on quantifying how much error and uncertainty exists in room-acoustic computer simulations. He also produced the group's multichannel anechoic recording of Beethoven's Symphony No. 8, used for acoustics and spatial-audio research.
