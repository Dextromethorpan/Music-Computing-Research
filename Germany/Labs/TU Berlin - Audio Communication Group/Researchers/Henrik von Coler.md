While at TU Berlin, von Coler worked on spatial audio interaction and sound synthesis and spatialization tools, including research on user-defined mappings for spatial sound synthesis presented at NIME (New Interfaces for Musical Expression). He co-authored the group's study on how sound power and timbre relate to perceived musical dynamics.
