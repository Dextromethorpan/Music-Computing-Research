The Audio Communication Group (Fachgebiet Audiokommunikation) at Technische Universität Berlin, led by Prof. Stefan Weinzierl, studies how music and speech are produced, transmitted, and perceived through acoustic and electroacoustic systems, with particular strength in room acoustics, spatial and binaural audio, and music performance research.
