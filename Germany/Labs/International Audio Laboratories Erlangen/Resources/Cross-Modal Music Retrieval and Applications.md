"Cross-modal" music retrieval means finding a piece of music using a different type of input than audio — for example, searching an audio archive using a scanned page of sheet music, or matching a piece of music to a symbolic score. This survey paper explains, in an accessible way, the main techniques — such as lining up audio with a score, or mapping both into a shared numeric "fingerprint" space — that let a computer connect these different representations of the same piece of music. It's aimed at giving newcomers a map of this research area and its real-world uses, such as digital music libraries and score-following systems for live performance.
