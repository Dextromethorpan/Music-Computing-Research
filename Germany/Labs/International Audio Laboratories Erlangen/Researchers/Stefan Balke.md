Balke works on Music Information Retrieval, including research on wind and concert-band music (such as an open dataset for chorale/wind-band recordings) and machine learning applied to music. He is also active in the MIR research community, serving on the Board of Directors of the International Society for Music Information Retrieval (ISMIR).
