Müller has led the AudioLabs Semantic Audio Processing group since 2012, researching music processing, Music Information Retrieval (MIR), audio signal processing, and human motion processing. He wrote the widely used textbook "Fundamentals of Music Processing," was named an IEEE Fellow in 2020 for contributions to music signal processing, and served as ISMIR President in 2020-2021.
