Zeitler has been a PhD student at AudioLabs since 2022, working on audio synchronization using neural features and differentiable alignment techniques — for example, "soft" versions of Dynamic Time Warping that can be trained end-to-end with neural networks to align an audio recording with a score. He co-taught an ISMIR 2025 tutorial on differentiable alignment techniques and co-authored the RWC Revisited dataset paper.
