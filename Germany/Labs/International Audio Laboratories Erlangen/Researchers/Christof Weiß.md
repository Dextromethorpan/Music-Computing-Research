While at AudioLabs (2015-2022), Weiß worked on automatic chord recognition, tonal and harmonic analysis, and computational style classification of classical music — for example, tracking how musical style evolved across the history of Western classical music using large annotated datasets like the Schubert Winterreise Dataset. He now leads his own Computational Humanities research group at Julius-Maximilians-Universität Würzburg.
