Özer completed his PhD at AudioLabs in 2024 on "Source Separation of Piano Music Recordings" — separating the different notes or voices being played on a piano within a single audio recording. His broader research interests include audio source separation, music synchronization, and audio decomposition and reconstruction. He is now a postdoctoral researcher at Japan's National Institute of Informatics.
