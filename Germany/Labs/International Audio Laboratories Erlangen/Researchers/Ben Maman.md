Maman joined the group in 2024 after a PhD at Tel Aviv University. He works on music synthesis using diffusion models and on automatic music transcription (recognizing which notes were played by which instrument in a multi-instrument recording), studying how music analysis and music synthesis can be used to improve each other. He also works on voice conversion, changing whose voice a recording sounds like while keeping the words the same.
