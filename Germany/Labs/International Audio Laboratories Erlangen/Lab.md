International Audio Laboratories Erlangen (AudioLabs) is a joint research institute of Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU) and Fraunhofer IIS. This entry covers Prof. Meinard Müller's Semantic Audio Processing group, which focuses on Music Information Retrieval (MIR) and audio signal processing — teaching computers to understand structure, tempo, chords, and alignment in music recordings.
