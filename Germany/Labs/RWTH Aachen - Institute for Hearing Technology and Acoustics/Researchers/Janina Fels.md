Janina Fels directs the Institute for Hearing Technology and Acoustics at RWTH Aachen. Her research centers on how people (including children) perceive and understand speech and sound in complex, noisy environments such as classrooms and open-plan offices, often using virtual reality and simulated room acoustics to test "listening effort" under realistic conditions.
