Michael Vorländer led what was long known as the Institute of Technical Acoustics (ITA) and built its reputation in room-acoustics simulation and auralization — computer models that predict how a room or hall will sound and turn that prediction into audio you can listen to. He remains active in the field after retirement, continuing to publish on auralization, concert-hall acoustics, and outdoor/urban sound simulation.
