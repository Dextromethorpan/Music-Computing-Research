"Diffraction" is what lets sound bend around corners and obstacles rather than simply being blocked, which is why you can still hear someone talking from around a building corner. This paper presents a method for auralizing (simulating and rendering as listenable audio) complex outdoor city environments while correctly accounting for diffraction around buildings and other structures. Although the direct application here is urban and environmental sound rather than concert music, the same diffraction-aware auralization techniques feed directly into the institute's music- and concert-hall-focused simulation work.
