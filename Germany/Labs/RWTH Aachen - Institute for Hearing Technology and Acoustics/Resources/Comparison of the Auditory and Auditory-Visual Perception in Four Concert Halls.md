Does seeing a concert hall change how it sounds to you? This study simulates four real concert halls acoustically and visually (using virtual reality), then asks listeners to judge qualities like spaciousness, clarity, and loudness both with and without the matching visuals. It's part of a broader research line asking whether audio-only lab tests of "which hall sounds best" give the same answer as being physically present and seeing the space, which matters for architects and acousticians designing new concert venues.
