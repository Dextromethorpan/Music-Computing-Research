Instruments don't radiate sound equally in all directions — a trumpet is much louder straight out of the bell than to the side, and this "directivity pattern" changes with pitch. This paper compares two ways of finding a wind instrument's directivity pattern: physically measuring it with microphones placed all around a real instrument, versus modeling it as a simple mathematical "point source." Knowing accurate directivity patterns matters for realistic room-acoustics simulation (auralization) of orchestras and ensembles, and for recording/microphone placement.
