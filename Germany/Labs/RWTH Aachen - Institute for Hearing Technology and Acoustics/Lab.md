Part of RWTH Aachen University's Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology. Known for decades as the Institute of Technical Acoustics (ITA) under Prof. Michael Vorländer, it was renamed the Institute for Hearing Technology and Acoustics (IHTA) and is now directed by Prof. Janina Fels. The institute's core strength is room acoustics simulation and auralization — computing what a room, concert hall, or outdoor space would actually sound like before it is built, and turning that simulation into audio a person can listen to — plus binaural/spatial audio, psychoacoustics, and (increasingly) how this applies to musicians and concert-hall listening.
