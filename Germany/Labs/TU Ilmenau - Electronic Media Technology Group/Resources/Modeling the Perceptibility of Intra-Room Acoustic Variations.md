A companion study to the group's later journal paper on the same topic, presented at the 156th AES Convention (Madrid, 2024). It looks at how much a room's acoustic "fingerprint" can vary within the same room — for example, when a sound source moves slightly — before a listener perceives any difference. This kind of finding is useful background for anyone building spatial-audio or VR systems that simulate walking around inside a virtual room.
