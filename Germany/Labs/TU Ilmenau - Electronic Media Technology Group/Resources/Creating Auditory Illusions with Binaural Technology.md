An "auditory illusion" here means tricking your ears into believing a sound is coming from outside your head (rather than from inside headphones), or into believing a virtual added sound truly belongs in the real room you're standing in. This book chapter, co-authored by mp3 co-inventor Karlheinz Brandenburg, surveys the binaural (two-ear) audio techniques the group uses to produce these illusions convincingly, and explains the perceptual science behind why they work.
