"Binaural synthesis" means generating sound over headphones so it seems to come from specific points in 3D space, as if you were really there — the technology behind convincing spatial and VR audio. "Auditory augmented reality" adds virtual sounds into your real acoustic environment, matching your head movements in real time ("position-dynamic"). This open-access paper describes the actual hardware and software system the group built to do this, explains the psychoacoustic (how-humans-perceive-sound) requirements it must satisfy, and reports listening-test results on how convincing it is.
