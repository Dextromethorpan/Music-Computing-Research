MusicSphere is a European research project the TU Ilmenau group is involved in, focused on capturing and preserving the acoustic character of historically or culturally significant spaces and music — "acoustical heritage" — for example, recreating how a concert hall or performance space actually sounded in the past. This paper, presented at DAGA (the annual German Acoustics Conference), introduces the project and shows the group's most recent work extending spatial-audio capture and modeling methods toward cultural preservation.
