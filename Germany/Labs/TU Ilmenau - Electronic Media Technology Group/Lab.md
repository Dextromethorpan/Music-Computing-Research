The Electronic Media Technology group (Fachgebiet Elektronische Medientechnik) at TU Ilmenau is part of the Institute for Media Technology and has close historical ties to Fraunhofer IDMT and to audio-coding pioneer Karlheinz Brandenburg (co-inventor of mp3), who was a professor there. Current work centers on binaural synthesis, auditory augmented reality, and the psychoacoustics of spatial audio.
