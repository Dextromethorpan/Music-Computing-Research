Werner is a long-time senior researcher whose own PhD dissertation studied how context affects the perception of "externality" and sound-source location in binaural audio. He is an extremely prolific co-author across the group's spatial-audio, binaural-synthesis, and quality-evaluation research, appearing on nearly every recent group publication.
