Brandenburg is the co-inventor of the mp3 format and of MPEG-2 AAC; his 1989 PhD work at Erlangen became the technical foundation of the mp3 codec. Since 2000 he has been a full professor at TU Ilmenau's Institute for Media Technology and was also founding director of Fraunhofer IDMT in Ilmenau. His current work focuses on binaural and spatial audio, auditory augmented reality, and perceptual audio quality. He is an AES Fellow, an IEEE Fellow, and a recipient of the German Future Prize (Deutscher Zukunftspreis).
