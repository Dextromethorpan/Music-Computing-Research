zplane.development is a Berlin-based audio technology company, founded in 2000, that researches and licenses "behind the scenes" audio processing software — such as time-stretching, pitch-shifting, automatic transcription, source separation, and music analysis (tempo, key, chords) — which is built into many well-known music production programs.
