Alexander Lerch co-founded zplane.development in 2000 and led its research work on music signal processing and music analysis algorithms — the kind of software that automatically detects a track's key, tempo, or chords. In 2013 he left day-to-day work at zplane to become a professor at Georgia Tech, where he now leads the Music Informatics Group researching machine learning and audio signal processing for music, continuing many of the same lines of research he started at zplane.
