PITCHES is a piece of software that listens to a recorded piece of music and works out which notes were played, converting the audio into MIDI — a digital format that stores notes, timing, and how hard each note was struck (velocity), rather than the actual sound waveform. It uses a neural network (a machine-learning model loosely inspired by the brain) trained to recognize pitches even in a full musical mix, without first needing to separate out individual instruments. zplane says its accuracy is comparable to well-known academic transcription systems like MT3, based on their own testing. This kind of tool is useful for musicians who want to turn a recording into an editable score or MIDI file, or for other music-analysis features like automatically detecting a song's key or chords.
