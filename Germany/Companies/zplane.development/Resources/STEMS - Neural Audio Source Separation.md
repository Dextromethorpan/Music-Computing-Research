STEMS is zplane's technology for splitting a finished song back apart into its main ingredients — vocals, bass, drums, and "everything else" — even though a normal audio file never stores those parts separately. It offers two versions: a high-quality mode meant for desktop software, and a faster, lighter mode for devices with less processing power, each with an optional extra neural-network cleanup step to make the separated tracks sound clearer. This kind of technology is used for things like making karaoke tracks, remixing songs, or isolating an instrument to practice along with it.
