ELASTIQUE is zplane's flagship audio engine for changing the speed of a recording without changing its pitch, and changing its pitch without changing its speed — the two problems known as time-stretching and pitch-shifting. It comes in different versions tuned for different jobs: ELASTIQUE PRO aims for the highest sound quality on any material, ELASTIQUE EFFICIENT is a lighter-weight version built to run fast on limited hardware, and ELASTIQUE TUNE works with single-melody audio so it can be edited note-by-note almost like MIDI. It is licensed inside many well-known music programs — including Ableton Live, FL Studio, Steinberg Cubase, and Native Instruments Traktor — to power features like tempo-matching, key-lock, and vocal pitch correction. The engine is specifically designed to avoid the "chipmunk" or robotic artifacts that come from simpler stretching methods.
