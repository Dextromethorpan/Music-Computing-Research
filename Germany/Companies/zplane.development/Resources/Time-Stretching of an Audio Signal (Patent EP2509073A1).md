This European patent describes a method for stretching or compressing the duration of an audio recording — making it play slower or faster — while trying to keep the pitch and sound quality of the original intact. It covers technical details of how the signal is analyzed and reassembled to avoid the clicking, smearing, or "robotic" artifacts that cruder time-stretching methods can produce, especially on difficult material like complex musical mixes. This patent sits behind zplane's ELASTIQUE product line and is an example of how the company's core signal-processing research is protected as intellectual property.
