Dr. Achim Kuntz was one of the three engineers awarded the 2023 Joseph von Fraunhofer Prize for the MPEG-H Audio system. In press interviews he has described the goal of the system as making sure "every production, transmission, and playback situation is covered" — meaning MPEG-H isn't just a compression format but a complete toolkit spanning studio production, file formats, transmission, and personalized playback of 3D object-based audio.
