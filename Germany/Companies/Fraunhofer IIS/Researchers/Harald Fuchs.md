Harald Fuchs was one of three engineers (alongside Achim Kuntz and Adrian Murtaza) recognized with the 2023 Joseph von Fraunhofer Prize for representing the larger team that built the MPEG-H Audio system — the object-based 3D sound format now used in Brazilian TV broadcasting and Sony's 360 Reality Audio. He works on the production tools, transmission formats, and playback methods that make up the full MPEG-H Audio production-to-playback chain.
