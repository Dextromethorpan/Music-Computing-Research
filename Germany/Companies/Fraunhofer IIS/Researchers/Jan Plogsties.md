Jan Plogsties leads the Mobile Audio Rendering group within Fraunhofer IIS's Audio and Media Technologies division, working on how spatial and immersive audio (like MPEG-H content) gets turned into actual loudspeaker or headphone signals on mobile and consumer devices — the "rendering" step that happens right before playback. He has appeared in Fraunhofer's own materials discussing what motivates the division's research work.
