Adrian Murtaza was one of the three engineers recognized with the 2023 Joseph von Fraunhofer Prize, representing the wider team (which peaked at around 100 people) that developed the MPEG-H Audio object-based 3D sound system over more than a decade, from its start in 2012 through its adoption as Brazil's mandatory digital TV audio standard.
