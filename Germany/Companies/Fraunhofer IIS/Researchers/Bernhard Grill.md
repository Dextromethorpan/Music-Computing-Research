Bernhard Grill leads Fraunhofer IIS's Audio and Media Technologies division, the group responsible for mp3, AAC, xHE-AAC, MPEG-H Audio, EVS, and LC3/LC3plus. In this role he oversees the division's overall codec and immersive-audio research direction and its licensing relationships with device makers and streaming services worldwide, rather than working on a single technical problem himself.
