Matteo Torcoli works on objective audio quality assessment at Fraunhofer IIS — building automated tools that predict how human listeners would rate the quality of compressed or processed audio, so that codec developers don't need a full panel of human listeners for every test. He has co-authored work extending the PEAQ quality-prediction standard and building open datasets (such as ODAQ) for comparing audio quality tools.
