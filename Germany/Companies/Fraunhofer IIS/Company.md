Fraunhofer IIS, based in Erlangen, is the applied-research institute behind mp3, AAC, and today's leading audio codecs (xHE-AAC, MPEG-H Audio, EVS, LC3/LC3plus). Its Audio and Media Technologies division develops and licenses codec, streaming, and immersive-audio technology used across billions of consumer devices, distinct from the academic AudioLabs Erlangen joint institute it also co-runs.
