This is Fraunhofer IIS's own overview of MPEG-H Audio, its open, standardized system for next-generation "object-based" 3D sound — covering the whole chain from studio production tools, through file/transmission formats, to the software that renders the final sound on a TV, phone, or streaming app. It's the practical, product-facing counterpart to the more academic MPEG-H research: rather than a single trick, MPEG-H is a full toolkit that lets broadcasters and streaming services deliver personalizable, spatial sound (e.g., adjustable dialogue level, selectable commentary language, positionable voices) instead of one fixed stereo or surround mix. For a beginner, this page is useful as a map of everything the "object-based audio" idea actually requires in a real product.
