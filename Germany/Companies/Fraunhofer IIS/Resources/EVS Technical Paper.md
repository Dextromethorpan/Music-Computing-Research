This paper describes EVS ("Enhanced Voice Services"), the 3GPP standard speech and audio codec used in modern mobile "HD Voice" calls, which Fraunhofer IIS co-developed. Unlike older phone codecs that were tuned only for speech, EVS was designed to sound good for speech, music, and mixed content at the same time, and to keep working even when parts of the data get lost over a shaky mobile network (packet loss). The paper walks through how the codec adapts its behavior depending on what kind of sound it is encoding and the available bitrate. For a beginner: this is the technology quietly running underneath a phone call whenever it suddenly sounds much clearer than an old-style call — that's usually EVS at work.
