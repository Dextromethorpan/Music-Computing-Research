Klangio is a research-driven AI music transcription company founded in 2018 in Karlsruhe, Germany, growing out of work at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). It builds instrument-specific AI models (for piano, guitar, voice, drums, and full bands) that turn audio recordings into sheet music, MIDI, and guitar tablature, and its team regularly publishes peer-reviewed research on the underlying transcription methods.
