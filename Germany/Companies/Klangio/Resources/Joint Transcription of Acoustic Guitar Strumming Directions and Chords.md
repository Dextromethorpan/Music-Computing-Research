Strummed acoustic guitar playing has two things a transcription tool needs to capture together: which chord is being played, and whether the strumming hand moves up or down across the strings at each moment. This paper builds a model that identifies both of these at the same time directly from an audio recording, rather than treating them as two separate problems. Getting the strumming direction right matters for anyone trying to actually learn or recreate a guitar part from a transcription, not just the notes but the physical playing technique behind them.
