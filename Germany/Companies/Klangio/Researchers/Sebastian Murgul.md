Sebastian Murgul co-founded Klangio in Karlsruhe in 2018 after transcribing a song for his sister by hand with an oscilloscope. He is also a doctoral researcher at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), where his work on automatic music transcription (getting a computer to turn a recording into readable notes) feeds directly into Klangio's products. He is the lead or corresponding author on several of Klangio's published research papers on transcribing guitar and piano performances.
