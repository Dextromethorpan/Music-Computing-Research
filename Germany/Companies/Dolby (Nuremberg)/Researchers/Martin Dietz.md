Martin Dietz was one of the engineers behind Spectral Band Replication (SBR), the technique that lets the HE-AAC audio codec (marketed as aacPlus) deliver good-sounding audio at very low bitrates by regenerating high frequencies from lower ones at playback time. He worked at Coding Technologies, the company whose German subsidiary became Dolby Germany GmbH in Nuremberg after Dolby's 2007 acquisition. Along with Lars Liljeryd and Kristofer Kjörling, he received the IEEE Masaru Ibuka Consumer Electronics Award in 2013 for developing and commercializing this technology, and he co-authored a widely cited IEEE Signal Processing Magazine explainer on HE-AAC with Fraunhofer IIS researcher Jürgen Herre.
