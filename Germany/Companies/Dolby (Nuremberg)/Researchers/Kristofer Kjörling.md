Kristofer Kjörling co-developed Spectral Band Replication (SBR) at Coding Technologies, the audio-codec company that became Dolby's German and Swedish audio-coding engineering base after its 2007 acquisition by Dolby Laboratories. SBR is the core technology behind the HE-AAC codec, which is used across digital radio (DAB+, HD Radio), mobile streaming, and broadcast. Along with Lars Liljeryd and Martin Dietz, Kjörling received the IEEE Masaru Ibuka Consumer Electronics Award in 2013 for this work.
