Dolby's Nuremberg site grew out of Coding Technologies GmbH, the German subsidiary of the Swedish company that invented Spectral Band Replication (SBR) — the technology behind the HE-AAC ("aacPlus") audio codec used in digital radio and mobile streaming worldwide. After Dolby Laboratories acquired Coding Technologies in 2007, the subsidiary was renamed Dolby Germany GmbH, and its Nuremberg-area engineers have continued audio-codec R&D closely tied to the neighboring Fraunhofer Institute for Integrated Circuits (IIS) in Erlangen, the birthplace of MP3.
