Georg Neumann GmbH ("Neumann.Berlin"), founded in 1928 and part of the Sennheiser Group since 1991, is a studio-microphone and monitor manufacturer with a distinct acoustic-engineering heritage (the U 47, M 49, U 67, U 87) that continues today in transducer design for microphones and, since absorbing Klein + Hummel's legacy in 2010, studio monitors. Its current publicly documented engineering output is concentrated in named historical inventions and product-level DSP/acoustic engineering rather than published papers or a technical blog.
