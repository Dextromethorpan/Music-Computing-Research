Georg Neumann founded the company in Berlin in 1928 and personally engineered its foundational microphones, including the U 47 (1949) — the first microphone with electronically switchable pickup patterns. Rather than reissuing pre-war designs after World War II, he chose to redesign from scratch around newer vacuum-tube technology, a decision that defined the acoustic character ("the Neumann sound") still associated with the brand today.
