Every room colors the sound of loudspeakers differently because walls, furniture, and room shape reflect and absorb different frequencies unevenly. The MA 1 is a measurement system that listens to test tones played through a studio's monitor speakers with a calibrated microphone, works out how the room is distorting the sound, and then automatically adjusts the speakers' internal DSP (digital signal processing) to correct for it — aiming to give any studio a more accurate, "reference-grade" listening experience regardless of room shape or size. It's a practical example of applied room acoustics: using measurement plus digital correction instead of expensive physical room treatment alone.
