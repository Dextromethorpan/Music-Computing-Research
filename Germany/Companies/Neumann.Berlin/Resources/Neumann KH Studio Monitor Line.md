Neumann's KH-series studio monitors continue the engineering lineage of Klein + Hummel, the German company that built the world's first active studio monitor (the OY) back in 1967 — "active" meaning the amplifier is built directly into the speaker cabinet and tuned specifically for its own drivers, rather than being a separate generic amplifier. Neumann took over this monitor line in 2010 and modernized it with newer transducer (loudspeaker driver) engineering and onboard DSP for shaping the frequency response with precision. The stated design goal across the range — from the compact KH 80 to the large KH 420 — is "neutrality": reproducing a mix exactly as it was made, so it translates predictably to other playback systems, which is the core job of any studio reference monitor.
