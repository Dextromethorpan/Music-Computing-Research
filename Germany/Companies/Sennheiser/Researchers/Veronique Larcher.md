Dr. Veronique Larcher directs Sennheiser's AMBEO Immersive Audio program, leading the company's push into 3D/spatial sound since joining Sennheiser roughly 13 years before 2020. She has overseen the launch of AMBEO's first hardware (the AMBEO VR Mic) and mobile 3D-audio recording products (the AMBEO Smart Headset), and speaks publicly about how immersive audio is moving from a niche cinema/VR technology into mainstream music listening.
