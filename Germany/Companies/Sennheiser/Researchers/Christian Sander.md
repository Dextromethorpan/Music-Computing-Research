Christian Sander co-founded Dear Reality in Düsseldorf in 2014, building binaural rendering software (algorithms that make a sound appear to come from a specific point in 3D space over ordinary headphones) for use in VR, AR, games, and music production. Sennheiser acquired Dear Reality in 2019 to strengthen its AMBEO immersive-audio division, folding Sander's spatial-audio algorithm work into the larger company.
