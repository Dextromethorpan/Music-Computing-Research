Achim Fell co-founded Dear Reality alongside Christian Sander in 2014, developing the company's binaural spatial-audio software before it became part of the Sennheiser Group in 2019, contributing to Sennheiser's AMBEO immersive-audio product line, including plugins used for mixing 3D audio and Dolby Atmos content over headphones.
