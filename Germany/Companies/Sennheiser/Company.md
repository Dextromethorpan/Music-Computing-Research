Sennheiser, headquartered in Wedemark near Hannover, is a German audio equipment manufacturer whose professional R&D today centers on the AMBEO immersive/spatial-audio program, run out of dedicated R&D sites in Germany, Denmark, Switzerland, Singapore, and the US, and reinforced by its 2019 acquisition of spatial-audio software company Dear Reality.
