In this interview, Sennheiser's Director of AMBEO Immersive Audio, Dr. Veronique Larcher, explains the company's thinking behind its move into 3D/immersive sound. She describes the AMBEO VR Mic (2016) as the first product of the program, points to Neumann's classic KU 100 "dummy head" binaural microphone as an early example of the same underlying idea (recording sound the way two human ears would hear it), and discusses the AMBEO Smart Headset — the first compact, mobile headset that lets anyone record 3D audio just by wearing it. It's a useful beginner-level window into why a traditional microphone and headphone manufacturer decided immersive audio was worth building a whole product division around.
