AMBEO is Sennheiser's umbrella program for "3D audio" — sound that surrounds a listener in height and depth, not just left-right stereo. It started in 2016 with the AMBEO VR Mic, a microphone built to capture full 360-degree sound for virtual reality, and later grew to include products like the AMBEO Smart Headset (a mobile 3D-audio recording headset) and software for mixing immersive music and film soundtracks. The general idea behind AMBEO is "object-based" and ambisonic-style capture and playback: recording or describing sound so its spatial position is preserved and can be reproduced accurately on very different playback systems, from headphones to full immersive speaker setups.
