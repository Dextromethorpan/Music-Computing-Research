Dear Reality is a Düsseldorf-founded spatial-audio software company that Sennheiser invested in (2018) and then fully acquired (2019) to strengthen its AMBEO immersive-audio work. Its core technology is "binaural rendering": taking a sound and processing it so that, played back over ordinary stereo headphones, it appears to come from a specific point in 3D space around the listener (e.g. behind, above, or to one side) — the same trick your two ears normally use to localize real-world sounds. Their plugins (dearVR PRO, dearVR MUSIC, dearVR MONITOR, and others) let audio engineers place and monitor sounds in 3D space directly inside their existing studio software, and have become widely used for mixing immersive formats like Dolby Atmos over headphones.
