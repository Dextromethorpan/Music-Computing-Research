Stems is an open audio file format developed by Native Instruments that packages a single track as four separate musical layers inside one file — for example a drums stem, a bassline stem, a melody stem, and a vocal stem. Instead of DJs and producers only being able to play or scratch a finished, fully-mixed song, a Stems file lets them mute, solo, or remix each of those four layers live, instantly creating new mixes, mashups, instrumentals, or a cappellas. It's a practical, product-facing take on the idea of keeping a song's separate musical parts accessible rather than permanently mixed together, aimed at DJs, producers, and software/hardware developers who build tools around the format.
