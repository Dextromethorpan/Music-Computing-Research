Native Instruments is a Berlin-based maker of music production software and hardware (Reaktor, Kontakt, Traktor, Maschine, Komplete). Its technical R&D has historically centered on modular/DSP-level sound synthesis and virtual-analog modeling; its public, citable research output is genuinely limited compared to the other companies in this vault — mostly one influential internally-authored technical text and one open file-format project, rather than a steady stream of papers or an active engineering blog. (Note: in 2026 the company entered insolvency proceedings and was subsequently acquired by inMusic Brands.)
