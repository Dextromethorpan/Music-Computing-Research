Stephan Schmitt co-founded Native Instruments and, together with Volker Hinz, built Generator (1996), the modular software synthesizer that evolved into Reaktor — a visual, patch-cable-style environment where musicians wire together modules (oscillators, filters, samplers) to build their own instruments and effects from scratch. This modular, build-your-own-instrument approach became one of Native Instruments' core technical identities.
