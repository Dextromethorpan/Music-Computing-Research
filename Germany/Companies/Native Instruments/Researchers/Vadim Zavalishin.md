Vadim Zavalishin joined Native Instruments in 2003, bringing his earlier "Sync Modular" software project with him, and built "Reaktor Core Technology," a deeper, DSP-level layer added to the Reaktor modular synthesis environment in 2005 that let sound designers work directly with lower-level digital signal processing building blocks. He is the author of "The Art of VA Filter Design," a widely used reference on making digital filters sound like real analog circuits.
