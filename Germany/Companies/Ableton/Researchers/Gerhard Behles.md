Gerhard Behles co-founded Ableton in 1999 and remains its CEO. Before Ableton, he worked on granular synthesis (a technique that builds sounds out of many tiny, reorganized "grains" of audio) for Native Instruments' Reaktor software, building on earlier synthesis software he developed on a Silicon Graphics workstation while at Technische Universität Berlin. That early technical work on real-time sound synthesis directly informed the design of Ableton Live, which was built to let musicians manipulate and reshape audio live, in real time, rather than only in a fixed studio timeline.
