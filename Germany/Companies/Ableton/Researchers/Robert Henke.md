Robert Henke co-created Ableton Live alongside Gerhard Behles and Bernd Roggendorf, bringing an engineering background into the software's design. Outside of Ableton he works as a computer-music artist under the name Monolake, building his own custom instruments, sound algorithms, and (more recently) high-power laser installations, and he lectures on sound and creative computer use at institutions including the Berlin University of the Arts and Stanford's Center for Computer Research in Music and Acoustics (CCRMA). His dual role as both a company co-founder and a practicing computer-music researcher/educator is part of why Ableton's product design has long been shaped by genuine experimentation with live electronic performance.
