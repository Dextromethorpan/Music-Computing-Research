Learning Music is a free, browser-based interactive course built by Ableton that teaches the basics of music-making — beats, tempo, scales, chords, basslines, melody, and song structure — without requiring any prior musical knowledge or any software installation. Instead of reading about music theory abstractly, a beginner clicks on-screen boxes to turn musical patterns on and off and hears the result immediately, learning concepts like "backbeats" or "minor scales" by direct experimentation, often illustrated using real, recognizable songs. The project is a genuine piece of applied music-education research and design, built by Ableton's education and documentation team to lower the barrier of entry to electronic music production. It reflects the same instructional thinking that shapes Ableton Live's own beginner-friendly workflow.
