Ableton is a Berlin-based company best known for the digital audio workstation Ableton Live and the Push hardware controller. Its R&D output shows up less as academic papers and more as open, documented engineering projects — a widely-adopted device-synchronization protocol, an open extension platform, and interactive music-education tools — reflecting a "learning by building and sharing" approach to music technology research.
