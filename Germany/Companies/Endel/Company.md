Endel is a Berlin-registered company (Endel Sound GmbH) that builds an app generating personalized, real-time ambient soundscapes intended to help people focus, relax, sleep, or manage ADHD. Its patented "Pacific" engine uses inputs like time of day, weather, heart rate, and motion to procedurally assemble sound on-device rather than streaming pre-recorded tracks, and the company has partnered with outside researchers to test whether its soundscapes measurably affect attention and focus.
