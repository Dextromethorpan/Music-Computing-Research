Dmitry Evgrafov is a co-founder of Endel and, as a composer, leads the design of the actual sound material — the tones, textures, and musical building blocks — that Endel's AI engine assembles into personalized soundscapes. He is also a named inventor on Endel's soundscape-generation patents, bridging the creative and technical sides of the product.
