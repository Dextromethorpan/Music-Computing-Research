Oleg Stavitsky co-founded Endel in 2018 and leads the company as CEO. He is listed as an inventor on Endel's core patents covering how the app reads sensor data (like motion or heart rate) and uses it to generate a personalized, continuously-changing soundscape, and he is Endel's public-facing spokesperson for how the technology and its focus/sleep/relax products work.
