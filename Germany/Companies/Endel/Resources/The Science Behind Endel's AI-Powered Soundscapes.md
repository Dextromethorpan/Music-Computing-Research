This Amazon Science feature explains the reasoning behind Endel's soundscapes: rather than using pre-recorded music, Endel builds sound in real time out of small components (like tones, textures, and rhythmic elements) that are combined and adjusted according to inputs such as time of day, weather, heart rate, and movement. The article describes how Endel's approach draws on ideas from psychoacoustics — the study of how humans perceive and respond to sound — to design soundscapes intended to support focus, relaxation, or sleep, and how the Alexa Fund's investment connects Endel to Amazon's smart-speaker ecosystem.
