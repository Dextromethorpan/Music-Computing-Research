This is Endel's foundational patent, covering the core idea behind its app: a system that reads signals from a phone or wearable device — like motion, time of day, or other sensor data — and uses them to automatically choose and continuously adjust sounds and visuals to match the user's current activity and state. Instead of picking a playlist yourself, the software decides in real time what should play and keeps changing it as your situation changes (for example, as you move from working to relaxing). This patent underlies Endel's later, more specific soundscape-generation patents.
