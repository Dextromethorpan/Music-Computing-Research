This page explains "Endel Pacific," the patented engine behind all of Endel's apps and hardware integrations (including partnerships with Sony headphones and a Mercedes-Benz in-car experience). It walks through the three-part pipeline: inputs (like time of day, weather, heart rate, or head position), the "Core AI Logic" that turns those inputs into a personalization profile, and a sound-generation stage where pre-designed sound elements — built by Endel's in-house sound team — are assembled and adapted on the fly. The soundscape is generated live, on the listener's own device, rather than streamed as a finished audio file.
