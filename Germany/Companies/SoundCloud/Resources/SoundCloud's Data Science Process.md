Six SoundCloud data scientists jointly wrote up the step-by-step process their team uses to turn a business question (e.g. "how do we recommend better music?") into a shipped, working machine learning system: defining the problem and success metrics, exploring the available data, prototyping and comparing candidate solutions against a simple baseline, deploying carefully (often as an A/B test on a subset of users first), and then continuously monitoring and retraining the system in production. It's less about one specific algorithm and more a practical, honest look at how a real music-streaming company organizes machine learning work as an iterative, collaborative process rather than a one-shot research exercise.
