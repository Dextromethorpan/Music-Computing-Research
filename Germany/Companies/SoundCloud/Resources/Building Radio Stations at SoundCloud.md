This post describes the design thinking behind SoundCloud's Artist and Track "Stations" — an automatically generated, continuously playing radio-like experience built on top of the same machine learning infrastructure as Suggested Tracks. The team identifies three ingredients borrowed from how good traditional radio works: novelty (playing something new), familiarity (keeping some songs recognizable so listeners don't lose their footing), and context (not jumping jarringly between unrelated genres or moods). The algorithm effectively acts as an automated radio curator, deciding what counts as new, familiar, or trending for each individual listener while keeping a consistent mood.
