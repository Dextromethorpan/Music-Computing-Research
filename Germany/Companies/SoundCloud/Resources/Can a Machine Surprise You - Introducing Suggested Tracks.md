This post introduces "Suggested Tracks," SoundCloud's first machine-learning-driven track recommendation feature, built by its Core Data Science team. The interesting framing here is "digital serendipity": rather than only optimizing for tracks a listener is statistically likely to already enjoy (safe, familiar recommendations), the team wanted a recommender that could genuinely surprise a listener with something new they hadn't heard but end up loving — treating the emotional experience of discovery, not just topical similarity, as something a recommendation algorithm should try to model, learning continuously from what a listener plays, likes, reposts, and shares.
