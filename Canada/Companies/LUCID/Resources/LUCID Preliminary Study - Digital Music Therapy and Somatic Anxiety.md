A clinical study with 264 participants experiencing moderate to severe anxiety, comparing a single listening session of LUCID's personalized digital music therapy against a "pink noise" control (a type of steady background noise used as a neutral comparison in hearing/relaxation research). The study found LUCID's session produced significantly larger reductions in both physical ("somatic," e.g. racing heart, tense muscles) and mental ("cognitive," e.g. worry, racing thoughts) anxiety symptoms than the control condition.
