This peer-reviewed paper lays out the design of a music-based digital therapeutic aimed at people living with Alzheimer's disease, whose agitation and anxiety symptoms are often difficult to treat with medication alone. LUCID's approach personalizes a listening session using biometric and self-reported mood data, then delivers music blended with auditory beat stimulation (subtle rhythmic pulses under the music). The paper documents the clinical reasoning and system design behind the product, rather than treating "personalized music" as a vague wellness claim.
