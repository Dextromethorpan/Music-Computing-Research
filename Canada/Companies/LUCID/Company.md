LUCID (LUCID Therapeutics), based in Toronto, builds personalized music-based digital therapeutics that combine AI, mood assessment, and auditory beat stimulation (rhythmic pulses layered under music, thought to nudge brain activity) to treat conditions like anxiety and Alzheimer's-related agitation. Included via path 1: LUCID has published peer-reviewed clinical research on its technology and formed a research partnership with Mila, Canada's largest deep learning institute.
