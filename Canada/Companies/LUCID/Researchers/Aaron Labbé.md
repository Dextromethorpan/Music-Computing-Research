Aaron Labbé developed the initial concept for LUCID during his undergraduate thesis in New Media and Computational Art at what is now Toronto Metropolitan University, drawing on his own experience with severe mental health struggles to research the therapeutic potential of sound and music. As CTO, he leads the technology behind LUCID's personalized, AI-driven music therapy platform.
