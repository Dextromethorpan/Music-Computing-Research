Pascal Pilon has been Chairman and CEO of LANDR since investing in the company as its lead seed investor in 2013. He is also President of YUL Ventures, a Montreal early-stage technology investment fund. At LANDR, he has overseen the company's growth from an automatic mastering tool into a broader AI-assisted music creation and distribution platform.
