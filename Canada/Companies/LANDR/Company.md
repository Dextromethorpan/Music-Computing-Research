LANDR, based in Montreal, built the first widely-used AI-powered automatic music mastering service (mastering is the last audio-engineering step that makes a finished song sound polished and consistent for release). Included via path 1: LANDR holds a real machine-learning patent (co-developed with researchers at Queen Mary University of London) and its technology has been the subject of independent academic study.
