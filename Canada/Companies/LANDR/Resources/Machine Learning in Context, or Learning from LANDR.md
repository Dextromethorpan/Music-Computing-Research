This is an independent academic paper (not written by LANDR itself) that closely examines how LANDR's automatic mastering actually works and what "artificial intelligence" means in that context. The authors interviewed engineers and studied the product to explain that LANDR's system is a form of supervised machine learning: it was trained by having engineers rate many mastered tracks, and the software learned patterns from those examples to apply similar-sounding processing to new songs. It's included here as a rigorous, technically-grounded outside account of LANDR's real underlying method, distinct from marketing claims of "AI."
