This patent describes a system that can automatically process a multi-track recording (a song with separate tracks for vocals, drums, bass, etc.) without a human engineer manually adjusting each one. Instead of hard-coded rules, the system analyzes the audio and makes automatic decisions about things like relative loudness between tracks and overall tonal balance, which is the core idea behind LANDR's automatic mastering product. The patent was filed jointly with academic audio-engineering researchers at Queen Mary University of London, showing the underlying technology grew out of real signal-processing research rather than simple presets.
