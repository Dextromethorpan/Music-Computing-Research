Zohaib Ahmed founded Resemble AI in Toronto in February 2019 after working as a lead software engineer at Magic Leap, having studied computer science at the University of Toronto. Under his leadership, Resemble AI has built both generative voice-cloning products and dedicated deepfake-detection tools (including the "PerTh Watermarker" for invisibly marking AI-generated audio), and he has testified before the U.S. Senate on AI oversight and election deepfakes.
