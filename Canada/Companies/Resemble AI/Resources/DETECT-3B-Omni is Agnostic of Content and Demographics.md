As AI voice generators get better, it becomes harder to tell a real recorded voice from a synthetic one just by listening, which creates a real risk for scams and misinformation. This paper tests Resemble AI's deepfake-voice detector and shows that its accuracy doesn't depend on what the speaker is saying, or on their gender, age, or accent/region — evidence that the detector is picking up on genuine acoustic artifacts left behind by AI voice generation, rather than accidentally learning shortcuts tied to a particular kind of voice or speech content.
