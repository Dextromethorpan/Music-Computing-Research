Resemblyzer is an open-source tool that turns a short clip of someone's voice into an "embedding" — a list of 256 numbers that summarizes the distinctive characteristics of that voice, similar to a fingerprint. Two voice clips can then be compared just by comparing their number lists, which is the basic building block behind tasks like checking whether two recordings are the same speaker, or flagging when a voice doesn't match a known reference (useful for both deepfake detection and simple speaker verification).
