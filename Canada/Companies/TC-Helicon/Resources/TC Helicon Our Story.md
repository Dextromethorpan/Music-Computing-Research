TC-Helicon's own account of its history: founded in 2000 in Victoria, British Columbia as a joint venture between Denmark's TC Group and the Canadian voice-processing specialists at IVL Technologies (the team behind some of the earliest commercial auto-harmony hardware). It explains the company's singular focus on the human voice, culminating in products like the VoiceLive series, which combine real-time pitch correction, automatic harmony generation, and vocal looping in one performance pedal.
