This patent describes how a device can automatically generate believable-sounding vocal harmonies underneath a singer's live voice. It works by feeding the device the accompanying instrumental track (so it knows the song's musical key at any moment), then detecting and correcting the pitch of the live vocal melody, and finally synthesizing new harmony voices that stay in key with the song as it changes chords — all in real time, so a solo singer can sound like they have backing vocalists.
