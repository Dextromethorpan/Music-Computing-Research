TC-Helicon, based in Victoria, British Columbia, builds hardware and software dedicated almost entirely to processing the human singing voice in real time — pitch correction, automatic vocal harmony generation, and looping. It grew out of IVL Technologies, a Victoria-based voice-processing specialist behind some of the earliest auto-harmony products. Included via path 1: TC-Helicon holds patents on its real-time harmony-generation methods and has decades of dedicated DSP engineering behind it.
