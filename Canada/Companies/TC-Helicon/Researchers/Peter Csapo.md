Peter Csapo is a software engineer who spent seven years at TC-Helicon as a lead developer designing the firmware for its voice-processing hardware, drawing on a background that includes optimizing beam-forming algorithms for sonar and writing low-level device drivers. He was later involved in developing a new line of voice-processing products aimed at online gamers and livestreamers.
