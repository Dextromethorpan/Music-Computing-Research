Daniel Winner started his career as a DSP engineer at IVL Audio before joining the founding team of TC-Helicon Vocal Technologies in 2001 as its principal DSP algorithm engineer. Over 16 years at the company, his algorithm work was integral to the software and firmware behind essentially every TC-Helicon vocal-processing product, including its pitch-correction and harmony-generation engines.
