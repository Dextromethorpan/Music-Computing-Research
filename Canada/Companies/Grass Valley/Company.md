Grass Valley, headquartered in Montreal, builds broadcast video and audio infrastructure — the routing, processing, and distribution equipment TV networks use to move signals around. Its Montreal roots trace back to Miranda Technologies, a Montreal broadcast-technology company it absorbed. Included via path 2 (specialized infrastructure/protocol work): Grass Valley's audio products handle Dolby E/AC-3 encode-decode, loudness compliance, and audio-over-IP transport (AES67/SMPTE ST 2110-30), and the company holds patents specifically on reducing audio-processing latency in its routers — no individual named researchers were verifiably identified for this specific work, so this org currently has 0 researcher profiles.
