When a broadcast router switches between different video sources, any audio traveling alongside that video can arrive slightly out of sync if it takes a different, slower path through the equipment. This patent (awarded to Montreal-based Miranda Technologies, whose products and IP are now part of Grass Valley) describes a method for cutting that audio delay down to as little as one line of video — an extremely small unit of time — even when the router has to shuffle audio between formats, keeping audio and video tightly synchronized during live broadcast switching.
