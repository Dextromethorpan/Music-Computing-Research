Television broadcasters need to move audio between many pieces of equipment reliably, often bundled together with video, and often needing format conversion along the way. This datasheet documents Grass Valley's Densité audio-processing cards, which can embed and un-embed audio into a video signal, decode/encode Dolby E and Dolby Digital (AC-3) surround formats, measure and automatically correct loudness to broadcast standards (like EBU R128), and transport audio over standard computer networks using the AES67 and SMPTE ST 2110-30 audio-over-IP standards — real infrastructure engineering behind how sound reaches viewers' TVs.
