A Game Developers Conference (GDC) 2023 technical session (delivered with Dolby) where Audiokinetic's engineers explain how Wwise renders spatial audio in real time for games, including how it combines room/portal acoustic modeling with object-based formats like Dolby Atmos so a game's sound can be mixed once and correctly reproduced on very different playback systems, from a phone speaker to a full home theatre.
