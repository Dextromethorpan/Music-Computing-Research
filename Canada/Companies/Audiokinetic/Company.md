Audiokinetic, headquartered in Montreal, makes Wwise, one of the two dominant audio middleware systems used to build sound and music for video games (the other being FMOD). Wwise is included via path 1 (traditional R&D): it has a dedicated research and technology team, real technical depth in real-time spatial/acoustic simulation, and a regular presence of technical talks at GDC (the Game Developers Conference).
