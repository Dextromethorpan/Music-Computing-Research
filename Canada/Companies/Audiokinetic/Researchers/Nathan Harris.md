Nathan Harris joined Audiokinetic in 2012 after working in the video game industry since the mid-2000s. Since 2016 he has led the design of Wwise's Spatial Audio library, applying techniques from reverb propagation and acoustic simulation so that games can automatically compute realistic-sounding spaces instead of requiring hand-tuned audio for every room. He now works on Audiokinetic's research and technology team as a spatial audio and software architecture specialist.
