This is a public listing of the roughly 30 patents Adamson holds for its loudspeaker technology. The patents cover things like arranging multiple mid-frequency drivers on either side of a high-frequency horn so their sound overlaps smoothly (avoiding the "holes" in coverage that can happen when different speaker drivers are combined), and systems for distributing power, audio signal, and control data to multiple powered speaker cabinets over a single connection. Together they document real, protected engineering work behind the acoustic and electrical design of Adamson's line-array speaker systems, not just manufacturing.
