An article from the Toronto section of the Audio Engineering Society profiling Adamson's engineering history and approach, written for an audience of professional audio engineers. It covers how the company, founded in 1983 by Brock Adamson, built the first line-array loudspeaker system designed and manufactured in North America, and how its current engineering team continues to design and test drivers, waveguides, and networked amplifier/control systems in-house in Ontario.
