Adamson Systems Engineering, based in Port Perry, Ontario, designs and manufactures professional line-array loudspeaker systems used in touring concerts, stadiums, and permanent installations. Included via path 1: the company holds roughly 30 patents covering driver design, waveguide/horn geometry, and cabinet and rigging systems, reflecting sustained in-house acoustic and mechanical engineering rather than off-the-shelf assembly.
