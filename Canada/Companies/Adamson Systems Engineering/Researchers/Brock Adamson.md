Brock Adamson founded Adamson Systems Engineering in 1983 and built it into a leading maker of line-array touring loudspeaker systems, credited with the first line array designed and manufactured in North America. He returned to lead the company as President and CEO in 2023 after a management restructuring, and has continued to push the company toward networked audio technologies such as AVB (Audio Video Bridging).
