Lyrebird AI was a Montreal voice-cloning startup founded in 2017 by three researchers who studied under deep-learning pioneer Yoshua Bengio at Mila (the University of Montreal's AI institute). Included via path 1: the team published real peer-reviewed research, most notably the widely-cited MelGAN paper, before the company was acquired by Descript in 2019, where its technology lives on as the "Overdub" voice-cloning feature.
