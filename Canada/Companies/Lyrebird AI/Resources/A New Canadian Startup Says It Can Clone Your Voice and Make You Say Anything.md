A CBC News report from Lyrebird's 2017 public launch, when the Montreal startup demonstrated that it could generate a convincing synthetic clone of a person's voice from just one minute of recorded audio, far less than voice-cloning systems typically needed at the time. The piece documents the company's Mila/University of Montreal research roots and the early public reaction to how realistic — and potentially concerning — synthetic voice technology had become.
