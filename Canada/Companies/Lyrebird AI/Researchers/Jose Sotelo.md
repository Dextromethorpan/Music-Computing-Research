Jose Sotelo co-founded Lyrebird AI in Montreal in 2017, having studied at the Université de Montréal under Yoshua Bengio's deep learning group. He was a core contributor to Lyrebird's voice-synthesis research, including the MelGAN paper, and continued working on speech synthesis at Descript after Lyrebird's 2019 acquisition.
