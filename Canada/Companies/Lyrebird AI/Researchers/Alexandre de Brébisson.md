Alexandre de Brébisson co-founded Lyrebird AI in 2017 while studying deep learning under Yoshua Bengio at Mila in Montreal, and served as the company's CEO. He worked on the underlying voice-synthesis research, including contributing to the MelGAN paper, before Lyrebird was acquired by Descript in 2019.
