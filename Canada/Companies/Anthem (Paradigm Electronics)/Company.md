Anthem, a home-theatre electronics brand made by Paradigm Electronics (Toronto, Ontario), develops Anthem Room Correction (ARC), a room-calibration technology built directly on decades of acoustics research done with Canada's National Research Council (NRC). Included via path 1: this is a genuine, long-running academic-to-industry research pipeline (the NRC's "Athena Project"), backed by patents and Paradigm's own dedicated acoustics research group.
