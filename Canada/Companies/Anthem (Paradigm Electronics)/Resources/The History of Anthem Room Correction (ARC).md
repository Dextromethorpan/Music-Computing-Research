Every room changes how a loudspeaker sounds — walls, furniture, and room shape all add unwanted bumps and dips to the sound. Anthem Room Correction (ARC) is a system that measures a listening room with a microphone and then automatically adjusts the audio signal to compensate. This page traces ARC's origins to a multi-year Canadian government research project (the "Athena Project") run at the National Research Council, which studied what an ideal in-room speaker response should actually sound like before Anthem's engineers turned that research into a practical measurement-and-correction product.
