This patent covers methods for automatically measuring a room's acoustic response and then computing a correction filter for one or more loudspeakers so that what a listener hears matches an ideal target sound as closely as possible, accounting for things like differing speaker sensitivities, uneven speaker placement, and asymmetric rooms. It is the protected technology underlying Anthem's Room Correction (ARC) products.
