A biography published by the Toronto chapter of the Audio Engineering Society describing Peter Schuck's career path from the University of Waterloo's Audio Research Group, through active noise-control research and the "Athena Project" at Canada's National Research Council, to founding Paradigm's in-house acoustics research center. It documents the direct research lineage between government acoustics research and Anthem/Paradigm's commercial room-correction and loudspeaker products.
