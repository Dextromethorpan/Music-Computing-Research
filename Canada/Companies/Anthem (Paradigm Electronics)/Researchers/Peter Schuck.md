Peter Schuck began his acoustics career at the University of Waterloo's Audio Research Group, then spent years at Canada's National Research Council researching active noise control and leading the "Athena Project," which studied what an ideal loudspeaker response should sound like inside a real room. In 1993 he joined Paradigm Electronics, where he co-founded the company's in-house Advanced Research Center and has since worked on automated loudspeaker measurement, loudspeaker modeling, and the DSP firmware behind Anthem's room-correction (ARC) and Paradigm's active speaker and subwoofer products.
