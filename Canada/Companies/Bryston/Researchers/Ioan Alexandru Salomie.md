Dr. Ioan Alexandru Salomie was a PhD engineer who approached Bryston with a new low-distortion input-circuit concept, which Bryston then hired him to co-develop into what became the patented "Salomie circuit" used across its Cubed Series amplifiers and preamplifiers. He passed away before seeing the final production implementation of his design.
