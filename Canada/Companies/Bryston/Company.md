Bryston, based in Peterborough, Ontario, designs and manufactures high-end amplifiers and preamplifiers. Included via path 3 (documented product/architectural innovation): its patented "Salomie circuit" is a specific, nameable input-stage architecture — a novel arrangement of twelve active components acting as a low-noise input buffer — that measurably cuts distortion to roughly a tenth of what conventional input stages achieve, and is documented in both a patent and Bryston's own technical materials.
