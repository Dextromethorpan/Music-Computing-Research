matralab is a research-creation space at Concordia University in Montreal, part of the Hexagram network, directed by Sandeep Bhagwati (Canada Research Chair in Inter-X Art Practice and Theory). It works at the crossroads of music, movement, and media art, developing new kinds of interactive musical notation, wearable and sensor-based performance systems, and intercultural/interdisciplinary art practice — with strong ties to the NIME and TENOR (Technologies for Notation and Representation) research communities.
