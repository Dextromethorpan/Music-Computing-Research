Jen Reimer is a Montreal-based sound artist and performer who coordinates matralab's day-to-day operations. Her own artistic work explores urban soundscapes and resonant spaces — recording and composing with sounds gathered from metro stations, parking garages, and abandoned buildings — bringing a practicing sound artist's perspective to the lab's mix of interactive-notation and performance-technology research.
