Navid Navab is a Montreal-based media artist, composer, and interaction designer, and a PhD candidate connected to matralab, who also co-directs the Topological Media Lab. He builds responsive, often robotic sound and performance environments — for example "Organism," a project that uses robotics to drive a century-old pipe organ into unconventional, unstable timbres — exploring how machines, materials, and sound can behave in ways that feel unpredictable or almost alive, rather than being fully pre-programmed.
