Terri Hron is a musician, performer, and multimedia artist who coordinated matralab's Technologies for Notation and Representation (TENOR) research network from 2017 to 2020, connecting matralab to the wider international TENOR conference community. Her own research and performance work focuses on collaborative composition and scoring practices in multimedia performance, drawing on training in recorder performance, contemporary music, and Carnatic (South Indian classical) improvisation.
