Marcelo Wanderley is a Professor of Music Technology at McGill University and Director of the Input Devices and Music Interaction Laboratory (IDMIL), a close collaborator of matralab on the :body:suit:score project. His research centers on how performers physically control sound — designing and evaluating new musical interfaces, sensors, and gesture-capture systems that translate a musician's movement into musical control.
