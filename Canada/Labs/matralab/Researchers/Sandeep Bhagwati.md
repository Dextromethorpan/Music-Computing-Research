Sandeep Bhagwati is a composer, theatre director, and media artist who founded and directs matralab at Concordia University, where he has held the Canada Research Chair in Inter-X Art Practice and Theory since 2006. His work explores how music, movement, and technology can combine into new interdisciplinary and intercultural art forms — new kinds of interactive scores, wearable performance systems, and "comprovisation" (part composition, part live improvisation) across musical traditions from India, China, the Arab world, and the West.
