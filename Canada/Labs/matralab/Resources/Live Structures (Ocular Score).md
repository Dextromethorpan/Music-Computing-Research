Live Structures is a matralab artist residency project (2017–2020) by composer Linda Bouchard and technical designer Joseph Browne that built a tool called Ocular Score. Instead of a person hand-drawing a graphic score, Ocular Score analyzes the actual sound coming from live musicians — its loudness, pitch range, and texture — and turns those sound qualities directly into moving, projected images in real time, with no delay, for other musicians to read and respond to as they play. The composer can adjust presets on the fly to change how the shapes, colors, and motion respond to the sound. It is a working example of a notation system that is generated live from audio itself rather than written out beforehand.
