This is the founding paper for matralab's :body:suit:score project, presented at the TENOR 2016 conference on Technologies for Music Notation and Representation. It introduces a wearable "score suit" that sends musicians instructions not through a page of sheet music but through vibrations felt on the body (vibro-tactile feedback) — buzzes and pulses on the torso and limbs that tell a mobile performer what to play and when, while they move freely through a space instead of standing still reading a stand. The paper explains why traditional printed notation breaks down once a piece calls for musicians to walk around, and lays out the basic vocabulary and design logic the team built to replace it. It is a foundational text for understanding how matralab treats the body itself as a channel for receiving a musical score.
