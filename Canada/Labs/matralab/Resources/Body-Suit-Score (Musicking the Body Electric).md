:body:suit:score is a four-year, SSHRC-funded research-creation project joining matralab with three other Montreal-area labs: McGill's Input Devices and Music Interaction Laboratory (sensors and control), XS Labs (electronic textiles and wearable garments), and McGill's Music Performance and Body Lab (how musicians' bodies work while playing). Together they built a wearable "score suit" that can both send a musician vibro-tactile cues (touch-based instructions replacing a paper score) and collect data about the musician's movement and playing, sending it back to the composer in real time. The project treats a performer's motion through space as a musical parameter in its own right, alongside pitch and rhythm, and required inventing a new way of teaching musicians to "read" a score by feel rather than by sight.
