Sound of Montréal is a long-running matralab ensemble project, co-founded by Sandeep Bhagwati and Kiya Tabassian (of Ensemble Constantinople), that brings together Montreal-based musicians trained in different traditions — Iranian, Rwandan, Turkish, and Indian classical music, plus jazz and contemporary art music — to develop new music collectively rather than perform pre-written scores. The ensemble meets for multi-day workshop periods every few months; matralab and university researchers study what happens during these sessions, and only music that actually emerged from the workshops gets performed. It is matralab's clearest example of "artistic research": the research findings are produced through the act of making music together across musical traditions, not written up separately afterward.
