Stanley P. Lipshitz co-founded the Audio Research Group at the University of Waterloo alongside John Vanderkooy and is now a Professor Emeritus in the Department of Applied Mathematics. His research centers on the mathematical theory of digital dither and quantization noise — how to add carefully designed noise to digital audio to avoid harsh, signal-correlated distortion — as well as loudspeaker sound radiation/diffraction and general digital signal processing for audio, including topics like room deconvolution, adaptive equalization, and surround-sound reproduction.
