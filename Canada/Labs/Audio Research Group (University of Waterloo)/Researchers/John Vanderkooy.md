John Vanderkooy co-founded the Audio Research Group at the University of Waterloo and is now a Distinguished Professor Emeritus in the Department of Physics and Astronomy. His work spans digital signal processing for audio, how sound diffracts and radiates from loudspeaker cabinets, and building precise acoustic measurement equipment (including maximum-length-sequence and swept-tone measurement systems). He is one of the two researchers behind the group's foundational work on digital dither. As of recent activity he has continued measurement-focused research, including infrasound measurements around wind turbines and the acoustics of the trumpet.
