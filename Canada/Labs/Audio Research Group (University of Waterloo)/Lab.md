The Audio Research Group (ARG) is a small, historically influential audio/electro-acoustics lab in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at the University of Waterloo, founded and run for over 30 years by John Vanderkooy and Stanley P. Lipshitz. Its output (especially foundational work on digital dither, loudspeaker diffraction, and precision acoustic measurement) shaped standard practice across the professional audio industry. Note on current status: both founders are now Emeritus faculty and the lab's own site frames its history primarily in retrospective terms (see the 2014 Toronto AES retrospective below); there is no evidence of an actively growing current research team beyond the two founders, so this entry should be read as covering a legacy/wind-down-phase lab rather than an actively expanding one.
