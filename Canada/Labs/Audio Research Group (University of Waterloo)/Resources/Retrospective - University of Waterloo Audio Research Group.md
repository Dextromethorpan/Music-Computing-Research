In June 2014, the Toronto section of the Audio Engineering Society held a special event honouring the University of Waterloo's Audio Research Group (ARG), which by then had operated for over 30 years. This page hosts video interviews and an "Audiolab tour" led by John Vanderkooy, walking through the group's history, its research equipment (Room 205 of the Physics Building), and its outsized influence on the professional audio industry — alumni went on to work at companies including Dolby, Harman, and Paradigm. It also profiles founding faculty John Vanderkooy and Stanley P. Lipshitz, along with more than a dozen ARG alumni and their later careers. This is the best single beginner-friendly overview of why this small university lab mattered so much to the audio engineering field, and it confirms the group's legacy/retrospective status even as of this event.
