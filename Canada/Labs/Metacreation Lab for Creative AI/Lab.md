The Metacreation Lab for Creative AI is a research lab in the School of Interactive Arts and Technology (SIAT) at Simon Fraser University (Vancouver/Surrey, Canada), directed by Prof. Philippe Pasquier. It builds AI systems for creative behaviours — chiefly generative music (multi-track MIDI generation, adaptive game music) and human-AI co-creation tools that composers and game developers can actually use.
