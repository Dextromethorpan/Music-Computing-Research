This paper introduces MIDI-RWKV, a newer, lighter-weight AI model for "infilling" music — meaning it fills in missing or requested sections of a piece given the notes around them, similar to how a text-completion AI fills in the middle of a sentence. It's built on RWKV, an alternative to the Transformer architecture that scales more efficiently to long musical pieces and can run on smaller "edge" devices (a phone or a small standalone box) rather than needing a powerful server. A key feature is personalization: the model can be fine-tuned to sound like a specific composer's style using only a handful of that composer's example pieces, rather than needing a huge dataset. This points toward practical, on-device co-writing tools rather than only cloud-based research demos.
