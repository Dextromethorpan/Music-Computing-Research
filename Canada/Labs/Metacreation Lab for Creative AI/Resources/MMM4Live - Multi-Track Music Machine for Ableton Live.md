MMM4Live packages the lab's MMM generative model as a plugin for Ableton Live, one of the most widely used music production programs ("digital audio workstations," or DAWs). Rather than making musicians leave their normal workflow to use a research demo, MMM4Live lets them generate new MIDI patterns — basslines, drum grooves, chord progressions — directly inside the software they already compose in, in whatever musical style matches the surrounding material. This is a good example of the lab's general approach: build a strong AI model first (MMM), then wrap it so working musicians and producers, not just researchers, can use it. It has also been distributed through Cycling '74's Max for Live plugin ecosystem.
