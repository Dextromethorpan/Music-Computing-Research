Mitacs is a Canadian government-backed program that funds partnerships between university researchers and companies. Under a Mitacs-funded project, the Metacreation Lab is collaborating with Audiokinetic, the maker of Wwise — one of the most widely used audio middleware tools in the video game industry (it's the software that lets a game trigger and blend sound and music in response to what the player is doing). Led by master's student Paul Triana under Philippe Pasquier's supervision, the project is building a generative music pipeline that chains together three AI techniques: automatic music transcription (turning recorded audio into a digital score), symbolic music generation (composing new material as MIDI-like data, building on the lab's MIDI-GPT and MMM work), and neural audio synthesis (turning that generated score back into realistic-sounding audio). The goal is smoother, more flexible musical transitions and variations for interactive media, giving game composers a more controllable AI co-writer rather than a black box.
