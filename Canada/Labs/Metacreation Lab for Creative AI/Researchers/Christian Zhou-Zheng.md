Christian Zhou-Zheng is a researcher at the Metacreation Lab working with Philippe Pasquier on symbolic music generation — building AI models that compose and complete music represented as note data (like a digital score) rather than raw audio. He created MIDI-RWKV, a compact model for filling in and personalizing musical passages that can run efficiently on small devices, and won the ISMIR-MIREX 2025 symbolic music generation competition.
