Paul Triana is a master's student at SFU's School of Interactive Arts and Technology and the Metacreation Lab, working on generative AI for creative tasks with a focus on AI-assisted music. He is currently leading the lab's Mitacs-funded research collaboration with Audiokinetic (maker of the Wwise game-audio middleware), building an AI pipeline for generating adaptive video game music, under Philippe Pasquier's supervision.
