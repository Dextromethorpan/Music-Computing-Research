Jeff Ens completed his PhD in Computer Science at Simon Fraser University (2018-2023) at the Metacreation Lab, where he co-created the MMM (Multi-Track Music Machine) generative model with Philippe Pasquier and later worked as a postdoc. He also built the MetaMIDI Dataset, a large training dataset of MIDI files used across the lab's music-generation research. He now works as a Senior Data Scientist at Beatdapp, applying machine learning outside the lab.
