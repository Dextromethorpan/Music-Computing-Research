Cale Plut is an interdisciplinary artist, designer, and researcher who completed a PhD at SFU's School of Interactive Arts and Technology, working in the Metacreation Lab on generative and adaptive music for video games. He created PreGLAM, a model that predicts how a player is emotionally responding to gameplay in real time, and combined it with the lab's MMM music generator (as PreGLAM-MMM) to produce a video game score that adapts to the action as it happens, rather than switching between a small set of pre-recorded music clips.
