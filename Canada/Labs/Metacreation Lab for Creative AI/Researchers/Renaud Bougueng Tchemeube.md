Renaud Bougueng Tchemeube was a PhD student in Generative Arts and Computational Creativity at Simon Fraser University's Metacreation Lab. He led the design of Calliope, the lab's web-based interface for AI-assisted multi-track music composition, and studies how interactive software design shapes human-AI creative collaboration — including how composers actually use and evaluate generative tools in practice.
