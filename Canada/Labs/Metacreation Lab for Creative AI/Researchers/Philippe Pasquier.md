Philippe Pasquier is a professor at Simon Fraser University's School of Interactive Arts and Technology and founding director of the Metacreation Lab for Creative AI. He works on giving machines autonomous, creative behaviour — spanning generative music systems (MMM, Calliope, MIDI-RWKV), multi-agent AI, and computational creativity theory — and is both a scientist and a practicing multidisciplinary artist. He has co-authored over 250 peer-reviewed publications with lab members and collaborates with industry partners including Audiokinetic, Steinberg, and Microsoft.
