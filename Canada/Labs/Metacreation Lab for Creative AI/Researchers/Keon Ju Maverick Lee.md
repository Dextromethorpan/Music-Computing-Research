Keon Ju Maverick Lee is a PhD candidate at the Metacreation Lab, and also an electronic percussionist and composer in his own right. His research designs "musical agents" — AI systems that can improvise alongside a human performer in real time, particularly for percussive performance — sitting at the intersection of human-AI co-creation and live musical interaction. He collaborates with other computer-music research groups internationally (including centres in London, Montréal, and Paris) and has shown work at venues like Ars Electronica and SIGGRAPH.
