Neon (Neume Editor Online) is a browser-based tool for editing digitized medieval and early music notation. "Neumes" are the symbols used in early musical notation (centuries before modern notes-on-a-staff) to indicate roughly how a melody should rise and fall, especially in chant. Neon lets a person view a scanned page of old music alongside a computer-readable version of the same music, and correct mistakes made by automatic recognition software. It renders the music using a symbolic format called MEI (Music Encoding Initiative), updating the display live as edits are made. The tool is meant to plug into an optical music recognition (OMR) pipeline: software scans and guesses at the notation, then a human uses Neon to fix errors and produce clean, searchable data for musicologists and librarians.
