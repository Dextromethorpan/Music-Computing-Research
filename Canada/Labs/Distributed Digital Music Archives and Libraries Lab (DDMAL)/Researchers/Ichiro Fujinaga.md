Ichiro Fujinaga is a Professor in Music Technology at McGill's Schulich School of Music and the founding director of DDMAL. He holds a Canada Research Chair in Music Information Retrieval, and his research centers on optical music recognition (OMR) — teaching computers to read scanned pages of musical notation, especially historical and handwritten scores — as well as the broader digital-library infrastructure needed to make huge collections of music scores searchable and analyzable.
