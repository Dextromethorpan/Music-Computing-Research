DDMAL is a research lab in the Music Technology Area of the Schulich School of Music at McGill University in Montreal, affiliated with CIRMMT (Centre for Interdisciplinary Research in Music Media and Technology). Directed by Prof. Ichiro Fujinaga, the lab builds tools and methods for digitizing, recognizing, and searching music scores — with a particular specialty in optical music recognition (OMR) for early and handwritten music notation, plus digital libraries and computational musicology more broadly.
