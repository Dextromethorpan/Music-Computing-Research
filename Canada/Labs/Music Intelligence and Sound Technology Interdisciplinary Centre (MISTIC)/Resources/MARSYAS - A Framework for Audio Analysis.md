This is the founding paper for Marsyas (Music Analysis, Retrieval and Synthesis for Audio Signals), the software toolkit George Tzanetakis built as a PhD student and later grew into MISTIC's flagship open-source project. "Framework" here means a reusable piece of software that other programmers build their own audio tools on top of, rather than a finished app — Marsyas gives researchers ready-made building blocks for things like breaking an audio file into short analysis windows, extracting numerical descriptors of sound, and classifying or comparing recordings. The paper explains the design choices that let a single system handle both audio *analysis* (extracting information from existing sound, like recognizing a genre) and audio *synthesis* (generating new sound), which was unusual at the time. Marsyas went on to become one of the most widely used open-source frameworks in music information retrieval and is still maintained today.
