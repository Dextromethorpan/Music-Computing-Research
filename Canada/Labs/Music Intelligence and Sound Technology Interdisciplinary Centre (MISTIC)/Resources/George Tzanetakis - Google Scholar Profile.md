This is George Tzanetakis's Google Scholar page — a free, automatically updated listing of his published papers and how often each one has been cited by other researchers. As a founding figure in music information retrieval (MIR) and one of the field's most-cited authors, his page is a good map of the breadth of MISTIC's output: genre classification, audio feature extraction, musical robotics, computational ethnomusicology (using computers to study musical traditions from around the world), and new interfaces for musicians. For someone new to the field, Google Scholar profiles like this one are a fast way to see which of a researcher's papers have had the most influence (ranked by citation count) and to find the original sources behind ideas summarized elsewhere.
