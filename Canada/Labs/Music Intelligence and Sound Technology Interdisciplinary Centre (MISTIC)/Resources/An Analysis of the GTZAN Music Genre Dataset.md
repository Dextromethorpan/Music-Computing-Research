Written by another researcher (not Tzanetakis himself), this paper is a critical audit of the GTZAN dataset that MISTIC produced a decade earlier — included here because it's essential context for understanding GTZAN's legacy. Sturm systematically checked the dataset and documented real problems: exact duplicate recordings appearing more than once, some tracks mislabeled with the wrong genre, and very low audio quality in places. Because GTZAN had become the field's most-used benchmark, these flaws meant that years of published "genre classification accuracy" results were being compared against an imperfect ruler. For a beginner, this paper is a useful lesson in scientific practice: even hugely influential, widely reused research datasets can have quiet errors that go unnoticed until someone checks carefully, and reported accuracy numbers are only as trustworthy as the data behind them.
