BRAMS is a joint research centre of Université de Montréal and McGill University, co-founded by Isabelle Peretz and Robert Zatorre and now home to 70+ affiliated faculty. It's a cognitive neuroscience institute, not a DSP/software lab — its work uses computational and signal-analysis methods (fMRI, EEG, MEG, computational modeling) to study how the human brain perceives, remembers, and produces music and speech, rather than to build music software or audio tools.
