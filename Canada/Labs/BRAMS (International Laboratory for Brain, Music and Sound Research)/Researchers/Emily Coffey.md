Emily Coffey runs the Coffey Lab: Audition, Sleep and Plasticity (CL:ASP) at Concordia University. She studies how the brain changes with complex, effortful listening tasks — using "hearing in noise" (following speech or music in a noisy environment) and musical training as test cases — and how sleep affects the brain's consolidation of what was learned during the day. Her lab combines several brain-imaging tools (MEG, EEG, fMRI) with newer approaches like sound cues delivered during sleep to try to improve memory consolidation.
