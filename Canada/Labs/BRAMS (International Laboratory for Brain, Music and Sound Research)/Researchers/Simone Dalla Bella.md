Simone Dalla Bella took over as BRAMS co-director from Isabelle Peretz in 2018. He studies the mental and brain processes behind perceiving and performing rhythm and pitch, in both healthy people and people with musical or movement deficits (such as "beat deafness," or difficulty feeling a musical beat). A major thread of his current work uses rhythmic sound and mobile technology to help improve motor skills — such as walking gait and speech — in patients with conditions like Parkinson's disease.
