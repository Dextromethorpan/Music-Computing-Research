Robert Zatorre co-founded BRAMS with Isabelle Peretz in 2006 and remains a co-director. Working out of the Montreal Neurological Institute, he studies how the brain handles complex sound — especially music and speech — using brain-imaging tools like fMRI and PET. His research covers how hearing and movement-control brain regions link up when musicians play (auditory-motor coupling), how the brain physically changes with musical training, and why music can trigger a genuine pleasure/reward response in the brain, similar to food or other rewards.
