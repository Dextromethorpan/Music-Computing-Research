Isabelle Peretz co-founded BRAMS in 2005 and directed it until 2018. She studies why ordinary people (not just trained musicians) are musical at all — how much of musical ability is present from birth versus learned, and how it relates to language ability. She is best known for her work defining and studying "congenital amusia" (tone deafness), including building the standard test used to diagnose it, and for research on how musical ability and disorders can run in families.
