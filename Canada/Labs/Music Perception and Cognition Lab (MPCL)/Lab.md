The Music Perception and Cognition Lab (MPCL) is part of McGill University's Schulich School of Music and CIRMMT (Centre for Interdisciplinary Research in Music Media and Technology) in Montreal. Directed by Prof. Stephen McAdams, the lab studies how people perceive and make sense of musical sound — especially timbre, auditory scene analysis, and the psychology behind orchestration practice.
