Bennett Smith is the lab's Technical Manager, responsible for the software, hardware, and experimental infrastructure that MPCL's perception studies run on. He trained in psychoacoustics under Manfred Schroeder at the University of Göttingen and previously worked at IRCAM (the Paris institute for music and acoustic research) before joining MPCL, giving him deep hands-on experience building the tools behind auditory experiments rather than running the studies himself.
