Stephen McAdams is a Distinguished James McGill Professor in the Department of Music Research at the Schulich School of Music and directs the Music Perception and Cognition Lab. He studies how people perceive and make sense of auditory scenes and musical sound — with a career-long focus on timbre (the tonal "color" of instruments and voices), how listeners perceptually group or separate simultaneous musical lines, and the psychological basis of orchestration practice. He directed the multi-institution ACTOR Partnership (Analysis, Creation and Teaching of Orchestration) from 2018–2025 and now co-directs its successor, the TONE Partnership (Timbre and Orchestration Network).
