Yuval Adler works on making music-perception research and tools more accessible to composers, a motivation that grew out of his own frustration as a composition student with the software available for contemporary composers. After a bachelor's in composition and a master's in Music, Science, and Technology at Stanford's CCRMA, he came to MPCL to help bridge the gap between researchers studying perception and orchestration and the musicians who could use that knowledge directly.
