Linglan Zhu is a PhD candidate researching how composers develop and use timbral practice, and the role of "inner hearing" (imagining sound internally before it's played) in that process. She's broadly interested in how timbre functions as an analytical parameter in contemporary music and how listener perception can inform the study of a composer's creative plan. She came to music research from a B.Sc. in chemistry, and is also a Student Coordinator for CIRMMT (the McGill-affiliated Centre for Interdisciplinary Research in Music Media and Technology).
