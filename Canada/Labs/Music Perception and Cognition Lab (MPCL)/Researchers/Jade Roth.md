Jade Roth is a music theorist whose doctoral research examines how timbre functions structurally in music, building on earlier master's work on rubato (expressive tempo flexibility) and how it shapes the perception of musical silence. She combines listening-based and score-based analysis to understand what audible features draw listeners into a piece of music, and is co-supervised by Stephen McAdams and music theorist Robert Hasegawa.
