Joshua Rosner studies how listeners perceive musical form — the large-scale structure of a piece — with particular focus on contemporary chamber music that uses unconventional or "non-default" instrumental playing techniques. His work draws on gestalt psychology (how people perceive organized wholes), embodied cognition, and phenomenology, informed by his own background as a composer and performer. He is co-supervised by Robert Hasegawa and Stephen McAdams, and previously founded a contemporary-music performance organization in Cleveland, Ohio.
