This is the Google Scholar page listing the published research output of Gary Scavone, the professor who founded and directs CAML at McGill University. Google Scholar is a free search tool that tracks academic papers and how often other researchers cite (reference) them. Scavone's page shows decades of work on acoustic modeling of musical instruments, measurement techniques, and sound-synthesis software, with his output cited by thousands of other researchers. For someone new to the field, this page is a useful map of his whole body of work and a jumping-off point to find specific papers on brass and woodwind instrument acoustics, physical modeling, and real-time synthesis.
