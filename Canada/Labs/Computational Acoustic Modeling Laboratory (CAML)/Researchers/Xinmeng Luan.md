Xinmeng Luan is a PhD student at McGill University in CAML, supported by a FRQNT (Fonds de recherche du Québec) doctoral scholarship. Her research centers on musical acoustics and acoustic signal processing, notably measuring and modeling the acoustic behavior of the dizi (a traditional Chinese transverse flute), and she has also explored physics-informed deep learning approaches to sound. In 2025 she conducted a research exchange with institutions in Spain and Denmark.
