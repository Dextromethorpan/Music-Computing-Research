Gary Scavone is a Professor of Music Technology at McGill University's Schulich School of Music, where he founded and has directed CAML since 2004. He earned his PhD in "Computer-Based Music Theory & Acoustics" at Stanford's CCRMA, where he was also Technical Director. His research covers measuring, modeling, and simulating how musical instruments (especially wind and brass instruments) actually produce sound, and building efficient real-time software to synthesize those sounds. He co-created the widely used Synthesis ToolKit (STK) with Perry Cook.
