Champ Darabundit is a Music Technology PhD candidate at McGill University working in CAML, supported by an NSERC Vanier Doctoral scholarship — one of Canada's most competitive graduate awards. His research focuses on physics-based ("physical modeling") synthesis of woodwind instruments, general audio signal processing, virtual analog modeling (simulating classic analog hardware like tape machines or synthesizers in software), and audio machine learning.
