CAML is a research lab at McGill University's Schulich School of Music (part of CIRMMT), founded and directed by Prof. Gary Scavone. It focuses on musical acoustics — measuring and modeling how real instruments (especially wind and brass) physically produce sound — and on turning those models into efficient real-time sound synthesis software.
