Erivan Gonçalves Duarte is a PhD researcher at McGill working at the intersection of music education, accessibility, and instrument design. Trained as both a physicist and a musician in Brazil, his research asks how digital musical instruments can be genuinely designed with and for people with disabilities — particularly d/Deaf musicians — rather than simply retrofitted for them. His doctoral work examines instrument design and music-making in Deaf-led contexts, drawing on frameworks from disability studies to evaluate whether accessibility technology actually reflects the needs and involvement of the people it is meant to serve.
