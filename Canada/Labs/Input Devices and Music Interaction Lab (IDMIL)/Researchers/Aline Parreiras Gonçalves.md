Aline Parreiras Gonçalves is a postdoctoral researcher at IDMIL and a trained flutist. Her research looks at the mental side of learning and performing music — things like mental practice (rehearsing a piece in your head without an instrument), self-talk, and motor learning — and how psychological-skills training used by athletes can be adapted to help musicians perform better in real, high-pressure settings rather than only in a practice room. She combines this empirical research with her own ongoing career as a performing flutist and music educator.
