Ziyue (Monica) Piao is a PhD student in Music Technology at McGill, co-supervised by Marcelo M. Wanderley and Isabelle Cossette since 2022. Her research is in musical haptics — using the sense of touch, usually through motorized or vibrating devices, to help people learn and perform music more effectively. She designs wearable and hand-held haptic devices, including a force-feedback knob that pushes back against a musician's fingers to convey information without requiring them to look at a screen, and studies how "feeling" musical cues through the body (rather than only hearing them) can improve music learning and shared performance experiences.
