Meesh Sara Fradkin is a doctoral candidate in music at McGill and a writer and artist who works across sound, text, and media. Within IDMIL their research touches on communication protocols (the technical "languages" different pieces of music software use to talk to each other), interface design and evaluation, mapping (connecting sensor input to sound output), and sonification — the practice of turning non-audio data into sound so it can be understood by ear rather than by reading a chart.
