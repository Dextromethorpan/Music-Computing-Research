libmapper is a free, open-source software library that solves a very specific problem for people who build custom musical instruments: how do you connect the electrical signals coming out of a sensor (say, a bend sensor on a glove, or a pressure pad) to the parameters of a sound-making program (like pitch or volume)? Without a tool like this, a musician-programmer has to hand-write custom code every time they want to try a new sensor-to-sound "mapping." libmapper instead lets any two pieces of software on a network discover each other and be connected live, with the ability to write simple math expressions (for example, scaling or smoothing a signal) in between. It grew out of IDMIL's work on custom digital instruments and is now used across the wider community of instrument builders, media artists, and researchers as a standard way to prototype new interactive systems quickly instead of starting from scratch each time.
