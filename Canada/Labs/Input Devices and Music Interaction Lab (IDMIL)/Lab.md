IDMIL is a research lab in the Music Technology Area of McGill University's Schulich School of Music, part of the wider CIRMMT interdisciplinary research centre, directed by Prof. Marcelo M. Wanderley. It focuses on designing and evaluating digital musical instruments (DMIs) — new, computer-based instruments built from sensors and software rather than acoustic parts — along with the gesture-based interaction, sensor technology, and mapping tools (like the open-source libmapper library) that make those instruments expressive to play.
