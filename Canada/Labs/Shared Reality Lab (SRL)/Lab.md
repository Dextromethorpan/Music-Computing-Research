The Shared Reality Lab (SRL) is directed by Prof. Jeremy Cooperstock and based at McGill University's Centre for Intelligent Machines (CIM), with ties to CIRMMT (Centre for Interdisciplinary Research in Music Media and Technology). SRL's long-running focus is high-fidelity, computer-mediated human communication: ultra-low-latency networked music and video performance over long distances, telepresence, and accessible technology such as audio/haptic sensory substitution for people who are blind or low-vision.
