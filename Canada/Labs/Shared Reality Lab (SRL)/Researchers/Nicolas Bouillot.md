Nicolas Bouillot was a postdoctoral researcher at SRL from 2007-2011, where he worked on low-latency audio streaming for interactive musical performance, co-authoring the NIME 2009 paper measuring and comparing streaming engines for high-fidelity, low-delay audio. He later joined Montreal's Société des arts technologiques (SAT) as a researcher and eventually co-directed research there, and is now a co-founder of the technology cooperative Lab148, working on R&D and software for digital and physical installations.
