Dalia El-Shimy completed her PhD in Electrical Engineering at McGill as a Research Assistant in the Shared Reality Lab under Jeremy Cooperstock, working at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction and networked music technology. Her NIME 2013 paper introduced a "reactive environment" that gives remote musicians new real-time controls (like adjustable stereo panning) to strengthen the feeling of a shared performance space. She later moved into industry UX research, building research teams at Shopify, Miro, and Wise.
