Jeremy Cooperstock is a Professor in McGill's Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering and directs the Shared Reality Lab. His research centers on computer-mediated human communication at the highest achievable fidelity: ultra-low-latency audio/video transport for telepresence and networked music performance, immersive multimodal environments, and accessible technology such as audio/haptic sensory substitution for blind and low-vision users. His 2001 Ultra-Videoconferencing demonstrations helped shape the design of later commercial telepresence systems.
