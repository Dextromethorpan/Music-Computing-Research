Any sound can be thought of as a mix of three kinds of ingredients: steady tones ("sines," like a sustained violin note), sharp onsets ("transients," like a drum hit or a pluck), and noise-like texture (like breath or bow noise). SiTraNo is a free, interactive software tool, built in MATLAB (a common programming environment for engineers), that lets a user split a recording into these three components and adjust or process each one separately. This kind of separation is especially useful for audio time-stretching — slowing a recording down without changing its pitch — because sines, transients, and noise each need to be stretched differently to still sound natural. The tool gives researchers and musicians a hands-on way to experiment with this decomposition instead of only reading about it in a paper.
