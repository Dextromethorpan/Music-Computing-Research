Karolina Prawda's doctoral dissertation studies how sound reverberates (echoes and decays) in real rooms, and how to predict and recreate that behaviour digitally. Part of the work involved measuring how sound decays in actual physical spaces and comparing those measurements against the simplified models normally used to build digital reverb effects, to find out where the models fall short. The other part focuses on designing better digital reverberation algorithms — the kind used in music production and virtual/augmented reality — that more accurately reproduce how real rooms sound, including cases where the decay behaves in complex, non-uniform ways across different frequencies.
