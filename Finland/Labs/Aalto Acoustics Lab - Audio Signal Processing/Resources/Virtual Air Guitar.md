The Virtual Air Guitar is a playful demo project that lets someone "play" an electric guitar sound just by miming the motions of playing air guitar. A camera watches the player's hands, computer-vision software (code that lets a computer interpret what a camera sees) tracks where the hands are and how they move, and that movement is turned into control signals — such as when a string is "plucked" and how long it should ring — that drive a physically modeled guitar synthesizer (a sound-generating program that simulates the physics of a real guitar string rather than playing a recording). It is a good example of how gesture-tracking and physics-based sound synthesis can be combined to create a new kind of playable instrument that requires no physical instrument at all.
