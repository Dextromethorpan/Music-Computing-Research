The Audio Signal Processing Research Group is part of the Aalto Acoustics Lab at Aalto University's Department of Information and Communications Engineering (Espoo, Finland), led by Prof. Vesa Välimäki. The group researches digital signal processing and machine learning for audio: physical modeling and virtual analog synthesis of instruments and effects, digital filter and equalizer design, artificial reverberation, audio time-stretching, and headphone/loudspeaker signal processing.
