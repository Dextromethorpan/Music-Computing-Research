Alec Wright completed his PhD at Aalto Acoustics Lab in 2023, focusing on using neural networks to imitate audio effects such as guitar amplifiers, distortion pedals, and reverb — an approach known as "black-box" modeling because the network learns the effect's behaviour purely from example recordings rather than from knowledge of its internal circuitry. His work covers both how to train these models and how to shrink them so they can run in real time on modest hardware. He now continues this research as a Chancellor's Fellow in Audio Machine Learning at the University of Edinburgh.
