Karolina Prawda completed her doctorate at Aalto Acoustics Lab in 2022 on how sound reverberates and decays in real rooms, and stayed on as a postdoctoral researcher until 2024. Her work focuses on measuring real room acoustics and using those measurements to build more accurate and efficient digital reverberation algorithms, including ones tuned with machine-learning-style optimization. She has since moved to a lecturer position at the University of York, UK.
