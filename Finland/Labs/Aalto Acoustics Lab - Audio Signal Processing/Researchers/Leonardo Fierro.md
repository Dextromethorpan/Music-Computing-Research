Leonardo Fierro completed his doctorate at Aalto Acoustics Lab in 2024, working on audio time-stretching — changing how fast a recording plays without changing its pitch. His research focused on splitting a sound into its "sines" (steady tones), "transients" (sharp onsets like drum hits), and "noise" components so that each part can be time-stretched with a technique suited to it, producing more natural-sounding results at extreme stretch factors. He built the open SiTraNo tool as part of this work and served as a teaching assistant for Aalto's audio signal processing courses.
