Vesa Välimäki is a Professor of Audio Signal Processing at Aalto University and leads the Audio Signal Processing Research Group within the Aalto Acoustics Lab. Over more than two decades he has worked on making computers simulate real instruments and analog electronic gear (physical modeling and virtual analog synthesis), on digital filter and equalizer design, and, more recently, on using machine learning for audio effects and reverberation. He is also Deputy Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of the Audio Engineering Society and a Fellow of both the Audio Engineering Society and IEEE.
