A review paper published in the Journal of the Acoustical Society of America surveying "active acoustic enhancement" (AAE) systems — technology installed in real rooms (concert halls, multi-purpose venues) that uses microphones, digital signal processing, and loudspeakers to add extra, carefully controlled reflections and reverberation to a live performance in real time. Unlike a digital reverb effect added afterward to a recording, AAE changes what the room itself actually sounds like while people are in it, letting a venue built for speech or sports also sound good for orchestral music. The paper compares different AAE system designs and how stable and natural-sounding they are.
