An open dataset of acoustic measurements taken in "Arni," a variable-acoustics room in Aalto's Acoustic Laboratories whose wall and ceiling panels can be opened or closed to switch the room between "dry" (little echo), "reverberant" (lots of echo), and in-between configurations. The measurements were made with an Eigenmike — a ball-shaped microphone with 32 tiny capsules spread over its surface that can capture sound arriving from every direction at once, similar to how a camera lens captures light from every direction. Datasets like this let researchers develop and test methods for reconstructing and navigating 3D sound fields (the pattern of sound pressure filling a space) for applications like virtual and augmented reality audio.
