Nils Meyer-Kahlen completed his PhD at Aalto on "Transfer-Plausible Acoustics for Augmented Reality" and now works as a postdoctoral researcher in the group. He focuses on audio for augmented and virtual reality — how to make binaural sound that convincingly adapts as a listener moves through and between real and virtual acoustic spaces — as well as spatial room impulse response measurement and datasets.
