Janis Heldmann is a doctoral researcher working on sustainable solutions for acoustics, including new bio-based (plant-derived) sound-absorbing materials as an alternative to conventional plastic foams. He has also researched "colorless" artificial reverberation — reverb that doesn't unintentionally emphasize particular pitches — and received a Best Paper Award at the DAFx conference for that work.
