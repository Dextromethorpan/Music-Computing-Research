Tapio Lokki leads the Virtual Acoustics Research Group and the wider Aalto Acoustics Lab, and is also Head of Aalto's Department of Information and Communications Engineering. His research is centered on understanding how rooms — especially concert halls — change the sound we hear, spanning measurement techniques, spatial sound reproduction, binaural (headphone-based 3D) audio, and physically-based room acoustics simulation. He has also worked on augmented reality audio, eyes-free user interfaces, and hearing in noisy or reverberant conditions, and is a Fellow of the Audio Engineering Society (AES).
