Sebastian Jiro Schlecht holds a position shared between the Aalto Media Lab and the Aalto Acoustics Lab, focused on sound for virtual reality. His research centers on artificial reverberation (digitally simulating how a room echoes), feedback delay networks (a computationally efficient technique for generating that reverberation), and processing measured room impulse responses — the acoustic "fingerprint" recordings used to recreate a room's sound.
