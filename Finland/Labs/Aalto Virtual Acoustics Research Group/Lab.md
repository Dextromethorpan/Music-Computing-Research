Part of the Aalto Acoustics Lab at Aalto University (Espoo, Finland), led by Prof. Tapio Lokki, AES Fellow. The group studies room acoustics — how physical spaces, especially concert halls, shape the sound we hear — through auralization, spatial sound capture and reproduction, binaural technology, augmented reality audio, and physically-based room acoustics modeling.
