A published interview marking Swiss artist-researcher Jan Schacher's appointment as Professor of Music Technology at the Sibelius Academy. Schacher explains his teaching philosophy — treating each student like an athlete who needs an individualized training plan — and describes how the department blends hands-on technical craft (building and using music technology) with "artistic research," the practice of investigating questions through making art itself. He also discusses how international students bring different cultural networks into the department's community, and how artists can influence society just by staying consistent and interacting with the world, without needing to become activists. Useful as a plain-language introduction to how the department thinks about its educational mission.
