Video documentation of "Synthestra," a concert staged as part of MuTeFest'20 ("Electrons on the Beat"), the department's annual music technology festival, held at the Black Box venue of the Helsinki Music Centre in November 2020. MuTeFest showcases student and staff work in electroacoustic music (music built from electronic sound sources), experimental performance with electronics, and other outputs of the Music Technology program. This kind of public concert documentation is a key form of "output" at an arts university: instead of a written paper, the performance itself — and its video record — is the shareable research/creative result. Useful as a concrete, watchable example of what "music creation with technology" looks and sounds like in practice at this department.
