Alejandro Olarte is a lecturer in electroacoustic music (music built from electronic sound sources) and heads the Music and Technology Study Programme at the Sibelius Academy. He works with sound and its artistic expression through technology, is interested in artistic research (research carried out through making art), sound and musical performance, and modern instrument-building ("lutherie" — traditionally the craft of building stringed instruments, extended here to electronic instrument design). He co-created the sound installation Aquarmonio. His ORCID profile is 0000-0001-8330-4584.
