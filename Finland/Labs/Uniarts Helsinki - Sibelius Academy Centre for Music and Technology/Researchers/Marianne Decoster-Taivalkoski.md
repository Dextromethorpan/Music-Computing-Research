Marianne Decoster-Taivalkoski is a French interactive media and sound artist based in Finland who teaches at the Sibelius Academy's Centre for Music and Technology. Her work spans sound installation, interactive media art, and electroacoustic composition — she was part of the team that built Aquarmonio, a water-themed sound installation.
