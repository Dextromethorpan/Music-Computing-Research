Alejandro Montes de Oca completed his artistic doctorate in music technology at the Sibelius Academy in 2022. His research introduced the concept of "Case-Specific Electroacoustic Systems" — custom electronic setups designed together with a specific piece of music rather than using generic, off-the-shelf gear — and produced five original sound works, including the installation Aquarmonio, created with colleagues Marianne Decoster-Taivalkoski and Alejandro Olarte.
