Jan Schacher is a Swiss artist, teacher, and researcher who became Professor of Music Technology at the Sibelius Academy in 2021. His work centers on gesture and movement as ways of controlling electronic music — for example, mapping a dancer's or performer's body motion to sound parameters in real time — and on sound spatialization (placing and moving sounds around a listener in physical space). He also holds an affiliation with the Institute for Computer Music and Sound Technology at Zurich University of the Arts. His ORCID profile is 0000-0001-8196-8985.
