The Centre for Music and Technology sits within the Department of Music and Technology at the Sibelius Academy, part of the University of the Arts Helsinki (Uniarts Helsinki). It focuses on music creation with technology — electroacoustic music, experimental performance with electronics, film and game music, live sound, and media/sonic arts — combining bachelor's, master's, and doctoral-level education with artistic research.
