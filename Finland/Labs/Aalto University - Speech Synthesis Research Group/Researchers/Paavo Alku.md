Paavo Alku is a professor of speech communication technology at Aalto University and was Lauri Juvela's PhD advisor; he remains an active senior researcher in the department. His work focuses on how the voice is physically produced — analyzing and modeling the "source" signal made by the vocal cords — and on statistical and neural methods for speech synthesis, spectral modeling of speech, and using speech as a biomarker for health conditions. He is a Fellow of both IEEE and ISCA (International Speech Communication Association), recognitions given for significant, sustained contributions to the field.
