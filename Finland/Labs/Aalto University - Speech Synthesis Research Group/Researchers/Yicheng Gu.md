Yicheng Gu is a visiting researcher working with Lauri Juvela at Aalto University, alongside a primary affiliation at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. His work focuses on singing voice and speech synthesis, differentiable digital signal processing, and neural audio generation — including building neural vocoders that give precise, interpretable control over speech characteristics like pitch and vowel resonance.
