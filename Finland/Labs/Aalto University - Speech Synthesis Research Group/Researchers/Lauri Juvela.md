Lauri Juvela leads the Speech Synthesis Research Group at Aalto University. He works on deep generative models (neural networks that learn to produce new, realistic-sounding audio) and differentiable digital signal processing (rewriting classic audio-engineering building blocks so machine learning can automatically tune them) applied to speech. His research spans efficient neural vocoders, instant voice cloning, and watermarking generated speech so that AI-made audio can be reliably detected as such.
