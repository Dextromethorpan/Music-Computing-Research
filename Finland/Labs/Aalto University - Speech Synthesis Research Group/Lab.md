Part of the Department of Information and Communications Engineering at Aalto University, Espoo, Finland. The group studies deep generative models and differentiable digital signal processing applied to speech and audio, with a focus on efficient neural speech synthesis, instant voice cloning, deepfake detection, and audio watermarking as a countermeasure.
