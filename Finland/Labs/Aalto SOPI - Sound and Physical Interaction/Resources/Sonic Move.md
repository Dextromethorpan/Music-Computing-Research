Sonic Move is a research collaboration between Aalto University, the Finnish technical research center VTT, and the University of Eastern Finland (UEF) that combines motion-tracking technology with real-time sound design. The project develops "sonification" techniques — ways of turning body movement data into sound and vibration — so that people can hear and feel new, expressive feedback generated from their own movements. The aim is to create new interactive experiences (for example in dance, sport, or wellbeing contexts) where audio and touch-based ("tactile") signals respond dynamically to what a person's body is doing, rather than being pre-recorded or fixed. Sonic Move is the umbrella project behind more specific research outputs such as the "Human-in-the-Loop" crossmodal AI paper published at NIME 2026.
