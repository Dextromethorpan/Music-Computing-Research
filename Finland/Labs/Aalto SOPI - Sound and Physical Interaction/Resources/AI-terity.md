AI-terity is a physical musical instrument built by the SOPI group that is soft, foldable and "non-rigid" — meaning it can be squeezed, folded and reshaped by hand, unlike a keyboard or guitar with fixed parts. Inside, it runs an AI model (GANSpaceSynth, a GAN-based sound generator combined with granular synthesis — see the related paper on this same technique) that generates new audio samples in real time. As a performer folds, presses or reshapes the instrument, those physical actions control which sounds the AI produces, letting the musician "feel their way" through an AI-generated space of timbres. AI-terity has been performed at major events including Ars Electronica, Sónar+D Festival, and the NIME conference, and is one of SOPI's flagship examples of combining AI-driven sound generation with a hands-on, tangible instrument.
