The Augmented Performer project builds an AI system that can "duet" with a live human pianist — listening to what the pianist plays and generating a musical response in real time, rather than just following a fixed backing track. Technically, it uses a "transformer" (the same type of neural network architecture behind large language models like GPT, here trained on musical note data called MIDI instead of text) running inside Pure Data, a visual programming environment commonly used for live audio. The project's goal is to keep the human musician at the creative center, with the AI reacting and adapting to the performer rather than the performer following pre-set AI output. The system was tested and premiered in concert with pianist and composer Jukka Nykänen in 2026, and the project also studies how audiences and performers experience this kind of human-AI musical collaboration.
