A "GAN" (Generative Adversarial Network) is a type of AI model made of two competing neural networks that learns to generate new, realistic-sounding audio by training on a dataset of real sounds. "Granular synthesis" is a sound-design technique that builds new sounds by chopping audio into tiny fragments ("grains," often just milliseconds long) and recombining them. This paper introduces a method (GANSpaceSynth, later used in the SOPI group's AI-terity instrument) that combines GAN-generated audio samples with granular synthesis, adding an extra "redundancy parameter" that controls how much repeated or overlapping material is used when reassembling grains. The result is a real-time sound-generation technique that lets a performer explore an AI-learned sound world interactively, which is the technical foundation behind several of SOPI's physical, gesture-controlled AI instruments.
