A "latent space" is the compressed, internal map that a trained AI model uses to represent all the sounds (or images, or other data) it has learned from — nearby points in this map tend to sound similar, so moving through it smoothly changes the sound. This paper, presented at the International Conference on Computational Creativity, explores how these latent spaces created by generative AI models can be treated as creative "instruments" in their own right — spaces that a musician can explore and navigate to discover new, sometimes unexpected sounds, rather than just a technical by-product of training a neural network. The authors discuss how composers and performers can work with these AI-generated sound spaces as a platform for musical creativity, raising questions about authorship and control when part of the "instrument" is a trained AI model rather than a hand-built device.
