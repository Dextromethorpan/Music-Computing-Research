Sound and Physical Interaction (SOPI) is a research group at Aalto University's Media Lab in Helsinki, part of the School of Arts, Design and Architecture. Founded and led by Koray Tahiroğlu, it focuses on AI and music creativity, New Interfaces for Musical Expression (NIME), digital musical instruments, and sonic interaction design.
