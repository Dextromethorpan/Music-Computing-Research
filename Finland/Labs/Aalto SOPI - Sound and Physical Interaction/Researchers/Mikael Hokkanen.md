Mikael Hokkanen is a master's student in Machine Learning, Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Aalto University, working as a research assistant within the SOPI group. He builds and trains the AI models behind SOPI's human-AI performance systems — for example, developing the transformer model architecture used in the Augmented Performer project and training the movement-to-sound alignment models used in the Sonic Move project's dance sonification work.
