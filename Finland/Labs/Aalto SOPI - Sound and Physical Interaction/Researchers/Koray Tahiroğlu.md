Koray Tahiroğlu founded and leads the SOPI research group at Aalto University's Media Lab in Helsinki. He works at the intersection of AI and music creativity, building new physical and digital musical instruments (including AI-driven ones like AI-terity) and studying how musicians form expressive, evolving relationships with the technology they perform with. His research combines hands-on instrument design, deep learning applied to audio, and performance practice — he regularly performs with the instruments his lab develops at venues such as Ars Electronica and Sónar+D.
