Agnes Kloft is a doctoral researcher in Aalto University's Engineering Psychology group (Department of Computer Science) with a background in psychology and neuroscience. On the Augmented Performer project she runs the evaluation side of the research — collecting performance data from rehearsals and concerts and studying how audiences and performers experience playing music together with an AI system.
