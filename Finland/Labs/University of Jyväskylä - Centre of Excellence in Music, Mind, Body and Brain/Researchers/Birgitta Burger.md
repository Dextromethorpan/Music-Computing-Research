Birgitta Burger completed her PhD in musicology at the University of Jyväskylä in 2013 and worked there as a postdoctoral researcher until 2019, before moving to an ERC-funded project on musical time perception at the University of Hamburg. While at Jyväskylä she co-created the MoCap Toolbox (the lab's core software for analyzing motion-capture recordings of dancers) and co-authored several of the foundational computational studies linking musical features to how people physically move and express emotion through dance — work that the current Centre of Excellence in Music, Mind, Body and Brain builds directly on.
