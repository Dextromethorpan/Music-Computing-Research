Petri Toiviainen leads the Centre of Excellence in Music, Mind, Body and Brain. He studies how music affects the mind, body, and brain, and is one of the main authors of the widely used open-source MATLAB toolboxes MIRtoolbox (for extracting numerical features from audio) and the MoCap Toolbox (for analyzing body-movement recordings). His computational work spans motion-capture studies of dance and music-induced movement, brain-imaging studies of music listening, and statistical/machine-learning modeling of musical structure.
