Martin Hartmann works on embodied music cognition — how people physically move and coordinate with each other while dancing or making music together. He builds computational models of "kinematics" (the mathematics of movement: speed, timing, synchrony) from motion-capture data to figure out what makes two dancers look coordinated to an outside observer, and also studies rhythmic/social synchrony more broadly, including in music therapy contexts. He maintains parts of the lab's MIRtoolbox and MoCap Toolbox infrastructure and teaches computational methods (machine learning, musical genre classification) to other researchers.
