Emily Carlson is a music therapist and postdoctoral researcher whose doctoral work explored coordination, empathy, and social relationships expressed through dance. She works with motion-capture data and machine learning to study how movement reveals both musical structure (like genre) and personal identity (individual "movement fingerprints"), and how well-coordinated a pair of dancers looks to an observer. Her research connects computational movement analysis to more applied questions about therapy and social bonding.
