MIRtoolbox is a free software toolbox for the programming language MATLAB, built by researchers who later became part of the Centre of Excellence in Music, Mind, Body and Brain (CoE MMBB) at the University of Jyväskylä. It automatically pulls numerical "features" out of an audio recording — things like estimated tempo, key, rhythm, and timbre (the tonal color or texture of a sound, e.g. what makes a violin sound different from a flute even at the same pitch). Researchers use these extracted features as raw material for studying, for example, which acoustic properties of a song make listeners feel it as happy or sad. It has been one of the most widely used academic tools of its kind since its release and underlies a large body of the Centre's music-emotion and music-cognition research.
