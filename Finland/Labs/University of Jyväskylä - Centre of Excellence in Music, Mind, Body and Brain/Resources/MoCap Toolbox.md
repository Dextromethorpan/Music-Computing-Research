The MoCap Toolbox is a free MATLAB software package for analyzing "motion capture" data — recordings of a person's body movement made with special cameras or sensors that track reflective markers on their joints (the same kind of technology used to animate movie characters). It was built at the University of Jyväskylä specifically to study how people move when they dance or otherwise respond physically to music, converting raw marker positions into measures like how fast a limb is moving or how much the whole body sways. It is a foundational open-source tool underlying most of the Centre's computational studies of music-induced movement, letting researchers turn videos of dancing into numbers they can statistically analyze.
