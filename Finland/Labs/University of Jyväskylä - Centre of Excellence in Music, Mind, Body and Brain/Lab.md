The Centre of Excellence in Music, Mind, Body and Brain (CoE MMBB) is a Research Council of Finland-funded consortium coordinated by the University of Jyväskylä, in partnership with the University of Helsinki. Its broader focus is music as a multimodal human experience across the lifespan; its computational strand — led by Petri Toiviainen and colleagues at Jyväskylä's Department of Music, Art and Culture Studies — builds open-source MATLAB tools (MIRtoolbox, MoCap Toolbox) and uses motion-capture and machine learning to study music-induced movement, dance coordination, and emotion expressed through the body.
