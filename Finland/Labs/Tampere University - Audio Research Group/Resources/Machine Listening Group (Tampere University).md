"Machine listening" is a general term for getting computers to make sense of everyday, non-speech, non-music sound — recognising what's happening in an audio recording (a door closing, traffic, birdsong) the way a person would just by listening. This research page describes the sub-team within the Audio Research Group, led by Associate Professor Annamaria Mesaros, that focuses specifically on this problem: building systems for sound event detection and classification, generating text descriptions of audio ("audio captioning"), and studying how such systems can keep learning new sounds over time without forgetting old ones ("continual learning"). The group is also closely tied to organizing the DCASE research challenge.
