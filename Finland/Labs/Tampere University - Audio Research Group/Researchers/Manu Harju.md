Manu Harju is a doctoral researcher in the Machine Listening Group, working on sound event detection — teaching computers to spot and time specific sounds in a recording — including how to train such systems using cheaper, less precise labels such as machine-generated audio captions instead of costly hand-marked event timestamps.
