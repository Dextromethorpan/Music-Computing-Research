Archontis Politis works on spatial audio: capturing, modeling, and reproducing how sound behaves in three-dimensional space, using both traditional signal processing and machine learning. This includes formats like Ambisonics (a way of recording/playing back sound from every direction around a listener) and neural methods for encoding microphone-array recordings into these spatial formats. He also co-founded Aani, a spatial audio technology startup, and is a regular organizer of the DCASE challenge.
