Michael Neri is a postdoctoral researcher who joined the group after completing a PhD in applied electronics at Roma Tre University on scene understanding with sound and AI. At Tampere he works on audio deepfake and voice-replay-attack detection using spatial audio (multiple microphones), and more broadly on combining computer vision and deep learning with audio processing.
