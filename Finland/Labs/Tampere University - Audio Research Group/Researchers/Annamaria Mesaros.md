Annamaria Mesaros leads the Machine Listening Group within the Audio Research Group, focusing on getting computers to recognise and describe everyday, non-speech sounds — sound event detection and classification, generating text captions for audio, and building systems that can keep learning new sound categories over time without forgetting older ones. She is also a long-time organizer of the DCASE challenge and workshop, serving as a task coordinator and vice-chair of the DCASE community.
