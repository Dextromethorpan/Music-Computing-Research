Tuomas Virtanen leads the Audio Research Group at Tampere University and founded DCASE (Detection and Classification of Acoustic Scenes and Events), the field's main recurring challenge and community for teaching computers to recognise everyday sounds. His research covers audio and speech signal processing, machine learning for sound content analysis, sound event detection, and acoustic scene classification. He is an IEEE Fellow and has led multiple large research projects on making machine "hearing" of real-world environments more accurate and robust.
