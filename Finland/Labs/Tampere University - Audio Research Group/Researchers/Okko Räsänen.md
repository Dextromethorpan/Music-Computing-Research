Okko Räsänen heads the Speech and Cognition research group within the Audio Research Group. His work sits between speech technology and cognitive science: he builds computational models of how infants and children learn to understand and produce spoken language, using machine learning and large audio datasets to simulate early language acquisition, and studies speech and affective (emotion-related) signal processing more broadly.
