The Audio Research Group is part of Tampere University's Faculty of Information Technology and Communication Sciences, Finland, led by Professor Tuomas Virtanen (founder of the DCASE — Detection and Classification of Acoustic Scenes and Events — challenge and research community). The group works on signal processing and machine learning for understanding sound: recognising sound events, separating mixed audio into its sources, transcribing music automatically, localising sounds in space, and building noise-robust speech recognition and synthesis systems.
