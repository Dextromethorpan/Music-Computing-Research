GLM (Genelec Loudspeaker Manager) is Genelec's control and calibration software for their "Smart Active Monitor" (SAM) speakers — loudspeakers with digital signal processing and networking built in. GLM lets a sound engineer connect many Genelec monitors together (from a single stereo pair up to a full surround or immersive multi-speaker setup), then automatically measure the room they're placed in and adjust each speaker's output (its loudness, timing/delay, and tone) to correct for that specific room's quirks, a process often called "room calibration." This turns what used to be a manual, expert-only tuning process into something more repeatable and consistent, and is a practical, real-world application of the room-measurement research Genelec's R&D team also publishes as academic papers.
