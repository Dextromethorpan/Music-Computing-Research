An "active" loudspeaker (like Genelec's studio monitors) has its power amplifiers built directly into the speaker cabinet, so choosing the right amplifier design matters for cost, sound quality, and how much heat and power the speaker uses. This AES (Audio Engineering Society) paper, written by an amplifier engineer at Genelec, compares "Class D" amplifiers — which work by rapidly switching the output signal on and off rather than smoothly varying it — against other common amplifier technologies. It measures each option's audio quality, output capability, energy efficiency, and cost, and argues that Class D amplifiers had, by 2013, become the best all-round value for building active loudspeakers.
