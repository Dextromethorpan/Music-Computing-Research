concept: Adaptive Music Systems
date_first_seen: 2026-08-12
---

Adaptive Music Systems are technology that continuously reshapes an already-existing, pre-recorded piece of music in real time to match what's happening around the listener — their pace while running, their heart rate, the time of day, or their mood — rather than generating a brand new piece of music from scratch. As the listener's context changes, the system can smoothly adjust things like tempo, key, instrumentation, or intensity, so the same song can feel different depending on the situation. This is a distinct idea from AI systems that compose new music from nothing (see "AI Music Generation") — adaptive systems work by transforming existing recordings on the fly.
