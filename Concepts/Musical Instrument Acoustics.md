date_first_seen: 2026-08-09

This is the scientific study of how musical instruments physically produce sound — how a string, reed, air column, or soundboard vibrates and radiates energy — and how factors like playing technique, instrument geometry, or materials change the resulting tone. It's the physics foundation that later gets used to build realistic synthesizers or to improve real instrument design.
