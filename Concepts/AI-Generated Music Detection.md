date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

As AI music generators became common, researchers built tools to answer the reverse question: was this track made by a human or a machine? These detectors typically look for tiny technical fingerprints — statistical patterns or subtle artifacts — left behind by the way generative AI models produce audio, patterns that don't usually appear in human-made recordings. This matters for copyright, royalty payouts, and simply knowing what you're listening to.
