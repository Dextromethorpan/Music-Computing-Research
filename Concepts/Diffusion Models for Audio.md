concept: Diffusion Models for Audio
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

Diffusion models are a family of AI generation techniques (also used for AI image generators) that work by starting from pure random noise and gradually "denoising" it step by step into a structured, meaningful result. Applied to audio, the model typically works on a spectrogram (a visual-like representation of sound over time and frequency) or a compressed audio code, gradually sculpting noise into music or sound effects.
