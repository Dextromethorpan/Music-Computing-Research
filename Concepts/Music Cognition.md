Music cognition is the scientific study of how the human mind and brain perceive, understand, and produce music — for example, how we recognize a beat, remember a melody, or feel emotion from a chord progression. Researchers in this field combine psychology, neuroscience (studying brain activity), and sometimes computational modeling to figure out which musical abilities are innate (present from birth or built into our biology) versus learned through cultural exposure. It's a foundational area for music computing because building better music technology often requires understanding how humans actually experience music in the first place.
