concept: Score Following
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

Score-following software listens to a live musician playing from written sheet music and tracks, in real time, exactly where they currently are in the piece — even through wrong notes, tempo changes, or skipped sections. Once it knows the performer's live position, it can trigger synchronized electronics, projected visuals, or an automatic accompaniment part to stay perfectly in sync with the human performer.
