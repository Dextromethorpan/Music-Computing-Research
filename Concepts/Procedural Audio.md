Procedural audio is sound generated algorithmically, on the fly, by a set of rules or a small model, rather than played back from a pre-recorded audio file. It's especially useful in video games, where footsteps, explosions, or ambient sounds need to vary naturally every time and avoid becoming repetitive or requiring huge libraries of pre-recorded variations.
