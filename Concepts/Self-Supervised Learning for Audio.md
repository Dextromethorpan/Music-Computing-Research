date_first_seen: 2026-08-09

Self-supervised learning is a way of training AI models without needing large sets of manually labeled examples. Instead, the system creates its own training signal automatically from the raw data itself — for example, by hiding part of an audio clip and having the model learn to predict the missing part. This lets researchers train powerful models on huge amounts of raw audio that would be far too expensive to label by hand.
