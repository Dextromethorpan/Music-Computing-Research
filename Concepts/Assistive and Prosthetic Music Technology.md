---
concept: Assistive and Prosthetic Music Technology
date_first_seen: 2026-08-10
---

This is the use of technology — including robotics, sensors, and software — to help people with disabilities or physical limitations make music. A well-known example is a robotic prosthetic limb that lets someone who has lost an arm or hand keep playing an instrument, sometimes even giving them abilities beyond what an unassisted human body can do (like extremely fast, precisely-timed motion). This overlaps with musical robotics and gesture-based interaction, but is specifically motivated by restoring or expanding a person's own ability to perform, rather than building a robot as an independent musical partner.
