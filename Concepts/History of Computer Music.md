This is the study of computer music's origins and pioneering figures — for example, composers and engineers at places like Bell Labs who, starting in the 1950s-60s, first used computers to synthesize and compose sound. Understanding this history helps explain why today's music computing research is organized the way it is, and which ideas are genuinely new versus decades-old.
