Neuroplasticity is the brain's ability to physically reorganize itself — changing the size, connections, or activity of its regions — in response to experience. "Auditory neuroplasticity" specifically refers to this happening in the brain areas that process sound and music. For example, learning to play an instrument over years can measurably enlarge or reshape the auditory cortex (the brain's sound-processing region). Studying this helps researchers separate which listening abilities people are simply born with versus which ones develop through training, and it also informs music-based therapies for hearing and developmental conditions.
