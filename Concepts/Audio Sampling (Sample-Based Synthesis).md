---
concept: Audio Sampling (Sample-Based Synthesis)
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

This is a way of building playable instrument sounds by recording real notes from a real instrument (at different pitches and playing intensities) and then having the computer play back and pitch-shift those recordings when you press a key. It's the classic alternative to synthesizing a sound purely from mathematical models — you're working with captured reality rather than a simulation.
