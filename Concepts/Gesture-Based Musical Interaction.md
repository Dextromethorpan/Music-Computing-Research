---
concept: Gesture-Based Musical Interaction
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

This is the general idea of controlling sound or music in real time using captured physical movement — hand gestures, body motion, or other performance actions detected by sensors, cameras, or touch surfaces — rather than through a traditional keyboard, fader, or mouse click. It's central to designing new, expressive digital musical instruments.
