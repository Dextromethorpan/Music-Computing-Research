concept: Real-Time Pitch Correction and Vocal Harmonization
date_first_seen: 2026-08-13
---

This is the technology behind live vocal-effects tools that most people know as "Auto-Tune"-style pitch correction, extended further into automatic harmony generation. Pitch correction detects the pitch of a singer's voice as they sing and nudges any off-key notes toward the nearest correct note in real time, with almost no perceptible delay. Vocal harmonization goes a step further: by also analyzing an accompanying instrumental track to know the song's current musical key and chords, a device can generate one or more extra harmony voices that automatically follow the lead singer and stay musically correct, letting a solo performer sound like they have backing vocalists.
