Wave Field Synthesis is a technique for recreating sound in a room using a large array of many loudspeakers, so precisely coordinated that a listener perceives a sound as coming from an exact point in space — even a point that has no actual speaker there, like the middle of the room or outside the wall. Unlike ordinary stereo or surround sound, which just changes volume between a few fixed speakers to trick your ears, Wave Field Synthesis physically reconstructs the actual sound wave pattern that a real source at that location would have produced, giving very precise and stable spatial placement of sound.
