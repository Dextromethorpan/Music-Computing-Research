---
concept: Spectral Masking and Unmasking
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

In a mix with many instruments, two sounds that occupy the same frequency range (the same part of the pitch/tone spectrum, from low bass to high treble) can "mask" each other — the ear has trouble hearing both clearly at once, the way it's hard to hear a whisper next to a shout at the same pitch. Spectral masking is this problem; spectral "unmasking" is the process of adjusting the tone (equalizing) or level of tracks so each instrument gets its own carved-out frequency space and the whole mix sounds clearer. Software can automate this by analyzing multiple tracks at once and shifting each one's frequency balance so they don't fight each other.
