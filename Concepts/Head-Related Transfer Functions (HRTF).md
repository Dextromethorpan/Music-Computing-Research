concept: Head-Related Transfer Functions (HRTF)
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

A head-related transfer function (HRTF) describes how a specific person's head, outer ears, and torso reshape sound on its way to the eardrums, depending on the direction the sound is coming from. This reshaping — subtle changes in loudness, timing, and tone color between the two ears — is exactly what lets humans judge where a sound is located in 3D space. By measuring or modeling someone's HRTFs (often using a dummy head or a personalized 3D scan), engineers can process ordinary stereo audio so that, over headphones, it sounds like it's coming from a specific point around the listener. This is the core technology behind binaural (headphone-based) 3D audio, VR sound, and auditory augmented reality.
