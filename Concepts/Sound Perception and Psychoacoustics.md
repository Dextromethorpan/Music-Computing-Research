Psychoacoustics is the scientific study of how humans actually perceive sound — which is often different from the raw physical properties of a sound wave. For example, two sounds can be physically very different but perceived as similar, or vice versa. This research feeds directly into designing better, more natural-sounding synthesizers, audio compression, and spatial audio systems, because it tells engineers what differences actually matter to a human listener.
