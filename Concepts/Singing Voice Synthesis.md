Singing voice synthesis is software that generates a sung vocal performance — not just spoken words, but a melody with pitch, rhythm, vibrato, and expressive qualities — typically from a musical score or text input, without needing a human singer to record it. Systems often model different aspects of the voice (pitch, timbre, articulation) separately and then recombine them into the final synthesized performance.
