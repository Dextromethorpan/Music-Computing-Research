True Analog Emulation is a technique (a term coined by synthesizer maker Arturia) for digitally simulating the specific electrical behavior and imperfections of vintage analog synthesizer circuits — instead of simply recording samples of an old analog machine, engineers model the actual physics of its circuitry so the digital version reacts and sounds like the original hardware would, including its quirks.
