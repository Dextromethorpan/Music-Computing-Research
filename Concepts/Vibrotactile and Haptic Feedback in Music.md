Vibrotactile and haptic feedback means delivering information through touch — usually vibrations felt on the skin, like the buzz of a phone. In music technology, this is used to let people feel aspects of music (rhythm, pitch changes, beats) rather than only hearing them, which can add a physical dimension to a performance for any listener, and can be especially valuable for people with hearing loss (for example, cochlear implant users) who may struggle to perceive fine musical detail through hearing alone.
