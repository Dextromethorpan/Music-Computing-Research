Rhythm perception is the specific mental ability to sense timing patterns in music — noticing a steady beat, expecting the next note to fall at a certain moment, or feeling when a rhythm is syncopated (off the beat). Studies of rhythm perception ask questions like: is this ability present from birth, or learned? Does the culture someone grows up in change how they physically perform a rhythm? Researchers sometimes use mathematical models (like Bayesian statistics, a way of reasoning about uncertainty) to describe how the brain balances what it actually hears against what it expects to hear.
