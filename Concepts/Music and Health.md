This is the study of how music and designed sound can be used for therapeutic or wellbeing purposes — for example helping with movement, mood, or recovery — studied by combining acoustics (the science of sound), psychology, and artistic practice. Research in this area often measures how people's bodies and emotions respond to specific sound or music interventions.
