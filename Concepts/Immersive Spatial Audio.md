Immersive or spatial audio refers to sound systems designed to make you feel surrounded by sound from every direction — including above and below — rather than just from left and right speakers or headphones. Instead of a fixed number of channels (like stereo's two, or 5.1 surround's six), these formats often place sound as objects positioned in 3D space, or add extra height-channel speakers, so a mix can move sound anywhere around a listener. This technology is used in cinemas, home theatre systems, virtual reality, and increasingly in music streaming (e.g. "spatial" or "3D" music mixes), aiming to recreate a more natural, enveloping sense of physical space around recorded sound.
