The "Internet of Musical Things" extends the idea of the "Internet of Things" (everyday objects connected to a network so they can share data and work together) to musical instruments and audio devices. It imagines instruments, effects pedals, and audio gadgets that can connect to each other over a network and interact in real time — for example, musicians in different rooms or cities jamming together as if they were in the same space, or a smart instrument that reacts to what other connected devices are doing. Making this work well requires extremely low-latency (near-zero delay) audio processing, since even a small lag would make musicians feel out of sync with each other.
