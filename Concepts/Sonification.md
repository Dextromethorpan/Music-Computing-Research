Sonification is the practice of representing information through carefully designed sound, instead of (or alongside) images, numbers, or text. For example, a sonification system might turn a person's movement data into changing tones in real time, so they can hear — rather than see — feedback about what their body is doing, which can be useful for training, rehabilitation, or accessibility.
