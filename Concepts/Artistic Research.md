Artistic research (sometimes called "practice-based research") is a research method used at arts universities in which the actual process of making art — composing, performing, building an installation — counts as part of the investigation itself, not just something a researcher writes about afterward. A doctoral project in artistic research usually produces both new artworks (pieces of music, performances, installations) and a written reflection connecting them to a research question. It differs from typical scientific research, which usually tests a hypothesis with data and writes up the results in a paper; here, the "data" can be the felt experience of making and presenting the work. Institutions like the Sibelius Academy document artistic research projects on platforms such as Research Catalogue, where composers and sound artists upload audio, video, and photos alongside written commentary.
