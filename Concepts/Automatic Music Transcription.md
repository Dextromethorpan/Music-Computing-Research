Automatic music transcription is the task of taking a recorded audio performance and automatically figuring out the actual musical notes played — pitch, timing, and duration — and converting that into a symbolic form like sheet music or a note-by-note digital score. It's much harder than it sounds because real recordings mix many overlapping notes and instrument timbres together.
