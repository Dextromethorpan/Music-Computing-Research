Text-to-music generation is a category of AI model that creates original music audio directly from a written text description — for example "an upbeat lo-fi hip-hop instrumental with piano" — sometimes also guided by an existing melody. The model has learned, from huge amounts of training data, statistical associations between descriptive words and the kinds of sounds and structures they typically refer to.
