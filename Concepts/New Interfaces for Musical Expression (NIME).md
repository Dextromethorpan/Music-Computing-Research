NIME stands for "New Interfaces for Musical Expression" — it's both the name of a research topic and an annual academic conference about designing brand-new ways for people to control and make music with electronics, beyond a traditional keyboard or guitar. Examples include instruments controlled by touch on bare circuitry, by hand gestures picked up by sensors, or by custom software reacting to a performer's movement. The goal of NIME research is usually to make electronic instruments that are genuinely expressive — letting a performer shape subtle qualities of the sound live, the way an acoustic instrument lets a skilled player shape tone and dynamics.
