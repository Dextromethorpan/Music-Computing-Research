Audio captioning is the task of getting a computer to listen to a sound clip and write a plain-language sentence describing what's happening in it — for example "a dog barks while a car drives past" — the audio equivalent of the "image captioning" AI systems that describe photos. It's harder than simply tagging a sound with a single label (like "dog bark") because a caption has to capture multiple sounds, their order, and how they relate to each other, in natural language. Audio captions are also useful as a cheaper, more flexible substitute for detailed manual sound-event labels: instead of someone marking the exact start and end time of every event, a system can learn partly from these looser text descriptions.
