A neural network is a machine-learning model loosely inspired by how brain cells connect and pass signals, trained by showing it many examples until it learns to recognize patterns on its own rather than following fixed, hand-written rules. Applied to audio, neural networks are the engine behind most modern music AI: source separation, generation, detection, and more all typically rely on some form of trained neural network.
