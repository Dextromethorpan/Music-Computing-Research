concept: Positional Encoding
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

In AI models that generate sequences (like a series of musical notes or words), positional encoding is a technique for explicitly telling the model "where" a given element sits within a larger structure — for example, which bar or section of a song it's in — so the model can keep track of long-range musical structure and organization rather than only reacting to the immediately preceding notes.
