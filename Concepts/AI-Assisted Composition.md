---
concept: AI-Assisted Composition
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

This is when an AI system generates raw musical material — a chord progression, a bassline, a full backing track — that a human musician then edits, arranges, or builds on, rather than the AI producing a finished, unchangeable piece on its own. The point is collaboration: the software handles the tedious or idea-generating part, and a person keeps creative control over the final result. It's common in modern music production tools that suggest ideas you can accept, tweak, or throw away.
