Room acoustics is the study of how a physical space changes sound — through echoes, reflections off walls and objects, and the way certain frequencies resonate more than others. The specific "fingerprint" a room leaves on a sound is called its room impulse response. This matters for music and audio computing because software often needs to remove or simulate this effect: removing it helps clean up recordings or improve speech recognition and echo cancellation in video calls, while simulating it is used to make virtual reality audio, game sound, or reconstructions of historical performance spaces feel physically realistic.
