Mixing is the process of balancing the volume, tone, and placement of many recorded tracks (vocals, drums, guitar, etc.) so they sound good together; mastering is the final polishing pass applied to the finished mix before release. Both traditionally require a trained human engineer listening carefully and adjusting tools like equalizers (EQ, which reshapes the tone by boosting or cutting frequencies) and compressors (which even out loud and quiet parts). "Automatic" or "intelligent" mixing and mastering tools use software — often built on machine learning models trained on many professionally mixed songs — to analyze audio and suggest or directly apply these adjustments in seconds instead of the hours a human would need, while usually still letting the user tweak or reject the result.
