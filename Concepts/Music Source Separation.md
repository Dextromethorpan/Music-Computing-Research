Music source separation is the task of taking a mixed audio recording — like a finished song — and automatically splitting it back into its individual ingredient tracks, such as isolated vocals, drums, or bass, even though the original mix never stored those parts separately. It's useful for remixing, karaoke tracks, sampling, and music analysis.
