Sound event detection is the task of teaching a computer to recognise specific sounds in an audio recording — for example, telling apart a doorbell, a dog bark, footsteps, or a car horn — and often also to figure out where in space that sound came from ("localization"), using recordings from multiple microphones. This is a foundational technique behind smart microphones, hearing aids that adapt to their surroundings, security and monitoring systems, and spatial/immersive audio production, where knowing what a sound is and where it sits in a room helps a system react to it or place it correctly for a listener.
