This is the design side of music technology: how do you build an interface so that a non-technical musician can actually use a powerful but complex AI or audio tool naturally, without needing to understand the math underneath? It covers things like how controls are laid out, how feedback is given, and how much creative control is handed to the user versus the algorithm.
