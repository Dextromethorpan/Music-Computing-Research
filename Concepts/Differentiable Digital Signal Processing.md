concept: Differentiable Digital Signal Processing
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09

Traditional audio engineering uses precise, hand-designed mathematical building blocks — filters, oscillators, envelopes — to shape sound; machine learning normally uses a different toolbox that automatically discovers patterns from data. "Differentiable DSP" bridges the two by rewriting those classic audio building blocks in a way that a neural network's training process can automatically adjust and fine-tune them, gaining the interpretability and audio quality of traditional DSP with the automatic learning power of AI.
