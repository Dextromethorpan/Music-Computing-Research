A "cover song" is a new performance of an existing song — a different artist, arrangement, tempo, or key, but recognizably the same underlying composition. Cover song retrieval is the Music Information Retrieval task of teaching a computer to automatically find cover versions of a song within a large music collection, even though the audio itself (instruments, singer, speed, key) may be completely different. It's a genuinely hard problem because the computer has to identify the musically important, invariant structure (like the melody and chord progression) underneath very different surface sound.
