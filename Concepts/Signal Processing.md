Signal processing is the broad engineering discipline of mathematically analyzing, transforming, and manipulating signals — in this context, audio waveforms — to extract information from them or reshape them. It's the mathematical foundation underneath almost every specific technique in this vault, from pitch estimation to spatial audio to synthesis.
