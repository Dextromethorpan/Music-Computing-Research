date_first_seen: 2026-08-09

Steerable generation refers to AI generation systems that let a user fix or lock certain parts of the output while the model automatically fills in or generates the rest — similar to autocomplete, but for creative content like music. For example, a user might specify the melody and let the AI generate matching harmonies underneath.
