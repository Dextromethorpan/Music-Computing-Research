Ambisonics is a method for recording and reproducing sound across a full sphere around a listener — left, right, front, back, above, and below — rather than just the left/right of stereo. Instead of storing separate signals for a fixed number of speakers, it captures how sound pressure varies across space using a small set of special channels, which can then be decoded to fit almost any speaker layout or headphones. It's a key tool behind immersive and virtual-reality audio.
