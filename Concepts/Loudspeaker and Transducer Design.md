A "transducer" is the physical part of a loudspeaker that converts an electrical audio signal into the actual movement of air that we hear as sound (usually a cone or diaphragm driven by a magnet and coil). Designing transducers involves real physical trade-offs: making a speaker smaller, cheaper, or a different shape usually costs you something in loudness, bass response, or clarity, so researchers look for clever engineering (like tuned resonances or new material designs) to get more performance out of a smaller or more flexible physical package.
