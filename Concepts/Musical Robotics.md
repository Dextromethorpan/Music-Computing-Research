Musical robotics is the study and design of robots that can make, respond to, or move along with music — for example, a humanoid robot that dances in time with a song, or a robotic arm that plays a physical instrument. Unlike a robot that just plays back a fixed, pre-programmed routine, the interesting research problem is getting a robot to listen to audio in real time (using techniques like beat tracking) and adjust its behavior on the fly, the way a human dancer or musician would react to a live band. This connects robotics, signal processing, and machine learning, and is often used as a testbed for questions about timing, perception, and expressive movement.
