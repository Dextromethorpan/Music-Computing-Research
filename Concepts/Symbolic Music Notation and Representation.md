---
concept: Symbolic Music Notation and Representation
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

This is the practice of representing a musical score as structured, computer-readable data or text — describing notes, timing, and structure symbolically — rather than as a picture (like a scanned page) or as recorded audio. Representing music this way lets software generate, search, transform, and render scores automatically.
