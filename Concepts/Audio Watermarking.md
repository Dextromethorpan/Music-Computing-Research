concept: Audio Watermarking
date_first_seen: 2026-08-10
---

Audio watermarking means hiding a small, inaudible signal inside a piece of audio at the moment it's created, so that it can later be automatically detected — for example, to prove a recording was generated by a particular AI system, or to spot which section of a clip has been AI-altered. Unlike detection methods that try to spot AI-generation after the fact by looking for subtle artifacts, watermarking builds the "signature" directly into the audio from the start, which tends to make it more reliable to detect later, even after some editing or compression.
