Music Information Retrieval (usually shortened to MIR) is the research field about getting a computer to automatically understand, organise, and search through music. Tasks in this field include recognising a song's chords or key, estimating its tempo or mood, telling genres apart, finding similar-sounding songs, or automatically transcribing a recording into sheet music. MIR is the academic backbone behind features you already use, like music-streaming recommendation systems, "identify this song" apps, and automatic playlist generation — it's the main conference community (ISMIR) referenced across most academic music computing research.
