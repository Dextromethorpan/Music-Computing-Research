---
concept: Corpus-Based Concatenative Synthesis
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

This is a sound-synthesis technique where, instead of designing a sound from scratch, software cuts a large library of pre-recorded audio into many tiny fragments ("grains"), measures qualities of each fragment like pitch, loudness, or brightness, and then in real time selects and stitches together whichever fragments best match a target sound the user is aiming for. It effectively lets you "paint" with the sonic material of an entire recorded library.
