Beat tracking is the task of getting a computer to find the steady, recurring pulse in a piece of music — the "beat" you would naturally tap your foot to — and follow it as the music plays. It's a core building block of many music technology applications: DJ software uses it to line up two songs' tempos, robots use it to dance in sync with music, and music apps use it to auto-generate visualizations or captions. Computationally, it usually involves analyzing how the loudness or energy of the audio signal rises and falls over time and picking out the repeating pattern, even when the tempo speeds up, slows down, or the music has a complicated rhythm.
