Audio inpainting is the sound equivalent of using AI to fill in a missing or edited patch of a photo. Given an audio clip with a gap or a region a user wants to change, an inpainting model generates new audio content for just that region so it blends naturally with what's around it, without needing to regenerate or manually re-record the whole clip.
