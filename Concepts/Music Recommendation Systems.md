date_first_seen: 2026-08-09

These are the algorithms behind "you might also like" playlists and personalized radio: software that predicts which songs or artists a listener will want to hear next, based on their past listening behavior, the audio content of tracks, or both. They're one of the most widely used real-world applications of music computing research.
