concept: AI-Assisted Music Performance
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

AI-assisted music performance refers to using artificial intelligence software as an active participant in a live musical performance, alongside human musicians — for instance, a system that listens to what a musician is playing and generates a responsive musical part in real time, rather than just playing back a fixed pre-recorded backing track. Key open questions in this area include how a musician can meaningfully influence and shape what the AI generates in the moment, and how to preserve the human performer's own creative voice when part of the "band" is a computer system.
