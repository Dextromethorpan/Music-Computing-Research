concept: Low-Distortion Analog Audio Circuit Design
date_first_seen: 2026-08-13
---

This is the engineering discipline of designing the analog electronic circuits inside amplifiers and preamplifiers (the physical stages a signal passes through as pure voltage, before or instead of any digital processing) to introduce as little unwanted distortion and noise as possible. A famous example is amplifier "input stages" — the first point where a weak incoming signal gets buffered and boosted — since any distortion or noise added there gets carried through and amplified by every stage after it. Improvements typically come from clever arrangements of transistors (like feedback pairs or cascode stages) rather than from software, which is why this kind of work is patentable hardware engineering distinct from digital signal processing.
