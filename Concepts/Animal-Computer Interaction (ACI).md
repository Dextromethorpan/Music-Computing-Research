Animal-Computer Interaction (ACI) is a research field that designs technology — interfaces, sensors, instruments — for non-human animals to use, treating the animal itself as the user whose senses, abilities, and preferences should shape the design, rather than designing purely for a human handler. In music computing, this has included building instruments that let animals such as parrots trigger and shape sound themselves, based on studies of what they can hear and what they seem to enjoy.
