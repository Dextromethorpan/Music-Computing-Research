An audio codec is a system for shrinking audio into a smaller, more storage- and bandwidth-friendly form (compression) and then reconstructing something close to the original when it's played back. Traditional codecs like MP3 use fixed mathematical rules based on human hearing; newer "neural" codecs use a trained AI model to squeeze audio into a compact code and rebuild it, and that compact code turns out to also be a convenient format for AI models that generate new audio.
