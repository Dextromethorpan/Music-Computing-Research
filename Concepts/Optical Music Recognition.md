Optical Music Recognition (OMR) is the musical equivalent of Optical Character Recognition (OCR), the technology that lets a computer read printed text from a photo or scan. OMR teaches software to look at an image of printed sheet music — a photo taken with a phone, or a scanned page — and understand which notes, rhythms, and symbols are on the page, well enough to convert it into a digital, machine-readable format like MIDI. It matters because it lets musicians and researchers quickly digitize the huge amount of music that exists only on paper, making it searchable, editable, and playable by software.
