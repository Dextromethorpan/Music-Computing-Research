Voice conversion is the task of taking a recording of one person's speech and modifying it so it sounds like a different target speaker said it — while keeping the same words, timing, and delivery. It's used in games and film dubbing to reuse, extend, or adapt a voice actor's performance without needing them to re-record every line.
