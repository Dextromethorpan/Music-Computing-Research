AI music generation is the use of machine-learning software to automatically compose or produce music, rather than a human writing every note by hand. Some systems generate a full piece from a text description (like "gritty lo-fi drum loop"), while others generate music that reacts live to a performer's gestures or movement. This differs from simply playing back a pre-recorded track — the music is newly created, often uniquely each time, by combining learned patterns from training data with rules or randomness.
