---
concept: Automatic Music Accompaniment
date_first_seen: 2026-08-09
---

This is software that plays a musical part alongside a live human performer in real time — much like a backing band — and automatically adjusts its own timing to stay together with the performer, even if the performer speeds up, slows down, or makes a mistake. It has to constantly "listen" and re-predict where the performer is in the piece.
