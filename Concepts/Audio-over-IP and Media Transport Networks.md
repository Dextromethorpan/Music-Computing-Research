---
concept: Audio-over-IP and Media Transport Networks
date_first_seen: 2026-08-12
---

Professional broadcasters and streaming services need to move live audio and video between studios, stadiums, and data centers over ordinary computer networks (IP networks) instead of dedicated cables, with almost no delay and no dropped frames — even if a network link briefly fails. Audio-over-IP and media transport technology covers the protocols, hardware, and standards (like AES67, SMPTE ST 2110, and JPEG XS) that make this possible: reliably synchronizing time across devices, seamlessly switching to a backup path if one connection drops (called "hitless failover"), and squeezing high-quality audio/video into as little bandwidth as possible without noticeable quality loss.
