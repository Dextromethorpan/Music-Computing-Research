Pitch estimation is the task of automatically figuring out the fundamental frequency — essentially, the musical note — being played or sung in an audio recording. It sounds simple but is genuinely difficult for real-world, noisy, or overlapping-instrument audio, and it underlies many other tasks like tuning correction, transcription, and singing synthesis.
