concept: Game Audio Middleware
date_first_seen: 2026-08-13

Game audio middleware is specialized software that sits between a game engine (like Unreal or Unity) and its sound output, so that sound designers and audio programmers don't have to write low-level audio code themselves. It handles things like mixing many sounds together, playing the right variation of a sound so footsteps don't repeat identically, blending music between game states, and simulating how sound travels through a game's 3D spaces (reverb, occlusion behind walls, distance attenuation). The two dominant tools in this space are Wwise (by Audiokinetic) and FMOD; both are used across a huge share of commercial video games.
