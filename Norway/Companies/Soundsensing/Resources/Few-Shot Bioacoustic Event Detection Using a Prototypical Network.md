This technical report describes an entry to the DCASE challenge (an annual academic competition for automatically recognizing sounds), tackling the detection of animal sounds ("bioacoustic" events) in nature recordings. The core challenge is that ecologists often only have a handful of labeled examples of a rare species' call rather than a huge training dataset, so the researchers use a "few-shot learning" approach — a machine-learning method designed to work well from just a few examples — to teach a model to recognize a new species' sound from very limited data.
