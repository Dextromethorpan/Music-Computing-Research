Nordby founded Soundsensing and works on machine learning for sound classification running on small, low-power embedded sensors — for example, detecting noise sources or animal calls directly on a wireless sensor rather than sending raw audio to the cloud. His published research covers both industrial noise monitoring and biodiversity/bioacoustic monitoring applications.
