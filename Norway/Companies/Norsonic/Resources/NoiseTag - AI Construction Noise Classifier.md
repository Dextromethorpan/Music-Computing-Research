NoiseTag is a practical product built using Norsonic's acoustic measurement equipment, designed to sit at a construction site and automatically classify which sounds belong to the construction project (like a jackhammer or excavator) versus unrelated background noise (like passing traffic). It replaces a slow, manual review process — where someone would otherwise have to listen through hours of recordings — with a model trained specifically for that site's expected sound sources.
