Today, teaching an AI system to recognize a new type of sound usually means collecting a large, freshly labeled dataset from scratch for every new use case, which is expensive. This project, funded by Norsonic and the Research Council of Norway and running until 2028, aims to build methods that let a sound-recognition system be adapted to new sound types with much less new data and manual effort — testing the approach on both city-noise monitoring and wildlife/biodiversity sound monitoring.
