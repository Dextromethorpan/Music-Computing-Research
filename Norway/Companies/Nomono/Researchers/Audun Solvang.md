Solvang co-founded Nomono and works on the audio signal processing behind its products, with a research background spanning microphone and speaker arrays, psychoacoustics, 3D audio, beamforming, and room acoustics — the technical foundations for turning multiple microphone signals into a coherent 3D sound picture.
