In this two-part podcast interview, Nomono's co-founder discusses the engineering choices behind the company's spatial-audio recording hardware — including how its microphone-array approach compares to other spatial recording techniques, and the tradeoffs involved in making immersive audio recording usable for non-technical podcasters working in the field, rather than only in a studio.
