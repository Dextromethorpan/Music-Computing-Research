The Sound Capsule is Nomono's field recording device: a central unit with a built-in ambisonic microphone array (a microphone setup that captures sound from every direction at once) paired with small wireless microphones worn by up to four speakers. It records each source separately and captures the full 3D sound of the room at the same time, so producers can later mix the recording into immersive formats such as binaural audio (3D sound for headphones) or Dolby Atmos, rather than a flat stereo track.
