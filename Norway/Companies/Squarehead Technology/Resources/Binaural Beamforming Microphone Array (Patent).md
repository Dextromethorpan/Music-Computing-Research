This US patent covers a specific design for a "binaural" beamforming microphone array — one engineered to produce a two-channel (left/right) output that preserves a natural sense of sound direction for a human listener, rather than just a single combined signal. This matters for applications like remote monitoring, where a human operator needs to be able to tell, just by listening, roughly where a sound came from.
