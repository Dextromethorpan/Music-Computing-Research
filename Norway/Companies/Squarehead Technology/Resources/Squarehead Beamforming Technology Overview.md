This page explains, in plain terms, how Squarehead's core technology works: by combining many microphones into an "array," a technique called beamforming can digitally point the combined microphone at a specific direction, making sound from that direction much clearer while muting everything else — similar to how having two ears lets a person tell which direction a sound came from. The company adds machine learning on top of this to automatically classify what kind of sound it is (for example, a drone, a gunshot, or a machine starting to fail), not just where it's coming from.
