Squarehead Technology is an Oslo-based company, started in 2004 as a University of Oslo physics student project, that builds microphone-array "acoustic cameras" — systems that can visually locate and classify sounds in space using beamforming and machine learning, originally aimed at broadcast and now used in defense, security, and industrial monitoring.
