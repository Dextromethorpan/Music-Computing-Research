Bach Technology, later rebranded MusicDNA, is a Bergen-based company that developed digital music-identification and "enhanced audio" technology in partnership with the Fraunhofer Institute (the German research institute that invented the MP3 format). It now runs a global radio-airplay monitoring service built on its music-fingerprinting technology.
