Bach founded Bach Technology (later MusicDNA) in Bergen and was involved in the original development of MP3 technology in the early 1990s. He later co-invented the MusicDNA audio fingerprinting patent and led the company's shift toward global radio-airplay monitoring built on the same music-identification technology.
