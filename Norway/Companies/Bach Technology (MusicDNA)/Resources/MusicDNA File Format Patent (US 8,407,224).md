This US patent describes a way to build a "collection profile" — essentially a fingerprint — of a set of audio files, by extracting multiple audio features from each track and combining them mathematically. That fingerprint can then be used to identify a piece of music or trigger extra content (like lyrics, videos, or social media) to play alongside it automatically. This is the technical basis of the MusicDNA format, which the company pitched as a possible successor to plain MP3 files.
