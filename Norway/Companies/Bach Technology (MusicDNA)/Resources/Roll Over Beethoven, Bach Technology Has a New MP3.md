This news article covers the public launch of MusicDNA, a proposed evolution of the MP3 audio format that could carry extra synchronized content — like lyrics, images, or artist updates — alongside the music itself, and update that content over the internet even after the file had already been downloaded. It explains why the company, founded by someone involved in the original development of MP3, believed a new format was needed as the music industry moved online.
