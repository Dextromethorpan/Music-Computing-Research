Rune Skramstad is co-founder and CTO of NNNN, an Oslo pro-audio loudspeaker company. He has designed loudspeakers since childhood and previously worked in Tandberg's/Cisco's audio research department before co-founding NNNN in 2016. He leads the company's horn-loudspeaker design and acoustic engineering work, including the patented horn-loading technology behind NNNN's speaker line.
