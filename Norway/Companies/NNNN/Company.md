NNNN is an Oslo-based pro-audio loudspeaker company, founded in 2016, that builds horn-loaded speakers for live sound, recording studios, and installed audio. NNNN qualifies for this vault on documented product/architectural innovation rather than academic publication: its patented horn-speaker design solves a long-standing physical trade-off in horn loudspeaker construction — normally a horn driver needs an impractically large rear chamber to move without being acoustically restricted. NNNN's patented approach (NO 341677 B1 / US 8,953,830 / EP 2060146, among others) uses controlled acoustic resistance behind the diaphragm so the driver behaves as if the rear chamber were infinitely large, even in a compact enclosure — letting the speaker keep the horn's efficiency, low distortion, and controlled directivity in a much smaller, lighter unit.
