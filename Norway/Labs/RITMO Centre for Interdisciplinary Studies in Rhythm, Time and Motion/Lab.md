RITMO is a Centre of Excellence at the University of Oslo that studies rhythm as a fundamental structuring force in human life, combining musicology, psychology, and computer science (informatics). Its researchers work on music technology, new digital instruments, and music information retrieval, and it coordinates a wider Nordic network for sound and music computing research.
