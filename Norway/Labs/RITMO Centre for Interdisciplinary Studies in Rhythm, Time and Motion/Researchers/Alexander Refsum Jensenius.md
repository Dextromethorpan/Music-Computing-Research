Jensenius directs RITMO and researches how people move when they make or listen to music, using motion capture and sensors to study the connection between body movement and sound. He chaired the NIME conference's steering committee from 2011 to 2022 and has been a central figure in shaping the New Interfaces for Musical Expression research community internationally.
