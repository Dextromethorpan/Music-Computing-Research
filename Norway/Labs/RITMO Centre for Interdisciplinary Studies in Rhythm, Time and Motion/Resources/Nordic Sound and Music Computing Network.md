This is a network project connecting leading sound and music computing researchers across all five Nordic countries (Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Iceland), coordinated from RITMO at the University of Oslo. Rather than a single study, it is an ongoing effort to build shared research capacity in this field across the Nordic region — for example, by organizing joint events like the "Nordic Sound and Music Computing Day." It is a useful map of who else in the region works on the same kinds of questions as RITMO.
