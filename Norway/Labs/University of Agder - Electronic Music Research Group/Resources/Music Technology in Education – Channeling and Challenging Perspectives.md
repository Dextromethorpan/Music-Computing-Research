This is a book chapter examining how popular electronic music students actually work when composing on laptops (sometimes called the "laptop-era" of music-making). The authors studied students' real creative practices — how they experiment, finish tracks, and use software tools — to inform how electronic music production should be taught, rather than assuming one fixed "correct" workflow.
