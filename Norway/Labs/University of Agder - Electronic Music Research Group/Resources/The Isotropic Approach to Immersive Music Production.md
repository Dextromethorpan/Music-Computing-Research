In this workshop, researcher Zachary Bresler explains different multi-speaker sound formats — including surround sound, ambisonics (a way of capturing sound from all directions), and binaural audio (stereo sound designed to trick the ears into perceiving 3D space through headphones) — and presents his own method for composing and mixing music across these formats, drawing equally from pop production and experimental sound-art traditions. It has been presented at UiA and at international venues including an Audio Engineering Society convention in New York.
