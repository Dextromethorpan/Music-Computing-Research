Bang is a Norwegian musician, producer, and professor of electronic music at UiA, known for live-sampling performance — reshaping recorded sound live during a concert — and for co-founding the Punkt festival, which explores "live remix" as an artistic and research practice combining acoustic performance with real-time electronic reprocessing.
