Svensson has led room acoustics research at NTNU's Acoustics Group since 1999, focusing on computational methods for auralization (simulating how a space sounds) and the physics of sound diffraction around edges and obstacles. His work is applied to designing music performance spaces and to virtual/augmented-reality audio.
