This is a research publication archive covering decades of work on room acoustics and auralization (computer simulation of how a room or hall will sound). A recurring theme is edge diffraction — how sound bends around the hard edges of walls, furniture, and other objects — which turns out to be important for making computer-simulated room sound convincingly realistic. This kind of research underlies virtual acoustics tools used for planning concert halls and for realistic sound in video games and virtual reality.
