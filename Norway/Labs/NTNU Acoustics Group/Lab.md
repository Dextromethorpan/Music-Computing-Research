The Acoustics Group sits within NTNU's Department of Electronic Systems in Trondheim and studies room acoustics, electroacoustics, and virtual acoustics (auralization) — how computers can simulate what a space will sound like before it's built, which matters for concert-hall and performance-space design as well as games and VR.
