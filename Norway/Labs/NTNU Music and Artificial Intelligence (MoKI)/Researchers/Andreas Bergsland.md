Andreas Bergsland is a Professor of Music Technology at NTNU in Trondheim, where he leads the Music and Artificial Intelligence (MoKI) research and development group. His research focuses on movement-to-sound interaction (including the MotionComposer, a device that turns body movement into music for people with and without disabilities), interactive dance, and the use of voice and live electronics in electroacoustic music. He also co-leads a work package on AI for health and well-being within NTNU's MishMash Centre for AI and Creativity.
