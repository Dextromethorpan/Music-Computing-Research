Music and Artificial Intelligence (MoKI) is a research and development group in the Department of Music at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) in Trondheim. It is a distinct group from the department's existing "NTNU Music Technology R&D Group" (led by Øyvind Brandtsegg), led instead by Professor Andreas Bergsland, and focuses on how AI can support co-creative composition and improvisation, and on using movement-tracking and sonification as therapeutic and accessibility tools — for example turning a person's body movement directly into music. The group coordinates NTNU's involvement in the MishMash Centre for AI and Creativity.
