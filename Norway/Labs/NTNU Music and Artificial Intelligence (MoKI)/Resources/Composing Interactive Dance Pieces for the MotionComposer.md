The MotionComposer is a device that turns a person's body movement into music in real time, using a camera to track motion and software that maps that movement to sound. It was designed so that people with physical, cognitive, or sensory disabilities — who might not be able to play a traditional instrument — can still make expressive music just by moving. This paper, presented at the NIME (New Interfaces for Musical Expression) conference, describes how the authors composed and tuned specific interactive dance pieces for the device, balancing the freedom to move naturally against giving the performer enough musical control to feel expressive. The work combines music technology, disability-inclusive design, and interactive dance.
