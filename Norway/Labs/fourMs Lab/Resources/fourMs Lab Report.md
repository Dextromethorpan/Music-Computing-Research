This is a descriptive report about the fourMs lab's facilities and methods, written for other researchers who study music-related body motion. It explains what kind of equipment the lab uses — such as motion-capture cameras that track reflective markers on a performer's body — and what kinds of questions this lets researchers answer, like exactly how a violinist's arm moves to produce a certain sound quality. It is useful background for understanding what "motion capture research" in music actually involves in practice.
