This is the music technology research and development group in the Department of Music at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) in Trondheim. It focuses on interaction between live acoustic performers and digital/electronic processing, and has organized major international conferences including DAFx (Digital Audio Effects) in 2015.
