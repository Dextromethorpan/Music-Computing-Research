Brandtsegg works at the intersection of live performance and digital signal processing, building software for real-time audio processing and cross-adaptive effects (where one performer's sound automatically shapes the processing applied to another's). He has been involved in NTNU projects combining acoustic instruments, electronics, and live-coded processing, including a project orchestrating Conlon Nancarrow's mechanical player-piano compositions for pipe organ and electronics.
