This paper describes a system that listens to a live performance of a fugue (a musical form built from interweaving melodic lines) played by a string quartet and generates a real-time visual display showing how the different melodic lines relate to each other as they are played. The goal is to help audiences — who may not be trained musicians — see the musical structure that is normally only audible, making complex classical music more approachable.
