This paper, presented at the DAFx (Digital Audio Effects) conference hosted by NTNU in Trondheim, describes a method for automatically figuring out a piece of music's underlying beat and bar structure ("metrical structure") directly from an audio recording, rather than from a written score. This kind of analysis is a building block for other tools, such as software that can automatically follow along with a live performance or align a recording to a musical score.
