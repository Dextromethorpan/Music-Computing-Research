This artistic-research PhD project investigated "mixed-initiative" music making — performance situations where control over the music is shared between a human performer and a computer system that can also make independent creative decisions, rather than the computer simply following the human's instructions. It explores what it feels like, as a performer, to give up some control to a machine collaborator in real time.
