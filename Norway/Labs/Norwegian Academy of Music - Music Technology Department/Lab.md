NMH (Norges musikkhøgskole) is Norway's largest music conservatory, based in Oslo. Its Music Technology department teaches and researches recording, composition, and live electronics, treating "music at the centre and technology as a tool," and hosts several artistic-research PhD projects exploring digital tools in performance and composition.
