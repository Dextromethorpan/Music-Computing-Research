SINTEF is Scandinavia's largest independent research institute, headquartered in Trondheim. Its Acoustics Research Group works on communication, environmental, industrial, and underwater acoustics, including using AI and deep learning to process sound — for example cleaning up speech in noisy environments, and figuring out how well a hearing-impaired listener can actually understand processed speech.
