"Speech enhancement" systems try to clean up noisy speech (for example in a hearing aid or a phone call) so it's easier to understand. Normally, testing whether an enhancement system actually helps requires two separate kinds of tests: one to check if people can understand the words (intelligibility), and a much more complex, time-consuming one to check how mentally tiring it is to listen (listening effort). This paper, presented at the IWAENC 2024 workshop in Denmark, proposes a single simple method to measure both intelligibility and listening effort at the same time, without extra strain on test subjects. It was validated using real listening tests with 76 people across Norway and Denmark.
