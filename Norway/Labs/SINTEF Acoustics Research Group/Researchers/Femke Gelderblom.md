Femke B. Gelderblom is a Senior Research Scientist at SINTEF Digital's Acoustics group in Trondheim, where she applies AI and deep learning to sound — mainly speech enhancement and noise reduction, plus how well those systems actually work for real listeners, including people with hearing loss. She holds a PhD in speech enhancement and deep learning from NTNU, and an MSc in Biomedical Engineering from TU Delft.
