IPEM (Institute for Psychoacoustics and Electronic Music) is the research centre for systematic musicology at Ghent University, founded in 1963. It studies how people experience music through their bodies — how movement, listening, performing, and even sport or rehabilitation connect to music — and builds technology (sensors, feedback systems) to study and support that connection.
