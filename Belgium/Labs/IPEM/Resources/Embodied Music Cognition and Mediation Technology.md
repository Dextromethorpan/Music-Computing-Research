This article (and the related MIT Press book by the same author) lays out the core idea behind IPEM's research: that we understand music largely through our bodies, not just our ears. When you tap your foot, sway, or feel an emotional reaction to a song, that is "embodied" musical understanding. The paper argues that computers and sensors ("mediation technology") can capture body movement during listening or performing, and use that data to build better music software, teaching tools, or rehabilitation exercises. It is one of the foundational texts explaining why IPEM builds motion-tracking and biofeedback systems around music.
