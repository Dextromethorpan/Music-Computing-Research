Marc Leman is the director of IPEM at Ghent University and holds a special "Methusalem" chair (a long-term Flemish government research grant) in systematic musicology. He founded the field of "embodied music cognition," which studies how people physically feel and respond to music, and has spent his career building technology — sensors, feedback devices, and computer models — that connect body movement to musical experience, with applications ranging from music education to physical rehabilitation.
