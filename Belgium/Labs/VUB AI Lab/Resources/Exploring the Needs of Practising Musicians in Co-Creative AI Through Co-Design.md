Instead of building an AI music tool first and testing it on musicians afterward, this paper studies what working musicians actually want and need from AI systems by involving them directly in the design process ("co-design"). "Co-creative AI" means software meant to work alongside a human as a creative partner, rather than just automatically generating a finished piece. The findings help explain why many AI music tools feel unsatisfying to professional musicians and point toward designs that support rather than replace human musical judgment.
