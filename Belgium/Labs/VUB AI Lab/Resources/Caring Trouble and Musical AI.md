This paper steps back from pure engineering questions and asks whose values and assumptions get built into musical AI systems — for example, whose musical traditions and creative processes the training data and design choices favour. Using a feminist theoretical lens, it argues for approaching musical AI design more carefully and inclusively, considering care, context, and whose creativity is being supported or overlooked. It reflects the VUB AI Lab's broader "computational creativity" tradition of asking not just "can AI make music?" but "what kind of creative relationship should AI have with people?"
