Geraint Wiggins is one of the founders of the research field of "computational creativity" — the study of how computers can simulate or support creative human activities like composing music. He moved to the VUB AI Lab in January 2018 (while also holding a position at Queen Mary University of London), and his work spans computational modelling of how humans perceive and cognitively process music, as well as broader questions in computational creativity and cognitive science.
