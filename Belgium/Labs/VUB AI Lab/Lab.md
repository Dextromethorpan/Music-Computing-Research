The Artificial Intelligence Lab at the Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB), founded in 1983, is a large interdisciplinary AI research group. Since 2018 it has hosted Professor Geraint Wiggins, one of the founders of the field of "computational creativity," which includes research on how AI can meaningfully collaborate with musicians and understand music cognitively rather than just generate audio.
