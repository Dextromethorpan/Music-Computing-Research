Toon van Waterschoot heads the KU Leuven Audio Engineering Lab and leads the STADIUS signal-processing research centre at KU Leuven's Department of Electrical Engineering. His research covers how sound behaves in rooms (room acoustics), removing echo and noise from recordings, locating where sounds come from, and automatic analysis of music recordings, including a project reconstructing the acoustics of historical music performance spaces.
