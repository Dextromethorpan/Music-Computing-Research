Arina Epure is a PhD student in the lab's "UNMUTED" project, supervised by Toon van Waterschoot. She works on automatically analysing multi-channel recordings of improvised, unscripted music-making, with the goal of applying this analysis in music therapy settings — for example, helping therapists understand what is happening musically and emotionally during an improvised music session.
