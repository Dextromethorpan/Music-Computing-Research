The KU Leuven Audio Engineering Lab is part of the Department of Electrical Engineering (ESAT) at KU Leuven, headed by Prof. Toon van Waterschoot. It does signal-processing research on audio, speech, music, and room acoustics — for example cleaning up noisy recordings, understanding how sound behaves in rooms, and analysing music recordings — and runs specialised acoustic labs for testing these ideas physically.
