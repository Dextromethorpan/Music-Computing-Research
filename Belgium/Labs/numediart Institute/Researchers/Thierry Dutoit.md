Thierry Dutoit is a professor at UMONS and director of the numediart Institute, specialising in speech and voice technology. He started the free MBROLA speech-synthesis project in 1995 (which led him to co-found the speech-technology company Acapela) and has since published widely on speech processing, expressive voice synthesis, and applied signal processing, holding several patents in speech technology.
