numediart is the Institute for Creative Technologies at the University of Mons (UMONS), created in 2010 and made up of around ten smaller labs (roughly 15 faculty and 60 students). It applies deep learning and signal processing to sound, image, video, gesture, and biosignals — including a long-running specialisation in speech and voice technology through its historical TCTS/ISIA speech-processing lab.
