This dataset was built to help train computer systems that can identify what sound is happening (a car horn, a dog bark, footsteps) and where it is coming from in space, using recordings made with multiple microphones at once. This task is called "sound event classification and localization." It matters for music and audio computing because the same underlying techniques — figuring out what a sound is and where it's positioned — are used in smart microphones, spatial audio mixing, and immersive audio production.
