EmoV-DB is a public dataset of recorded speech in which actors spoke the same sentences while performing different emotions (such as anger, amusement, or sleepiness). Researchers use datasets like this to train computer systems that generate expressive, emotional-sounding speech or singing voices, instead of the flat, neutral voice you get from a typical text-to-speech app. This is a building block for expressive voice synthesis, which overlaps with music technology whenever a system needs to generate or analyse a convincingly emotional singing or speaking voice.
