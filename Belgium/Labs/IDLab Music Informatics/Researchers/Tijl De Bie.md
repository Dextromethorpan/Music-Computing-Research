Tijl De Bie is a professor at Ghent University who co-leads the AI and Data Analytics (AIDA) group within IDLab. His broader research is on the foundations of machine learning and data science, applied to areas including bioinformatics, online news analysis, and — through IDLab's music informatics line — understanding music as a cultural and social phenomenon, such as automatically estimating a song's mood or chords, or spotting emerging music genres from data.
