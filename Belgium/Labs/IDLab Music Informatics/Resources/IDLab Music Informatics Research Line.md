This research group studies "music informatics" — using data-science and machine-learning techniques to understand music as a cultural phenomenon, not just as sound. Their main expertise is "machine listening": training computers to estimate the mood of a song or figure out its chords directly from the audio. They also analyse other data around music, such as lyrics, artist and fan information from social media, and streaming trends, to study how the popular music scene changes over time — for example, one funded project (DS4DEMS) investigates how to automatically detect emerging music genres before they become mainstream.
