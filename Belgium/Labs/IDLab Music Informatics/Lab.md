IDLab is a joint internet-technology and data-science research institute of Ghent University, the University of Antwerp, and imec. Its "Music Informatics" research line, co-led by Prof. Tijl De Bie, studies music as a cultural and social phenomenon using computational tools — analysing streaming/audio/social-media data to understand things like listener mood, chord content of songs, and how new musical genres emerge and spread.
