ASPIRE (Audio and Signal Processing, Interpretation and Enhancement) is a research group at Ghent University and imec, embedded within IDLab and the ELIS department. The team works on speech enhancement, audio analytics, and spatial audio, combining classical signal-processing methods with deep learning.
