Nilesh Madhu leads the ASPIRE research group at Ghent University and imec, working on speech enhancement, microphone-array processing, and spatial audio. He earned his doctorate at Ruhr-Universität Bochum and did postdoctoral work at KU Leuven on hearing prostheses before joining Ghent University in 2017.
