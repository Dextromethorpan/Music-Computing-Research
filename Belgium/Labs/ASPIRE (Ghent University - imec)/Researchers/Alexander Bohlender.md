Alexander Bohlender completed his PhD at Ghent University's ASPIRE group on spatially selective speaker separation — using the direction a voice comes from to help a neural network pull that voice out of a noisy, multi-speaker recording. He has since moved into industry as a machine learning engineer working on audio algorithms.
