Wilfried Van Baelen founded Galaxy Studios and later Auro Technologies, and is the inventor of the Auro-3D immersive sound format, first introduced at an AES (Audio Engineering Society) convention in 2006. He holds multiple patents covering the technology, which packages extra "height" audio channels alongside standard surround channels to give a more natural, enveloping sense of space in recorded and cinema sound.
