This patent, filed by Galaxy Studios NV (the parent of Auro Technologies), describes a method for combining and packaging multiple audio channels — including extra height/ceiling channels — into a single compatible data stream, and for decoding it back out again. This is the core technical idea behind the Auro-3D format: adding an extra layer of "height" speakers above a normal surround system so that sound can come from above the listener as well as around them, while staying compatible with older playback equipment. It underlies the immersive cinema and home-theatre sound systems the company still licenses today.
