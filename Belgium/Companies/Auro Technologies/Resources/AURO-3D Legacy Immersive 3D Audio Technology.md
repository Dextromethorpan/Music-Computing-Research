This company page explains the history and design goals of Auro-3D, the immersive sound format invented at Galaxy Studios in Mol. It describes how Auro-3D uses a three-layer speaker arrangement (a normal ring around the listener, a ring of speakers above that, and an optional overhead "voice of god" speaker) to spread sound more naturally through a room, aiming for a more enveloping listening experience than standard surround sound. The page also traces how the format grew from a 2006 studio-industry showcase into a cinema and home-theatre standard used worldwide.
