Auro Technologies is the sound-technology development company of the Galaxy Studios Group, based at the Galaxy Studios recording-studio complex in Mol, Belgium. It invented and patented Auro-3D, an immersive, multi-layered surround-sound format used in cinemas and home theatre systems, and holds a portfolio of related patents.
