Anthony Cioppa is a civil engineer specialising in computer vision and data analysis, appointed as the lead of an academic research chair at the University of Liège that is funded by EVS Broadcast Equipment. His research focuses on automatically understanding sports images and video, including combining multiple types of data — video, audio, and sensor signals — to get a fuller picture of what's happening in a sports broadcast. He is also co-founder of SoccerNet, a widely used open dataset for sports video analysis research.
