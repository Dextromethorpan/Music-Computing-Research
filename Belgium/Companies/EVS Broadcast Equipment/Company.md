EVS Broadcast Equipment, headquartered in Liège, is a global supplier of live video production technology (best known for instant sports replay systems). It holds 27+ patents and, in 2025, funded a new academic research chair at the University of Liège focused on AI for understanding sports content — including combining audio with video and sensor data.
