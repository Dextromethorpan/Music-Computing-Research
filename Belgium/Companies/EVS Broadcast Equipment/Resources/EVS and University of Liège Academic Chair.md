EVS, a company that makes live video-replay systems for sports broadcasting, funded a new research chair at the University of Liège called "Computer Vision and Data Analysis for Sports Understanding," led by Professor Anthony Cioppa. While the chair's main focus is analysing sports video, one of its four listed research directions is explicitly "multimodal combinations" — using video, audio, and sensor signals together to better understand what is happening in a sports broadcast (for example, using crowd noise, commentary, or an impact sound alongside the picture). This is a concrete example of a commercial broadcast-technology company funding academic research where audio processing is one ingredient in a larger AI system.
