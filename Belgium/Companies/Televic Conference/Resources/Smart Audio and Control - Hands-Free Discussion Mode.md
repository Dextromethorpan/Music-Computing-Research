In a meeting room with many microphones, someone has to decide which microphone to make loud at any moment — usually the person does this by pressing a button before they talk. Televic's "Smart Audio & Control" system removes that button entirely: every microphone stays live all the time, and the system automatically decides, moment to moment, which microphone(s) to boost based on who is actually speaking, while keeping background noise and feedback under control. The company documents this as the first system to fully automate that decision for hands-free, natural conversation in both in-person and hybrid (some people remote) meetings. It's a concrete example of a "no manual intervention" automatic audio-routing architecture, not just a marketing claim — Televic ties it to a specific product line (Confidea/D-Cerno) and a DSP integration mode that lets installers route the processed audio elsewhere.
