This patent describes the "brain" of a large conference or interpretation system — a central unit that keeps track of which interpreter is translating into which language channel, and which listener is tuned into which channel, so it can show everyone (interpreters included) real-time feedback such as how many people are listening to a given language. It's a piece of the plumbing that makes large multilingual meetings (like those at the EU or UN) function smoothly: interpreters can see if their channel is empty or full, and the system can route and count listeners automatically instead of by hand.
