Televic Conference (part of the Televic Group, based in Izegem, Belgium) builds conference, interpretation, and meeting-room audio systems, backed by a portfolio of over 100 patents. Included via documented product/architectural innovation (path 3): its "Smart Audio & Control" platform is the first system documented to remove the need for delegates to press a talk button at all, by combining multiple audio-processing techniques to automatically manage which of many always-on microphones is boosted, based on who is currently speaking.
