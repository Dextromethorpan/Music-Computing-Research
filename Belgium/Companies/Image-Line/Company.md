Image-Line, based in Ghent, Belgium, makes FL Studio, one of the world's most widely used digital audio workstations. Included via documented product/architectural innovation (path 3): its Harmor synthesizer's "Image Resynthesis" engine is a specific, well-documented technical mechanism — it converts any sample into an editable 2D image (separate gain and pitch planes) built from up to 516 additive sine-wave partials per note, letting a musician "draw" changes onto a real recording and hear them resynthesized in real time.
