Didier Dambrin, known to FL Studio users as "Gol," is the lead programmer behind FL Studio (originally FruityLoops) and its built-in synthesizers, including Harmor's additive/image-resynthesis engine. He joined Image-Line as a teenager after winning a multimedia programming contest in 1995 and has been the primary architect of the software's engine and workflow ever since.
