# Influencers — Neural Networks for Audio

## Valerio Velardo
**Type:** Content creator
**Platform(s):** YouTube — "Valerio Velardo - The Sound of AI" (thesoundofai.com); GitHub @musikalkemist
**Focus:** Runs a dedicated YouTube channel and course business built entirely around deep learning applied to sound and music — audio data prep, sound generation, and hands-on Python/PyTorch tutorials.
**Why relevant to Neural Networks for Audio:** His whole channel is essentially a public curriculum on neural networks for audio, aimed at practitioners rather than academics.

## Dadabots (CJ Carr & Zack Zukowski)
**Type:** Practitioner/thought leader
**Platform(s):** dadabots.com; long-running 24/7 YouTube livestreams of neural-network-generated music
**Focus:** Trains generative neural networks (SampleRNN-derived and newer architectures) on raw audio waveforms to produce continuous, novel death-metal and other genre streams, and speaks/writes publicly about the technique.
**Why relevant to Neural Networks for Audio:** One of the most visible public demonstrations of raw-waveform neural audio generation, running continuously in public rather than as a paper demo.

## Christian J. Steinmetz
**Type:** Both (researcher with real public-facing presence)
**Platform(s):** X @csteinmetz1; personal site christiansteinmetz.com; GitHub @csteinmetz1
**Focus:** Publishes and actively discusses differentiable DSP and neural audio effects work (e.g. DeepAFx-ST) in public, with code and demos shared alongside papers.
**Why relevant to Neural Networks for Audio:** Overlaps with academic output (he may also appear as a lab Researcher elsewhere in the vault), but is distinct here for being an active public voice — sharing work, code, and commentary directly with the practitioner community on X/GitHub rather than only through papers.

## Antoine Caillon
**Type:** Both (researcher with real public-facing presence)
**Platform(s):** Personal site caillonantoine.github.io; RAVE project (acids-ircam/RAVE on GitHub) with a public "artistic usage" community page
**Focus:** Creator of RAVE, a real-time neural audio synthesis variational autoencoder that a large community of sound artists has adopted for live neural timbre transfer.
**Why relevant to Neural Networks for Audio:** RAVE's community page and widespread artist adoption make him a de facto thought leader for real-time neural audio synthesis, beyond his lab publication record — worth noting overlap if a Researcher note already exists for him.

## Steven Atkinson
**Type:** Practitioner/thought leader
**Platform(s):** GitHub @sdatkinson (neural-amp-modeler, NeuralAmpModelerPlugin); interviewed publicly by Tone3000
**Focus:** Built and maintains Neural Amp Modeler (NAM), an open-source neural-network guitar-amp emulator with a large user/trainer community and wide plugin ecosystem coverage.
**Why relevant to Neural Networks for Audio:** NAM is one of the most widely used real-world applications of neural networks to audio outside a lab context, with an active open-source community built around it.
