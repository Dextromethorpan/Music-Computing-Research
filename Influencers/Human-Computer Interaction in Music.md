# Influencers — Human-Computer Interaction in Music

## Rebecca Fiebrink
**Type:** Both
**Platform(s):** Kadenze course "Machine Learning for Musicians and Artists," public talks/interviews (RITMO seminar series, Goldsmiths research interviews), creator of the Wekinator
**Focus:** Teaches and speaks widely on how interactive machine learning lets musicians shape instrument behavior directly, without needing to code or understand the underlying algorithms — the practical, hands-on side of HCI in music.
**Why relevant to Human-Computer Interaction in Music:** Her public course and talks are a primary on-ramp for practitioners learning how to design ML-driven musical interfaces; also the researcher behind the Wekinator system already linked to this Concept.

## Andrew McPherson
**Type:** Both
**Platform(s):** Distinguished lectures/podcast appearances (e.g. "Augmented Instruments Laboratory" episode), personal site andrewmcpherson.org, co-founder of Bela (embedded audio platform with its own blog/community)
**Focus:** Speaks and writes on digital musical instrument design, sensorimotor skill, and how embedded hardware platforms like Bela let instrument builders prototype real-time, low-latency interactions.
**Why relevant to Human-Computer Interaction in Music:** Regularly gives public distinguished talks and interviews specifically on instrument/interaction design, and built a widely-used open platform (Bela) that other practitioners use and discuss publicly.

## loopop
**Type:** Content creator
**Platform(s):** YouTube @loopop (loopopmusic.com)
**Focus:** Reviews synths and music gear with a strong focus on how the interface, workflow, and hands-on feel of an instrument affects usability — not just sound quality.
**Why relevant to Human-Computer Interaction in Music:** A high-visibility, ongoing YouTube channel where interface and workflow design of music hardware/software is the recurring evaluative lens, directly shaping how a large audience of musicians think about instrument usability.

## Sile O'Modhrain
**Type:** Practitioner/thought leader
**Platform(s):** TEI 2021 keynote speaker, public interview (Medium, "On electronic music, human-computer interactions, and accessibility"), personal site sileomo.github.io
**Focus:** Speaks and writes on haptic feedback and accessible design for musical interaction, drawing on her own experience as a blind researcher/musician.
**Why relevant to Human-Computer Interaction in Music:** Delivers public keynotes and interviews specifically framed around HCI and accessibility in music, distinct public-facing voice beyond her academic output.

## Ge Wang
**Type:** Practitioner/thought leader
**Platform(s):** TED speaker profile, book "Artful Design: Technology in Search of the Sublime" (artful.design), podcast guest (Sean Carroll's "Mindscape")
**Focus:** Writes and speaks broadly on designing technology (including musical instruments and languages like ChucK) for expressive, humane interaction rather than pure efficiency.
**Why relevant to Human-Computer Interaction in Music:** His book and talks directly address how musical/creative software should be designed around human expression and interaction, a core HCI-in-music theme, reaching an audience well beyond academia via TED and podcasts.
