# Influencers — Sound Perception and Psychoacoustics

## Diana Deutsch
**Type:** Practitioner/thought leader
**Platform(s):** Public talks and interviews (e.g. "Musical Illusions and Phantom Words" on YouTube), book-length interview "Believing Your Ears: Examining Auditory Illusions", personal demo site philomel.com
**Focus:** Psychology professor whose research on auditory illusions (tritone paradox, speech-to-song illusion) she also explains directly to general audiences through recorded talks and audio-illusion demos.
**Why relevant to Sound Perception and Psychoacoustics:** One of the most publicly recognizable voices translating auditory-illusion research into material a non-specialist audience can actually hear and understand.

## Trevor Cox
**Type:** Both
**Platform(s):** Personal blog "The Sound Blog" (trevorcox.me), X/Twitter @trevor_cox, author of the popular-science book "Sonic Wonderland"
**Focus:** Acoustic engineering professor who writes for a general audience about how humans perceive real-world sound environments, from concert halls to unusual acoustic phenomena.
**Why relevant to Sound Perception and Psychoacoustics:** Regularly publishes accessible writing bridging acoustics research and everyday sound perception, aimed explicitly at non-academic readers.

## Julian Treasure
**Type:** Practitioner/thought leader
**Platform(s):** TED talks (5, including "5 ways to listen better" and "How to speak so that people want to listen"), juliantreasure.com
**Focus:** Sound and communication consultant whose talks and workshops focus on how listening habits and sound environments shape attention and wellbeing.
**Why relevant to Sound Perception and Psychoacoustics:** His most-watched TED talks are specifically about how humans process and are affected by sound and listening, reaching a very large general audience.

## Dr. Cliff Olson (Doctor Cliff, AuD)
**Type:** Content creator
**Platform(s):** YouTube channel "Doctor Cliff, AuD"
**Focus:** Practicing audiologist producing regular videos on how hearing works, hearing loss, and how hearing aids interact with real-world auditory perception.
**Why relevant to Sound Perception and Psychoacoustics:** A high-visibility, currently active channel translating hearing science and auditory perception topics for a broad public audience, distinct from academic lab output.

## Dallas Taylor (Twenty Thousand Hertz)
**Type:** Content creator
**Platform(s):** Podcast "Twenty Thousand Hertz" (Spotify, Apple Podcasts)
**Focus:** Sound designer hosting a long-running podcast that digs into the stories and science behind how specific sounds are made and perceived.
**Why relevant to Sound Perception and Psychoacoustics:** A dedicated, sound-perception-adjacent podcast format that regularly covers why particular sounds are perceived the way they are, reaching a large audio-industry and general listener base.
