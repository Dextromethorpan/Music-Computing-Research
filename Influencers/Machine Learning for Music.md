# Influencers — Machine Learning for Music

## Valerio Velardo
**Type:** Content creator
**Platform(s):** YouTube (The Sound of AI, @ValerioVelardoTheSoundofAI), GitHub (musikalkemist), thesoundofai.com
**Focus:** Runs a dedicated channel and course business teaching audio/music deep learning from scratch — neural network fundamentals, melody generation, generative music AI — aimed at engineers wanting to build ML systems for sound.
**Why relevant to Machine Learning for Music:** His entire channel and product line is built specifically around teaching applied ML for music/audio, making him one of the most direct public teaching voices in this exact space.

## Cherie Hu
**Type:** Both
**Platform(s):** Water & Music newsletter/podcast (waterandmusic.com), Spotify podcast, regular guest on music-tech podcasts
**Focus:** Covers the business and technology of the music industry, with recurring deep coverage of AI/ML's effect on music creation, rights, and the streaming ecosystem, often interviewing engineers and founders building these systems.
**Why relevant to Machine Learning for Music:** Water & Music is a go-to industry-watched publication for how ML-driven tools are actually reshaping music creation and the music business, giving practitioners context beyond the pure research.

## Dadabots (CJ Carr)
**Type:** Practitioner/thought leader
**Platform(s):** YouTube (24/7 AI-generated music livestreams), dadabots.com
**Focus:** Builds and runs neural-network models (trained on artists' back catalogs) that generate continuous, unsupervised music streams, and speaks/writes about the technical approach behind it.
**Why relevant to Machine Learning for Music:** A widely covered, hands-on demonstration of ML music generation running live and continuously, frequently cited as a reference point for what generative ML in music can do in practice.

## Keunwoo Choi
**Type:** Practitioner/thought leader
**Platform(s):** X (@keunwoochoi), GitHub (keunwoochoi), personal site/blog (keunwoochoi.github.io)
**Focus:** ML engineer/researcher (worked on music recommendation and audio ML at companies including Spotify/Gaudio) who authored a widely used introductory tutorial on deep learning for music information retrieval and stays active discussing music ML work publicly.
**Why relevant to Machine Learning for Music:** His DL4MIR tutorial and open-source code are commonly the first stop for practitioners learning to apply deep learning to music tasks, and he keeps an active public presence discussing the field.

## Sander Dieleman
**Type:** Practitioner/thought leader
**Platform(s):** Blog (sander.ai), X (@sedielem), conference talks (e.g. ICLR, Creative AI meetup) on YouTube/SlideShare
**Focus:** Research scientist (Google DeepMind) who writes long-form, widely read blog posts explaining generative modeling techniques for raw audio and music, and gives conference talks on generating music in the waveform domain.
**Why relevant to Machine Learning for Music:** His blog posts on generative audio modeling (e.g. "Generating music in the waveform domain") are frequently referenced by practitioners as some of the clearest public explanations of how ML music-generation systems actually work.
