Peter van Kranenburg researches computational approaches to folk song and melody analysis at the Meertens Institute, building and studying digital corpora such as the Meertens Tune Collections. His work focuses on measuring melodic similarity computationally — a core problem for both folk song scholarship and Music Information Retrieval.
