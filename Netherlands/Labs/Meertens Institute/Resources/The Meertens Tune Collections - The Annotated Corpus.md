The Meertens Tune Collections are large sets of digitized Dutch and Flemish melodies (over 18,000 tunes), drawn from the Meertens Institute's Dutch Song Database, and made available specifically so researchers can test computer methods on them. This resource describes the "annotated" version of the corpus (MTC-ANN) — a smaller, carefully hand-labeled subset where experts have marked things like melodic phrase boundaries and similarity relationships, which other researchers can use as a trusted reference to check whether their automatic analysis methods are actually working correctly.
