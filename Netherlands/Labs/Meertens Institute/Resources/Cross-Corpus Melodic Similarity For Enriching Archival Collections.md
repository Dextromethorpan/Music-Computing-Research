Archives often hold many separate collections of old melodies (for example, different folk song archives from different countries) that were catalogued independently and are hard to search across. This paper presents methods for automatically measuring "melodic similarity" — how alike two melodies are — across different archival collections at once, so that a researcher searching one collection can also surface related melodies sitting in a completely different archive. The goal is to make historical musical archives more connected and searchable.
