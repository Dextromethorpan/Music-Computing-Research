A research institute of the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences (KNAW) in Amsterdam, focused on Dutch language and culture. Its music-related research centers on the "Dutch Song Database" (Nederlandse Liederenbank), a huge digitized archive of Dutch folk songs used for computational musicology and Music Information Retrieval research.
