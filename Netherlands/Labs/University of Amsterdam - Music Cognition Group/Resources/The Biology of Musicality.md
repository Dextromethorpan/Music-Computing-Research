"Musicality" is the human capacity to perceive, enjoy, and produce music — things like feeling a beat or recognizing a melody — as distinct from actual trained musical skill. This essay argues that decades of progress in psychology, neuroscience, and genetics now let researchers study where musicality comes from as a real, testable biological question rather than speculation. It focuses on capacities like sensing rhythm and melody that appear early in child development and asks how much of musicality is shared with other animals versus uniquely human.
