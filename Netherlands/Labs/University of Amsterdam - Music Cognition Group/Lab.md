Part of the Institute for Logic, Language and Computation (ILLC) and Amsterdam Brain and Cognition at the University of Amsterdam. The group studies "musicality" — the biological and cognitive capacities that let humans (and other animals) perceive and make music — combining psychology, neuroscience, and computational modeling.
