Henkjan Honing studies "musicality" — what makes humans (and possibly other animals) capable of perceiving and enjoying music — combining cognitive science, developmental psychology, and evolutionary biology. He has authored over 250 scientific publications and several books on music cognition, and received a Lifetime Achievement Award from the Society for Music Perception and Cognition in 2024.
