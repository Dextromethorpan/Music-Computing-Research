Cynthia Liem is an Associate Professor at TU Delft's Multimedia Computing Group. She trained both as a computer scientist and as a classical pianist (she holds performance degrees from the Royal Conservatoire in The Hague), and her research centers on Music Information Retrieval — techniques for analyzing and indexing music so it can be searched, compared and better discovered. She has also worked on broader questions of trustworthy and responsible AI, and has industry experience from Bell Labs Netherlands, Philips Research, and Google.
