A "cover song" is a different performance or recording of the same underlying song — think of a jazz cover of a pop hit. This is hard for a computer to recognize automatically because the melody may be transposed to a different key, the tempo can change, and the instruments and singer are different, yet the song is still musically "the same." This paper, presented at ISMIR 2009, compares several existing computer systems designed to automatically find cover versions of a song in a large audio collection, evaluating which approaches work best and why.
