"Transfer learning" is a machine-learning trick where a model trained on one task (say, recognizing speech) is reused, with some retraining, on a related but different task (say, recognizing musical instruments) — this saves time and data compared to training from scratch. This book chapter explains why transfer learning is especially useful for music: labeled music data (data where someone has manually tagged genre, mood, instrument, etc.) is scarce and expensive to produce, so being able to reuse models trained on more abundant data is valuable. It's written as a guide for researchers new to applying transfer learning in Music Information Retrieval.
