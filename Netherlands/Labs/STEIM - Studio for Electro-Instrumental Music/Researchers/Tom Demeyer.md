Tom Demeyer began developing real-time audiovisual software at STEIM in 1987, building tools like BigEye and Image/ine as well as sensor-based hardware interfaces such as Sensorlab, aimed at letting performers control sound and image live through gesture. He later became Head of Technology at Waag (a related Amsterdam creative-technology organization).
