Michel Waisvisz was a composer and instrument builder who directed STEIM for 27 years. He invented several influential electronic instruments, including the Cracklebox (a small circuit played by touch) and "The Hands" (worn, gesture-controlled MIDI controllers), and helped establish STEIM's hands-on, performer-first approach to building new electronic instruments. He passed away in 2008.
