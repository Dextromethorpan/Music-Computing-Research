STEIM, founded in Amsterdam in 1969, is a research and development center for new electronic musical instruments, focused on tools that let performers make direct, physical, expressive gestures with electronics in live performance rather than just studio composition. It has long been a key hub for the community behind NIME (New Interfaces for Musical Expression) research.
