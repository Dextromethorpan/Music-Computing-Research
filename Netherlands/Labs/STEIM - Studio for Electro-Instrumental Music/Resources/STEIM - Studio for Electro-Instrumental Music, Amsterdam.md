This article (published in ACM Interactions magazine) profiles STEIM, a center in Amsterdam where artists and engineers build brand-new electronic musical instruments together. Rather than aiming for technically impressive gadgets, STEIM's design philosophy prioritizes instruments that genuinely support expressive live performance — meaning a performer can control subtle, nuanced qualities of the sound in real time, the way a violinist controls tone with a bow. The piece traces STEIM's history and its influence on the wider field of building new musical interfaces.
