This is a historical research account of STEIM's founding in the countercultural atmosphere of late-1960s Amsterdam, subtitled "Ambiguities of Electro-Instrumental Music." It traces how the studio grew out of collectives of musicians and engineers building instruments by hand, including the Cracklebox — a small handmade electronic instrument played by touching exposed circuitry directly with your fingers, so the performer's own body becomes part of the electrical circuit that generates the sound.
