Part of the Royal Conservatoire in The Hague, the Institute of Sonology has a roughly 60-year history of education, research, and production in electroacoustic and computer music. It combines a historic voltage-controlled analogue studio with modern facilities including a Wave Field Synthesis system (a technique for precisely placing sound sources anywhere in a room using many loudspeakers).
