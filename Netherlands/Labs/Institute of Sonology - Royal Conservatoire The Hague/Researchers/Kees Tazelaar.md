Kees Tazelaar has led the Institute of Sonology since 2006. His own work focuses on composing electronic music for multichannel loudspeaker playback and on Wave Field Synthesis, a technique that uses arrays of many loudspeakers to place virtual sound sources precisely in physical space. He has also worked on historically reconstructing important early electronic music works.
