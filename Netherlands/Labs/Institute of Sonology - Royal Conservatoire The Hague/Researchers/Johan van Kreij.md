Johan van Kreij is a performer, composer and instrument builder who graduated from the Institute of Sonology and has taught there since 2001. He designs both the physical (sensor-based) and software parts of his own electronic instruments, and his recent research explores decentralized, networked improvisation as well as artistic studies of the works of composer Dick Raaijmakers.
