Before digital computers, composers made electronic music using racks of physical hardware — oscillators, filters, and tape machines wired together by hand ("voltage-controlled" analogue synthesis). In this recorded talk, Kees Tazelaar, head of the Institute of Sonology, presents the institute's historic analogue electronic music studio, which is one of the most complete of its kind still in operation, and explains its role in both teaching and ongoing research into electronic music history.
