Johan van Kreij builds his own electronic musical instruments from scratch — both the physical, touchable part (sensors that respond to a performer's gestures) and the software that turns those gestures into sound using synthesis models (mathematical recipes for generating sound electronically). This text describes his approach to organizing and structuring the underlying information (the data that controls the sound) so that improvised, real-time electronic performances stay musically coherent rather than becoming random noise.
