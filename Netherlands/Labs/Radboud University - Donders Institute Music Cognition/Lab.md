Part of the Donders Institute for Brain, Cognition and Behaviour at Radboud University Nijmegen (with close ties to the University of Amsterdam). Researchers here study how people perceive and produce musical rhythm, often using computational models (including Bayesian statistics — a mathematical way of reasoning about uncertainty) and cross-cultural comparisons.
