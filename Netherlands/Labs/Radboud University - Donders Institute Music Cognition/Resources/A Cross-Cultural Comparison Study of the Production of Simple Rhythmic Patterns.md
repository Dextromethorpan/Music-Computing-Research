Does the culture you grew up in change how you physically play a simple rhythm? This study compared Dutch and Japanese musicians as they performed the same short rhythmic patterns, measuring subtle differences in timing. The results showed measurable, systematic differences between the two groups, suggesting that even something as basic as tapping a two-note rhythm is shaped by cultural exposure to particular musical traditions, not just universal biology.
