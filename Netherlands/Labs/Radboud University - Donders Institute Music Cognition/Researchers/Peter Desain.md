Peter Desain is a professor at Radboud University Nijmegen whose research combines artificial intelligence with music cognition. He builds computational models of how people perceive and produce musical rhythm, and has also worked on brain-computer interfaces and using EEG (brain-signal recordings) to study how the brain processes music.
