Part of the Department of Information and Computing Sciences at Utrecht University. The group studies music computationally — building software models of melody, rhythm and musical structure to answer questions in musicology, music therapy, and music technology, and is closely tied to the ISMIR (International Society for Music Information Retrieval) research community.
