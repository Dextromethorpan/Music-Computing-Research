Anja Volk has a background in both mathematics and musicology and leads research on Music Information Retrieval (MIR) — building software that can analyze, search, and compare musical data. She is Editor-in-Chief of TISMIR, the main academic journal of the ISMIR research community, and co-founded the Women in MIR (WIMIR) mentoring program. Her work spans mathematical models of music theory, computational musicology, and applying music technology to health and wellbeing (e.g. music therapy).
