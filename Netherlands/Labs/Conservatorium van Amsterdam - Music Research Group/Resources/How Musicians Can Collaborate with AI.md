An interview-style article from the Amsterdam University of the Arts' research center discussing the "Live Performing with AI" project, in which researchers explore how AI software can join a live musical performance alongside acoustic and electronic instruments. It discusses practical questions such as how a musician can meaningfully respond to and shape what an AI system generates in real time, rather than just triggering a pre-made backing track.
