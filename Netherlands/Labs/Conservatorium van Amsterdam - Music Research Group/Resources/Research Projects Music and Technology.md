This is the Conservatorium van Amsterdam's overview page for its "Music & Technology" research theme, which looks at how new technology — especially artificial intelligence — changes what's possible in musical performance and composition. The page lists active projects exploring how musicians can use AI creatively without losing their own artistic voice, and raises questions about authorship and collaboration when part of the "performer" is a computer system.
