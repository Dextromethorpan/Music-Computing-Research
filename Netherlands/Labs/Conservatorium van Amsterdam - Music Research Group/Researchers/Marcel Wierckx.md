Marcel Wierckx teaches composition and software design (at HKU since 2002) and live electronic music (at the Conservatorium van Amsterdam since 2013). His research, including the "Live Performing with AI" project, focuses on how AI systems can take part in live musical performance alongside human musicians playing acoustic and electronic instruments.
