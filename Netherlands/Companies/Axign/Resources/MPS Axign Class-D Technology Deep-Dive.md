This is a technical webinar deck from Monolithic Power Systems (MPS), the company that acquired Axign, explaining Axign's Class-D audio amplifier technology in detail after the acquisition. It covers how Axign's digital feedback design (checking and correcting the actual output signal, not just the input) suppresses distortion, and how the technology is being combined with newer power-transistor materials (gallium nitride, or GaN) to build smaller, more efficient audio amplifiers for consumer and automotive products.
