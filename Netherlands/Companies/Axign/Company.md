Axign is a fabless (design-only, no factory) semiconductor company from Enschede, founded in 2014 by former Philips and NXP engineers who had earlier pioneered "Class-D" digital amplifier technology. It designs audio chips — small processors and amplifier controllers — used inside consumer and automotive audio products, and was acquired by US chipmaker Monolithic Power Systems (MPS) in 2024.
