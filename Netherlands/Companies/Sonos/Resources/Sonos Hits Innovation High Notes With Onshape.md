This case study describes how Sonos's engineering teams design hardware, and mentions that Sonos's transducer team — the engineers who design the physical component of a speaker that turns electrical signals into sound waves — is based in the Netherlands. That team traces back to Sonos's 2022 acquisition of the Dutch startup Mayht, which had developed a way to shrink transducers to roughly a quarter or fifth of their usual size while keeping full sound quality, useful for making smaller, more portable speakers.
