Sonos is a US-based wireless speaker company that gained a hardware research team in the Netherlands through its 2022 acquisition of Mayht Holding BV, a Dutch startup that developed a way to make audio transducers (the parts of a speaker that turn electrical signal into physical sound) dramatically smaller without losing sound quality. That team continues to work on next-generation transducer technology from the Netherlands.
