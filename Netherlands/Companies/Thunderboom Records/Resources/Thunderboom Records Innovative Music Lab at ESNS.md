This article covers how Thunderboom Records tested new AI, augmented reality (AR — digital graphics overlaid on the real world), and digital avatar technologies at Eurosonic Noorderslag (ESNS), a major European music showcase festival. It describes Thunderboom's approach of prototyping new music-technology tools and then testing them directly with real audiences and musicians at festivals, to learn what actually works before wider release.
