Thunderboom Records is an Amsterdam-based independent innovation lab working at the intersection of music and emerging technology such as AI, augmented reality, and digital avatars. It builds open-source tools and prototypes and tests them with musicians and audiences, aiming to make emerging music technology accessible and fair for the Dutch music industry.
