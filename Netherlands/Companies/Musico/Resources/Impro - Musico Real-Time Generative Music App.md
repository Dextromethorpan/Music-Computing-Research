Impro is an app built on Musico's music-generation engine that lets a performer control an "automatic composer" using intuitive gestures — for example, moving a hand or device to shape how the generated music develops in real time. It's a demo of how "generative music" (music produced algorithmically rather than fully hand-composed) can respond live to a human performer instead of just playing back a fixed track.
