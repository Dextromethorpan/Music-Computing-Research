Philips Research, based in Eindhoven, is the research arm of the Dutch electronics company Philips. It has a long history of fundamental audio technology research, most famously co-developing the Compact Disc (CD) with Sony in the late 1970s and early 1980s, and later contributing to formats like DCC and DSD, along with decades of work on loudspeaker and electroacoustic design.
