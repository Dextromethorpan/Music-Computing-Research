Ronald M. Aarts is a Dutch electrical engineer and physicist who worked at Philips Research Laboratories in Eindhoven on electroacoustics — the physics and engineering of converting electrical signals into sound and back, including loudspeaker and microphone design. He is also a professor working on signal processing for audio.
