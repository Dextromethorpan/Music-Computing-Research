Kees Schouhamer Immink is a Dutch electrical engineer who worked on the Philips/Sony team that developed the Compact Disc standard. He invented the Eight-to-Fourteen Modulation (EFM) coding scheme used to store digital audio reliably on a CD, and holds more than 1,100 patents related to digital audio, video, and data recording.
