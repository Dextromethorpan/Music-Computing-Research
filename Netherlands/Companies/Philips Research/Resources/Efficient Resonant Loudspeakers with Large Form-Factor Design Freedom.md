Normally, making a loudspeaker smaller or a different shape tends to make it sound worse, because the size and shape of a speaker's moving parts are closely tied to how efficiently it can produce sound and bass. This Audio Engineering Society (AES) paper presents an approach for designing "resonant" loudspeakers (ones that use a tuned physical resonance to boost output) that can be built in more flexible shapes and sizes without a big loss of efficiency — useful for products like slim TVs or unusually-shaped devices where a normal round speaker driver doesn't fit well.
