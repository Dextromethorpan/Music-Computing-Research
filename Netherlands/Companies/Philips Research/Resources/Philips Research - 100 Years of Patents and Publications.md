This document is a retrospective overview of a century of research and inventions from Philips, based in Eindhoven, including its audio work. It covers how Philips Research grew from an in-house engineering lab into a source of technologies used worldwide, and touches on landmark audio inventions such as the Compact Cassette and the Compact Disc, showing how patents (legal protection for an invention) and published research papers worked together to turn lab ideas into products used by billions of people.
