Audoo is a London-based music technology company, founded by Ryan Edwards in 2018, that builds the Audoo Audio Meter™ — a small plug-in hardware device installed in shops, gyms, bars and other public venues that automatically recognises the music being played, so that royalty organisations can pay artists and songwriters based on real, measured plays instead of estimates.
