This is an interview between Audoo founder Ryan Edwards and patent attorney firm GJE (Gill Jennings & Every), published as part of GJE's "Startup Stories" series. Edwards explains that Audoo was built to fix a real problem: royalties for music played in public places (shops, gyms, restaurants) used to be estimated from sample data rather than measured directly. He describes the Audoo Audio Meter™ — a small plug-in device, developed entirely in-house — as "multi-patented," and discusses how filing patents and trademarks (with GJE's help) was important for proving the company's technology to investors like Sir Elton John and Björn Ulvaeus. The piece is light on deep engineering detail but confirms real, verifiable facts: the device is GDPR-compliant, no audio is stored or transmitted from the device itself, and by the end of 2024 Audoo was deployed in 15 countries.
