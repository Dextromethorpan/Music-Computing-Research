In August 2023, the UK's two main music royalty collection societies — PRS for Music (which pays songwriters, composers and publishers) and PPL (which pays performers and record labels) — jointly announced they were installing Audoo Audio Meters™ in cafés, bars, hair salons, restaurants and shops across Great Britain. The devices identify the background music playing in each venue and report that usage data back to PRS and PPL so payments to their combined 300,000+ members can be based on what was actually played, "in quasi real-time," rather than on estimates. The release states the meters are GDPR-compliant and capture only music-identification data, not spoken conversation or other non-music sound. This partnership is a real, named deployment of Audoo's recognition technology at national scale, giving independent validation that the system works well enough for two major rights organisations to rely on it operationally.
