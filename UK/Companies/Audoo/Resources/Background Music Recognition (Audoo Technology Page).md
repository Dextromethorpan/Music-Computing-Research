This is Audoo's own technical overview of how the Audoo Audio Meter™ works. The device is plugged into a normal power socket in a venue (shop, café, gym, etc.), where it listens to whatever music is playing and turns short snippets into a compact digital "fingerprint" — a unique numerical summary of the sound, not a recording of it — which it matches against a reference library the company says now holds over 200 million tracks. The page states the device has built-in cellular connectivity (so it needs no Wi-Fi setup), never stores or transmits actual audio (only fingerprint/match data), and is aimed at performing right organisations (PROs), music publishers, and venues who need accurate, real-world data on what music is actually being played, as opposed to estimates.
