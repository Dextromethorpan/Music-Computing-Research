Oliver James Clay is named as the sole inventor on Audoo's granted "Audio Interface" patent (US12401336), which covers the circuitry that automatically adjusts incoming audio signal levels inside the Audio Meter hardware, and as co-inventor (with CTO Stephen Robbins) on Audoo's "Media Identification System" patent (US12524462), which covers the logic for extracting and classifying audio clips before sending them for music matching. His patent record indicates he works on the hardware/signal-processing side of the Audio Meter device.
