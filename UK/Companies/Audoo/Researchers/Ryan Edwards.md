Ryan Edwards is a former musician (drummer in the band The Lines, who had a UK top-ten single) and FinTech executive (Visa, Vodafone, Bink) who founded Audoo in 2018 after discovering he wasn't being paid for his own music being played in a London department store. He leads the company's overall strategy, has driven its expansion to 15+ countries, and has attracted investment from figures including Sir Elton John and Björn Ulvaeus. His public interviews (e.g. with GJE) focus on how Audoo built and protected its music-recognition hardware technology and IP as it scaled.
