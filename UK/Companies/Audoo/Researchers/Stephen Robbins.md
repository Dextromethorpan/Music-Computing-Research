Stephen (Steve) Robbins joined Audoo as CTO in 2021, having previously been Head of Software at the loudspeaker company Bowers & Wilkins, where he built out the software development team. At Audoo he has led the design and implementation of the company's cloud platform and is a named co-inventor (as Stephen John Robbins) on Audoo's granted US patent for its media identification system, which decides whether a captured audio clip actually contains music before sending it off for song matching.
