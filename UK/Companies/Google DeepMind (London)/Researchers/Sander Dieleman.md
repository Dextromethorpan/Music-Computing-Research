Sander Dieleman co-created WaveNet at DeepMind and has continued to work on generative models for audio and images, including diffusion models (the technique behind much of today's AI image and audio generation). He is known both for his research papers and for widely-read technical blog writing that explains generative modelling ideas to a broader audience.
