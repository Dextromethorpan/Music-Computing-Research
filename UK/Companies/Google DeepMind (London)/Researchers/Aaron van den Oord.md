Aäron van den Oord led the creation of WaveNet at DeepMind in London, the neural network that generates raw audio sample-by-sample and dramatically improved the naturalness of AI-generated speech, while also showing the same approach could generate music. His broader research at DeepMind has focused on generative modelling — building AI systems that create realistic new data (images, audio, and beyond) rather than just classifying existing data.
