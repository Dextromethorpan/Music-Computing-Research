Google DeepMind is an AI research lab headquartered in London (with an earlier history as the independent company DeepMind, founded in London in 2010 and acquired by Google in 2014). Its London researchers created WaveNet, a landmark neural network for generating raw audio that reshaped both speech synthesis and AI music generation, and DeepMind now builds Lyria, Google's family of AI music-generation models.
