DeepMind's own announcement of WaveNet, written for a general audience, explains the idea with audio samples you can listen to directly: side-by-side comparisons of WaveNet-generated speech against older "parametric" and "concatenative" (recordings stitched together) text-to-speech systems, in both English and Mandarin. It also includes eerie "babbling" audio clips of WaveNet generating speech-like sounds with no real words when it isn't told what to say, and short AI-generated piano music clips, making it a good beginner-friendly entry point before reading the full technical paper.
