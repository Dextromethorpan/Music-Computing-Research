Lyria is Google DeepMind's music-generation AI: type a description of the music you want (genre, instruments, tempo, vocal style, even an uploaded image) and it produces a finished, high-fidelity track up to a few minutes long, including sung lyrics in multiple languages. It descends conceptually from earlier DeepMind audio work like WaveNet, but works at a much larger scale, aiming for full songs rather than short raw-audio samples. Every track Lyria produces is invisibly watermarked using DeepMind's SynthID technology, so AI-generated music can later be identified even after edits or compression — an attempt to keep AI music transparent and traceable as it becomes widespread.
