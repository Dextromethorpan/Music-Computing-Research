Richard Furse founded Blue Ripple Sound in 2008 to research and commercialize Higher Order Ambisonics, a mathematical approach to 3D sound. He holds a mathematics degree from St John's College, Oxford, has presented on Ambisonics and OpenAL at Audio Engineering Society (AES) conferences, and is the company's lead technical figure behind its Rapture3D, O3A, and O7A spatial-audio products used in games, VR, and professional studios.
