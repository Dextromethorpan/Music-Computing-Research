Blue Ripple Sound is a small London-based company, founded in 2008, that specializes in Higher Order Ambisonics (a mathematical way of recording and reproducing 3D sound as a "soundfield" rather than as fixed speaker channels). It builds professional plugins and SDKs (Rapture3D, the O3A and O7A product lines) used in games, VR, and studio spatial-audio production, and its tools are used to convert ambisonic mixes into formats like Dolby Atmos.
