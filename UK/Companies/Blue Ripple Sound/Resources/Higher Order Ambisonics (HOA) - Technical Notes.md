This is Blue Ripple Sound's own plain-English explainer of Higher Order Ambisonics (HOA), the core technology behind all of their products. Ambisonics records or creates a full 3D "soundfield" around a point, rather than recording separate channels for each speaker (like stereo or 5.1 do). "Higher order" means using more mathematical detail to describe that soundfield, which gives sharper, more precise 3D positioning at the cost of needing more audio channels and more computing power. The page is part of a larger set of technical notes covering related ideas like B-Format (the audio format Ambisonics uses), decoding (turning a soundfield into real speaker feeds), and binaural rendering for headphones.
