O7A is Blue Ripple Sound's most advanced ambisonic plugin range, supporting "seventh-order" Ambisonics: a very high level of spatial detail using 64 audio channels internally (compared to 16 for their standard O3A products). Because it is so demanding on a computer's processing power, Blue Ripple positions O7A specifically as a research tool, aimed at studios, universities, and labs pushing the limits of spatial-audio accuracy rather than everyday music production. It reflects the company's ongoing, self-funded R&D into how far Ambisonics can be pushed for extremely precise 3D sound imaging.
