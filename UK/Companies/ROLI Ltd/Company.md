ROLI Ltd is a London-based music technology company, best known for inventing the Seaboard family of "multidimensional" MPE (MIDI Polyphonic Expression) keyboards, owning and publishing the widely-used open-source JUCE C++ audio framework, and co-authoring the official MPE specification with the MIDI Manufacturers Association. ROLI went through financial administration in 2021-2022 and its assets/team were carried forward into a new company, Luminary Ltd, which has continued to ship ROLI-branded instruments such as the Seaboard RISE 2.
