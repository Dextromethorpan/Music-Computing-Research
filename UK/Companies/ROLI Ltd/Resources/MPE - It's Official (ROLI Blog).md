This ROLI blog post tells the behind-the-scenes story of how the MPE (MIDI Polyphonic Expression) standard came to be: a 2015 poolside conversation at the NAMM music trade show between ROLI founder Roland Lamb, Roger Linn (inventor of the Linnstrument controller), and other instrument makers, who realized their new touch-sensitive instruments needed a shared technical rulebook to work with the same software. It explains, in plain language, why a shared standard matters (so a "multidimensional" controller like the Seaboard works with many different synth programs, not just ROLI's own) and quotes ROLI's Dr Ben Supper, who led the drafting of the specification, on what its official 2018 approval unlocked for musicians and developers.
