Reuben Thomas has used JUCE since 2013, building a room-acoustics simulator during his MA at the University of Huddersfield, audio analysis tools at IRCAM, and consumer music software at ROLI. In early 2020 he joined the JUCE team as a full-time maintainer, later becoming its lead engineer, contributing features such as CMake build support (JUCE 6) and LV2 plug-in format support (JUCE 7) — infrastructure that keeps JUCE, the framework ROLI owns, usable across modern build systems and plug-in standards.
