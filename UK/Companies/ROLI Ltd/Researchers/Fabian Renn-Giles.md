Fabian Renn-Giles worked as a staff engineer at ROLI, where he became the lead maintainer and developer of the JUCE C++ framework, guiding its evolution as it grew into a framework used by thousands of commercial audio companies. Before ROLI, he completed a PhD at Imperial College London building a numerical quantum optics solver using C++ and modern digital signal processing techniques. He is a regular speaker at the Audio Developer Conference (ADC) and has since become a freelance consultant specializing in low-level real-time C++, embedded audio, and audio plug-in formats.
