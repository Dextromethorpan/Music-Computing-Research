Ben Supper, who holds a PhD, was ROLI's Head of Research and later Head of R&D, responsible for the sensor and firmware design behind the Seaboard's touch-sensing electronics and for shipping ROLI's first hardware products. He also served as ROLI's Technical Programme Manager and chaired the industry-wide MPE Working Group at the MIDI Manufacturers Association, personally leading the drafting of the official MIDI Polyphonic Expression (MPE) specification adopted in 2018. Since 2018 he has worked as a freelance audio/electronics developer and consultant, helping other companies build music technology products.
