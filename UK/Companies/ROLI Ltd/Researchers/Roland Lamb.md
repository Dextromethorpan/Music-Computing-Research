Roland Lamb founded ROLI in 2009, growing the company out of his PhD research in Design Products at London's Royal College of Art. As a jazz pianist, he wanted a keyboard that could produce the pitch-bending and timbral effects normally only possible on bowed string or brass instruments, which led to the invention of the Seaboard's soft, continuous touch surface. He holds several of ROLI's foundational hardware patents (including the core sensor-surface patent) and was one of the founding members of the MPE Working Group that created the MIDI Polyphonic Expression standard. After ROLI's 2021 administration, he co-founded and continues to lead the successor company, Luminary.
