Julian ("Jules") Storer is the original single-handed creator of the JUCE C++ audio framework, built through his company Raw Materials Software starting in the early-to-mid 2000s. When ROLI acquired JUCE in 2014, Storer joined ROLI as Head of Software Architecture and continued to act as JUCE's lead architect and "editor-in-chief," steering the framework's development and its use inside ROLI's own products. He has also created other audio-developer tools such as the Tracktion DAW and the Cmajor audio programming language.
