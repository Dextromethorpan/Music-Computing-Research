AI Music Ltd was a London startup (founded 2016, emerging from the Abbey Road Red incubator) that built the "Infinite Music Engine" — technology that reshapes existing, prerecorded music in real time to match a listener's context, such as tempo, heart rate, or activity (e.g. changing a track's genre or intensity as a workout speeds up). Apple acquired the company in February 2022.
