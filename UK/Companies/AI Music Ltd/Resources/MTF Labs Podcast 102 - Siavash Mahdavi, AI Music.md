This is a podcast interview (also posted as a video on YouTube) in which AI Music CEO Siavash Mahdavi talks through his background — studying mechatronics, a master's in artificial intelligence, and a PhD in evolutionary robotics — and how he combined that with a lifelong interest in music (he still takes piano lessons) to found AI Music in 2017 out of the Abbey Road Studios Red incubator, run jointly with Universal Music Group. He describes the company's mission as using AI to "infinitely scale and adapt human creativity," delivering a tailored musical track for any use case rather than replacing musicians. It's a useful beginner-friendly overview of the founder's motivation and the company's origin story, in his own words, from a respected music-technology conference community (Music Tech Fest).
