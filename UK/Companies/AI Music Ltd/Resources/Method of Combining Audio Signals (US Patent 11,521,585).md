This is AI Music Ltd's granted US patent (filed February 2019, granted December 2022) for a method that automatically extends a piece of music. The system takes a "source audio signal" (an existing song or track), analyzes it to identify musical characteristics like tempo and key (a "musical parameter"), then finds a "supplemental audio signal" — another piece of pre-recorded or generated audio — that matches those characteristics, and combines the two into a longer, seamless "extended audio signal." In plain terms: it is the underlying patented technique behind the Infinite Music Engine's ability to stretch, extend, or reshape a track on the fly rather than generating music from nothing. The three inventors listed are the company's founders (Siavash Mahdavi, Andy Khavand) and engineer David Ronan, and the patent is assigned directly to AI Music Limited.
