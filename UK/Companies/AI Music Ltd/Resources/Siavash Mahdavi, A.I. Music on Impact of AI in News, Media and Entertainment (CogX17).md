A recorded conference talk/highlight from CogX (a London AI conference) in which Siavash Mahdavi discusses how artificial intelligence is changing news, media, and entertainment, with AI Music as his working example. Rather than diving into deep technical detail, this is a good beginner-facing companion piece to the patent and podcast — it shows how the founder pitched the idea of "adaptive" music to a general tech audience early in the company's life (the same year AI Music launched publicly), framing music personalization as part of a broader wave of AI reshaping creative industries.
