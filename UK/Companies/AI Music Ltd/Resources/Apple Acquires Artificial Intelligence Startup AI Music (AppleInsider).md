A press report confirming Apple's acquisition of the London-based AI Music (deal completed in "the last few weeks" before the 7 February 2022 report, per Bloomberg), noting the company's social media and website had already been taken offline by early January 2022 — a common sign a quiet acquisition has happened. The article speculates Apple could use AI Music's adaptive-music technology to enhance existing products like Apple Music, HomePod mini, or Apple Fitness+ (where a workout soundtrack could shift automatically with exercise intensity or heart rate). It also notes AI Music had previously worked with the BBC and Wired, giving a sense of the company's commercial reach before acquisition.
