A company blog interview with co-founder Andy Khavand (Head of Music) that gives rare insight into how AI Music actually produced its music: human producers first build musical "blocks" (short segments, chord progressions, instrument recordings) with an understanding of how the AI engine will later combine and vary them, and the music team works with the research/engineering team to figure out how to automate those combinations at scale for a given genre. He describes the "Lofi House Project" as a landmark case where this process let the company generate what he calls an "almost infinite" amount of usable lo-fi house music from a limited set of human-made source material — a concrete example of the human-plus-AI production pipeline behind the Infinite Music Engine, rather than pure music generation from scratch.
