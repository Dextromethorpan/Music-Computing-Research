Music-industry trade site Music Ally reported Apple's February 2022 acquisition of AI Music and, importantly, included a direct quote from CEO Siavash Mahdavi dating back to 2017 that explains the company's core idea in his own words: "we're not generating music from scratch... we're looking at using AI to shift the way in which music is consumed" — for example, a song could play as an acoustic version in the morning and a "deep-house or drum'n'bass version" on the way to the gym, with "the entire genre" or musical key changing automatically. The article traces AI Music's product history: a £5m funding round, a consumer remix app called Ossia, "Sympaphonic Ads" (AI-matched music for audio adverts), a partnership with social network Hornet for adaptive post soundtracks, and a spin-off called VentureSonic for brand music. It also notes AI Music's technology had proven a "tough sell" to music rightsholders — useful context for a beginner on why adaptive-music startups struggle commercially even when the tech works.
