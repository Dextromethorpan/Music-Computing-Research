Andy Khavand (full name Andrew Shayan Khavand) co-founded AI Music alongside Siavash Mahdavi. As a classically trained musician and drum and bass producer, he led the company's music production team, building the human-made musical "building blocks" — chord progressions, instrument recordings, genre-specific segments — that the company's AI engine then learned to recombine and vary automatically for a given mood or context. He is also a named co-inventor on AI Music's US patent for automatically combining audio signals.
