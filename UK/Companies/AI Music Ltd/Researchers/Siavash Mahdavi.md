Siavash Mahdavi co-founded AI Music in 2016/2017 and led it as CEO through to its acquisition by Apple in 2022. His background combines engineering and AI (he studied mechatronics, holds a master's in artificial intelligence, and a PhD in evolutionary robotics) with a lifelong interest in music. He is listed as lead inventor on AI Music's US patent for automatically combining and extending audio signals, and has spoken publicly (podcasts, conference talks) about using AI to adapt existing music to a listener's context — such as tempo, mood, or activity — rather than generating music from nothing.
