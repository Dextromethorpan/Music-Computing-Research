Spotify opened a dedicated tech/R&D hub in London in late 2018, and it has since grown into a major site for Spotify's "Tech Research" personalization organisation — the group that studies and builds the algorithms behind music and podcast search, recommendation, and discovery. This note covers only the London-attributable slice of Spotify's research output, not the company's global research programme.
