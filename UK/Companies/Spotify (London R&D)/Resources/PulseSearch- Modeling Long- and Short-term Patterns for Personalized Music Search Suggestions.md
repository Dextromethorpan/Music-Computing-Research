PulseSearch is a system Spotify built to suggest search queries while you type into the music search box (like autocomplete, but smarter). Instead of only looking at what you just typed, it also uses a "listener profile" — a summary of your longer-term music taste — plus signals about the time of day, so the suggestions feel more personally relevant. It uses "GenAI" (generative AI, meaning AI models that can generate new text rather than just pick from a fixed list) to produce these suggestions. The team tested it both with real users (an "online" test) and with historical data (an "offline" test), and found it beat standard retrieval methods on personalization, variety, and freshness of suggestions. Co-authored by Mounia Lalmas, Spotify's London-based Head of Tech Research for Personalization, and presented at the ACM CHIIR conference (Conference on Human Information Interaction and Retrieval).
