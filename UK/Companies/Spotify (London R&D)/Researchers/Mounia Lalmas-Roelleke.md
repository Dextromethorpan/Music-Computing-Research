Mounia Lalmas-Roelleke leads Spotify's Tech Research organisation from the company's London office, overseeing roughly two dozen research scientists who work on the algorithms behind personalization: matching listeners with music and podcasts they'll like while also encouraging them to discover new content. Her group's research spans search, recommendation, user engagement measurement, and evaluation methods (including using AI models to judge recommendation quality). She also holds an honorary professorship at University College London and was previously Professor of Information Retrieval at Queen Mary University of London, and has chaired major research conferences including SIGIR, WWW, WSDM, and CIKM.
