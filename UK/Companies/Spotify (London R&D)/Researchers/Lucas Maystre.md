Lucas Maystre is a Senior Research Scientist who splits his time between Spotify's London office and remote work from his home in Belsize Park, London. He works across Spotify's Personalization teams on machine learning, modelling, and engineering problems, and has spent time on projects that try to measure and improve listeners' long-term satisfaction with the service — building recommendation approaches explicitly aimed at keeping users happy over the long run rather than just in a single session. He also regularly hosts student interns and PhD collaborations (including with University College London) exploring research questions adjacent to Spotify's product needs.
