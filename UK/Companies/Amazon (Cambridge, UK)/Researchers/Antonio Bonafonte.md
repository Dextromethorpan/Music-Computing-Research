Antonio Bonafonte is a senior applied scientist on Amazon's Text-to-Speech team, working on transferring qualities like prosody (the rhythm and melody of speech), accent, and speaker identity between synthetic voices. He summarized several of the team's Interspeech 2022 papers on Amazon's science blog, covering topics from expressive prosody to building new voices with limited training data.
