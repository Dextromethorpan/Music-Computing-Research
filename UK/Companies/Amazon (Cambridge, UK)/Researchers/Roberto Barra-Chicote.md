Roberto Barra-Chicote is a Principal Applied Scientist on Amazon's Text-to-Speech team, with a long publication record spanning text-to-speech, voice conversion, prosody and accent modelling, and speech-privacy techniques. His research covers both making synthetic voices (like Alexa's) sound more natural and expressive, and creating entirely new voices without needing recordings from a real target speaker.
