"Prosody" is the melody and rhythm of speech — the rise and fall in pitch, the pacing, the emphasis on certain words — and it's a big part of what makes synthetic voices sound natural or robotic. CopyCat2 is a model that learns to represent prosody word-by-word from real recorded speech, in a way that isn't tied to any one specific speaker, so that expressive "delivery style" can be transferred between different voices. Predicting these prosody patterns directly from text (rather than needing a reference recording) lets a single text-to-speech model sound noticeably more natural and appropriate to what it's actually saying.
