This Amazon Science blog post rounds up more than a dozen research papers the Amazon Text-to-Speech (the team that gives Alexa its voice) presented at Interspeech 2022, a major speech-technology conference. It covers work on making Alexa's speech sound more naturally expressive (matching tone to context, like sounding more upbeat for a fun fact versus neutral for a plain answer), reducing how much recorded speech is needed to build a new expressive voice, extending a single voice across multiple languages while keeping a consistent "personality," and creating entirely new synthetic voices the system was never directly trained on. It's a good beginner-friendly overview of how much research goes into making a voice assistant sound human.
