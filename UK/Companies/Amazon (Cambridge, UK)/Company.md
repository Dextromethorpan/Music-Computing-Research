Amazon operates a major research site in Cambridge, UK, which grew out of its 2012 acquisition of the Cambridge-based voice-assistant startup Evi Technologies and has since become a hub for Amazon's Alexa AI and Text-to-Speech (TTS) research organisation. This entry covers published research from Amazon's Alexa speech-synthesis and voice-technology teams, which include Cambridge-based scientists and regularly publish at speech and audio conferences such as Interspeech.
