Focusrite Novation plc is a UK audio hardware group (brands include Focusrite, Novation, Ampify Music, Martin Audio, and others) making audio interfaces, synthesizers, and music-making hardware and software. Its engineering teams are active in the DSP/plugin developer community, publishing open-source workshop materials and talks at conferences like the Audio Developer Conference (ADC).
