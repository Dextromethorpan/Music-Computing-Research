This is the open-source teaching material from a hands-on workshop Focusrite engineers ran at the Audio Developer Conference (ADC) 2025 in Bristol, walking attendees through the process of turning an academic digital signal processing (DSP) research paper into a working audio plugin. It includes Python notebooks (for prototyping and testing the underlying algorithm) alongside a JUCE-based C++ project (JUCE being the industry-standard framework for building real audio plugins). It's a genuinely useful, concrete example of how commercial audio companies translate published DSP research into shipped products.
