Philip Inghelbrecht co-founded Shazam in London in December 1999 with Chris Barton and Dhiraj Mukherjee and was involved in all aspects of the company's early operations, from fundraising to product. Like Barton, his contribution was on the business/founding side rather than the technical algorithm itself. After Shazam he went on to be an early Google/YouTube partnerships lead, president of TrueCar, and later founder and CEO of the TV-advertising platform Tatari.
