Avery Wang is the digital signal processing specialist who invented the audio fingerprinting algorithm that powers Shazam. He holds a PhD in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University and was hired by Shazam's business co-founders (Chris Barton, Philip Inghelbrecht, Dhiraj Mukherjee) to solve the technical problem of recognizing a song from a short, noisy recording — a problem many experts at the time considered essentially impossible. He published the field-defining paper on this method in 2003, served as Shazam's Chief Scientist for about 18 years, holds over 150 patents, and continued the work at Apple as Principal Research Scientist after the 2018 acquisition.
