Dhiraj Mukherjee was one of Shazam's three business co-founders, joining Chris Barton and Philip Inghelbrecht in London in 1999-2000. Before Shazam he worked at Viant, a London-based internet consulting firm. Like his co-founders, his role was building and growing the company (fundraising, partnerships, strategy) rather than developing the underlying audio recognition algorithm, which was Avery Wang's contribution.
