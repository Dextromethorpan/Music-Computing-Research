Chris Barton conceived the original idea for Shazam in 1999 (a service to identify what song is playing from a short recording) and co-founded the company in London in 2000 with Philip Inghelbrecht and Dhiraj Mukherjee, later bringing in Avery Wang to build the core recognition algorithm. As Shazam's first CEO he raised the company's early funding and secured distribution deals with UK mobile operators that let Shazam commercially launch in 2002. He is not a technical researcher himself but was central to turning Wang's algorithm into a real product and business; he later worked at Google and Dropbox on mobile partnerships.
