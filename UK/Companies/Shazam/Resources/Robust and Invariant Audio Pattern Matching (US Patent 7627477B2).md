This patent extends Shazam's core fingerprinting method to make song matching "fast, robust, invariant, and scalable" over huge audio libraries. "Invariant" here means the matching still works even if the recording is shifted in time, sped up or slowed down slightly, or distorted by background noise — the fingerprint stays recognizable. "Scalable" means the indexing and search techniques described can look up a match quickly even as the song database grows to millions of tracks. Ownership of this patent moved from Shazam Investments Limited to Apple Inc. in 2020, reflecting Apple's 2018 acquisition of Shazam and its continued use of this technology inside products like Shazam and Siri's music recognition.
