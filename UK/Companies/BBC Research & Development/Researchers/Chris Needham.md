Chris Needham is a BBC R&D engineer associated with the team behind audiowaveform and peaks.js, the BBC's widely-used open-source tools for generating and displaying audio waveforms. His work sits at the practical end of audio computing: building reliable, reusable infrastructure that other audio tools and interfaces (inside and outside the BBC) are built on top of.
