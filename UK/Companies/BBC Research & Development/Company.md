BBC Research & Development (BBC R&D) is the BBC's in-house technology research division, working on broadcast, internet, and audio/video technology — including object-based and spatial audio, audio waveform tools, and other technology used across BBC production and distribution. Much of its practical audio tooling is released as open-source software on GitHub.
