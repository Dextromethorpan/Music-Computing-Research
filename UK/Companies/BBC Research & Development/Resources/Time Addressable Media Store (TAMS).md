TAMS is an open-source system design and API from BBC R&D for storing and retrieving audio and video content by precise points in time, rather than as whole fixed files. This "time addressable" approach is aimed at modern, cloud-based television and radio production, where different systems need to grab, edit, or stream small time-based chunks of a live or recorded broadcast (including its audio) without waiting for an entire file to be ready. It reflects BBC R&D's broader, ongoing work on the underlying media infrastructure that professional audio and video production increasingly depends on.
