Maciek (Maciej) Tomczak was a PhD researcher in the DMT Lab's SOMA group, working on automated music transformations using deep learning — most notably neural drum synthesis, where a system learns to generate and rhythmically manipulate percussion sounds rather than just recognise existing ones. He co-authored the lab's papers on generative adversarial network (GAN) based drum sound synthesis, including work on style-based synthesis and GAN inversion (recovering the internal settings that would produce a given real sound). He has since moved into industry as a data scientist.
