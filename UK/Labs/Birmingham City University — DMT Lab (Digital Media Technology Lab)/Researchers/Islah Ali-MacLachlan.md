Professor Islah Ali-MacLachlan works across acoustics, audio engineering, and product design, with nearly three decades of academic and industry experience. Within the DMT Lab's SOMA group his research focuses on the computational analysis of traditional/folk music performance style — specifically automatically detecting and analysing the ornaments (fast decorative notes) that define individual playing style in Irish traditional flute music, the subject of his own PhD thesis.
