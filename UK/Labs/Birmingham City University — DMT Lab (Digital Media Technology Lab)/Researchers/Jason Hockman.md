Jason Hockman led the Sound and Music Analysis (SOMA) group within the DMT Lab, working at the intersection of audio signal processing, music information retrieval, and machine learning. His research centres on automatic drum transcription (teaching computers to identify which drum was hit and when, from audio alone) and, more recently, on using generative adversarial networks to synthesize and transform drum sounds. He is a co-author on the majority of the DMT Lab's music-computing publications gathered here.
