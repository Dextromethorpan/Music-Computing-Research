Professor Ian Williams heads the DMT Lab overall and sets research strategy across its four core areas: Human Computer Interaction, Computer Graphics and Computer Vision, Sound and Music Analysis, and Broadcast Systems. His own research background is mainly in mixed and augmented reality interaction rather than audio specifically, but as lab head he oversees and supports the SOMA group's music-computing work described in this note set.
