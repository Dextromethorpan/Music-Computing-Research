The Digital Media Technology (DMT) Lab is a research lab within Birmingham City University's School of Computing and Digital Technology (Faculty of Computing, Engineering and the Built Environment). Its Sound and Music Analysis (SOMA) research group focuses on music information retrieval, automatic drum transcription, intelligent music production, and computational analysis of traditional/folk music styles.
