ADTWeb takes the DMT Lab's research on automatic drum transcription (getting a computer to write out which drum was hit and when, just from listening to audio) and packages it as a tool that runs directly inside a web browser, with no separate software installation needed. Anyone can upload a piece of music and get an automatic breakdown of the drum part, making the lab's research usable by musicians, producers, and other researchers rather than staying locked away as an academic prototype. It was presented as a demo at the ISMIR 2017 conference and released as open-source software.
