A follow-up to the DMT Lab's earlier drum-sound-generation work, this ISMIR 2021 paper adopts the "style-based" generative adversarial network (GAN) design popularized for AI-generated faces and applies it to drum sounds. "GAN inversion" is the technique of taking a real, existing drum sample and finding the exact internal setting inside the trained network that would have produced it — like reverse-engineering the recipe for an already-baked cake. Once a real sound has been mapped this way, its style can be smoothly blended with another sound's style, letting a producer transform a real snare hit into something between that snare and a completely different drum sound, while keeping the result natural-sounding.
