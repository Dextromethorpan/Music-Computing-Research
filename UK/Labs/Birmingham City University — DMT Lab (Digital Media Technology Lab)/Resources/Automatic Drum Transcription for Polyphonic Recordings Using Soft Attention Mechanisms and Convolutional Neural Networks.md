Detecting drum hits is much harder in a "polyphonic" recording — one where drums are mixed together with guitars, vocals, bass and everything else a full band plays — than it is in an isolated drum track. This ISMIR 2017 paper improves on the team's earlier drum-transcription system by adding "soft attention," a mechanism that lets the neural network learn to focus more on the audio moments that matter most for identifying a drum hit, and by testing a convolutional neural network (a network type especially good at spotting patterns in something like a spectrogram, which is a visual map of sound frequencies over time). The result was noticeably better transcription accuracy on real full-band recordings, a harder and more realistic test case than drum-only audio.
