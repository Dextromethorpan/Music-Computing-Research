Presented at NIME (New Interfaces for Musical Expression) 2021, this paper describes a system that pairs a live coder (a musician who writes code live to make music) with a machine-learning "virtual agent." The performer gives the agent a few example sounds, and the agent then automatically filters and picks matching sounds pulled from Freesound, an online crowdsourced sound library, during the performance itself — rather than the human having to search manually while also coding. It's a concrete example of MTI2 researcher Anna Xambó's broader work on using AI to help musicians find and use huge shared collections of sound in real time.
