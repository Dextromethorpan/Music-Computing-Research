EAnalysis is a piece of free software built at MTI2 to help researchers study "electroacoustic" music — music made from recorded or synthesized sounds rather than notes on a score, which is hard to analyse with normal sheet-music tools because there's no score to look at. It lets a researcher draw graphic annotations directly on top of a sound's waveform and spectrogram (a picture showing which frequencies are present over time), load several recordings side by side to compare different works or performances, and build a visual "map" of how a piece is structured. It grew out of an AHRC-funded project called "New Multimedia Tools for Electroacoustic Music Analysis" run at MTI2, and its source code is openly available on GitHub.
