This is a short "studio report" presented at the International Computer Music Conference (ICMC) 2005, in which the (then newly established) MTI Research Centre at De Montfort University describes its facilities, people, and research directions to the wider computer music community. Studio reports like this are a standard way university and institutional electronic-music studios introduce themselves at conferences — summarizing their equipment, staff, and recent projects. Reading it gives a useful historical snapshot of how the centre (now called MTI2) was set up in its early years, shortly after being founded in 1999, before it grew into one of the UK's leading electroacoustic music research groups.
