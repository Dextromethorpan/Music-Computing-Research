GRIDs is an ongoing series of sound installations by MTI2 composer Peter Batchelor that uses grids of many small loudspeakers (for example, 30 flat panel speakers arranged in a 6x5 grid) to build detailed, moving "pictures" made entirely out of sound rather than light. In the first work in the series, "Studies on Canvas" (2004), visitors look at a blank canvas while the sounds coming from the hidden speaker grid behind it suggest shapes and movement that the listener has to imagine — demonstrating how carefully placing many individual speakers can create an illusion of images and space using audio alone. This is a hands-on example of MTI2's long-running research into spatial sound and installation art.
