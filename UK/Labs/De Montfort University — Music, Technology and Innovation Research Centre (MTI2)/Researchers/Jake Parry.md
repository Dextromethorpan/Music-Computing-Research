Jake Parry is a doctoral researcher at MTI2 studying spatial audio composition — music designed to surround or immerse a listener using many loudspeakers. His PhD moves beyond simply chasing "immersion," instead critically examining what spatial sound technology means culturally, using experimental sound-processing and unconventional speaker arrangements. He has presented his work at the IRCAM Forum in Paris.
