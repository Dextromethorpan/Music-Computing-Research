Bret Battey is a composer and professor at MTI2 whose work combines algorithmic (rule- and code-driven) composition with tightly integrated image and sound, often drawing on Indian classical music. He developed "Compression-Controlled Feedback Synthesis," a sound-generation technique built around automatic gain control in a feedback loop, and has explored networks of mathematical "iterated maps" as a way to generate evolving musical textures.
