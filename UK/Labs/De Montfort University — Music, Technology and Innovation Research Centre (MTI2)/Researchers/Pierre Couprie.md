Pierre Couprie is a researcher and software developer whose work bridges musicology, composition, and computer music. At MTI2 he developed EAnalysis, an open-source tool for visually analysing electroacoustic music, and more broadly works on software and multimedia tools that let researchers study, teach, and preserve sound-based music that has no traditional written score.
