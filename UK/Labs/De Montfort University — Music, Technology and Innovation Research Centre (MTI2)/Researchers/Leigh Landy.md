Leigh Landy is a composer and musicologist who founded and long directed MTI2. He coined the term "sound-based music" to describe electroacoustic art music, turntable composition, and sound installations as a single category, and built the ElectroAcoustic Resource Site (EARS), an online reference for the field. His research focuses on giving listeners and newcomers accessible frameworks for understanding music made from recorded and synthesized sound rather than traditional notation.
