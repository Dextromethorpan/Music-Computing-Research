Anna Xambó is a researcher and electronic music producer who was Senior Lecturer in Music and Audio Technology at MTI2 from 2020-2023. Her work sits at the crossroads of live coding (writing code live on stage to make music), collaborative and participatory music systems, and multichannel spatial audio. A recurring theme in her research is using AI "virtual agents" and large online sound libraries like Freesound to help live coders find and use sounds in real time during a performance.
