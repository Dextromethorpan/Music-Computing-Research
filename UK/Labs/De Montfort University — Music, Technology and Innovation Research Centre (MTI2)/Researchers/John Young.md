John Young is a composer at MTI2 working in both electroacoustic and instrumental music. His research examines how everyday environmental sounds can be recognised and transformed within a composition, and the aesthetics of digitally manipulating recorded sound — essentially, what makes an altered recording still feel meaningfully connected to its real-world source.
