The Music, Technology and Innovation Research Group (MTI2, formerly MTIRC, also styled "Institute for Sonic Creativity") is a research centre in the School of Arts at De Montfort University in Leicester, UK, established in 1999. It is one of the world's longest-running centres for electroacoustic music, sonic art, and computer-assisted music analysis, combining hands-on composition with academic research into spatial sound, live coding, and software tools for studying sound-based music.
