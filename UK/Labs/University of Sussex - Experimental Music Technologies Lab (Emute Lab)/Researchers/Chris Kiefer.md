Chris Kiefer designs new digital musical instruments, often using many sensors at once to capture detailed human gestures and feed them into computational sound-mapping processes. He co-created the Feedback Cello (a modified acoustic cello capable of controllable self-resonating feedback) and co-develops the Sema live-coding/machine-learning platform.
