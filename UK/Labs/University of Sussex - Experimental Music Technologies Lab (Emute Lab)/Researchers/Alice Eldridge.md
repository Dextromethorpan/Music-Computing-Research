Alice Eldridge works at the intersection of music, ecology, and computer science. She researches ecoacoustics (using sound to study and monitor ecosystems), biologically-inspired music technology, and co-created the Feedback Cello, an actuated instrument that lets performers shape self-resonating feedback as a musical material.
