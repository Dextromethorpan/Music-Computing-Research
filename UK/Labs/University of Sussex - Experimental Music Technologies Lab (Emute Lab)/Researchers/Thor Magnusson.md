Thor Magnusson co-founded ixi audio and co-directs the Emute Lab. His research explores how digital technology changes musical creativity, especially through live coding (writing code as a live performance) — he has released systems including ixi lang, Threnoscope, and Sema, and wrote the book "Sonic Writing" on the long history of technologies for capturing and notating sound.
