The Experimental Music Technologies Lab (Emute Lab), founded in 2014, is based at the University of Sussex, spanning the Music department and links to the Sussex Humanities Lab and the School of Engineering and Informatics. It researches new digital and hybrid musical instruments, live coding (writing computer code as a live musical performance), and the crossover between sound, ecology and computation.
