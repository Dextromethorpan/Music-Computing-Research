A "lab report" presented at the NIME (New Interfaces for Musical Expression) conference, summarizing several years of the Emute Lab's projects for building new kinds of musical instruments — instruments that use sensors, feedback, and computation rather than just traditional acoustic mechanisms. It's a good overview document for understanding the lab's overall approach: treating instrument-building itself as a form of research into how humans and technology can make music together.
