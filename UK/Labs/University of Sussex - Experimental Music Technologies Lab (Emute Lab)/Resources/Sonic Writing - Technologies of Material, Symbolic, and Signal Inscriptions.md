This book (the outcome of an AHRC-funded research project) traces the whole history of how humans have "written down" sound and music — from musical instruments themselves, to notation on paper, to recording technology, to today's software. Magnusson's argument is that every musical instrument or piece of notation is itself a kind of writing system that shapes what music gets made with it. The book connects this long history directly to modern live coding and AI music tools, situating them as the newest chapter in a very old story about how technology for capturing sound also shapes musical creativity.
