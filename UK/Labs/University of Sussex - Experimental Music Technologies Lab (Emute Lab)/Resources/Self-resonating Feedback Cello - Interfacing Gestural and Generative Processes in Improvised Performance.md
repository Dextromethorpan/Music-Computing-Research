The Feedback Cello is a real acoustic cello modified with pickups (small sensors under each string) and tiny speakers/vibration devices built into the body, so each string can be made to feed back and self-resonate independently, similar to how an electric guitar squeals near a loud amplifier — but controllable and playable as a musical technique. This NIME paper describes how the instrument was built and how musicians actually play and improvise with it, exploring the space between a performer's physical gestures and the instrument's own semi-autonomous, generative feedback behaviour.
