In this video interview, Professor Eduardo Miranda talks through ICCMR's core research directions in his own words, connecting the dots between projects that might otherwise look unrelated: brain-computer music interfacing for people with disabilities, "biocomputers" built from living organisms, and — the newest strand — quantum computing applied to music composition and sound synthesis. It's a good starting point for understanding ICCMR's overall philosophy: exploring radically different kinds of computing "hardware" (brains, living cells, quantum processors) as new creative tools for music, rather than only writing new software for ordinary computers.
