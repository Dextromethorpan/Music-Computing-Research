quantumaudio is a free, open-source Python toolkit (code library) published by ICCMR's quantum-music team as part of the QuTune project. It provides ready-made tools and example circuits for representing audio signals — i.e., turning a sound wave into a format that a quantum computer can process — and for running those representations through quantum hardware or simulators. In effect, it's a starter kit that lets musicians and researchers who aren't quantum-computing specialists experiment with encoding and manipulating sound using quantum algorithms, without building everything from scratch.
