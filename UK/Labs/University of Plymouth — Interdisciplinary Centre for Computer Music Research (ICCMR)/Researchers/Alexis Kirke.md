Alexis Kirke is a Senior Research Fellow at ICCMR and composer-in-residence for the Plymouth Marine Institute, holding two PhDs from the University of Plymouth. Before academia he worked as a quantitative analyst in finance and as an R&D manager in technology. His research combines interests in quantum mechanics, marine science, stock markets and AI, and his more recent work has explored using controlled quantum dynamics to create quantum computer music.
