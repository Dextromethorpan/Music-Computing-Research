Joel Eaton completed his PhD at ICCMR in 2016 on designing practical brain-computer music interfacing (BCMI) systems for creative use, working closely with Eduardo Miranda. His research covered turning EEG brainwave signals into usable musical control data and building real performable BCMI systems, including a piece called "Flex." He has since moved into the tech industry (working at companies including Arm, Unity, and Amazon on customer education) while remaining active in the BCMI research community as a journal reviewer and workshop chair.
