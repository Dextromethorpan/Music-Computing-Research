Eduardo Reck Miranda is a composer and Professor in Computer Music who founded and directs ICCMR. He has been making music with artificial intelligence since the 1980s and defended what is thought to be the UK's first PhD on AI for music (University of Edinburgh, 1995). His current research spans brain-computer music interfacing for people with severe disabilities, "biocomputers" built from living organisms, and quantum computing applied to music composition and sound synthesis — he has raised over £5M in research funding and been featured in Forbes for combining AI and quantum computing in his music.
