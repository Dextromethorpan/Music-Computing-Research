Dr Nuria Bonet is a Lecturer in Music at ICCMR whose PhD research (2019) focused on data sonification — turning data into sound and music — in creative practice. Her work often takes nature and environmental data as its subject matter, finding musical structures in the natural world and in datasets such as climate data, and she has also connected her work to dementia care and military charities.
