Satvik Venkatesh completed a Master's and PhD at ICCMR, finishing his doctorate in 2022 on deep learning for audio segmentation (automatically splitting an audio stream into meaningful sections, such as speech vs. music) and intelligent remixing for live radio broadcast. His broader research interests include deep learning, brain-computer interfaces, and unconventional computing for music. He now works as an engineer applying deep learning to music/audio at L-Acoustics, a professional audio company.
