ICCMR is a research centre at the University of Plymouth (UK), led by Professor Eduardo Reck Miranda. It works at the interface of music, computers, and the brain, and is internationally known for pioneering brain-computer music interfacing (letting people control music with brainwaves), "biocomputer" music (using living organisms as computing components), and — more recently — quantum computing applied to music.
