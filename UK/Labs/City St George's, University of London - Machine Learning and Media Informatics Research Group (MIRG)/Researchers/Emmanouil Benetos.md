Emmanouil Benetos worked at MIRG on automatic music transcription (turning audio recordings into musical notation), multi-pitch detection, and acoustic scene/event classification, including award-winning work at SMC and ISMIR conferences. He has since moved his research (now much of it based at Queen Mary University of London) but his MIRG-era work on transcription and source separation remains a core part of the group's published output.
