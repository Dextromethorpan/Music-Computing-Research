Tillman Weyde leads MIRG and works broadly on machine learning and deep learning applied to music and media: music information retrieval, source separation, speech emotion recognition, and building neural networks that generalize better ("inductive biases"). He has co-authored work spanning singing-voice separation, jazz harmony modelling, and quaternion-based speech emotion recognition.
