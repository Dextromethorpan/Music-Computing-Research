MIRG (originally the Music Informatics Research Group) is based in the Department of Computer Science at City St George's, University of London (formerly City University London). It researches machine learning and AI applied to music and media: music information retrieval, automatic music transcription, computational musicology, and increasingly wider topics in deep learning, multimodal emotion recognition, and language.
