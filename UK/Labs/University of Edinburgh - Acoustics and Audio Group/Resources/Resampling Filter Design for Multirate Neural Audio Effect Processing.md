A follow-up to the group's work on sample-rate-independent neural audio effects. Here the researchers focus specifically on the "resampling filters" used inside such models — the small pieces of signal processing that convert audio between sample rates without introducing unwanted noise or distortion (a problem related to what's called aliasing). They test different filter designs to find ones that keep the sound clean when a trained guitar-amp or effects model has to run at a sample rate different from what it was trained on. This is applied, practical digital signal processing (DSP) work aimed at making machine-learning-based audio effects reliable in real products.
