The Acoustics and Audio Group (AAG) is based mainly in the Reid School of Music at Edinburgh College of Art, part of the University of Edinburgh. It researches the science, technology and art of sound across three areas: acoustics (including musical acoustics), audio signal processing, and music technology (sound synthesis, virtual analogue effects, and real-time audio programming). Note: the University of Edinburgh also hosts the Centre for Speech Technology Research (CSTR), a long-running and very active speech-technology lab, but CSTR's work is focused on speech recognition/synthesis rather than music, so this entry covers the genuinely music-focused AAG instead.
