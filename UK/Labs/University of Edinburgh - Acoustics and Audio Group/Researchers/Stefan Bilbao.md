Stefan Bilbao leads much of the group's work on computational acoustics: using detailed numerical simulation to generate realistic sound synthesis and virtual acoustic spaces. He was the principal investigator on the NESS (Next Generation Sound Synthesis) project and continues to work on physical modeling of instruments and rooms, including combining physics-based simulation with modern machine learning.
