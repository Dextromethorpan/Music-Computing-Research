Alistair Carson is a PhD student researching machine learning for audio synthesis, particularly neural network models of guitar amplifiers and audio effects. He won the Best Presentation award at DAFx-24 for work on making these models work correctly regardless of the audio sample rate they are run at, and has continued this line of research with follow-up papers on synthetic training data and resampling filter design.
