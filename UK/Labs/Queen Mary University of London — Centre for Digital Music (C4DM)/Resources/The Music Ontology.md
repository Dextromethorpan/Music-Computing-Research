This ISMIR 2007 paper, co-authored by C4DM's Mark Sandler, introduces the Music Ontology: a shared, formal vocabulary for describing music-related facts (artists, recordings, performances, editorial and cultural information such as reviews or genre) so that different databases and websites can publish and link this information in a consistent, computer-readable way, following "Semantic Web" / "Linked Data" principles (a way of publishing structured facts on the web so machines can connect related information across different sites, much like Wikipedia articles link to each other). The Music Ontology became a foundational piece of infrastructure for music data on the web, influencing how services like the BBC and MusicBrainz structure and interlink their music metadata.
