"Mixing" is the studio process of balancing volume, panning (left/right placement), and effects across all the recorded tracks of a song so it sounds polished and coherent. This paper, from C4DM's Josh Reiss and PhD researcher Brecht De Man, presents a system that mixes a multi-track recording automatically, without a human sound engineer. Instead of learning purely from data, it encodes rules taken directly from professional mixing textbooks (for example, "give the lead vocal a bit more presence" or "spread instruments across the stereo field to avoid clashing") and combines them with measurements of the actual audio, such as loudness and frequency content, to decide how to treat each track. The work was an early, influential step in C4DM's long-running "automatic mixing" research programme, which has since grown into a major strand of intelligent audio production research and several spin-out companies.
