Sonic Visualiser is a free, open-source desktop program built at C4DM that lets researchers, musicologists, and audio engineers look "inside" an audio recording — displaying waveforms, spectrograms (a picture showing how loud different pitches are over time), and other analysis views side by side, in sync with playback. It can also run "Vamp plugins," small add-on programs (also developed at C4DM) that automatically detect things like beats, chords, or note onsets and draw them as annotations on top of the audio. Since its 2006 release it has become a standard tool used across music research worldwide for tasks like transcribing recordings, checking algorithm output, and annotating audio corpora by hand. The project remains actively maintained and open source on GitHub.
