C4DM is a research centre within the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London, founded by Prof Mark Sandler. It is one of the world's leading music and audio technology research groups, covering areas from music signal processing and machine listening to new musical instruments, semantic audio, and AI-generated music, and it runs the UKRI Centre for Doctoral Training in Artificial Intelligence and Music.
