Simon Dixon works on music informatics and signal processing — building systems that extract musical content such as beat, rhythm, harmony, and pitch directly from audio recordings. He created BeatRoot, an influential beat-tracking system, and now also directs the UKRI Centre for Doctoral Training in Artificial Intelligence and Music, which trains PhD researchers across AI and music.
