Josh Reiss leads C4DM's audio engineering research, focused on using signal processing and machine learning to automate sound design and audio production tasks such as mixing, mastering, and sound effect synthesis. He is a serial academic entrepreneur, having co-founded several spin-out companies from his research (including LANDR, RoEx, and Nemisindo) and served as President of the Audio Engineering Society.
