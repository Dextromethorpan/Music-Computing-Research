Andrew McPherson designs new and "augmented" musical instruments — taking existing instruments like the piano and adding sensors and actuators (motors or electromagnets that can move or vibrate parts of the instrument) to give players new expressive possibilities, such as the Magnetic Resonator Piano. He led this research within C4DM before moving to Imperial College London in 2023, where his lab remains jointly affiliated with C4DM; he also co-founded Bela, an open-source hardware company for low-latency interactive audio.
