Mark Sandler founded C4DM and has led it since its creation, working on digital signal processing applied to audio and music since the late 1970s. His current focus is "semantic audio" — using semantic web / linked data techniques (shared, machine-readable vocabularies) to describe, connect, and search music data — alongside immersive and studio audio technology.
