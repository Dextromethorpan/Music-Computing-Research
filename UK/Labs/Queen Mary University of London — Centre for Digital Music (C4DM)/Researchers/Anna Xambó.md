Anna Xambó is a researcher and experimental electronic musician who studies human-computer interaction in music, especially live coding (writing code on stage to generate music in real time) and new interfaces for musical expression. Her recent work also explores acoustic ecology and the Internet of Things, including a project on sonifying forest environments.
