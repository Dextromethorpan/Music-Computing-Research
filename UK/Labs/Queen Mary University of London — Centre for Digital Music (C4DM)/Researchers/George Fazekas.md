George Fazekas works on "semantic audio" — combining audio analysis with semantic web technology (structured, linkable descriptions of data) to make music information easier to search, organise, and reason about. His research spans music information retrieval, music emotion recognition, and machine learning applied to intelligent, interactive music production and editing tools.
