Emmanouil Benetos works on "machine listening" (also called computer audition) — teaching computers to analyse and understand sound, whether that's music, urban noise, or sounds from nature. A major thread of his work is automatic music transcription, turning audio recordings into readable musical notation, and more broadly resource-efficient machine learning for audio and multimodal AI.
