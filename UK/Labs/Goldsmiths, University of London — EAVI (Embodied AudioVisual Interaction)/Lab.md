EAVI (Embodied AudioVisual Interaction) is a research unit in the Department of Computing at Goldsmiths, University of London, led by Prof. Atau Tanaka. It researches how the human body — gesture, muscle signals, brain activity — can be used to control sound and image, and how machine learning can help build new digital musical instruments. (Note: Goldsmiths' separate Computational Creativity Group, associated with Prof. Simon Colton, focuses on general AI creativity rather than music computing specifically, and Colton himself is now primarily based at Queen Mary University of London / Monash University — so this entry focuses on EAVI.)
