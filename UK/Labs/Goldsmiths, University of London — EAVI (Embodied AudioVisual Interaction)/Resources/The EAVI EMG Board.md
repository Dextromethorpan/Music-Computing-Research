This paper describes a custom piece of hardware built at EAVI: a small wearable circuit board that picks up the electrical signal your muscles generate when they contract (EMG, or electromyography), plus movement data from a built-in accelerometer. Unlike consumer products like the Myo armband, this board was designed specifically for musicians and artists, so it is open, affordable, and easy to combine with music software. The paper covers how the board was engineered, tested, and used in performance to translate muscle tension and gesture directly into sound. It's a foundational piece of hardware behind much of EAVI's later work on physiological (body-signal-based) musical instruments.
