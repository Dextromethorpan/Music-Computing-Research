This paper presents an upgraded version of EAVI's custom wearable sensor board, now able to read both muscle signals (EMG) and, for the first time, brain signals (EEG, or electroencephalogram — the tiny electrical activity detectable on the scalp). Combining the two is called "ExG." The device is wireless, works over Bluetooth or USB, and is designed from the ground up as a musical instrument rather than a repurposed medical device — it acts like a class-compliant audio/MIDI device, so it plugs straight into normal music software. The paper explains the electronics design and reports it being used in performance and in studies with both trained musicians and neurodiverse performers, extending the idea of a "digital musical instrument" to sensing not just gesture but internal muscular and neural activity.
