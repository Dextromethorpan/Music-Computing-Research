Wekinator is a free software tool that lets musicians and artists teach a computer to turn body movements or sensor data into sound, without having to write code or understand the math behind machine learning. You perform an action (say, moving your hand a certain way), tell the software "this is what a loud, low note should sound like," repeat this a few times for different actions, and the software learns the pattern — so from then on your movements control the music. This paper introduces the system and its "interactive machine learning" approach, where the human trains, tests, and retrains the model live, rather than handing off a big dataset and waiting. Wekinator became one of the most widely used tools for building new gesture-controlled digital instruments, developed at Goldsmiths/Princeton by Rebecca Fiebrink, who was later based at EAVI, Goldsmiths.
