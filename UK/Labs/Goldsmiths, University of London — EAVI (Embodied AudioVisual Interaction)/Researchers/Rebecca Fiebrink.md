Rebecca Fiebrink created Wekinator, a widely used tool that lets musicians and artists teach machine-learning models to turn body movement or sensor input into sound, without needing to write code. She was based at Goldsmiths/EAVI for a substantial part of her career (she has since moved to the Creative Computing Institute at University of the Arts London) and has continued to research how interactive machine learning can be made accessible to non-programmers, including artists, disabled musicians, and children.
