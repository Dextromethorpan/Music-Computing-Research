Balandino Di Donato was a Research Associate at EAVI, where he worked on the ERC-funded BioMusic project, helping build the EAVI's wireless EMG (muscle-sensing) hardware and the software that turns muscle and gesture data into musical control signals. He also created Myo Mapper, an open-source tool for connecting the Myo gesture armband to music software. He has since moved into an academic lecturing role focused on interactive audio and embodied human-computer interaction in music performance.
