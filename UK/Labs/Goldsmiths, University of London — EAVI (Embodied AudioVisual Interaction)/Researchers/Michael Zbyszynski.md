Michael Zbyszynski worked as a researcher and later lecturer at Goldsmiths/EAVI (roughly 2015–2022), contributing to projects on interactive machine learning for musical instrument design, including work connecting gesture data to sound timbre. He also has a background in music composition and electroacoustic music teaching. He now works on spatial and immersive audio software at L-Acoustics.
