Baptiste Caramiaux worked at EAVI on gesture recognition and interactive machine learning for musical expression, including studying how muscle sensing relates to expressive movement. He is now a tenured CNRS researcher in Paris, working more broadly on human-computer interaction and machine learning, including interactive/collaborative machine learning and trust between humans and AI systems.
