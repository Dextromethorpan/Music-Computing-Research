Atau Tanaka leads the EAVI research group at Goldsmiths. His research sits at the intersection of human-computer interaction and musical performance, focusing on how the body — through gesture, muscle signals (EMG), and more recently brain activity (EEG) — can be turned into a musical instrument. He has built sensor-based instruments for decades, previously working at IRCAM and Sony Computer Science Laboratory Paris, and continues to lead work on wearable physiological sensing hardware for musicians.
