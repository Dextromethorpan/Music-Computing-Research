A follow-on Cadenza competition, run for the ICASSP signal processing conference, that focuses on "demixing" music — splitting a finished recording back into its separate parts (vocals, drums, bass, other instruments) — and then remixing those parts so the result is easier to hear for someone using a hearing aid. This is much harder than it sounds because commercial recordings are mixed down into a single stereo file with no separate tracks provided, so the separation itself has to be done automatically by software before the remixing can happen. The paper describes the challenge dataset, rules, and evaluation method (again combining computer predictions with real listening tests from people with hearing loss), giving researchers worldwide a shared benchmark to compete on and improve.
