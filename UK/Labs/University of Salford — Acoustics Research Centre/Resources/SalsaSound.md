SalsaSound is a company spun out of Salford Acoustics Research Centre's work (originating from the EU-funded FascinatE broadcast research project) that builds AI software to automatically mix live sports audio — for example, deciding in real time how loud the crowd, commentary and pitch-side sound effects should each be, a job normally done manually by a skilled sound engineer during a broadcast. It's a practical, commercial example of "automatic mixing," where machine-learning models learn to make the moment-to-moment loudness and balance decisions a human mixer would make, but fast enough to run live and at a scale (many simultaneous matches) that would be impractical to staff manually. Ben Shirley, an Associate Professor at Salford working on spatial audio and audio accessibility, is a co-founder and director of the company.
