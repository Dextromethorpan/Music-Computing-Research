Bruno Fazenda holds a PhD in Room Acoustics and Psychoacoustics from Salford and researches how the acoustics of a room, and the technology and psychological state of a listener, shape the perceived quality of sound. His work covers critical listening/control room design, low-frequency (bass) room behaviour, and how listeners judge recorded music quality — including as part of Salford's "Good Recording Project." He is also a co-author on the Cadenza music-and-hearing-loss challenge papers.
