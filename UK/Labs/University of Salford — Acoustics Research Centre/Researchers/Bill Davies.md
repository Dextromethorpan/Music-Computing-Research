Bill Davies is a Professor at Salford's Acoustics Research Centre whose work covers soundscape research, psychoacoustics, spatial audio and room acoustics. He led the perceptual research strand of the "Making Sense of Sounds" project, building datasets and classification schemes for how people perceive everyday sound. He also directs LAURA, a research hub focused on "aural diversity" — the idea that people hear and experience sound differently, which matters for accessible audio design.
