Simone Graetzer works on speech acoustics, psychoacoustics, sound quality assessment and hearing-aid signal processing. She is a key contributor to the Cadenza project, which runs open machine-learning competitions to improve how music sounds for people with hearing loss, helping design the listening tests and evaluation methods used to judge competing systems.
