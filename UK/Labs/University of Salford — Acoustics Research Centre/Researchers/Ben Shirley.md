Ben Shirley researches spatial audio and audio accessibility, particularly how to personalise object-based broadcast audio so it works better for people with hearing loss (his PhD focused on improving TV sound for hearing-impaired viewers). He worked on the S3A spatial audio programme's accessibility trials with BBC Casualty, and is co-founder and Director of SalsaSound, a Salford spin-out building AI tools for automatic live sports audio mixing.
