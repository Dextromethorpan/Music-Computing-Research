Duncan Williams works on psychoacoustics, audio production, video game audio and digital signal processing, with a research thread stretching back to his PhD work on "affectively driven algorithmic composition" — computer systems that generate music aimed at producing a specific emotional response in the listener. His publications combine music-generation systems with rigorous listener testing to check whether the intended emotional effect actually lands.
