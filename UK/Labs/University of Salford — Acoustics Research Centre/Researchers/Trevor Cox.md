Trevor Cox is a Professor of Acoustic Engineering and a long-standing figure at Salford's Acoustics Research Centre, and a past president of the UK's Institute of Acoustics. His research spans architectural acoustics, room diffusers, and psychoacoustics, and he co-led the "Making Sense of Sounds" project on teaching machines to classify everyday sounds the way humans do. He is also known for public science communication about sound and hearing.
