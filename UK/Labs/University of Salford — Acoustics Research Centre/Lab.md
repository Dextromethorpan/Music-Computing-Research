The Acoustics Research Centre (part of the University of Salford's Acoustics Innovation Institute, based at MediaCityUK, Manchester) is one of Europe's largest acoustics research groups, active since 1958. This note covers only its music/audio-relevant subset — spatial audio, room acoustics for critical listening, psychoacoustics of speech and music, and machine-learning-driven audio engineering — not its broader work on building acoustics, environmental noise, or vibro-acoustics.
