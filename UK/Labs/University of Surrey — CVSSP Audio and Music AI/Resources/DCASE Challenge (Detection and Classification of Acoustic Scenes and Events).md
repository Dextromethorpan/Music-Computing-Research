DCASE is an annual international challenge and workshop where research teams compete to build the best AI systems for recognising everyday sounds — things like detecting a smoke alarm, classifying whether a recording was made on a street or in a park, or figuring out how many different sound sources are overlapping in a noisy scene. It was founded with CVSSP involvement (Professor Mark Plumbley) and has grown into the central meeting point for the "machine listening" research community worldwide, similar to how ISMIR is the main venue for music information retrieval. Surrey's audio group has both organised challenge tasks and won them (including the 2017 large-scale sound event detection task), making DCASE both a piece of research infrastructure the lab helped build and a recurring showcase for its own results.
