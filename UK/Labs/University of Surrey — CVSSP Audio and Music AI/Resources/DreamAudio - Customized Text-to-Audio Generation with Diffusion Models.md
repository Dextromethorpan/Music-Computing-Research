DreamAudio extends text-to-audio generation (typing a description and getting a matching sound or piece of music back) with "customization": instead of only using generic descriptions, you can give the system a reference sound — say, a specific dog's bark, or a particular instrument's tone — and it will generate new audio that keeps that same distinctive character while following your text instructions for what else should happen. This is similar in spirit to how image generators can be customized to draw "your dog" in different scenes, but applied to audio. Like the other generation systems from this lab, it's built on a diffusion model, the noise-to-signal generation approach.
