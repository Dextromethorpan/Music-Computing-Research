The Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing (CVSSP) at the University of Surrey is one of the UK's largest signal-processing research centres. Its audio and machine-listening group (sometimes called the Machine Audition Lab) works on teaching computers to understand, generate, and enhance sound — from tagging what sounds are present in a recording, to generating new music and sound effects from text, to tracking where a speaker is in a room using microphone arrays. The group founded and helps run the influential DCASE (Detection and Classification of Acoustic Scenes and Events) challenge, and regularly publishes at ICASSP, WASPAA, and DCASE.
