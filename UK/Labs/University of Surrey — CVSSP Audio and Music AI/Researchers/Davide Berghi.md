Davide Berghi completed his PhD at CVSSP (supervised by Philip Jackson) on detecting and locating who is speaking using microphone arrays and video together, work that feeds into object-based media production (making broadcasts that can be personalised or re-mixed for each viewer). He created datasets like "Tragic Talkers," a synchronized multi-camera, multi-microphone recording of Shakespeare performances built specifically to support audio-visual machine learning research, and works on sound event localization and detection that combines audio and visual embeddings.
