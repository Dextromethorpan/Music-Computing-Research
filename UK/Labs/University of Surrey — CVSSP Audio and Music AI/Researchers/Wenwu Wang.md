Wenwu Wang co-directs the Machine Audition Lab within CVSSP and is currently the group's most active lead across text-to-audio generation, sound event detection and localization, and audio-visual speaker tracking. He has authored or co-authored over 400 publications, including two books on machine audition and blind source separation (separating mixed sound sources — for example, isolating individual instruments from a recording). He is a co-author on most of this lab's recent flagship work, including AudioLDM 2, AudioSR, PSELDNets, and DreamAudio.
