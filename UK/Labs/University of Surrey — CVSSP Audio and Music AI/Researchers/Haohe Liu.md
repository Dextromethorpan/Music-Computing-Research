Haohe Liu completed his PhD at CVSSP under Mark Plumbley and Wenwu Wang, jointly funded by BBC R&D, and is the creator of the AudioLDM series of text-to-audio generation models — among the most widely used open-source tools for turning written descriptions into sound and music. He is first author on AudioLDM, AudioLDM 2, and AudioSR, and his broader work spans generative AI for audio, source separation (splitting mixed audio into its components), and audio quality enhancement. He is now a Research Scientist at Meta's FAIR lab.
