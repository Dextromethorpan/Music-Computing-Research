Mark Plumbley works on AI, machine learning, and signal processing for understanding, recognising, and generating sound — from everyday noises to speech and music. He founded the influential DCASE (Detection and Classification of Acoustic Scenes and Events) challenge and led CVSSP's audio group through its major achievements, including the DCASE 2017 challenge win and the AudioLDM text-to-audio generation systems. He led CVSSP's "AI for Sound" EPSRC Fellowship, focused on teaching AI to understand everyday sounds. In October 2025 he moved from Surrey to King's College London to become Head of the Department of Informatics, but his Surrey-affiliated body of work (2015–2025) remains the core of CVSSP's audio and music AI output covered in this vault.
