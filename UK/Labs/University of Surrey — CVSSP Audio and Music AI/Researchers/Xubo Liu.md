Xubo Liu was a PhD student at CVSSP supervised by Wenwu Wang and Mark Plumbley, working on machine listening, multimodal learning (combining audio with text or other data types), and computational creativity. He is a co-author on several of the lab's text-to-audio and text-to-music generation papers, including AudioLDM 2 and DreamAudio, and has also worked on retrieval-augmented audio generation (using a search step to pull in relevant reference sounds before generating new audio). He is now at Meta Superintelligence Labs.
