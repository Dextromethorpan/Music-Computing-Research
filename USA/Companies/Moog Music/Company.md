Moog Music is the Asheville, North Carolina synthesizer maker founded by Bob Moog. It qualifies through documented product/architectural innovation (path 3): its 1969 "ladder filter" circuit is a specific, patented, nameable voltage-controlled filter design that is still the core filter topology built into every modern Moog synthesizer, and its Animoog software instrument runs on a separately documented, nameable synthesis mechanism called the Anisotropic Synthesis Engine — both verified through Moog's own patent filing and official product documentation rather than academic papers.
