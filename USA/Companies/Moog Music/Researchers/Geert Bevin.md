Geert Bevin leads software engineering at Moog Music, overseeing the code behind Moog's synthesizer apps such as Animoog, including the software implementation of the Anisotropic Synthesis Engine. He came up through the company as a senior software engineer before becoming director, and has also built other music software and controllers outside of Moog.
