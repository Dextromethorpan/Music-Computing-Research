Robert "Bob" Moog (1934-2005) was the electrical engineer and company founder who designed the original Moog modular synthesizer and personally filed the 1969 ladder filter patent that still defines the circuit inside every modern Moog instrument. He founded Moog Music and remained closely involved in its engineering culture until his death, and is one of the most widely credited inventors in the history of electronic musical instruments.
