Cyril Lance is Moog Music's Chief Engineer, who took over leadership of Moog's engineering effort in 2005 after meeting Bob Moog and being asked to step in during Moog's illness. He has co-designed numerous Moog instruments including the Little Phatty, Sub 37, Animoog (and its Anisotropic Synthesis Engine), and the Theremini, and holds a degree in Applied and Engineering Physics from Cornell University.
