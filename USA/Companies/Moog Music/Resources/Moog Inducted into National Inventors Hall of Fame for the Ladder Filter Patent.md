This trade-press article covers Bob Moog's induction into the U.S. National Inventors Hall of Fame specifically for Patent No. 3,475,623 — the ladder filter. It explains in plain terms why this single circuit mattered so much: it is the part of the original Moog synthesizer design that Moog actually patented, and it became the "voltage-controlled filter" heart of the classic Minimoog and every Moog instrument since. It's useful as independent confirmation, outside of Moog's own marketing, that this specific patented circuit is treated by the wider audio engineering community as a landmark, still-in-use invention rather than just a historical curiosity.
