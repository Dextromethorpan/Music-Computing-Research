This is a research overview from Dolby's Advanced Technology Group describing the directions its spatial audio research is currently exploring: combining acoustics (the physics of how sound moves through space), AI-based audio generation, creative production tools for mixing spatially, and augmented-reality audio (sound that reacts to a person's real surroundings, for AR/VR devices). It gives a beginner-accessible summary of where Dolby's spatial audio work — the research foundation behind consumer products like Dolby Atmos — is headed next.
