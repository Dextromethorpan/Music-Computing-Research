This is Dolby's official technical specification document (Issue 4, April 2024) that defines exactly how a cinema (and, by extension, other listening spaces) needs to be built and equipped to correctly play back Dolby Atmos content. Dolby Atmos is an "object-based" surround sound system: instead of mixing sound into a small fixed number of channels (like left/right/center speakers), individual sounds are kept as separate "objects" with position data, and the system decides in real time how to spread them across however many speakers — including ones mounted in the ceiling — are actually present in the room. The specification covers required and recommended speaker layouts, performance targets, and calibration so that a mix made in one Atmos-equipped room translates properly to another.
