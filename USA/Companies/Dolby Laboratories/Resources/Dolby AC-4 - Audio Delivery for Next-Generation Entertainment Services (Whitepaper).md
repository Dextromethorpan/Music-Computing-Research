AC-4 is Dolby's modern audio codec (a system for compressing audio into a smaller file or stream, then reconstructing it for playback) designed for streaming, broadcast TV, and mobile delivery. This whitepaper explains its engineering goals: carrying both traditional channel-based sound and "object-based" audio (where individual sounds, like a single voice, are kept as separate movable elements with position information rather than being permanently mixed into fixed channels) in one efficient stream. A notable feature is that AC-4's audio frames can be aligned exactly with video frames, so broadcasters can cut or switch a live stream at any video frame without having to decode and re-encode the audio — something older codecs could not do cleanly.
