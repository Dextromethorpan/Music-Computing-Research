This survey paper, co-authored by Dolby researcher Mark R. P. Thomas together with academics from several universities, walks through the history and current state of "spatial audio" — technology that captures or recreates sound so it feels like it's coming from specific places around a listener — and "room acoustics" — how a physical space's shape and materials change the way sound behaves in it (e.g. reflections and reverberation). It was accepted at ICASSP 2025, one of the top academic conferences for audio and speech signal processing, and covers both how sound is recorded/reproduced spatially and how rooms themselves can be simulated, modeled, and even acoustically controlled.
