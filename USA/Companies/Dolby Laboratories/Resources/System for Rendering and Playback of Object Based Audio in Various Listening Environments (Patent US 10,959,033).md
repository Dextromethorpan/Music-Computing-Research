This granted US patent describes core engineering behind the Dolby Atmos platform: a way of playing back "object-based" audio — sound elements stored with position metadata rather than baked into fixed channels — correctly across many different kinds of rooms and speaker setups, from a cinema with dozens of speakers down to a home soundbar or a pair of headphones. The system automatically figures out, based on which speakers are actually available, how to reproduce the intended spatial positions of each sound object as faithfully as possible, which is what lets the same Atmos mix "just work" on very different playback equipment.
