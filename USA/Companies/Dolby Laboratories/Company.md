Dolby Laboratories is a San Francisco-headquartered audio and imaging technology company whose Advanced Technology Group runs an active research program into spatial/immersive audio (Dolby Atmos), audio coding (like the AC-4 codec), room acoustics, and AI applications such as audio deepfake detection — publishing at venues including ICASSP, WASPAA, and AES, and holding a large patent portfolio around object-based audio.
