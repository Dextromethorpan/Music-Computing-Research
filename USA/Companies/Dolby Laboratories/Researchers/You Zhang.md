You (Neil) Zhang is a Senior Researcher at Dolby Laboratories working on spatial audio and multimodal AI (systems that combine audio with other data, like video). Before joining Dolby he earned his PhD at the Audio Information Research Lab at the University of Rochester, where his work focused on audio deepfake detection — identifying AI-generated or manipulated speech and singing. His research has been published at ICASSP, WASPAA, and Interspeech, and he received an IEEE WASPAA Best Student Paper Award.
