Christophe Chabanne is one of the named co-inventors on Dolby's patent for rendering and playing back object-based audio across different listening environments, which describes core engineering underlying the Dolby Atmos platform — how the system adapts a spatial audio mix to whatever speaker setup is actually available in a given room.
