Mark R. P. Thomas is a researcher at Dolby Laboratories in San Francisco whose work covers spatial audio capture, reproduction, compression, and perception — how sound is recorded, encoded, and made to feel like it's coming from real positions in space. Before Dolby he worked at Microsoft Research on audio capture and rendering for products including Kinect, Surface, and HoloLens. He holds a PhD from Imperial College London and regularly publishes and presents at AES conventions.
