Emanuele Coviello is a principal applied scientist at Amazon Music working on scalable, personalized, context-aware recommendation systems, as well as newer work on multimodal music tokenization for generative retrieval. He holds a PhD in machine learning from UC San Diego and previously co-founded a music/video analysis startup before joining Amazon.
