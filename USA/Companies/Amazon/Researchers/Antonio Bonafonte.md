Antonio Bonafonte was a senior applied scientist in Amazon's Text-to-Speech group, working on Alexa's synthetic voice technology, especially transferring expressive qualities such as prosody, accent, and speaker identity between synthetic voices. He has an extensive academic background in speech processing and voice conversion, and has since moved to Sanas AI.
