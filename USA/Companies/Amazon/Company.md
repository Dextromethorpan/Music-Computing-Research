Amazon's audio research spans two applied science groups publishing openly via Amazon Science: Amazon Music, which builds large-scale personalized and context-aware music recommendation and discovery systems, and the Amazon Text-to-Speech group, which develops Alexa's neural speech-synthesis technology (also powering AWS Polly).
