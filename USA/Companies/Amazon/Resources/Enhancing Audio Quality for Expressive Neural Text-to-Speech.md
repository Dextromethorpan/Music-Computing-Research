"Expressive" text-to-speech systems — ones that can sound excited, sad, or use a newscaster voice — sometimes trade away some raw audio fidelity to achieve that expressiveness, producing artifacts or a slightly less clean sound than a neutral voice. This Amazon paper looks at ways to close that quality gap so that expressive Alexa voices sound as clean and natural as plain, neutral ones, rather than forcing a choice between expressiveness and audio quality.
