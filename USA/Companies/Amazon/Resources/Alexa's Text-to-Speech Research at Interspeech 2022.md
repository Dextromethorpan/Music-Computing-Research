This blog post from an Amazon Text-to-Speech senior scientist recaps more than a dozen research papers the team presented at Interspeech 2022, a major speech-research conference. The unifying theme is "transference" — being able to take one thing (an emotional tone, an accent, or the identity of a specific speaker) that a system learned from one voice and apply it to a different synthetic voice, without needing to record hours of new training audio for every combination. This is part of how Alexa's voice keeps getting more expressive and natural-sounding over time.
