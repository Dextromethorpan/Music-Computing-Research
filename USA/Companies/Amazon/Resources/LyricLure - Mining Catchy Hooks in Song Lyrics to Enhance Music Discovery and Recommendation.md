People increasingly search for songs by typing a memorable line from the lyrics rather than the song title — think "search that song about the sky falling." That is hard for a normal text search engine because lyrics are long, and simple keyword matching (a scoring method called BM25) doesn't know which lines are actually catchy or memorable versus filler. This Amazon Music paper describes LyricLure, a system that automatically mines the "hook" lines out of song lyrics — the most catchy, searchable snippets — and uses them to improve both search and recommendation, so a partial or paraphrased lyric can still surface the right track.
