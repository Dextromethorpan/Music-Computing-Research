Amazon Music researchers describe how to build a personalized recommender that can react to "context" — for example, the time of day, the device being used, or what a listener has picked recently — while still running fast enough to serve recommendations live to millions of users. The paper's contribution is a design that keeps this contextual, personalized model scalable in production, a real engineering constraint that's easy to overlook when only optimizing for accuracy in a research setting.
