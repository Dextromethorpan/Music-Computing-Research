If a recommendation list is only optimized for relevance, it can end up repetitive — ten songs that all sound almost the same. This early Amazon Music paper compares two mathematical approaches for adding variety to a recommendation list without hurting quality: a simple similarity-based method (Jaccard) and a more sophisticated "submodular" optimization method, which factors in each song's individual relevance score while still spreading out the picks. The submodular approach won, producing lists that were both more diverse and more engaging to listeners.
