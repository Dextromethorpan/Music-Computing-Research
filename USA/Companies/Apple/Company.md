Apple's music-recognition research centers on the audio fingerprinting technology invented by Shazam, which Apple acquired in 2018; the original Shazam engineering and research team (led by algorithm inventor Avery Wang) now operates as part of Apple, working on robust song identification, audio search, and related signal-processing research.
