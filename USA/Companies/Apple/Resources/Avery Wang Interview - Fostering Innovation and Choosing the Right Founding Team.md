In this rare interview, Avery Wang — Shazam's co-founder, former Chief Scientist, and now Principal Research Scientist at Apple — walks through how he invented the Shazam algorithm after being told by experts it was impossible. He describes the months of struggle before landing on the "constellation map" idea of matching audio through robust time-frequency landmarks, and reflects on what it took to turn a hard signal-processing research problem into a product used by hundreds of millions of people. It's a useful beginner read for understanding the human side of how a foundational piece of music-computing technology actually got built.
