This is Apple's official 2018 announcement confirming it had completed its acquisition of Shazam, the music-recognition app built on Avery Wang's audio fingerprinting algorithm. Apple framed the deal as strengthening Apple Music's music-discovery features and made the Shazam app ad-free for everyone afterward. This is a useful anchor resource for understanding why Shazam's fingerprinting research now lives inside Apple, and why Avery Wang and other former Shazam engineers appear as Apple researchers today.
