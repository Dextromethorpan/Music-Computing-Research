Avery Wang invented the audio fingerprinting algorithm that powers Shazam — a way of identifying a song from a few noisy seconds of audio captured on a phone. He holds a PhD in Electrical Engineering from Stanford and has more than 150 patents related to audio signal processing. After Apple's acquisition of Shazam in 2018, he continues this work as a Principal Research Scientist at Apple, focused on robust audio search and recognition technology.
