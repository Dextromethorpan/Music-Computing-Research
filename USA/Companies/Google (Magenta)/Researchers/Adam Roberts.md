Adam Roberts is a longtime engineer on the Magenta team, co-authoring core projects including NSynth, DDSP, and MusicLM. He has focused on building and scaling the training infrastructure and open-source implementations that let Magenta's generative audio models actually be released, reproduced, and used by outside researchers and musicians.
