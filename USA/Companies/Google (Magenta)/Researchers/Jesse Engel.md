Jesse Engel has been a lead research scientist on Magenta since joining Google Brain in 2016 (now part of Google DeepMind). He works on generative models for audio, including NSynth and DDSP, and on symbolic (note-based) music generation like MusicVAE, as well as bridging research and real musician-facing tools such as Magenta Studio.
