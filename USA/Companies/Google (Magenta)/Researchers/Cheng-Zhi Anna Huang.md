Anna Huang is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind working on the Magenta project, and also holds an academic appointment at MIT. She is the creator of Music Transformer, a landmark model for generating symbolic (note-based) music with coherent long-term structure, and of Coconet, the model behind Google's Bach Doodle. Her research broadly explores generative models and interfaces that support human musical creativity.
