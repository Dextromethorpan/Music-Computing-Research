Douglas Eck is one of the founders of the Magenta project at Google, working out of San Francisco with Google DeepMind. He has driven Magenta's overall research direction of using machine learning as a creative tool for musicians and artists, and has co-authored many of the team's foundational papers, including Music Transformer and NSynth.
