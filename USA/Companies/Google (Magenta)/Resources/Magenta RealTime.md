Magenta RealTime (Magenta RT) is an open-weights AI model, released in June 2025, that generates music live, in a continuous stream, instead of producing a finished clip all at once and only then playing it back. It is an 800-million-parameter "transformer" model (a type of neural network good at handling long sequences, here sequences of audio) trained on roughly 190,000 hours of mostly instrumental stock music. It builds each next short chunk of audio based on the audio it already generated plus a "style" input, so a person can nudge the music in a new direction while it keeps playing — similar in spirit to a live DJ set. Unlike Google's closed products, its code and weights are published so anyone can run or study it, including on free cloud GPUs/TPUs.
