Magenta is the umbrella open-source code library (built on TensorFlow, Google's machine learning framework) that houses most of the models the Magenta team has published, including NSynth, Music Transformer, MusicVAE, and Magenta Studio (a set of downloadable plug-ins that let musicians use these AI models directly inside music production software). The GitHub repository is the practical, code-level home of the project, in contrast to the individual research papers, and is what allowed thousands of outside musicians, students, and developers to actually experiment with Magenta's generative models rather than just read about them.
