Magenta is a research project, originally started within Google Brain and now continued under Google DeepMind / Google Research, that explores how machine learning can be used as a tool in the creative process for music and art. Its focus spans symbolic music generation, neural audio synthesis, differentiable signal processing, and — most recently — real-time streaming text-to-music models.
