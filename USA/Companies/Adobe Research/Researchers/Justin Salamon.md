Justin Salamon leads Adobe's Sound Design AI (SODA) group, which builds AI models that can generate and understand sound for creative video and audio work — for example, models that can create sound effects from a text description or match music to the mood of a video. Before Adobe he was a Senior Research Scientist at New York University, where he worked on machine listening (getting computers to recognize and classify real-world sounds), sound event detection, and bioacoustics (studying animal sounds, e.g. bird migration monitoring). His Adobe publication record spans text-to-audio generation, audio-visual sound effect retrieval, and music-to-video correspondence.
