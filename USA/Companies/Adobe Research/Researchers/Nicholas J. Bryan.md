Nicholas J. Bryan leads Adobe's Music AI research efforts, working on generative AI for music — systems that can create, remix, or edit musical audio, including projects like a music co-creation tool previewed by Adobe Research. His broader research background covers audio and speech AI more generally, including co-authored work on controllable neural speech synthesis (AI-generated speech where you can adjust qualities like tone or pacing) and audio similarity/quality metrics. He holds a PhD from Stanford University's Center for Computer Research in Music and Acoustics (CCRMA).
