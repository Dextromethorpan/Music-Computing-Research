Zeyu Jin works on AI models that generate and edit human speech — his focus areas include studio-quality speech enhancement (cleaning up bad recordings), speech quality assessment (getting AI to judge how "good" a recording sounds), and personalized voice generation (making synthetic speech sound like a specific person). He was the lead researcher behind Project VoCo, the "Photoshop for voice" system demoed at Adobe MAX in 2016, and his later work fed directly into the Enhance Speech feature in Adobe Podcast. He holds a PhD in computer science from Princeton University.
