Jiaqi Su works on deep learning for audio and speech, focusing on problems like speech enhancement (removing noise), bandwidth extension (adding back missing high or low frequencies to make thin-sounding audio richer), acoustic matching (making two recordings sound like they were captured in the same room/microphone), and speech source separation (splitting a mixed recording into its individual voices or sounds). She was a lead author on the HiFi-GAN and HiFi-GAN-2 papers, which are foundational to the technology behind Adobe Podcast's Enhance Speech feature. She holds a PhD in computer science from Princeton University.
