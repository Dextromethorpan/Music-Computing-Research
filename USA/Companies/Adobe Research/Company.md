Adobe Research is the corporate research division of Adobe Inc. (maker of Photoshop, Premiere Pro, and Adobe Podcast). Its Audio group — including the Sound Design AI (SODA) team — builds AI for speech and sound: cleaning up noisy recordings, editing spoken narration by typing text, converting one voice into another, and generating sound effects, and this work ships into real products like Adobe Podcast's "Enhance Speech" feature.
