Published at Interspeech 2020 (a leading speech-research conference), this paper introduces a method for cleaning up recorded speech that suffers from two common problems: background noise ("denoising") and echo/room reverberation ("dereverberation," i.e. removing the boomy, hollow sound of a room). It uses a GAN — a "generative adversarial network," a type of AI model made of two competing neural networks, one that tries to produce realistic clean audio and one that tries to catch fakes — trained specifically so the output sounds natural to human ears rather than just technically "less noisy." This is one of the foundational research papers behind what later became Adobe Podcast's Enhance Speech feature.
