This Adobe Research blog post (with an embedded video interview) explains the story behind "Enhance Speech," the AI feature in Adobe Podcast that automatically cleans up bad-sounding audio — removing background noise, echo, and dullness — to make a recording sound like it was captured in a professional studio. Researchers Jiaqi Su and Zeyu Jin describe how the underlying speech-enhancement research (originally lab experiments) was turned into a tool used by millions of everyday podcasters and video creators. It's a good non-technical entry point into how Adobe turns academic-style audio research into shipped consumer products.
