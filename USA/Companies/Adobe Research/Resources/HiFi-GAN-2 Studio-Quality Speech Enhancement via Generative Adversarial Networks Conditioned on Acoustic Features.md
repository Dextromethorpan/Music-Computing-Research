Presented at WASPAA 2021 (a workshop on audio and acoustic signal processing), this paper is a follow-up to the original HiFi-GAN work and pushes speech enhancement further toward "studio quality" — meaning audio that sounds like it was recorded with professional equipment in a treated room, even if the original recording was made on a phone in a noisy space. The system is "conditioned on acoustic features," meaning the AI model is guided by extracted properties of the sound (like pitch and tone) so it can better preserve the speaker's natural voice while removing unwanted noise and echo. This research directly informed the AI behind Adobe Podcast's Enhance Speech tool.
