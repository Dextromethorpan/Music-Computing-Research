Published at ICASSP 2024 (a top conference for speech and signal processing), this paper tackles "voice conversion" — changing a recording so it sounds like it was spoken by a different person, while keeping the same words and delivery. "Zero-shot" means the system can convert to a brand-new target voice from just a short sample, without needing to be specially retrained for that voice first. It uses "self-supervised learning," a training approach where the AI model learns useful patterns from raw audio without needing humans to manually label every example. This kind of research underlies features like dubbing a video into another language while keeping the original speaker's vocal character.
