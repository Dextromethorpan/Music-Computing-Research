Rachel Bittner leads applied machine-learning-on-audio research at Spotify, focusing on automatic music transcription (turning a recording into notes), musical source separation (splitting a song into its individual instrument or vocal tracks), and building datasets used to train these kinds of models. She led the research and development behind Basic Pitch, Spotify's open-source tool that converts audio recordings into MIDI note data, and is a co-author of the LLark multimodal music-language-model paper. She holds a PhD from the Music and Audio Research Lab at New York University.
