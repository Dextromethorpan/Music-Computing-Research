Peter Sobot leads audio-engineering research work at Spotify, building software tools that let machine-learning teams and engineers process and manipulate sound. He created Pedalboard, Spotify's open-source Python library for applying studio-quality audio effects, which is now used both internally (for example, to prepare training data for machine-learning models) and by outside developers and musicians. He has also worked on other Spotify audio and recommendation systems, including early work on Discover Weekly, Spotify's personalized playlist feature.
