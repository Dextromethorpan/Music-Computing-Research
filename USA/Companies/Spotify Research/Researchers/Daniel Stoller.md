Daniel Stoller works on music source separation — building neural networks that can take a finished song and split it back into its individual instrument and vocal tracks — and on multimodal models that connect audio with language. He co-authored early influential source-separation papers (including "Wave-U-Net") and is a co-author of Spotify's LLark multimodal music-language-model paper, as well as the "Understanding by Unmixing" research blog post.
