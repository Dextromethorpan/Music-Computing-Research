Simon Durand works on Music Information Retrieval (the general field of getting computers to automatically understand things about music, like genre, instruments, or structure), audio signal processing, and machine learning for music recommendation. He co-authored the LLark paper on teaching a language model to understand and discuss music, and earlier work on identifying which instruments are playing in a recording. Before joining Spotify, he worked at Stability AI on music-generation models including Stable Audio.
