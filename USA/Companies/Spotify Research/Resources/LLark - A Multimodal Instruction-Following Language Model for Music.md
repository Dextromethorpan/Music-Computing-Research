LLark is a research project that connects a music-understanding model with a large language model (the kind of AI behind chatbots), so that a person can ask natural-language questions about a piece of music and get a natural-language answer back. "Multimodal" means the AI combines more than one type of input — here, actual audio and text — instead of working with just one. "Instruction-following" means the model is trained to respond usefully to a wide range of typed requests, like "describe the mood of this song" or "what instruments are playing here," rather than only doing one fixed task. To build it, the researchers first had to turn many existing open music datasets into a large collection of question-and-answer style training examples. In tests, LLark matched or beat prior specialized systems at understanding music, and its captions and explanations agreed well with what human listeners said. The paper was published at ICML 2024, a leading machine-learning conference, and the code is public.
