This blog post summarizes eight research papers Spotify presented at RecSys 2025, the main academic conference for "recommender systems" (software that predicts what a user might want next, such as which song, podcast, or playlist to suggest). The post groups the work into two themes: helping people search for and discover content in smarter ways (using things like AI "agents" that can take multiple steps to satisfy a request, and synthetic — artificially generated — training data), and building the next generation of recommendation engines using generative AI models (systems that create new outputs rather than just picking from a fixed list) and multimodal large language models. One highlighted project uses natural-language summaries of a listener's habits to help an AI judge whether podcast recommendations are actually good, without needing constant human graders. This resource is a useful, beginner-readable snapshot of where Spotify's recommendation research is headed.
