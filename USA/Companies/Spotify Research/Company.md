Spotify Research is the internal research organization of Spotify, the music and audio streaming company. Its scientists and engineers publish work on music recommendation systems, generative and agentic AI, audio machine learning, and Music Information Retrieval (getting computers to automatically understand things about audio, like which notes or instruments are present), and also release open-source audio tools used well beyond Spotify itself.
