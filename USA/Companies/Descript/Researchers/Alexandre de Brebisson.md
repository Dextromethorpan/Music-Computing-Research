Alexandre de Brébisson co-founded and served as CEO of Lyrebird, the voice-cloning startup he started in 2017 with Kundan Kumar and Jose Sotelo while a PhD student at MILA (University of Montreal) under Yoshua Bengio. After Descript acquired Lyrebird in 2019, he continued as an AI researcher there, contributing to speech-synthesis and waveform-generation research such as the MelGAN vocoder paper.
