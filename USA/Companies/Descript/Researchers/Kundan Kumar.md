Kundan Kumar co-founded Lyrebird in 2017 while studying deep learning at MILA (University of Montreal) under Yoshua Bengio, and after Descript's 2019 acquisition of Lyrebird he led Descript's AI team for several years, shipping AI features including Overdub. His research spans speech synthesis and GAN-based waveform generation, including the Char2Wav and MelGAN papers.
