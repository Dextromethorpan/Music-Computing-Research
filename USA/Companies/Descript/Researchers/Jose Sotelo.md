Jose Sotelo co-founded the voice-cloning startup Lyrebird in 2017 while a PhD student at MILA (University of Montreal) under Yoshua Bengio, and stayed on as a researcher after Descript acquired Lyrebird in 2019. He has worked on end-to-end speech synthesis (Char2Wav) and GAN-based vocoders (MelGAN), and continues to appear as an author on Descript's current audio-generation research, including its 2026 neural audio codec work (PoDAR).
