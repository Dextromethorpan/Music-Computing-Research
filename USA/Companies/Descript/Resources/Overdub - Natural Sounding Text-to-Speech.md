Overdub is Descript's shipped voice-cloning feature, built from the Lyrebird acquisition, that lets a user create a synthetic version of their own voice and then generate new spoken audio for it just by typing text. To use it, a person records a consent statement plus roughly 10+ minutes of sample speech; the system first learns general patterns of human speech from many speakers, then fine-tunes on the individual's voice so it can generate new sentences that person never actually said. This page is Descript's own product description of the feature and gives a beginner-friendly, concrete look at how voice cloning is packaged for real users (podcasters and video editors) rather than left as a research demo.
