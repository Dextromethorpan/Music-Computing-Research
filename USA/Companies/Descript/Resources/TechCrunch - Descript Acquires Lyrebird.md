This TechCrunch article covers Descript's September 2019 acquisition of Lyrebird, the Montreal voice-cloning startup founded by three MILA (University of Montreal) PhD researchers, alongside a $15 million funding round. It explains why Descript wanted Lyrebird's voice-synthesis technology: to let podcast and video editors fix or add spoken lines by typing text rather than re-recording, using a synthetic clone of their own voice — the feature that became Overdub. The article also notes the deliberate guardrail Descript built in: Overdub can only clone a voice with that person's own recorded consent, so the technology can't be used to impersonate someone else's voice without their permission.
