Descript is an AI-powered audio/video editing company best known for Overdub, a voice-cloning feature that lets creators fix or add spoken lines by typing text in a synthetic clone of their own voice. Its research roots trace to Lyrebird, a Montreal voice-cloning startup founded by MILA (University of Montreal) PhD researchers under Yoshua Bengio, which Descript acquired in 2019; the founding team's speech-synthesis research continues today under Descript Research, most recently in neural audio-editing systems that regenerate edited speech seamlessly.
