Andy Sarroff is a research engineer who worked on machine learning for audio at iZotope, with a PhD in Computer Science from Dartmouth College (dissertation on complex-valued neural networks for audio) and an MM in Music Technology from NYU. Before iZotope he was a visiting researcher at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL) and Columbia's LabROSA speech and audio group. He has chaired the Audio Engineering Society's Technical Committee on Machine Learning and AI for Audio and has served on the board of the International Society for Music Information Retrieval.
