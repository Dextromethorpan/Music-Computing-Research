Jean-Marc Jot is an internationally recognized expert in 3D and spatial audio, sound spatialization, and artificial reverberation, who joined iZotope in 2020 as Vice President of Research and Chief Scientist to lead its expansion into machine learning and DSP research. Earlier in his career he was a Research Scientist at IRCAM, Research Fellow at Creative Labs, and Senior VP of R&D at DTS; he also founded the audio technology consultancy Virtuel Works. He has since moved on to an audio technology innovation role at Apple, and co-chairs the Audio Engineering Society's Technical Committee on Spatial Audio.
