Alexey Lukin is a longtime iZotope DSP (digital signal processing) engineer, often called the "godfather" of iZotope's audio-repair tool RX, and a core contributor to the Ozone mastering plugin. He holds a Ph.D. in computer science from Lomonosov Moscow State University and specializes in spectral analysis, noise reduction, and reverb-related signal processing, with over 30 published papers and several patents. He has more recently helped develop neural-network-based modules such as Unlimiter and Stem EQ in Ozone.
