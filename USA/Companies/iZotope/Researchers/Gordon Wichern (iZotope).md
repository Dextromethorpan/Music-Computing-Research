Gordon Wichern worked as a DSP engineer at iZotope before moving to Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL) in 2018, where he is now a Principal Research Scientist. While at iZotope he co-authored work on automatic level adjustment in mixing, quantitative masking analysis in multitrack mixes (the research behind the sound-masking patent below), and recurrent-neural-network-based removal of lavalier microphone rustle noise. His research sits at the intersection of signal processing and machine learning applied to speech, music, and environmental sound.
