iZotope, founded in Cambridge, Massachusetts and now part of the Native Instruments Group (via Boris FX), makes audio mixing, mastering, and repair software (Ozone, Neutron, RX, Nectar). It runs a dedicated in-house Research Team publishing peer-reviewed digital signal processing (DSP) and machine learning work — much of it presented at DAFx and AES — that powers assistive, AI-driven features across its products, such as automatic mix balancing, sound masking detection, and reverb matching.
