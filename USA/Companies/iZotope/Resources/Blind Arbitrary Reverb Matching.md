"Reverb" is the echo-like trail a sound leaves as it bounces around a physical space; every room has its own reverb "fingerprint." This iZotope Research paper, presented at DAFx (Digital Audio Effects) 2020, tackles "reverb matching": automatically adding artificial reverb to a dry (untreated) audio track so it sounds like it was recorded in the same room as a separate reference recording, without needing to know anything about that room in advance ("blind" matching, arbitrary meaning any room or reverb type). The authors built a model architecture for this task and ran listening tests showing it can perform close to a skilled human engineer doing the same job by ear. This technology underlies iZotope's Dialogue Match plug-in, which helps film/TV dialogue editors and re-recording mixers make dialogue from different takes or locations sound like it belongs in the same scene.
