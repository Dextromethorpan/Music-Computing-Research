"Music Rebalance" is a tool in iZotope's RX 7 audio-repair software that lets an editor turn up, turn down, or fully isolate individual musical elements — like vocals, bass, or percussion — inside an already-finished, mixed-down song, even though those elements were never stored separately. This blog post, written by iZotope's Research Team, explains the underlying technology: a neural network (a machine-learning model loosely inspired by how brain cells connect) trained on many songs to recognize and separate different instrument types, a task known as source separation. The post walks through why this is difficult (the network has to "guess" the boundaries between sounds that were mixed together) and how iZotope's approach extended their earlier voice/background separation research to handle full musical mixes with several instrument categories at once.
