"Dereverberation" means removing the echo-like effect a room adds to a recording (called reverberation, or reverb), so that a voice recorded in a boomy or echoey space sounds cleaner and closer, as if recorded somewhere quieter. This iZotope Research Team paper, presented at DAFx (Digital Audio Effects), a leading academic audio-processing conference, shows how a technique normally used for separating mixed sound sources can be adapted for this problem instead, training a "bidirectional recurrent neural network" (a neural network that looks both backward and forward in time through the audio) to strip out reverb from speech.
