AudioCraft is Meta's open-source software library that bundles together its main generative audio models — MusicGen (text-to-music), AudioGen (text-to-sound-effect), and EnCodec (the neural audio codec/tokenizer that both of the others are built on). Releasing it as a single, well-documented open-source package (rather than only publishing separate research papers) let outside developers, musicians, and researchers actually run, fine-tune, and build products on top of Meta's audio generation research.
