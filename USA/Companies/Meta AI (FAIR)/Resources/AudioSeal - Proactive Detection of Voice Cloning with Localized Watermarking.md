AudioSeal is a "watermarking" system for AI-generated speech: it embeds an invisible (inaudible) signal into audio as it's generated, so that later, a detector can check whether a given clip was produced by an AI model and even pinpoint exactly which parts of the clip are AI-made, down to individual fractions of a second. This is aimed at problems like detecting voice cloning (an AI mimicking someone's voice) and other forms of AI audio misuse. Its detector is designed to be extremely fast, checking audio up to about 100 times faster than comparable prior systems, which matters for scanning large volumes of audio in real time.
