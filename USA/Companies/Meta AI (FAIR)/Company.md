Meta AI (through its Fundamental AI Research, or FAIR, division) is Meta's research organization, headquartered in Menlo Park, California, with additional labs including one in Paris. Its audio and music teams have published widely-used open-source work in neural audio compression (EnCodec), text-to-music generation (MusicGen, part of the AudioCraft framework), music source separation (Demucs), and audio watermarking for detecting AI-generated speech (AudioSeal).
