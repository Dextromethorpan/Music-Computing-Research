Alexandre Défossez worked as a research scientist at Meta AI/FAIR from 2020 to 2023, where he created Demucs (music source separation), co-created EnCodec (neural audio compression), and led the effort behind MusicGen and the broader AudioCraft framework. His research centers on audio processing and generation, source separation, and optimization for deep learning. He has since moved on to the AI research lab Kyutai.
