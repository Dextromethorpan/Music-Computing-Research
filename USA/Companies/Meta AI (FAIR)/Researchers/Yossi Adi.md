Yossi Adi is a research scientist with Meta AI's FAIR team who also holds a faculty position at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. He co-authored EnCodec and MusicGen, and his broader research develops and analyzes machine learning algorithms for speech, audio, and language, including audio watermarking and detection of AI-generated audio.
