The Center for New Music and Audio Technologies (CNMAT) is UC Berkeley's research center at the intersection of music composition and audio technology, founded in the 1980s and modeled in part on Stanford's CCRMA and IRCAM in Paris. CNMAT is known for developing widely adopted, freely released standards and tools — most famously Open Sound Control — and today works on sensor-based instruments, computer-assisted orchestration, and AI as a creative collaborator.
