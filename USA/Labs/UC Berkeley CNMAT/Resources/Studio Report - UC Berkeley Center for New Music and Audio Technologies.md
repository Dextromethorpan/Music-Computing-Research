This "studio report" is a conference-style overview document describing CNMAT's facilities, ongoing research programs, and recent tools at the time of writing (2008) — a format common in computer music conferences where labs periodically summarize their equipment and work for the wider research community. It covers areas like new sensor technologies for musical interfaces, many-channel digital audio systems, and CNMAT's software tools including Open Sound World and its Max/MSP externals, giving a snapshot of how a major computer music research center organizes its hardware, software, and research priorities.
