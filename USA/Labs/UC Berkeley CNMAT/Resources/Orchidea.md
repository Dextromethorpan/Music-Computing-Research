Orchidea is software for "computer-assisted orchestration": a composer describes or provides a target sound (say, dripping water, or any other complex sound), and the software searches through a large database of real instrument recordings to find a combination of instruments, notes, and playing techniques that — played together by a live orchestra — will recreate a close musical approximation of that target sound. It uses machine learning and mathematical optimization to search efficiently through enormous numbers of possible instrument combinations. The project is a joint effort between CNMAT, IRCAM in Paris, and the Haute École de Musique in Geneva, led by CNMAT's Carmine-Emanuele Cella.
