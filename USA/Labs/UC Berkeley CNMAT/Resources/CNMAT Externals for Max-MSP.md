The CNMAT Externals are a long-running collection of additional building-block objects ("externals") for Max/MSP, the widely used visual programming environment for music and multimedia. They add capabilities Max doesn't include out of the box — including OSC message handling, additional synthesis and analysis algorithms, and tools that grew directly out of CNMAT's research into sensors and audio description formats. They are one of the more concrete, hands-on ways CNMAT's research work has been distributed for other musicians and developers to actually use in their own patches.
