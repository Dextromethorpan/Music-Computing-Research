Edmund Campion is a composer and CNMAT's director, focused on electroacoustic music, computer-assisted composition, and real-time interactive performance systems that combine digital sound synthesis with live performers. He joined CNMAT as composer-in-residence in 1996, became director in 2014, and is preparing to hand leadership to co-director Carmine-Emanuele Cella upon his planned 2027 retirement.
