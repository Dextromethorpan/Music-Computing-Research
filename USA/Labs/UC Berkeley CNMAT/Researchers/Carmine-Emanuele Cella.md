Carmine-Emanuele Cella is a composer with a PhD in applied mathematics who leads Orchidea, a computer-assisted orchestration tool that uses machine learning to help composers turn a target sound into a playable orchestral score. He is CNMAT's co-director and is set to take over full leadership of the center from Edmund Campion around 2027, with plans to expand CNMAT's work on generative audio systems and "explicable AI" (making AI music tools more understandable and accountable).
