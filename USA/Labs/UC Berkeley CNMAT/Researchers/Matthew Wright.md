Matthew Wright co-invented Open Sound Control with Adrian Freed while working as CNMAT's Musical Systems Designer, and also helped develop and promote the Sound Description Interchange Format (SDIF). He later became Technical Director of Stanford's CCRMA and worked on interactive digital musical instrument design across a nearly 30-year career in computer music research.
