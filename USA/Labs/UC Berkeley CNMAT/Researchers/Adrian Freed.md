Adrian Freed directed CNMAT's research program for nearly three decades, co-creating Open Sound Control and the Sound Description Interchange Format (SDIF), and building many of CNMAT's Max/MSP tools. His work centered on new sensor technologies for musical interfaces and on formats/protocols that let different computer music software and hardware interoperate; he now works as a senior software engineer at Adobe.
