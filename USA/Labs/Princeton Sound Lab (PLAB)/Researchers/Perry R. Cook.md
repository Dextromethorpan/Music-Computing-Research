Cook's research centers on physical modeling sound synthesis (simulating the physics of real instruments and voices to generate sound computationally), audio signal analysis, and interfaces for musical performance. He co-created the ChucK language and the Synthesis ToolKit (STK), advised numerous PhD students who became major figures in computer music, and co-founded the Princeton Laptop Orchestra (PLOrk) with Dan Trueman in 2005.
