Wang designed and built ChucK, the on-the-fly, "strongly-timed" audio programming language central to much of the Sound Lab's later output. At Stanford's CCRMA he continued this line of work, co-founding the Stanford Laptop Orchestra (SLOrk) and researching programming languages and interactive software design for music.
