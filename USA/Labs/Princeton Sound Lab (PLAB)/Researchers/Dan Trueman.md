Trueman is a composer and violinist whose research bridges acoustic performance practice and electronic/computational music, including designing spherical speakers that radiate sound in all directions the way acoustic instruments do. He co-founded the Princeton Laptop Orchestra (PLOrk) with Perry Cook in 2005, both as a teaching lab and a performing ensemble, and continues to compose for laptop-ensemble and hybrid acoustic-electronic performance.
