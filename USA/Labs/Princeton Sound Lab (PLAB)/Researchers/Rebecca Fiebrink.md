Fiebrink's research applies machine learning to music and interactive performance systems, most notably creating Wekinator, a tool that lets musicians train gesture-to-sound mappings by demonstration rather than by writing explicit code. Her Sound Lab-era work with Trueman and Cook laid the groundwork for interactive, on-the-fly machine learning in creative-coding contexts.
