Snyder is a composer, instrument designer, and improviser who took over direction of the Princeton Laptop Orchestra (PLOrk) in 2013, continuing its tradition while expanding it with new self-designed electronic instruments. He heads Princeton's New Instrument Research Lab (NIRL), focusing on new physical interfaces for electronic music and embedded synthesis hardware.
