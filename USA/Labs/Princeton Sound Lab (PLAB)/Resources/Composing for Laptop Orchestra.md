Published in Computer Music Journal, this article reflects on the artistic challenges of writing music specifically for a laptop orchestra, where the "instruments" are software rather than physical objects. The authors discuss how composers had to invent new notation and coordination methods since laptop performers do not get the same physical, tactile feedback that string or wind players do. It covers questions like how to give an ensemble of computers a shared musical feel, and how to design pieces that highlight — rather than hide — the fact that performers are using laptops. It is considered a key reference for laptop-ensemble composition as its own artistic practice.
