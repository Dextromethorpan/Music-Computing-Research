STK is a free, open-source set of C++ building blocks for making computer-generated sound and music, especially using "physical modeling" synthesis — a technique that simulates the physics of a real instrument (like a vibrating string or a blown pipe) with math and code rather than playing back recorded samples. Programmers use STK's pre-built pieces — virtual strings, reeds, filters, and more — to build new instruments and audio effects instead of writing the underlying digital signal processing (DSP) code from scratch. It has been used for decades in academic computer music courses and research worldwide, and is one of the most widely cited software packages to come out of Cook's research group.
