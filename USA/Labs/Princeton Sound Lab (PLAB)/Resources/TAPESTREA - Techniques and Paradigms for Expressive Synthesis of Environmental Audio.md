TAPESTREA is a sound-design tool for taking a recording of a real-world soundscape (like a park or a busy street) and breaking it apart into separate elements — for example isolating a bird call from traffic noise — so a composer can recombine, edit, or "recompose" them into new sound scenes. It works by analyzing a recording to detect distinct sound events and background textures, then letting the user manipulate each piece independently, somewhat like layers in an image-editing program but for sound. It won the 2006 ICMA Distinguished Paper Award and is a well-cited example of interactive environmental sound-scene modeling software.
