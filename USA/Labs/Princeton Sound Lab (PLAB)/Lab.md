The Princeton Sound Lab is a research group jointly affiliated with Princeton University's Department of Computer Science and Department of Music. It focuses on computer music broadly: physical modeling sound synthesis, real-time audio programming languages (most famously ChucK), interactive controller/instrument design, and collaborative laptop-ensemble performance — including founding the Princeton Laptop Orchestra (PLOrk) in 2005.
