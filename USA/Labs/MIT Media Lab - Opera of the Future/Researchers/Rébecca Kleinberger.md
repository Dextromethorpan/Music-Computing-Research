Rébecca Kleinberger did her graduate research in the Opera of the Future group, where she studied how people relate to their own voice, including the "Vocal Vibrations" project that lets people feel their singing as touch. She earned her PhD on the future of voice technology at MIT and is now a professor at Northeastern University, where she continues researching voice, sound, and even cross-species communication (studying how animals and humans might "talk").
