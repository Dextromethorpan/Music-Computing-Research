Nicole L'Huillier is a Chilean artist, musician, and architect who did her PhD in the Opera of the Future group, completing it in 2022. Her research and art practice explore sound and vibration as physical, almost sculptural materials, examining how sound shapes our sense of space, identity, and connection to others. She now works as a researcher and artist bridging MIT's Media Lab and Art, Culture and Technology programs.
