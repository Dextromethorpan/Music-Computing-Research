Elena "Elly" Jessop earned her PhD in the Opera of the Future group, focusing on capturing expressive human movement and voice for live performance. She built the "Vocal Augmentation and Manipulation Prosthesis (VAMP)," a conducting-based controller for vocal performance, and worked as interaction designer on "Death and the Powers," helping build the robotic Chandelier instrument and the Disembodied Performance system. She later worked in software engineering, including at Apple.
