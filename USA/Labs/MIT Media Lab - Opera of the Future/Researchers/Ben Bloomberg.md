Ben Bloomberg has worked with Tod Machover since 2007, first as a student and later as sound designer and technologist for the group's operas (including "Death and the Powers" and "Schoenberg in Hollywood") and for six of the "City Symphonies" projects. His PhD research focused on how to design live audio production systems that guide what an audience hears and pays attention to during a performance. Outside MIT, he has designed live-show technology for major touring musicians, including Björk and Jacob Collier.
