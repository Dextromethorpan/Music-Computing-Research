Peter Torpey earned his PhD in the Opera of the Future group, where he built the "Media Scores" framework and the "Disembodied Performance" system used in Tod Machover's opera "Death and the Powers." His work focused on live, multimodal performance: capturing an actor's voice, gestures, and movement in real time and translating them into robotic and visual effects on stage. He now works independently as a "media experience artist," designing technology-driven experiences at the intersection of theater, music, and storytelling.
