Tod Machover is a composer and inventor who founded and leads the Opera of the Future group. He has spent decades building new musical instruments and technologies (starting with "Hyperinstruments" in the 1980s) and using them in his own operas and orchestral works, including the robotic opera "Death and the Powers" and the crowdsourced "City Symphonies" series. His broader goal is to use technology to make deep musical expression accessible to everyone, from world-class virtuosos to people with disabilities to entire city populations.
