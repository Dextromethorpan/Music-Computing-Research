This NIME (New Interfaces for Musical Expression) paper explains the technology built for Tod Machover's opera "Death and the Powers," in which a human actor's final moments are represented by a stage full of robots, lights, and a giant instrument called "The Chandelier" that respond live to his voice, gestures, and body signals. The authors describe how sensors captured the actor's performance in real time and translated it into sound, light, and robotic movement, so the technology itself became a character in the story. It shows how live performance technology can be designed not just as a tool but as an expressive, almost living part of a theatrical work.
