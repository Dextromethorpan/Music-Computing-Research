"Death and the Powers" is a full opera composed by Tod Machover that premiered in Monaco in 2010 and was later a finalist for the Pulitzer Prize. It tells the story of a dying inventor who tries to upload his mind into a machine to live forever. What makes it a landmark music-technology project is its staging: nine singing robots, an animatronic (robot-controlled) set, and a large musical instrument called "The Chandelier" that responds to touch and to the human voice, all built by the Opera of the Future research group. Many of the interactive technologies developed for this opera (robotic performers, real-time sensor tracking of actors) were later reused in other MIT Media Lab projects.
