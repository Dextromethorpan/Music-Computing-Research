This NIME (New Interfaces for Musical Expression) conference paper describes an installation that lets people feel their own singing voice as physical vibrations on their body, not just hear it. A person sings or hums, a system captures their voice, and the vibrations are played back through touch (for example on a chair or handheld object) so the singer experiences their own sound as a tactile sensation. The idea is to deepen self-awareness and emotional connection to one's voice, and it has since informed work in music therapy and wellness. It is a good example of "multisensory" design: using more than one sense (sound plus touch) to create a richer musical experience.
