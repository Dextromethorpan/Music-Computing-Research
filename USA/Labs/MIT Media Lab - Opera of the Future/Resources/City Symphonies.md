City Symphonies is an ongoing series of community art projects where Tod Machover and his team invite the residents of a city (so far including Toronto, Edinburgh, Perth, Detroit, and Philadelphia) to record everyday sounds around them — car engines, local music, street noise, voices — using a phone app. Thousands of these submitted sounds and short musical ideas are then woven by Machover into a real orchestral symphony that premieres with a professional orchestra, so the piece is literally made "by and for" the city's own people. The Detroit edition, called "Symphony in D," collected more than 15,000 sound samples from residents. It is a large-scale example of crowdsourced composition: turning public participation and everyday sound into a piece of formal orchestral music.
