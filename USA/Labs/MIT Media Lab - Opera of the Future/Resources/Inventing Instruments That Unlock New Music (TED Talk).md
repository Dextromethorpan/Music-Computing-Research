In this TED talk, Tod Machover explains why he builds new kinds of musical instruments and software, then brings on stage Dan Ellsey, a composer with cerebral palsy who cannot walk or speak but who writes and conducts his own music. Ellsey does this using "Hyperscore," a visual composition program developed at the Opera of the Future group that lets someone draw shapes and colors on screen to build a piece of music, instead of needing to read or write traditional notation or play an instrument physically. The talk is a clear, human illustration of the group's core mission: making music technology that opens up creative expression to people who would otherwise be excluded from it, including people with disabilities.
