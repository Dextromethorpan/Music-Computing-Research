Opera of the Future is a research group at the MIT Media Lab (Massachusetts Institute of Technology), led by composer Tod Machover. The group invents new technologies — sensor-based instruments, robotic performers, haptic (touch-based) devices, and crowdsourced composition tools — to expand who can make music and how it can be experienced, spanning professional opera to community art projects and music therapy.
