Takako Fujioka directs CCRMA's Neuromusic Lab, where she studies how the brain processes music using non-invasive brain-recording methods (EEG and MEG, which measure the brain's electrical and magnetic activity from outside the head). Her work looks at rhythm and melody perception, how the brain and body coordinate during music-making, and how music-based training might help rehabilitation after conditions like stroke or Parkinson's disease.
