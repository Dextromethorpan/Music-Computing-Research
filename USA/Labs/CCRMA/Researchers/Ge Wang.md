Ge Wang created the ChucK music programming language, which lets people write and modify sound-generating code live during a performance ("live coding"). He founded the Stanford Laptop Orchestra (SLOrk) and researches programming languages, interaction design, and mobile music; outside academia he co-founded Smule and designed popular music apps such as Ocarina and Magic Piano.
