Julius Smith is a pioneer of digital waveguide synthesis, a physical-modeling technique for simulating instruments like strings and wind instruments efficiently in software, which was later adopted in commercial synthesizers such as the Yamaha VL-1. His research spans digital filter design and signal processing applied to music and audio, and he is also a co-inventor of the core audio-fingerprinting algorithm behind the music-identification service Shazam.
