Romain Michon teaches and researches the Faust programming language at CCRMA, a specialized language for building audio effects and synthesizers. He builds toolkits that make it easier to design "physical models" of instruments (software simulations based on the physics of real instruments) and has taught CCRMA courses on physical interaction design for music and Faust-based signal processing. He is also affiliated with GRAME and INRIA in France, where Faust was originally developed.
