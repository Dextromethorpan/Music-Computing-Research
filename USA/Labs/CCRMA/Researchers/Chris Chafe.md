Chris Chafe is a composer, cellist, and researcher who leads CCRMA's SoundWIRE group, studying how musicians can perform together over the internet in real time. He co-created JackTrip, software used worldwide for low-delay networked music performance, and has researched exactly how much timing delay ("latency") people can tolerate before remote musical collaboration starts to feel unsynchronized.
