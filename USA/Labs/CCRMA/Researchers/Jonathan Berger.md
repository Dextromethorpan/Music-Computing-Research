Jonathan Berger is a composer and Director of CCRMA. He writes opera, orchestral, chamber, and electro-acoustic music, and his academic work explores the intersection of composition, music theory, and cognition — including how listeners perceive and process musical structure. He also directs CCRMA's overall research and academic program at Stanford.
