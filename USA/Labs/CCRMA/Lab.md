CCRMA (Center for Computer Research in Music and Acoustics) is a research center within Stanford University's Department of Music, founded in the 1970s. It brings together engineers, composers, computer scientists, and psychologists to work on computer-based music technology, spanning digital signal processing, programming languages for sound, physical modeling synthesis, networked music performance, and the neuroscience of music.
