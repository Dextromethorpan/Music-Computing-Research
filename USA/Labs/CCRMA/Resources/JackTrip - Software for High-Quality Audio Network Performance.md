JackTrip is free, open-source software that lets musicians in different physical locations play together in real time over the internet, with the highest possible audio quality and the lowest possible delay ("latency"). It was created by CCRMA's SoundWIRE research group, led by Chris Chafe, which studies how far internet audio can be pushed before delay makes live musical collaboration impossible. JackTrip has been used for cross-continent concerts, remote lessons, and research into exactly how many milliseconds of delay a listener or performer can tolerate before timing feels "off."
