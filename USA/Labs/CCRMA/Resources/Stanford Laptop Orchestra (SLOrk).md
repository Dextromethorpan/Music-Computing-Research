SLOrk is an ensemble founded at CCRMA in 2008 where performers use laptop computers, paired with custom multi-speaker "hemispherical" speaker enclosures, as their musical instruments instead of violins or trumpets. Performers write and run their own sound-generating code (often in ChucK) live during the performance. SLOrk works both as a real concert ensemble and as a research platform and classroom for studying how people design and perform new digital instruments together as a group, inspired by the earlier Princeton Laptop Orchestra.
