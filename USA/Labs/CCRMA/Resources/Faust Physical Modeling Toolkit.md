This is a collection of ready-made building blocks, written by CCRMA researcher Romain Michon, for designing "physical models" of musical instruments in Faust — a specialized programming language for audio signal processing. Physical modeling means simulating the physics of a real instrument (like a vibrating string or a column of air in a flute) with mathematical equations, so the computer produces sound the way the real instrument would. The toolkit makes this easier by providing pre-built pieces (strings, reeds, resonators, etc.) that can be combined like LEGO blocks instead of deriving the physics equations yourself. It is used in CCRMA's teaching and in Michon's own instrument-design research.
