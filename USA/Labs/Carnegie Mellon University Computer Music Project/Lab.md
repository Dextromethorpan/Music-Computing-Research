The Computer Music Project in Carnegie Mellon's School of Computer Science began in 1982 when Roger Dannenberg joined the CS faculty, and studies how computing can serve music — from sound synthesis languages and real-time interactive performance systems to, more recently, machine learning for music understanding and generation led by Chris Donahue's Generative Creativity Lab (G-CLef).
