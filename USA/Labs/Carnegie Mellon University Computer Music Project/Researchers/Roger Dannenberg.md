Roger Dannenberg founded CMU's Computer Music Project in 1982 and has spent decades building programming languages and systems for computer music, including Nyquist (a sound synthesis and composition language), Arco (a real-time audio framework), and pioneering work on score following (automatically tracking a live musician's position in a written score). He also advised Dominic Mazzoni's creation of Audacity and continues to publish on interactive performance systems and, more recently, music understanding models like MERT.
