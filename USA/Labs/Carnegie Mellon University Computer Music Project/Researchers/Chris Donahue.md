Chris Donahue leads CMU's Generative Creativity Lab (G-CLef), which develops generative AI methods for music and audio — including controllable models for generating and understanding music — and studies how to deploy them as real-world creative tools that a broad, non-technical audience can actually use. He is also a part-time research scientist on Google DeepMind's Magenta team, which builds AI tools for music and art.
