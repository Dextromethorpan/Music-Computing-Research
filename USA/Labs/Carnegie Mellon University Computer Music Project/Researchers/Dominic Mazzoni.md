Dominic Mazzoni created Audacity in 1999 as a CMU graduate student under Roger Dannenberg's supervision, originally to visualize audio analysis algorithms for a research project. Audacity grew into one of the most widely downloaded free and open-source audio editors in the world; Mazzoni later worked as a software engineer at Google, JPL, and Apple.
