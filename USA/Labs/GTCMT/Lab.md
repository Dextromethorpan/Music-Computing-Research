The Georgia Tech Center for Music Technology (GTCMT), part of the Georgia Tech School of Music, is an interdisciplinary research center founded in 2009 that develops new musical technologies spanning robotics, AI, signal processing, and human-computer interaction. It is best known for founding director Gil Weinberg's Robotic Musicianship group, which builds robots and AI systems that listen to, improvise with, and physically perform music alongside humans, as well as music information retrieval (audio content analysis) research and computing/music education tools like EarSketch.
