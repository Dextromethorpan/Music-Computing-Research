Published in the journal Autonomous Robots, this paper is the fuller, peer-reviewed follow-up to the earlier conference report on Shimon, GTCMT's robotic marimba player. It explains in detail how Shimon's mechanical design and motion-control software work together, and introduces the idea of "anticipatory action" — the robot predicts where a human musician's playing is heading and times its own movements so the two can stay in sync, similar to how a drummer anticipates a bandmate's next beat rather than just reacting after the fact. This kind of physical, gesture-based coordination is what lets Shimon improvise convincingly alongside a live player instead of just following a fixed script.
