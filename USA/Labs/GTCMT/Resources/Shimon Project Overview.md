This is GTCMT's official project page describing Shimon, its four-armed marimba-playing robot, and its ongoing research goals. The page explains that Shimon is meant to combine human creativity with a computer's ability to calculate and generate musical patterns a person might not think of, producing music neither a human nor a computer alone would create. Because Shimon has a physical robot body rather than just speakers, audiences can visually see its arms move and anticipate its playing the way they would with a human bandmate — something the researchers argue speaker-based computer music systems cannot offer. Shimon has performed live at venues including the Kennedy Center and Google I/O, and the page lists the center's key publications and press coverage about the robot.
