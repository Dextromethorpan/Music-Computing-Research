In this TED talk, Gil Weinberg introduces general audiences to his work on "robotic musicianship" — robots, like Shimon, designed to make music alongside humans. He demonstrates how these robots listen to live playing and generate responses that are inspired by, but not copied from, human input, and discusses the broader philosophical question of whether a machine's musical output can be considered genuinely "creative" or whether creativity requires a human mind. The talk is a good, non-technical introduction to why GTCMT builds physical, listening robots instead of purely software-based music AI.
