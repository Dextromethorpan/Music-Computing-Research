This paper introduces Shimon, a robot built by GTCMT that plays a marimba (a wooden xylophone-like instrument) using four robotic arms. Unlike a robot that just plays a pre-programmed song, Shimon listens to a live human musician and improvises — meaning it makes up its musical response on the spot, the way a jazz musician reacts to their bandmates. The paper describes how the robot analyzes the sounds it hears and turns that analysis into physical arm movements to strike the marimba bars in time. This project was one of the first steps in GTCMT's larger research area of "robotic musicianship," which studies how robots can be genuine musical collaborators rather than tools.
