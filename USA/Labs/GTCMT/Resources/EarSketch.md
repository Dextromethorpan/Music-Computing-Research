EarSketch is a free, web-based learning environment created at Georgia Tech that teaches students to write real computer code (in Python or JavaScript) by using it to make music — remixing and arranging loops and samples through programming commands rather than a typical point-and-click music app. The idea is to make an otherwise abstract subject like coding feel immediately creative and personally meaningful, especially for students who wouldn't normally see themselves as "computer science people." It's aimed mainly at middle school through university level and has been studied in published education research showing it increases students' interest in and intent to continue with computing. EarSketch has reportedly been used by more than one million students worldwide and is a flagship example of combining music technology research with computer-science education.
