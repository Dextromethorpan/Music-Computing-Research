This is a textbook by Alexander Lerch, head of GTCMT's Music Informatics Group, that teaches the fundamentals of "audio content analysis" — the general problem of getting a computer to automatically extract useful information from a sound recording, such as its tempo, key, genre, which instruments are playing, or even its emotional mood. This overlaps heavily with a field called Music Information Retrieval (MIR), which is about searching, organizing, and understanding large collections of music using software rather than manual listening. The book walks through the standard building blocks used across the field: turning raw audio into numerical "features" a computer can work with, then applying pattern-recognition and machine-learning methods to those features to answer a specific question about the music. It is widely used as an entry point for students and engineers starting out in music information retrieval and audio machine learning.
