This paper describes two related systems for automatically composing music to match a film. The first has the robot Shimon watch a video and physically compose and perform a musical score for it live. The second, called DeepScore, is a software-only system that uses an LSTM (a type of neural network well-suited to learning patterns in sequences, like the shape of a story over time) to write a film score by first analyzing the movie's visuals — including characters, their emotions, and pacing — to build a computational understanding of the film's narrative arc, and then generating music to fit it. It's an example of GTCMT extending robotic musicianship research from live musical improvisation into AI-generated composition guided by non-musical, visual information.
