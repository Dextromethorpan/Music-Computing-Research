Mason Bretan was a PhD researcher at GTCMT who worked closely with Gil Weinberg on the Shimon robot for around seven years, focusing on deep learning methods that let Shimon compose its own original music rather than only improvising over human input. He trained neural networks on thousands of songs so Shimon could generate its own melodies and harmonic structure, and performed live with Shimon in venues such as the Kennedy Center as part of the "Shimon and Friends" tour.
