Claire Arthur is an associate professor at Georgia Tech who leads the Computational and Cognitive Musicology Lab (CCML), part of the GTCMT community. Her research combines music theory, music perception, and statistics to study how structural features of music (like melody and harmony) shape listeners' expectations, memory, and emotional response, and she publishes in venues such as Music Perception and ISMIR (the International Society for Music Information Retrieval).
