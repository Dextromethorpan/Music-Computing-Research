Gil Weinberg is the founding director of GTCMT and a professor in Georgia Tech's School of Music, where he leads the Robotic Musicianship research group. His work focuses on building robots and AI systems, most notably the marimba-playing robot Shimon, that can listen to music, improvise, and physically perform alongside humans. He also led the development of a robotic prosthetic drumming arm for amputee musician Jason Barnes, applying his robotic-musicianship research to help restore and enhance a person's ability to play music.
