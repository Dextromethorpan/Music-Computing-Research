Alexander Lerch heads GTCMT's Music Informatics Group, where he designs machine-learning algorithms that let computers analyze and understand audio and music — for example, automatically identifying musical structure or extracting information from a recording. Before joining Georgia Tech he co-founded zplane.development, a company whose music-processing algorithms have been licensed by companies including Ableton and Native Instruments, and he wrote the textbook "An Introduction to Audio Content Analysis."
