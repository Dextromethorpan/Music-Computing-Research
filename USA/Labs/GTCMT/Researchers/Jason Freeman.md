Jason Freeman is a composer and professor who chairs Georgia Tech's School of Music and is affiliated with GTCMT. His work uses technology to let audiences participate directly in creating music, such as compositions where audience members' movements or input shape a live performance, and he co-founded EarSketch, a free web tool that teaches coding through making music.
