JAMS is a standardized file format for storing the "annotations" (labels) attached to a piece of music, such as chord labels, beat positions, or genre tags. Before JAMS, every research group used its own ad hoc file format, which made it hard to share datasets or compare results between labs. JAMS solves this by giving everyone a common, structured way — based on JSON, a common text format used in software development — to store this information, which makes music information retrieval (MIR) research easier to reproduce and datasets easier to combine. It was first introduced at ISMIR 2014 and is still maintained.
