SONYC is a citywide network of sound sensors placed around New York City to constantly monitor noise pollution — an application of "machine listening" (AI that understands sound) that goes beyond music, aimed at helping city agencies act on noise complaints. The related SONYC-UST dataset takes recordings from these sensors and has them labeled, by experts and citizen volunteers, with 23 categories of noise such as car horns, construction, or idling engines, so AI models can be trained to recognize these sounds automatically. The project was funded by a $4.6 million National Science Foundation grant and is a joint effort between MARL and NYU's Center for Urban Science and Progress.
