MedleyDB is a collection of royalty-free songs where every individual instrument track (vocals, guitar, drums, etc.) is kept separate, plus hand-labeled information like the melody's pitch over time and which instrument is playing when. This matters because most music datasets only offer the finished, mixed-down song, which makes it hard to test algorithms that need access to individual parts — for example, algorithms that find a song's main melody ("melody extraction"), pull instruments apart from a mix ("source separation"), or automatically mix a recording. Presented at ISMIR 2014, MedleyDB became a standard benchmark dataset used across the music information retrieval (MIR) research community, and is offered free for non-commercial research.
