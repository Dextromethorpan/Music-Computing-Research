This line of work trains a neural network to listen to a "polyphonic" recording (multiple instruments playing at once) and figure out, moment by moment, which instruments are sounding and what pitch is being played. "Frame-level" means the AI makes a decision many times per second rather than judging a whole clip at once, giving fine-grained detail over time. This kind of research underlies applications like automatic music transcription (turning audio into sheet music) and automatically tagging the instrument content of large audio libraries, and builds on MARL's earlier MedleyDB dataset for training and testing.
