This is MARL's own catalog of the datasets, code libraries, and tools it has publicly released (including MedleyDB, CREPE, OpenL3, and JAMS), alongside descriptions of active research projects like immersive 3D audio, the SONYC urban-sound project, and music cognition studies. It is the best single starting point for browsing everything the lab has produced, since it is maintained directly by the lab rather than scattered across individual papers.
