CREPE is a tool that automatically figures out the pitch (how high or low a musical note sounds) of an audio recording, moment by moment. Older pitch-tracking methods used hand-crafted mathematical formulas; CREPE instead uses a "convolutional neural network" — a type of AI model that learns patterns directly from raw sound waves after being trained on many labeled examples. This made it more accurate than the leading traditional method (pYIN) at the time it was published (ICASSP 2018). It is now widely used as a building block inside other music AI tools, for example to automatically transcribe melodies or power auto-tune style pitch correction.
