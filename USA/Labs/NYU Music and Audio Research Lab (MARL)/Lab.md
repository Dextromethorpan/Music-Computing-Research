The Music and Audio Research Laboratory (MARL) is a research center at New York University (jointly based in NYU Steinhardt's Music Technology program with affiliated faculty at NYU Tandon School of Engineering), directed by Juan Pablo Bello. It focuses on "machine listening" — using signal processing and machine learning to make computers understand music, speech, and environmental sound — spanning music information retrieval, urban sound sensing, and music cognition.
