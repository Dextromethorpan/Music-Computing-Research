The Audio Information Research (AIR) Lab is a research group in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (cross-listed with Computer Science and Data Science) at the University of Rochester, led by Professor Zhiyao Duan. It works on "computer audition" — building computational systems that understand music, speech, and environmental sound — including music information retrieval, automatic music transcription, source separation, and audio deepfake detection.
