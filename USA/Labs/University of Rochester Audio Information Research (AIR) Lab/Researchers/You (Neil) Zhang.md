Zhang works on machine learning applications in speech and audio processing, especially synthetic speech/singing voice deepfake detection (co-organizer of the SVDD Challenge), personalized spatial audio, and audio-visual rendering. He received a 2023 National Institute of Justice Graduate Research Fellowship and a Best Student Paper Award at WASPAA 2025; as of Summer 2025 he is a Senior Researcher at Dolby Labs.
