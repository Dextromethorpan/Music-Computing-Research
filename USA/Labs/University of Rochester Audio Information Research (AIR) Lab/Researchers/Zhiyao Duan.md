Duan is the founder and PI of the AIR Lab, working broadly across computer audition, music information retrieval, speech processing, and audiovisual/multimodal learning. He received an NSF CAREER Award (2019) for "Human-Computer Collaborative Music Making" and served as President of the International Society for Music Information Retrieval (ISMIR) for 2024-2025.
