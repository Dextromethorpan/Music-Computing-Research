Yan's doctoral research focused on automatic piano transcription; he created Transkun, a transformer-based audio-to-MIDI piano transcription system that received a best paper nomination at ISMIR 2024. He defended his PhD in 2025 and moved into a full-time researcher role at Cartesia AI.
