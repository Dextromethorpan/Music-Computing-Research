Cwitkowitz's research sits at the intersection of music information retrieval and machine learning, with a particular focus on automatic music transcription. He built and maintains "amt-tools," an open-source machine learning framework for automatic music transcription hosted under the AIR Lab's GitHub organization, and has done research internships at Chordify, Yousician, and Sony.
