Vroom! is a web-based search engine where, instead of typing a text description of a sound you want (like "dog barking" or "car horn"), you simply imitate the sound with your own voice into a microphone — called "query by vocal imitation." The system uses a pre-trained deep learning model to compare your vocal imitation against a database of real sounds and returns the closest matches. User studies found people rated Vroom! easier to use than a traditional text-search baseline, especially for sounds that are hard to describe in words. It's a playful, practical demonstration of audio machine learning applied to general "sound retrieval" beyond music.
