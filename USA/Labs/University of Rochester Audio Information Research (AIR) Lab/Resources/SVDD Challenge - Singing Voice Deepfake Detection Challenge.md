A "deepfake" is audio or video that has been artificially generated or altered by AI to sound or look real, such as an AI-cloned singing voice. This project organizes a public research competition that invites teams worldwide to build the best AI detectors able to tell a real human singing voice apart from an AI-synthesized or AI-converted one. It has two tracks: one using controlled, lab-made fake singing voices, and one using "in the wild" fakes pulled from real internet content. Over 47 teams took part, with the best system correctly separating real from fake singing more than 98% of the time. The challenge is co-organized with Carnegie Mellon University and Nagoya University.
