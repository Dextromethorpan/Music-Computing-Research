"Automatic music transcription" is the task of converting an audio recording into a symbolic score or MIDI file — getting a computer to write down what notes were played and when, just by listening. Transkun does this for piano recordings, using a transformer-based model (a modern neural network architecture that's good at handling long sequences and context, originally popularized in language AI) to predict precise note timings and pitches directly from audio. It received a best paper nomination at ISMIR 2024, the top international conference for music information retrieval, and is released as free, installable open-source software that anyone can run on their own piano recordings.
