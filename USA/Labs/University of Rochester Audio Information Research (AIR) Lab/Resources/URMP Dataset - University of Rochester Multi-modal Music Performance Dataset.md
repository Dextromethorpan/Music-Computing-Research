URMP is a collection of 44 short classical chamber-music pieces, covering 13 different instruments, created by having musicians record their individual instrumental parts separately and then mixing them into a synchronized ensemble performance. For each piece the dataset provides the musical score, individual and mixed audio tracks, synchronized video of the performance, and detailed "ground truth" labels showing exactly which note is being played at every moment. Because each instrument's audio is kept separate before mixing, researchers can use it to test computer systems that try to separate voices or instruments from a mixed recording, transcribe notes automatically, or line up audio with video and sheet music. It remains one of the most widely used datasets for "multimodal" (multi-sense) music research combining audio, video, and score.
