"Source separation" means pulling apart a mixed recording, such as a full song, into its individual ingredients — for example isolating just the singer's voice from the instrumental backing. This paper improves that process for singing voice specifically by adding visual information, footage of the singer's mouth and face movements, as an extra clue alongside the audio, since a singer's mouth movements correlate with the sounds they are producing. Combining audio and video (called "audiovisual" or "multimodal" learning) helps the system separate vocals more cleanly than audio alone, especially when the backing music is loud or close in pitch to the voice. This has applications in karaoke track generation, vocal remixing, and music production tools.
