BachDuet lets a person improvise a musical duet in real time with an AI "musician," in the style of J.S. Bach's counterpoint (two melodic lines played together following historical composition rules). The human plays one melodic line on a MIDI keyboard, and a recurrent neural network — a type of AI model good at handling sequences like notes over time — listens and generates a second, complementary melodic line live in response. In listening tests, 48 participants often could not tell whether a duet was created by two humans or by a human and BachDuet, suggesting the AI's responses sound musically convincing. It's a hands-on example of human-computer collaborative music making, with possible uses in music education and therapy.
