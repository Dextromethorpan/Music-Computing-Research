Max Morrison defended his PhD at the Interactive Audio Lab in 2024, advised by Bryan Pardo, where he worked on the Voogle vocal-imitation search engine and on interpretable, controllable representations of audio for tasks like editing and pitch/prosody manipulation. His widely used open-source pitch-tracking tool, TorchCrepe, has surpassed two million downloads. He is now a research engineer at ElevenLabs, building AI audio software for podcasts, film dialogue, and educational content.
