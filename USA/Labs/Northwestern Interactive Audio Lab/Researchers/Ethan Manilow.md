Ethan Manilow completed his PhD in 2022 at the Interactive Audio Lab under Bryan Pardo, where he was a lead contributor to nussl, the lab's open-source source-separation library. He also spent time as a student researcher on Google's Magenta team and at Mitsubishi Electric Research Labs (MERL) before joining Google DeepMind full-time, where his work focuses on machine listening systems that help artists work with musical audio, including source separation and automatic music transcription.
