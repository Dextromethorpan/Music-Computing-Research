Bryan Pardo is a Professor of Computer Science at Northwestern University and head of the Interactive Audio Lab. His research spans generative modeling, signal processing, and human-computer interaction, applied to problems like separating overlapping sounds, generating music and speech, labeling audio scenes, and building accessible audio production tools. He is co-director of Northwestern's Center for Human-Computer Interaction + Design and has authored well over a hundred peer-reviewed publications.
