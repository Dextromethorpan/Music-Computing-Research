Hugo Flores Garcia defended his dissertation at the Interactive Audio Lab in July 2025, where he led development of VampNet (a masked-token music generation model), the HARP plug-in for bringing deep learning into DAWs, and Sketch2Sound with Adobe. A computer musician as well as a researcher, his work focuses on building AI systems and interfaces that support creative expression for musicians and sound artists. He is now a research scientist at Adobe Research.
