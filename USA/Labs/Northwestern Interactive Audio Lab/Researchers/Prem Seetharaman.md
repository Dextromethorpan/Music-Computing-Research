Prem Seetharaman earned his PhD at the Interactive Audio Lab in 2019, advised by Bryan Pardo, and helped build the nussl source-separation library and the Voogle audio search engine. He later worked at Descript on audio enhancement and generation before joining Adobe Research, where he now works on generative models for everyday sounds, music, and speech, aiming to make audio content creation easier for non-experts.
