Lindsey Qixin Deng is a doctoral student in the Interactive Audio Lab, holding an MS in Electrical Engineering from Northwestern and a BS in Audio and Music Engineering from the University of Rochester. She is currently working on the perceptual grounding of deep audio embeddings — that is, connecting the internal numeric representations that AI models build from sound to how humans actually perceive that sound. Her broader interests include audio signal processing, music information retrieval, and generative models for audio.
