Annie Chu is a doctoral student in the Interactive Audio Lab. Her research focuses on accessible and inclusive musical interfaces — technology designed so that people with different abilities can make and interact with music — as well as audio and machine learning, human-computer interaction, and systems that combine multiple types of input (multimodal systems).
