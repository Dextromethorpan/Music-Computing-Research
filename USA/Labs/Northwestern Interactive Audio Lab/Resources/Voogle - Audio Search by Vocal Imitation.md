Voogle is an experimental search engine for sound files that works differently from a normal search box: instead of typing keywords, a user hums, sings, or otherwise vocally imitates the sound they're looking for (say, a dog bark or a car engine), or plays a similar-sounding example clip. Voogle then compares that imitation against a library of sounds and returns the closest matches, even if none of them is an exact copy of the input. This kind of search is useful for podcasters, video editors, or sound designers hunting for a specific sound effect without knowing its filename or exact recording. The lab also adapted the same underlying technique to a car-mechanic diagnosis tool that identifies vehicle problems from imitated engine noises.
