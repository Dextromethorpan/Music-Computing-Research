The Multi-resolution Common Fate Transform (MCFT) is a mathematical way of representing an audio recording that was designed specifically to make source separation easier. Ordinary methods break sound into frequencies at one fixed level of detail, but MCFT looks at how sounds change over time at multiple levels of detail simultaneously, borrowing from the idea that sounds belonging to the same source tend to move together over time (a "common fate"), similar to how leaves moving in the same direction suggest they're on the same branch. This gives algorithms more useful clues for telling apart different instruments or voices that overlap in the same recording. It's a foundational signal-processing building block that later separation tools in the lab can draw on.
