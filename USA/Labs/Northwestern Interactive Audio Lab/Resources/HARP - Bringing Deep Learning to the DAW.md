HARP is a plug-in that lets musicians use powerful, cutting-edge AI audio models directly inside a Digital Audio Workstation (DAW — the software, like Reaper or Audacity, that musicians use to record and edit tracks) without needing to install or run those models themselves. It works by sending the audio out to a "Gradio" web app (a simple way researchers publish interactive demos of their AI models online, which can run either on someone's own computer or in the cloud, e.g., on HuggingFace Spaces), letting that app process it, and bringing the result back into the DAW project. This means researchers can put brand-new AI tools directly into artists' hands without doing custom software-engineering work for every DAW, and artists get access to state-of-the-art models without leaving their usual workflow. HARP builds on the lab's earlier work bringing deep-learning tools into Audacity.
