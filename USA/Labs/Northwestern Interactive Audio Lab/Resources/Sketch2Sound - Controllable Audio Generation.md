Sketch2Sound, built in collaboration with Adobe, is a generative AI model that creates realistic sound effects from simple, human-friendly controls instead of requiring a full recording as a starting point. A user can sketch out how loud, bright, or high/low-pitched a sound should be over time, or a text description, or even imitate the sound with their own voice, and the model synthesizes matching audio. This kind of "sonic imitation" input (e.g., a person going "vroom vroom" to describe a car sound) makes sound design more accessible to people without deep audio-engineering skills, and the underlying technology has already been incorporated into one of Adobe's AI-powered audio products.
