VimSketch is a large public dataset combining two earlier lab datasets (VocalSketch and Vocal Imitation Set) into one resource for training and testing "query by vocal imitation" systems, i.e. software that finds a sound by matching it to a person's vocal imitation of that sound. It contains 542 real reference sounds (animal noises, musical snippets, everyday environmental sounds) paired with over 12,500 recorded human vocal imitations of them. Researchers anywhere can download this dataset for free and use it to train machine learning models that learn to recognize what sound a person is trying to imitate with their voice. It is one of the most widely reused datasets to come out of the Interactive Audio Lab.
