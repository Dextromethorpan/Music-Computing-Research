The Interactive Audio Lab is part of the Computer Science Department at Northwestern University in Evanston, Illinois, headed by Professor Bryan Pardo. The lab develops methods in generative modeling, signal processing, and human-computer interaction to build new tools for finding, separating, generating, and manipulating sound and music.
