This is the original conference paper in which Miller Puckette (Department of Music, UCSD) introduced Pure Data, describing it as "another integrated computer music environment" — meaning a single program that lets a musician both design a sound-generating patch and control/perform it live. The paper lays out the design goals behind Pd: a lightweight, real-time, graphical way to combine audio signal processing with control logic (like MIDI, timing, and user interaction), building on lessons learned from Puckette's earlier language Max. It's a useful historical document for understanding why Pd looks and works the way it still does today.
