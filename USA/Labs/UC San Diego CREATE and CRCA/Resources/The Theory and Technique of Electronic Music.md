This is Miller Puckette's textbook, published by World Scientific and also posted free online through his UCSD site, which teaches the mathematics and engineering ideas behind electronic and computer music — things like how digital audio is represented as numbers, how filters shape sound, and how synthesizers generate tones — using Pure Data patches as the worked examples throughout. His UCSD site also hosts recorded lectures and class materials that walk through the same material step by step, aimed at students with little prior background. It functions as the foundational reference connecting CRCA/UCSD's computer-music teaching to the design of Pd itself.
