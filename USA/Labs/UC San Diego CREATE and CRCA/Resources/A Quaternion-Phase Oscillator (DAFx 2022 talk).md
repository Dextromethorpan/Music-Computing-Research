This is Miller Puckette's recorded conference talk presenting the "Quaternion-Phase Oscillator" paper (also gathered separately in this vault) at the DAFx 2022 conference. In plain terms, he walks through, with audio examples, why a normal digital oscillator (the part of a synthesizer that produces a repeating sound wave) is limited to simple repeating loops, and demonstrates how tracking a 3D rotation instead lets the same basic idea produce a much wider range of evolving, less mechanical-sounding electronic tones. Watching the talk is a more approachable way into the idea than reading the paper's mathematics directly, since he demonstrates the sounds live.
