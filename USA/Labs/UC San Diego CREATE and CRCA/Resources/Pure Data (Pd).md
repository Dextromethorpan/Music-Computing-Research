Pure Data (Pd) is a free, open-source visual programming environment for making interactive sound, music, and multimedia. Instead of writing lines of text-based code, users connect small boxes ("objects") on screen with virtual wires to build a signal-processing patch — one box might generate a tone, another might filter it, another might respond to a MIDI keyboard. Miller Puckette began developing Pd while Associate Director of UCSD's Center for Research in Computing and the Arts, as an open-source successor to Max (which he had earlier created at IRCAM in Paris). Pd remains one of the most widely used tools worldwide for live electronic music performance, sound installations, and teaching computer music.
