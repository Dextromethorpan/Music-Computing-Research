The Center for Research in Computing and the Arts (CRCA) was UC San Diego's organized research unit for computing and the arts from 1972 until it was folded into Calit2/Qualcomm Institute in 2012; its music/audio research lineage continues today through the Sonic Arts R&D group and Shlomo Dubnov's Center for Research in Entertainment and Learning (CREATE/CREL), both housed at the Qualcomm Institute and closely tied to UCSD's Music department, historically home to Miller Puckette (creator of Max and Pure Data).
