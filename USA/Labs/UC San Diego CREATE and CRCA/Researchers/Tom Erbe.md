Tom Erbe teaches computer and electronic music at UC San Diego and has spent over three decades building spectral sound-processing software and hardware, most notably SoundHack, one of the earliest freeware computer music applications. His work focuses on spectral analysis/resynthesis techniques for creative sound transformation, and he has also designed synthesis modules for the Eurorack modular synthesizer ecosystem.
