Shlomo Dubnov is a professor at UC San Diego with a joint appointment in Music and Computer Science and Engineering, and directs the Center for Research in Entertainment and Learning (CREATE/CREL) at the Qualcomm Institute. His research covers machine improvisation, AI music generation and separation using diffusion models, singing voice conversion, and computational creativity — treating AI systems as active collaborators in composition and performance rather than passive tools.
