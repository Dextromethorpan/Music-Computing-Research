Miller Puckette is a computer music pioneer who created Max at IRCAM in Paris in the late 1980s and later, as Associate Director of UCSD's Center for Research in Computing and the Arts, created Pure Data (Pd) — an open-source visual programming environment for real-time sound and multimedia that remains one of the most widely used tools in computer music today. His broader research concerns real-time signal processing and software environments for interactive music performance.
