Enrique Tomás is listed on Google Scholar as based at the Tangible Music Lab, University of Arts Linz, with an institutional email verified at kunstuni-linz.at. He works on tangible and gesture-based musical interfaces, co-authoring papers with Martin Kaltenbrunner on "tangible scores" (instruments whose physical shape guides how they're played) and on "embodied gestures" — sculpting physical objects that translate bodily motion and energy into musical expression.
