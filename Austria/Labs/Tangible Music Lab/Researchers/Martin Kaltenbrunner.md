Martin Kaltenbrunner leads the Tangible Music Lab and directs its Postdigital Lutherie master's program at the University of Art and Design Linz. He co-created reacTIVision (open-source software that tracks physical markers and fingertips on a camera-based tabletop) and the TUIO protocol (the widely-adopted standard for sending that tracking data to other software), both originally built for the reacTable tangible tabletop synthesizer he co-developed at the Music Technology Group in Barcelona. His current research focuses on tangible user interfaces (interacting with software through real, graspable objects) and "postdigital" musical instrument design, which combines traditional instrument-making craft with digital technology. His Google Scholar profile lists an institutional email verified at kunstuni-linz.at, but no individually publishable address was found.
