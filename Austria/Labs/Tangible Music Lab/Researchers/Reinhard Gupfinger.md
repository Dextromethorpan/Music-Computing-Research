Reinhard Gupfinger (published as "R. Gupfinger") co-authored the Tangible Music Lab's series of papers on musical instruments and sonic interfaces designed for grey parrots, working closely with Martin Kaltenbrunner. His research sits within Animal-Computer Interaction (ACI), a field that designs technology around non-human animals as genuine users, and specifically studied what sounds and instrument designs captive parrots actually respond to and can operate themselves. According to his Google Scholar profile he is currently affiliated with Mozarteum University Salzburg's Fine Arts & Design department, after this earlier work with the Tangible Music Lab.
