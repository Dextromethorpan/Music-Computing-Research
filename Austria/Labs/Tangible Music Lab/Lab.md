The Tangible Music Lab is an artistic-research group within the Institute of Media at the University of Art and Design Linz (Kunstuniversität Linz), Austria, led by Prof. Martin Kaltenbrunner. It designs new digital and tangible musical instruments — interfaces you interact with by physically touching and moving real objects rather than a screen or keyboard — and is best known for creating the open-source reacTIVision tracking software and the TUIO protocol, both originally built for the reacTable tabletop synthesizer.
