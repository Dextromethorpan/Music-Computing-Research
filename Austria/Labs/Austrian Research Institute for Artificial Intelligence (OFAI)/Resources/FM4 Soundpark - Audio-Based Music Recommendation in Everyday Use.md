FM4 Soundpark was a website run by Austrian public radio station FM4 where independent musicians could upload their tracks. This paper describes an OFAI system that automatically recommended similar songs to listeners on that site, based purely on analyzing the sound of the audio itself (not on what other listeners clicked, which is how services like Spotify often work). The researchers describe what it took to make a research-grade "audio similarity" algorithm work reliably on a real, messy, publicly-used website rather than in a clean lab test, and report on lessons learned from actual listener behavior. It is a good example of OFAI's long-running specialty in content-based audio similarity — figuring out algorithmically which songs "sound alike" — deployed in a real product rather than just a paper demo.
