The MA ("Music Analysis") Toolbox is a free software package for the numerical computing program Matlab that implements several published methods for measuring how similar two pieces of audio sound to each other, purely from the audio signal. Rather than each researcher having to reimplement these audio-similarity formulas from scratch, the toolbox packaged working versions that other music-computing researchers could plug into their own projects. It reflects a common pattern in music computing research: publishing not just a paper describing a method, but reusable code so other labs can build on it directly. This toolbox underpinned much of OFAI's other work on music similarity and recommendation from the mid-2000s.
