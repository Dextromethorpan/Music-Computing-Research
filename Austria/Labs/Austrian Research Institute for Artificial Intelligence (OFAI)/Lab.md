OFAI is an independent, non-profit AI research institute in Vienna, founded in 1984. Its Intelligent Music Processing and Machine Learning Group (active from the 1990s through the 2010s, headed by Gerhard Widmer) was one of Europe's early academic groups working on machine learning for music — pioneering research on audio similarity, music recommendation, and computational models of expressive musical performance; that group's most active current members and output have since moved primarily to JKU Linz's Institute of Computational Perception (CP-JKU), profiled separately.
