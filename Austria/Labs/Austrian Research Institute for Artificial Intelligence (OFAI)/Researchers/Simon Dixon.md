Simon Dixon worked in OFAI's Intelligent Music Processing Group before moving to the Centre for Digital Music at Queen Mary University of London, where he continued developing his OFAI-era software BeatRoot (a beat-tracking system that automatically finds the beat in a music recording) and MATCH (an audio alignment tool). His OFAI work sits in the music information retrieval tradition — extracting structured, useful information like tempo and beat position directly from raw audio.
