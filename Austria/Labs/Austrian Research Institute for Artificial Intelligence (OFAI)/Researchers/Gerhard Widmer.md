Gerhard Widmer founded and led OFAI's Intelligent Music Processing and Machine Learning Group, where he pioneered using machine learning to study "expressive performance" — the timing, loudness, and phrasing choices that make a human musical performance sound alive rather than mechanical. His OFAI-era work on AI models of musical expression was covered by Wired Magazine and Der Spiegel in the early 2000s. He is also a professor at Johannes Kepler University Linz and head of the Institute of Computational Perception (CP-JKU), a separate, more recently active lab profiled elsewhere in this vault — this note covers only his historical OFAI-affiliated work.
