Martin Gasser is listed on OFAI's current people page as an Associate Researcher working on intelligent music processing, alongside applied machine learning, audio signal processing, and human-computer interaction more broadly. Across nearly two decades at OFAI he worked on audio similarity and hubness (why some songs get wrongly recommended as similar to everything), music recommendation systems like FM4 Soundpark, browsing tools for audio streams, and visualizations of expressive piano performance — and more recently co-authored work on AI-generated chord sequences for electronic dance music.
