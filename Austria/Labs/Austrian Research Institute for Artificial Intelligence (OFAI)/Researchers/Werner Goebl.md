Werner Goebl worked at OFAI on expressive piano performance, including how pianists control timing and touch to shape musical phrases. His OFAI page confirms he has since moved his research homepage to IWK at the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna (mdw), where he continues research into music performance and the psychology of how musicians play together and listen to each other. His OFAI-era work is part of the same expressive-performance-modeling research thread as Gerhard Widmer's and Martin Gasser's.
