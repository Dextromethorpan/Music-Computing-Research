Elias Pampalk worked at OFAI on content-based music similarity and recommendation — building systems that figure out algorithmically which songs "sound alike" and using that to organize music collections or generate playlists. His best-known project, Islands of Music, visualizes a music collection as a map where similar-sounding songs cluster into "islands." He also released the MA Toolbox, a reusable Matlab software package implementing several published audio-similarity methods. His OFAI page notes he has since left the institute; his personal site is pampalk.at.
