Today's music collections aren't just audio files — they include scanned sheet music, album art, lyrics, videos, and more. This survey paper reviews "cross-modal" music retrieval: search techniques that let you look something up in one format and find matching information in another, for example humming a short melody and having the system find the matching page of sheet music, or the title and artist of the song. This goes well beyond simple audio fingerprinting apps like Shazam, which only recognize an exact known recording. The paper is a broad overview of methods, challenges, and open research questions in this area, making it a useful map of the field for newcomers.
