MSMD is a free, open research dataset that pairs images of printed sheet music with the corresponding audio recordings and machine-readable note data, all carefully aligned so a computer can learn to connect "what the score looks like" with "what it sounds like." Datasets like this are what make it possible to train AI systems for tasks such as automatically following along in a printed score while music plays (score following) or converting a photo of sheet music into playable audio. Because building this kind of matched, labeled data is time-consuming, releasing it publicly lets other researchers build on the lab's work without starting from scratch.
