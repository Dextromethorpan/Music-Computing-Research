Partitura is a free, open-source software library for working with written musical scores in computer programs — the modern digital equivalent of sheet music (in formats like MusicXML or MIDI). Before a computer can study "how" a piece of music should be interpreted, it first needs to understand "what" the score actually says: which notes, at what time, with what markings. Partitura provides the tools to read, represent, and manipulate this information programmatically, and it underpins much of the lab's research into expressive performance, score following, and computational musicology.
