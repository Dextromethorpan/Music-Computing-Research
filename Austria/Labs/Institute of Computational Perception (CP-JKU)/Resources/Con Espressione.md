Con Espressione was a large European-funded research project (2016-2021, led by Gerhard Widmer, funded by an ERC Advanced Grant of about 2.3 million euros) that asked a simple but hard question: what makes a musical performance expressive? A computer can already detect the beat or identify a song, but it cannot tell whether a pianist is playing "playfully" or "solemnly." The project built AI systems that try to recognize, describe, and even generate these expressive qualities — tiny variations in timing, loudness, and articulation that make one performance of the same piece feel completely different from another. One outcome was "ACCompanion," a system demonstrated live that can musically accompany a human performer in real time, reacting to their expressive choices. The project was named a Falling Walls 2021 "Science Breakthrough of the Year."
