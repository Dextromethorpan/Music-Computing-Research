This is an introductory tutorial (originally presented at the ISMIR music-research conference) explaining how "deep learning" — a family of AI techniques based on artificial neural networks — is used to analyze and organize music. It is written for people who are new to the topic: it explains the basics of neural networks first, then walks through three main types used in music research: networks good at recognizing local patterns in a sound (useful for identifying instruments or genres), networks good at understanding sequences over time (useful for detecting the beat or a spoken syllable's start), and networks trained to judge whether two pieces of music sound similar. A companion code repository accompanies the tutorial for hands-on learning.
