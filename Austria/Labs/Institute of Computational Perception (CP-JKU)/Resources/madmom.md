Madmom is a free, open-source software toolkit (written in the programming language Python) for analyzing audio and extracting musical information from it, such as finding the beat, detecting the start of individual notes, tracking tempo, or recognizing chords. It packages up years of the lab's published research into ready-to-use building blocks that other researchers and developers can install and use directly, rather than reimplementing published algorithms from scratch. It has become one of the most widely used tools in the Music Information Retrieval research community, with well over a thousand users on GitHub.
