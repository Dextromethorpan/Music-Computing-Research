"Beat tracking" is the task of getting a computer to find the steady pulse in a piece of music — the same pulse you tap your foot to. Beat This! is a free, open-source neural-network-based beat and downbeat tracker built by the lab, designed to work accurately across many different musical styles without needing to be retuned for each one. Beat tracking is a foundational building block for many other music applications, such as DJ software, automatic accompaniment, and music recommendation, so a more accurate and general tracker benefits the wider field.
