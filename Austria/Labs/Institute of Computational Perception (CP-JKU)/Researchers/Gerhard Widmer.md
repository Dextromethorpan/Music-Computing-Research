Gerhard Widmer founded and heads the Institute of Computational Perception at JKU Linz. He is one of the most prominent researchers worldwide in AI and music, known for pioneering work on computational models of expressive music performance — how computers can recognize, predict, and even generate the subtle timing and loudness choices that make a performance feel "expressive." He holds an ERC Advanced Grant (for the Con Espressione project) and won Austria's Wittgenstein Prize in 2009, the country's top science award.
