Carlos Cancino-Chacón researches computational models of expressive music performance — building systems that model how musicians shape timing, dynamics, and articulation when they play, and using tools like the open-source Partitura library (which he co-develops) to connect written scores with real performances. In 2026 he received the FWF ASTRA Prize, a major Austrian research funding award.
