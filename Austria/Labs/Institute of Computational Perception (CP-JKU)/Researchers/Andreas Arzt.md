Andreas Arzt is a longtime CP-JKU researcher (listed on the institute's official page, currently under former/past members) known for his work on "score following" — real-time systems that track a live musical performance and automatically follow along in the written score, the way a human page-turner or accompanist would. This kind of technology underlies automatic accompaniment systems that can play along with a live musician in real time.
