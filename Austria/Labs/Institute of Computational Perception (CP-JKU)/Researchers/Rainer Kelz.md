Rainer Kelz is a former CP-JKU researcher (listed on the institute's official past-members page) who worked on automatic music transcription — teaching computers to turn a raw piano audio recording into a written or symbolic representation of the notes being played, including subtle details like how a note is struck and released. His transcription research is cited as a reference implementation inside the lab's open-source madmom toolkit.
