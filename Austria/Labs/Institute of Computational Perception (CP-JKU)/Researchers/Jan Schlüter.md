Jan Schlüter works on applying neural networks (deep learning) to audio and music analysis tasks such as beat tracking, onset detection (finding where notes start), and sound classification. He is a core contributor to the lab's widely used open-source toolkits, including madmom and the newer Beat This! beat tracker, and has co-authored numerous Music Information Retrieval papers over the years.
