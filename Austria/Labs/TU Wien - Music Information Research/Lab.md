Part of TU Wien (Vienna University of Technology), spanning work in the Information & Software Engineering group (Faculty of Informatics) and the older Interactive Media Systems group. Focus areas include deep learning for automatic drum transcription, music information retrieval, and general audio analysis (sound recognition, feature extraction, automated composing).
