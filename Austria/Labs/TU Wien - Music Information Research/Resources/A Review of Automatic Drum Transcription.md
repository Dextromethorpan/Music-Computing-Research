Published in IEEE Transactions on Audio, Speech and Language Processing, this is a survey paper written by a team of researchers across several institutions (including TU Wien's Richard Vogl) that summarizes the entire field of "automatic drum transcription" — the task of getting a computer to detect drum hits in audio and write them down as symbolic data. It's a good beginner-friendly starting point because it explains the history of the field, the different approaches (older signal-processing methods versus newer deep-learning methods), the standard datasets researchers test on, and the open challenges that remain, such as handling drum kits the system has never heard before.
