This is Richard Vogl's PhD dissertation. "Drum transcription" means teaching a computer to listen to a song and automatically write down which drum (kick, snare, hi-hat, etc.) was hit and when, similar to how a person might write sheet music by ear. The thesis develops deep learning (a type of machine learning that uses many-layered neural networks, loosely inspired by the brain) methods that can do this even when the drums are mixed together with other instruments. It also explores using similar neural networks in reverse — to generate new, realistic drum patterns rather than just recognizing existing ones. This work was done partly at TU Wien and partly at Johannes Kepler University (JKU) Linz, where Vogl earned his PhD.
