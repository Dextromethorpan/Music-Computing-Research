Presented at DAFx-18 (the International Conference on Digital Audio Effects) and winner of the conference's 2nd Best Paper Award, this paper tackles a harder version of "drum transcription" (automatically detecting which drum sounds occur and when in a recording): instead of only recognizing three basic drum types (kick, snare, hi-hat), the authors extend a neural-network-based system to recognize many more individual drum and percussion instruments in a full band mix. This matters for building tools that can automatically create drum sheet music or MIDI data from any song, not just simplified ones.
