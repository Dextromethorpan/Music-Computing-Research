This is a project page (not a single paper) from TU Wien's older Interactive Media Systems group, describing an umbrella of research efforts around audio signal processing and categorization. The page itself is short and lists the group's scope rather than results in detail: it covers "environmental sound recognition" (teaching a computer to recognize everyday non-musical sounds like a door slamming or a car horn), audio feature extraction and machine learning methods (turning raw sound into numeric descriptions a computer can learn from), and "automated composing" (software that writes music on its own) for both desktop and mobile devices. It is included here as a real, verifiable pointer to this line of TU Wien work, even though the page does not itself contain a full publication.
