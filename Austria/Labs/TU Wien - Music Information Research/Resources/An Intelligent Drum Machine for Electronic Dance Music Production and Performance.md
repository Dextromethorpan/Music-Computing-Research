Presented at NIME 2017 (New Interfaces for Musical Expression, a conference about new tools and instruments for making music), this paper describes a "drum machine" — a device or piece of software that plays back or generates drum beats — that uses machine learning to suggest and vary drum patterns intelligently, aimed at electronic dance music (EDM) producers. Rather than a musician programming every single beat by hand, the system can generate plausible new patterns or variations on a pattern the user gives it, intended for use both in the studio and live on stage.
