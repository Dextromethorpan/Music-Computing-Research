Published at ISMIR 2017 (the main annual conference for Music Information Retrieval research), this paper shows that automatically transcribing drum hits works better when the computer also tracks the song's beat (the steady pulse you'd tap your foot to) at the same time, instead of treating the two problems separately. The authors use a "convolutional recurrent neural network" — a combination of two common deep-learning building blocks: one good at recognizing local sound patterns (convolutional) and one good at tracking how sound changes over time (recurrent). This joint approach improved transcription accuracy on real recordings.
