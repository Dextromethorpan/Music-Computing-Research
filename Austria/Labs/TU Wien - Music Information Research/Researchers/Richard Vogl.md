Richard Vogl works at the intersection of music and artificial intelligence, with his main focus on deep learning for automatic drum transcription (getting a computer to detect drum hits in a recording) and on generating new drum patterns with neural networks. He holds a PhD and Master's degree in computer science from Johannes Kepler University (JKU) Linz, and has worked on projects including GiantSteps (an EU-funded music technology project) and SmarterJam (a TU Wien project on recommending musicians and tracks for online collaboration).
