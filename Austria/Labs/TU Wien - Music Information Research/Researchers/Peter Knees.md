Peter Knees is an Assistant Professor at TU Wien whose research covers Music Information Retrieval (MIR) — using computers to search, organize, and understand music collections — as well as web information retrieval, multimedia retrieval, recommender systems (like the algorithms behind song recommendations), and digital media arts. He co-authored the book "Music Similarity and Retrieval" and a book chapter on music recommender systems, has supervised and co-authored much of Richard Vogl's drum-transcription work, and organizes recurring workshops such as MILC (Intelligent Music Interfaces for Listening and Creation).
