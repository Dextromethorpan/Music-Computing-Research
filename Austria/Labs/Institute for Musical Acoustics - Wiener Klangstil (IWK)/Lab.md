The Institute for Musical Acoustics – Wiener Klangstil (IWK) is a research institute at the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna (mdw), Austria. It studies how musical instruments produce sound (musical acoustics), how musicians move and coordinate while performing (performance science, using motion capture and eye tracking), and how music notation can be represented digitally for research (digital music encoding).
