Gregor Widholm founded the IWK in 1980, establishing musical acoustics as a research field in Austria. A trained horn player and engineer, he built measurement systems for studying how the different parts of an instrument (valves, mouthpieces, strings, fittings) affect its intonation and sound, including the BIAS system for testing brass instruments. He remains active as professor emeritus.
