David M. Weigl combines computer science and web technology with musicology and digital humanities research. He works on "semantic web" and "linked data" methods (ways of connecting pieces of information on the web so computers can understand relationships between them, not just display text) applied to music, on digital editions of manuscripts, and on tools for encoding and annotating music notation, such as mei-friend. He is leading the FWF-funded "Let's Encode!" project on citizen-science music encoding infrastructure.
