Werner Goebl leads the IWK and researches how pianists move their bodies while playing (using motion capture, a technology that tracks body movement with cameras and reflective markers), how duet performers synchronize with each other, and how expressive musical performance can be visualized and analyzed. He also co-develops digital tools for encoding and studying music notation, such as mei-friend.
