Alexander Mayer runs the IWK's acoustic laboratory, building sensor and measurement equipment for studying musical instruments — for example, artificial "mouths" that blow reed instruments in a controlled way, and robotic arms that bow or pluck strings with precisely reproducible motion. His work supports many of the institute's experimental acoustics studies.
