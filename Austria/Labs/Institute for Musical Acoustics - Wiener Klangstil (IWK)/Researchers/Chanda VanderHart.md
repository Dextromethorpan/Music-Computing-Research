Chanda VanderHart works at the meeting point of historical musicology and digital humanities. She studies 19th-century Viennese song and the Strauss family's music (including the Vienna New Year's Concert repertoire), and builds digital tools and datasets that make this repertoire searchable and analyzable for other researchers, in close collaboration with David M. Weigl.
