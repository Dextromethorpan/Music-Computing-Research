Montserrat Pàmies-Vilà researches how the interaction between a player and their instrument shapes sound, focusing especially on woodwind instruments (clarinet, saxophone) and string instruments (cello, harp, piano). She uses experimental methods — including robotic reproduction of bowing and blowing — to study playing technique in a controlled, repeatable way.
