Alex Hofmann works on live electronics (real-time electronic transformation of acoustic sound during a live performance), sound synthesis and design, and new sensor-based interfaces that let musicians control electronics with their instruments or bodies. He also studies the technical and physical side of wind-instrument performance and leads artistic research projects on machine-readable notation for live-electronic works.
