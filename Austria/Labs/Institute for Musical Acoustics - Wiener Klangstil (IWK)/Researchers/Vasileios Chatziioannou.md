Vasileios Chatziioannou studies the acoustics of musical instruments — how instruments like clarinets, saxophones, brass, and bowed strings physically produce sound — and builds computer models ("physics-based sound synthesis") that simulate this behavior mathematically. He also works on the numerical accuracy of these simulations and on virtual reality audio.
