"MIR" stands for Music Information Retrieval, the field of building software that can automatically find, compare, or analyze music (for example, finding all performances of the same piece, or spotting a particular chord pattern). This conference abstract, presented at ISMIR 2025 (the International Society for Music Information Retrieval Conference), describes how researchers can attach notes, comments, and analysis markers ("annotations") directly onto digital sheet music using two tools built partly at IWK: Verovio (a program that renders MEI music-encoding files as viewable notation) and mei-friend (the institute's browser-based music encoding editor). The work supports IWK's mission of building shared digital infrastructure for music research.
