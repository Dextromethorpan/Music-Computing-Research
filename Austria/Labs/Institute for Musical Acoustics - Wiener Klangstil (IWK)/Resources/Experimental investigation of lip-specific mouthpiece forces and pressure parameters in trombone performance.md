To play a brass instrument like a trombone, a musician presses their lips against a metal mouthpiece and buzzes them to create the sound, a technique called "embouchure." This paper describes an experiment measuring exactly how much force and pressure a trombonist's lips exert on the mouthpiece during normal, realistic playing, using a specially built sensor mouthpiece. Understanding these forces helps explain playing technique, fatigue, and even injury risk for brass players. It was published in Acta Acustica and led by researchers connected to Vasileios Chatziioannou, who leads the IWK's work on the acoustics of wind and brass instruments (musical acoustics is the branch of physics that studies how instruments and voices produce and shape sound).
