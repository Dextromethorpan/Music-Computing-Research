This is the lab's own overview page, explaining that it is a shared sound-research infrastructure jointly run by three Viennese institutions. It describes three physical labs: a Mobile Lab for taking recording and sensing equipment out into the field, a Sound Projection Lab for testing experimental loudspeaker arrangements and spatial sound playback, and a Field Recording & Digitization/Restoration Lab focused on preserving and digitizing historical sound recordings, some dating back over a century.
