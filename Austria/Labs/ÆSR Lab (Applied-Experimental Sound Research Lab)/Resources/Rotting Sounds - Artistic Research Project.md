Rotting Sounds was a multi-year (2018-2022) artistic research project led by Thomas Grill, who now leads the ÆSR Lab's Sound Projection Lab, exploring how sound recordings and sound-carrying media physically decay, degrade, and change over time, and what that decay itself can sound like or be used creatively. The project connects directly to the ÆSR Lab's ongoing focus on digitizing and restoring old sound recordings before they are lost.
