This article from the mdw university magazine introduces the ÆSR Lab project to students and staff, explaining that it is funded by Austria's Federal Ministry of Education, Science and Research together with EU recovery funding, and that it gives students and researchers hands-on access to specialized recording, sound-projection, and archival equipment they would not otherwise have.
