Kerstin Klenke directs the Phonogrammarchiv (Sound Archive) of the Austrian Academy of Sciences, the world's oldest sound archive (founded 1899), and leads the ÆSR Lab's Field Recording & Digitization/Restoration Lab, which specializes in field-recording methods and in conserving, restoring, and digitizing historical sound recordings.
