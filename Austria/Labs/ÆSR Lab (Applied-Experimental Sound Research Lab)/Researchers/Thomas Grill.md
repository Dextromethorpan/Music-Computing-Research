Thomas Grill is a composer, performer, and media artist working in electroacoustic music who leads the ÆSR Lab's Sound Projection Lab. At the mdw University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna he directs the university course for electroacoustic and experimental music (ELAK), and from 2018-2022 he led the multi-year artistic research project "Rotting Sounds" on the decay of sound recordings.
