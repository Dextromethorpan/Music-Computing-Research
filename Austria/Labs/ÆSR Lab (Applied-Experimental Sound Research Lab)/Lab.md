The ÆSR Lab is a Vienna-based sound research infrastructure (2023-2026) run jointly by the University of Applied Arts Vienna, the mdw University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna, and the Austrian Academy of Sciences' Phonogrammarchiv (the world's oldest sound archive, founded 1899). It provides three shared labs — a Mobile Lab for field recording, a Sound Projection Lab for experimental spatial sound playback, and a Field Recording & Digitization/Restoration Lab for rescuing and digitizing old sound recordings — and supports research spanning machine learning, sound spatialization, and sensor-based ("physical computing") interaction with sound.
