Part of the Department of Psychology at the University of Graz, Austria, this section is the successor to the Centre for Systematic Musicology (2009-2023), which was led by Prof. Richard Parncutt and was one of Europe's leading centres for music psychology and systematic musicology. The group studies how the brain perceives music and sound (psychoacoustics), how musical skill develops and changes the brain (neuroplasticity), and — through Parncutt's earlier work — how mathematical/computational models can explain why certain combinations of tones sound pleasant (consonance) or tense (dissonance) and how we perceive musical tonality and rhythm.
