Annemarie Seither-Preisler is the current head of the Music Psychology and Brain Research Section at the University of Graz. Her research examines how auditory and musical skills develop across the lifespan, individual differences in how people perceive pitch and timbre (e.g. whether someone focuses more on the fundamental tone or the overtones of a sound), and how musical training reshapes hearing-related processing in the brain. She also studies auditory processing in children with conditions such as central auditory processing disorder (CAPD), dyslexia, and ADHD.
