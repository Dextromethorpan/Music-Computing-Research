Peter Schneider is a researcher in the Music Psychology and Brain Research Section who studies the neuroscience of hearing, using brain imaging methods (MRI, EEG, MEG, fMRI) to investigate how the auditory cortex processes pitch and timbre. He is known for identifying brain-structure markers linked to absolute pitch and to individual "hearing types" (whether someone perceives sound more through the fundamental tone or the overtones), and for studying how musical training changes the auditory brain over time.
