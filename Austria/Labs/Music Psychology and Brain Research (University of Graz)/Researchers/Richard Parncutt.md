Richard Parncutt is a systematic musicologist, music psychologist, and psychoacoustician who was Professor of Systematic Musicology at the University of Graz from 1998 to 2023 and directed the university's Centre for Systematic Musicology from 2009 until it was dissolved in 2023. His research builds computational and psychoacoustic models to explain how humans perceive musical consonance/dissonance, tonality (the sense of a musical "home" note or key), and rhythm. He is a former president of the European Society for the Cognitive Sciences of Music (ESCOM) and, in recent years, has also published on global warming and academic sustainability.
