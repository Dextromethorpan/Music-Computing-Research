Markus Christiner researches the connection between musical ability and language learning. He develops tests that measure how well people perceive the "melody" of speech (its rising and falling pitch pattern, called prosody) and studies how singing ability, musical training, and general auditory skills relate to how easily someone learns to speak or sing in tonal languages such as Mandarin. His work is supported by an APART scholarship from the Austrian Academy of Sciences.
