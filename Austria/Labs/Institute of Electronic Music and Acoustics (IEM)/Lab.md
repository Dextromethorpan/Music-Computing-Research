The Institute of Electronic Music and Acoustics (IEM) is an institute of the University of Music and Performing Arts Graz (KUG), Austria, founded in 1965. It works at the intersection of digital signal processing, audio engineering, psychoacoustics, spatial audio (ambisonics), and computer music composition, and is known for the open-source IEM Plug-in Suite and for hosting the CUBE, a 49-channel loudspeaker concert venue.
