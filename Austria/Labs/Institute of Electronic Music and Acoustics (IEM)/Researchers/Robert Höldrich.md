Robert Höldrich is a long-standing leading figure at IEM, associated with the institute's research direction in audio signal processing, acoustics, and computer music. As a senior figure at the institute he has helped shape IEM's broad research program spanning psychoacoustics (how humans perceive sound), digital signal processing, and computer music composition.
