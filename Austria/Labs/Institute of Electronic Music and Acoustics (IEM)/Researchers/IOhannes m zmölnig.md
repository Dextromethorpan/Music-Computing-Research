IOhannes m zmölnig is a researcher at IEM and one of the core developers of Pure Data ("Pd"), the visual programming language for audio and multimedia that IEM's community portal (puredata.info) hosts. He has authored several widely used Pd extension libraries and, together with Winfried Ritsch, co-wrote a published overview describing IEM's role as a hub for open-source audio software.
