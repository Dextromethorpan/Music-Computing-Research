Winfried Ritsch works at IEM on realtime software systems for computer music and algorithmic composition (music generated or controlled by computer algorithms), including custom electromechanical and sound-installation projects. He co-authored, together with IOhannes m zmölnig, a published overview of IEM's role in open-source audio software, confirming his long association with the institute's open-source and Pure Data community work.
