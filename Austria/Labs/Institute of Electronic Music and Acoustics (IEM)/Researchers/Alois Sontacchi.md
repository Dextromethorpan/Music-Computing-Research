Alois Sontacchi works at IEM in the areas of audio engineering and psychoacoustics — the study of how humans perceive sound qualities like loudness, pitch, and spatial position. His work sits at the intersection of the institute's signal-processing research and its degree program in audio engineering, which IEM runs jointly with Graz University of Technology.
