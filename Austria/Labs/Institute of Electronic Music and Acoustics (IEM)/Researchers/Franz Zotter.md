Franz Zotter is a researcher at IEM known for work on Ambisonics — the technique for recording and reproducing sound so it surrounds a listener in every direction — including how to design "decoders" that translate an Ambisonics signal into the specific loudspeaker layout of a room. His work underpins tools like the AllRADecoder plug-in in the IEM Plug-in Suite, and connects the mathematics of spatial audio to real-world listening rooms such as IEM's CUBE.
