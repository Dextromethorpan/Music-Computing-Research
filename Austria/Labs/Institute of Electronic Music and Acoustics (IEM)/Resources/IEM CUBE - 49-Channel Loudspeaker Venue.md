The CUBE is a 120 square-metre, fully soundproofed concert hall built into the IEM building in Graz, fitted with a 49-channel sound system using over 80 loudspeakers surrounding and covering the audience from every direction, including overhead. Rather than playing music through just two speakers (stereo) or the typical home-cinema surround-sound setup, a room like this lets researchers and composers place sound anywhere in three-dimensional space around the listener — which is exactly what technologies like Ambisonics (see that concept note) are designed to control. The CUBE is used both for IEM's own spatial-audio research and for the "OPEN CUBE" public concert series, making it a rare piece of infrastructure that lets a lab test ideas about 3D sound and psychoacoustics (how humans perceive sound) with real audiences.
