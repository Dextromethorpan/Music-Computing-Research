Sound and Music Computing (SMC) is an annual international academic conference where researchers present new work on the technical and creative side of audio and music software — things like sound synthesis, spatial audio, music analysis software, and new digital instruments. IEM in Graz organized and hosted the 2025 edition of the conference. Hosting a major international conference like this is a sign of an institute's standing in the field, and it brings together many of the researchers and topics referenced throughout this vault's Austria and broader Music Computing notes.
