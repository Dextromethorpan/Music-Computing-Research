The IEM Plug-in Suite is a free, open-source collection of audio plug-ins for working with Ambisonics — a technique for recording and mixing sound so it can be played back from speakers arranged all around (and above/below) a listener, instead of just left and right like stereo. The suite includes tools to "encode" a normal mono or stereo recording into this all-around format, to "decode" it back out to a real speaker layout, to visualize where sound is coming from on a virtual sphere, and even a granular synthesizer (a technique that chops audio into tiny grains and reassembles them) built for Ambisonics. It works up to 7th-order Ambisonics (a measure of how precisely direction is represented — higher orders give sharper spatial detail) and runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux inside standard music software. It was built by IEM staff and students using the JUCE audio programming framework and is licensed under the GPLv3 open-source license.
