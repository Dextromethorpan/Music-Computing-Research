The Auditory Modeling Toolbox (AMT) is a free, open-source software package (for Matlab/Octave) maintained by ARI that packages up computer models of how the human ear and brain process sound. Instead of every lab re-implementing published hearing models from scratch, researchers can download the AMT and run tested, documented versions of dozens of models covering different stages of hearing — from the inner ear up to spatial perception. This matters for music/audio computing because these models are used to test how compression, spatial audio, or synthesized sounds will actually be perceived by a listener, not just measured on paper. The AMT is released under the GNU GPL license and has contributors from institutions across Europe and the US (e.g. DTU Denmark, TU Berlin, Aalto University).
