This is a publicly available dataset of Head-Related Transfer Function (HRTF) measurements collected at ARI, distributed in the SOFA file format that ARI itself helped create. HRTFs describe how sound is filtered by an individual person's head, torso, and outer ear before it reaches the eardrum — this is the physical basis for why humans can tell where a sound is coming from. By publishing real measured data (from human listeners and mannequin "dummy heads") for free, ARI lets other researchers, students, and audio-software developers build and test spatial-audio and virtual-reality sound systems without having to run their own expensive measurement setup.
