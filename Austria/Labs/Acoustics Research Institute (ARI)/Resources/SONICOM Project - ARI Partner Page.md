SONICOM is a large EU-funded (Horizon 2020) research project bringing together universities and labs across Europe to improve immersive, personalized 3D audio for headphones, AR, and VR. ARI is one of the project partners. Its role is to build "auditory models" — computer simulations of how a specific listener's ears and brain interpret spatial sound — so that headphones could eventually adjust automatically to fit an individual person's hearing ("self-personalizing headphones"), rather than using a generic one-size-fits-all setting. ARI also contributes to a shared open-source "SONICOM ecosystem" of software, experiment data, and machine-learning tools for spatial audio research. Piotr Majdak leads ARI's part of the project, working on a parametric model of the outer ear (pinna) shape, which strongly affects how each person perceives sound direction.
