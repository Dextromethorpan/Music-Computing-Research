AUDIO GHOSTS is an artistic research project (funded by the Austrian Science Fund, running October 2023 to September 2026) led by composer Bernhard Gál at the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna (mdw), with ARI as the scientific research partner. It investigates "auditory illusions" — cases where a listener perceives a sound that doesn't physically match what's actually happening, for example thinking a sound is moving or coming from a different location than it really is. The project uses these illusions to design sound installations ("audio ghosts") where where you stand in a room changes what you personally hear. ARI scientists Piotr Majdak and Florian Pausch bring psychoacoustics and spatial-audio-signal-processing expertise, combining artistic composition methods with scientific listening experiments in a custom-built listening lab.
