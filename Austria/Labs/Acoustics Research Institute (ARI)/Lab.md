The Acoustics Research Institute (ARI, part of the Institut für Schallforschung / ISF) is an institute of the Austrian Academy of Sciences in Vienna that studies sound "from generation through perception." For music/sound computing, its most important contributions are in spatial hearing and auditory modeling: how humans localize sound, how to measure and simulate that with head-related transfer functions (HRTFs), and open-source tools (SOFA, the Auditory Modeling Toolbox) that other researchers and companies build on.
