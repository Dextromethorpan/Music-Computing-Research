Piotr Majdak studies spatial hearing — how humans figure out the direction and distance of a sound — and applies that understanding to improve acoustic and audio systems. He is a lead figure behind the SOFA file format for storing directional acoustic data (like Head-Related Transfer Functions) and the Auditory Modeling Toolbox, and he is the principal investigator for ARI's role in the EU SONICOM project on personalized 3D audio. He also co-supervises the artistic research project AUDIO GHOSTS.
