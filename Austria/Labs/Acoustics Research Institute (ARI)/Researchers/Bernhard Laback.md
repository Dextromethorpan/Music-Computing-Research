Bernhard Laback leads ARI's Hearing research group. His work is in psychoacoustics — how people perceive pitch, loudness, and spatial cues in sound — with a particular focus on cochlear implants, devices that electrically stimulate the hearing nerve for people with severe hearing loss. This research on how limited or altered hearing input is perceived is closely related to the broader spatial-hearing and auditory-modeling work at ARI relevant to music and audio technology.
