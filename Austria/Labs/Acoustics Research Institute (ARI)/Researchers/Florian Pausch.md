Florian Pausch is named as one of the ARI scientists (alongside Piotr Majdak) supporting the AUDIO GHOSTS artistic research project on auditory illusions, bringing psychoacoustics and spatial audio signal-processing expertise to help composer Bernhard Gál design sound installations and design the listening experiments that evaluate them.
