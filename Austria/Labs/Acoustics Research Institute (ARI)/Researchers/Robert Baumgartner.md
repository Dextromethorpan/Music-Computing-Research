Robert Baumgartner is the deputy director of ARI and works in its Hearing research group. His research focuses on how the human auditory system localizes sound sources, including building computational models of this process (some of which are part of the Auditory Modeling Toolbox) and running listening experiments on how quickly people adapt to unfamiliar sounds when judging where they come from.
