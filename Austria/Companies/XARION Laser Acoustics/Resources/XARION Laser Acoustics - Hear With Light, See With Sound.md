This is XARION's own company and technology overview, explaining in plain terms how its optical microphones work and what they're used for. It describes the core idea — detecting sound with light instead of a vibrating membrane — and the practical benefits this brings: no resonance, an extremely wide frequency range, and immunity to the kind of wear and distortion that affects traditional microphones. The company was founded in 2012 as a spin-off from Vienna's TU Wien (Vienna University of Technology).
