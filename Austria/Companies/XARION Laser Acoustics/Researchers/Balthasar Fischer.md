Balthasar Fischer is a physicist, sound engineer, and trained Tonmeister (recording engineer) who invented XARION's membrane-free optical microphone. He earned his PhD at TU Wien's Photonics Institute and previously worked in acoustics R&D at Knowles Electronics Austria before founding XARION in 2012 to commercialize his laser-based sound-sensing invention.
