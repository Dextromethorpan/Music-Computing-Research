XARION Laser Acoustics is a Vienna-based company (spun off from TU Wien in 2012) that builds a genuinely novel kind of microphone: instead of a vibrating membrane connected to electronics, it uses a laser beam whose travel time through a small gap changes very slightly when a sound wave passes through it, and that tiny change is measured optically. Included via path 3/path 2 — the documented innovation is this "membrane-free optical microphone" (patented Fabry-Pérot interferometer sensing), which has no moving parts and can pick up an enormously wider range of frequencies (from 5 Hz up to 1 MHz) than any conventional microphone, mainly used today for industrial ultrasound inspection but built on the same core acoustic-sensing science as music microphones.
