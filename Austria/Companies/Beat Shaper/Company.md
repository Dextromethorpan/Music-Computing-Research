Beat Shaper is a Vienna-based startup, founded in 2024, that builds an AI "co-pilot" for electronic music producers — converting plain-language text prompts into editable, multitrack MIDI loops (drums, basslines, synth parts) using a custom-trained symbolic-music AI model, developed with support from the Austrian Scientific Cluster supercomputer.
