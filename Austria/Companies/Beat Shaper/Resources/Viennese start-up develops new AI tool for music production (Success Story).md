This is the official "Success Story" write-up of Beat Shaper published in the HPC-in-Europe materials library (a case-study format used to showcase companies that benefited from European supercomputing support). It covers the same underlying story as the companion blog post — describing Beat Shaper's small (5-million-parameter) AI model, its "symbolic music" training approach (learning to output note, rhythm, and synthesizer-parameter data rather than raw audio), and the training of 500+ candidate models on the Austrian Scientific Cluster supercomputer — but is framed for HPC-industry readers as evidence of what free access to a national supercomputer can enable for a small startup. It also highlights that Beat Shaper's training data was fully licensed or fair-use, so users retain full rights to what they create with the tool, a legal point that differentiates it from many larger AI music competitors.
