Beat Shaper's main website describes the product as an "AI-powered MIDI generator" for electronic music producers: it turns plain-language prompts into editable drums, basslines, synth parts, loops, and full arrangements, directly in a web browser. MIDI (Musical Instrument Digital Interface) is a long-standing standard format for representing musical notes, timing, and control data digitally, rather than as recorded sound — it is what makes Beat Shaper's output editable after generation. Producers can fine-tune results with sliders for genre, mood, and energy, then export their loops as MIDI, audio (WAV/MP3), or full Ableton Live project files, so the AI-generated material slots into their existing DAW (Digital Audio Workstation) workflow. The page states training data was custom-licensed, so users retain rights to what they create.
