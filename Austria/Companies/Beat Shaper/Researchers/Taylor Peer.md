Taylor Peer is one of Beat Shaper's four co-founders and the company's public-facing spokesperson on its AI technology, quoted extensively in HPC Austria's coverage explaining how the team built and trained their symbolic-music AI model (a system that generates structured musical data — notes, rhythm, and synthesizer parameters — rather than raw audio) using the Austrian Scientific Cluster supercomputer. Beat Shaper's legal notice lists him (Dipl.-Ing. Taylor Peer, BSc) as the company's sole authorized representative for management and content responsibility.
