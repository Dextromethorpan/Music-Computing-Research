Every pair of active noise-cancelling (ANC) headphones needs its cancellation circuit "tuned" so the anti-noise signal it generates is the right shape and timing to cancel real-world noise for that specific headphone design. This patent describes a calibration method: placing the headphones on a measuring rig, playing test signals through the ANC electronics, measuring how sound travels between the speaker, the ANC microphone, and the ear, and then using this measurement (via a mathematical technique called the Prony method) to automatically calculate the best filter settings for that model. In effect, it's an automated "tuning" process manufacturers can run on a production line rather than tuning each unit by ear.
