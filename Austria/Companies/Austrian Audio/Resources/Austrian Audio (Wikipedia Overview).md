A general-audience encyclopedia summary of Austrian Audio's history: how it was founded in July 2017 by 22 former employees of AKG Acoustics (a much older, famous Vienna microphone maker) after AKG's Vienna site was shut down following Samsung's acquisition of parent company Harman. It notes that Austrian Audio spent more than a year developing its own microphone capsule, the CKR12, before releasing its first products (the OC818 and OC18 microphones) in 2019, and that in 2025 the Danish company DPA Microphones acquired a majority stake in Austrian Audio while it kept its base in Austria. Useful as a quick, verifiable timeline before reading the more technical interviews.
