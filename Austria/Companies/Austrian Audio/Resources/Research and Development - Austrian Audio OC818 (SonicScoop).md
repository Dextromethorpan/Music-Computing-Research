Senior Acoustics Engineer Christoph Frank walks through the multi-year process of designing the OC818, a "large-diaphragm condenser" (LDC) microphone — the type with a bigger, more sensitive vibrating membrane commonly used in studios. It explains Austrian Audio's "Open Acoustics Technology": mounting the capsule so it floats freely inside the microphone body with almost no rigid supports touching it, reducing unwanted internal echoes and vibrations that would otherwise color the sound. It also covers how the mic's "polar pattern" (the shape of directions it picks up sound from) is electronically adjustable and remotely controllable via a smartphone app, and how the microphone can record two separate signals so the recording engineer can change the polar pattern after recording is finished.
