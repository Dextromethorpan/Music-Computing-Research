"Active noise cancelling" (ANC) headphones don't just physically block outside noise — they also use a tiny built-in microphone to "listen" to unwanted sound and then produce an inverted (opposite) sound wave through the speaker to cancel it out, similar to how two matching ripples in water can flatten each other. This patent describes a self-contained ANC module — a speaker plus microphone and control electronics — designed so it reacts correctly even when a headphone's seal against the ear changes (for example, if it's pushed slightly off-ear), which normally throws off how well ANC works. The module detects this seal change electrically and adjusts its noise-cancelling signal to compensate.
