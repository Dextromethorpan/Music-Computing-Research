Kent Iverson is Head of Marketing and PR at Austrian Audio, and was previously AKG's Director of Marketing and Product Management. In press interviews he has explained the company's founding story, its acquisition of former AKG manufacturing and testing equipment, and Austrian Audio's early product roadmap, including its Aurora production-line testing system and its first condenser microphones.
