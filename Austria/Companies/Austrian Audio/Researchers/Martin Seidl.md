Martin Seidl is the CEO and co-founder of Austrian Audio, and was formerly Director of Sales at AKG. He led the fundraising and formation of Austrian Audio after AKG's Vienna site closed, and is closely involved in the technical direction of the company's microphone capsule design, including the material and manufacturing choices behind the CKR12 ceramic capsule and the broader "Open Acoustics" design philosophy. He is credited as an inventor on Austrian Audio's design patents for its microphones.
