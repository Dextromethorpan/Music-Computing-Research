Christoph Frank is Senior Acoustics Engineer at Austrian Audio and holds a Master of Science in Electrical Engineering & Audio Engineering from the Technical University of Graz. He was part of the core team that designed and built the OC818 microphone and its CKR12 ceramic capsule, working on the acoustic measurement, tuning, and polar-pattern-consistency challenges of hand-built condenser microphone capsules. He is a named co-inventor on Austrian Audio's condenser microphone patent (US10602280) and its proximity-effect microphone circuit patent.
