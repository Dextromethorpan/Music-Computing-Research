Bernhard Pinter is a named inventor on several Austrian Audio headphone patents, including a self-contained active noise-cancelling (ANC) module design (US11189260), an ear cushion assembly patent, and a microphone circuit patent for linearizing the "proximity effect" (the bass boost that happens when a sound source is very close to a directional microphone). His patent record indicates a focus on the electronics and mechanical design side of Austrian Audio's headphone and microphone products.
