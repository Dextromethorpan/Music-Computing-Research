Austrian Audio is a Vienna-based manufacturer of microphones and headphones, founded in July 2017 by a core team of about 22 former AKG engineers after AKG's Vienna facility closed. The company designs and hand-builds its own microphone capsules in-house (notably the patented CKR12 ceramic capsule) rather than buying mass-produced parts, and holds multiple patents covering acoustic, mechanical, and active noise-cancellation technology.
