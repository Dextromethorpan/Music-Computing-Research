smart:EQ 4 is sonible's flagship "intelligent" equalizer plug-in — an EQ is a tool that reshapes a sound's tone by boosting or cutting different frequency (pitch) ranges. Its core feature, the "smart:filter," uses AI-based analysis to listen to a track for a few seconds, compare its frequency balance against a target profile (e.g. "vocal," "acoustic guitar," or a reference song you supply), and then automatically generate a custom EQ curve to correct tonal imbalances. A more advanced feature lets it compare up to 10 tracks at once and adjust each one to reduce "masking" — when two instruments compete for the same frequency space and become hard to hear individually — a process the company calls spectral unmasking. All AI-generated changes remain visible and fully editable by the user.
