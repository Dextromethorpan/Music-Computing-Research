An educational blog post from sonible aimed at non-technical readers, defining core AI vocabulary: artificial intelligence (AI) as the umbrella goal of making machines perform tasks that would need human-like reasoning; "GOFAI" (Good Old-Fashioned AI), meaning older hand-coded rule-based systems ("if this, then that"); machine learning (ML), where a computer improves at a task by learning patterns from example data rather than being explicitly programmed; and its two common sub-techniques, deep learning (which uses many-layered artificial neural networks loosely modeled on brain cells) and reinforcement learning (where a system learns by trial-and-error, adjusting based on rewards, like a child learning to walk). Useful as a jargon primer before reading any of sonible's other, more product-specific AI explanations.
