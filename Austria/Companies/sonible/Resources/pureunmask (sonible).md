pure:unmask is a plug-in dedicated entirely to "spectral unmasking": you load it on a track that needs to make room in the mix (e.g. a pad), feed it a second "sidechain" track as reference (e.g. lead vocal), and it automatically and continuously reduces the overlap between the two tracks' frequency content so both stay audible without one drowning out the other. It uses AI-powered signal analysis plus instrument-specific "profiles" to decide how much to reduce and where, and offers controls for how wide the affected frequency bands are (Resolution) and how quickly the effect reacts (Speed), so it can suit both slow musical passages and fast rhythmic ones.
