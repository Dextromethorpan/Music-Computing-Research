This is sonible's most detailed public explanation of how its core "smart:filter" AI-EQ technology actually works under the hood (written for the earlier smart:EQ 2, but the same underlying method still powers smart:EQ 4 today). According to the post, the smart:filter analyzes and compares the energy levels across different frequency (spectral) regions of the incoming audio over time, learning the temporal and spectral patterns in the signal. It then applies "psychoacoustic models" — mathematical models of how humans actually perceive sound — plus rules of thumb drawn from professional mixing experience to decide which frequencies sound like unwanted problems (e.g. harsh resonances) versus which are missing and need a boost, and generates a custom correction curve accordingly. The post includes concrete before/after audio examples on vocals, guitar, and drums.
