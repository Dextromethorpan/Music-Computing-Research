This company blog post announces the "autoSeries" plug-in line, a collaboration between sonible and British console maker Solid State Logic (SSL). SSL supplies the classic analog-style processing models (based on their famous 4000 series mixing consoles), while sonible supplies the "intelligent audio analysis" layer: software that listens to the incoming track and suggests a starting-point setting (an EQ curve, compression settings, or bus-processing profile) that a human engineer can then adjust. It's a concrete example of an established hardware/analog company partnering with an Austrian AI-audio-analysis specialist to package machine analysis on top of traditional processing.
