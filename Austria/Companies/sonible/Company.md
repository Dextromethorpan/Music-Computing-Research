sonible is a Graz-based audio technology company, founded in 2013 by graduates of the joint audio engineering program run by the University of Music and Performing Arts Graz (KUG) and TU Graz. It builds AI-assisted mixing and mastering plug-ins (the "smart:", "pure:", and "learn:" product lines, including smart:EQ, smart:comp, smart:reverb, and pure:unmask) that use real-time signal analysis and machine-learning models to automate tasks like equalization, compression, and reducing frequency "masking" between tracks, alongside some audio hardware.
