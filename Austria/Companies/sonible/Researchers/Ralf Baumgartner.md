Ralf Baumgartner co-founded sonible in 2013, coming out of the joint audio engineering program run by the University of Music and Performing Arts Graz (KUG) and TU Graz, and now leads the company as CEO. He oversees sonible's overall direction across its AI-assisted mixing/mastering plug-in lines (smart:, pure:, learn:) and its audio hardware products.
