Roman Perschon studied electronics and worked at AKG Acoustics in Vienna before founding Lewitt in 2010 to build a new generation of microphones and audio interfaces. He leads the company's product design and engineering out of its Vienna headquarters, which has grown into an international team developing technologies like the AURA distance-sensing microphone system.
