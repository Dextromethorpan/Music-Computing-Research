This is Lewitt's own technical explanation of AURA, a sensor system built into its RAY microphone. AURA uses a time-of-flight sensor — the same basic principle used by some phone cameras to judge distance — to continuously measure how far a speaker's mouth is from the microphone. Because moving closer to or farther from a microphone naturally changes its volume and tone (an effect called "proximity effect"), AURA uses this distance reading to automatically compensate in real time, so a podcaster or streamer who moves around still sounds consistent without manually riding a gain knob. The technology is patent-pending.
