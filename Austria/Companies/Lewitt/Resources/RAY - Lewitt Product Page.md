RAY is Lewitt's flagship microphone and the first product to use its AURA distance-sensing technology. This product page describes how the microphone combines the distance sensor with onboard sound processing to keep a speaker's voice sounding even and controlled regardless of how far they are from the mic, aimed at podcasters, streamers, and musicians who move while recording.
