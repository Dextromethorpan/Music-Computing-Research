Lewitt is a Vienna-based microphone and audio interface company founded in 2010 by ex-AKG engineer Roman Perschon. Included via path 3 — its documented innovation is AURA, a patent-pending sensor built into its RAY microphone that uses a time-of-flight (ToF) light sensor (the same kind of technology used for camera autofocus) to continuously measure how far away a speaker's mouth is, and automatically adjusts the microphone's tone and volume in real time to compensate as the person moves closer or farther away.
