VSL's own history page tells the story of founder Herbert Tucmandl, a former cellist with the Vienna Philharmonic who became frustrated with how unrealistic existing sample libraries sounded, and who spent years recording individual notes and every kind of note-to-note transition from real orchestral musicians to build a more convincing virtual orchestra. It traces the company from its founding in 2000 through the release of its first library in 2002 up to its modern Synchron Stage recording facility in Vienna.
