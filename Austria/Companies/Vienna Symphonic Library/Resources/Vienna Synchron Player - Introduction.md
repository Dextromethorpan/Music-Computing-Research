This is VSL's own manual introducing the Synchron Player, the software that plays back its orchestral sample libraries today. It explains how the player organizes huge numbers of recorded "articulations" (different playing styles like legato, staccato, or tremolo) into a browsable instrument, and how it lets a user mix between several microphone positions recorded at the same time in VSL's scoring stage — from a close, detailed sound to a distant, roomy concert-hall sound — so a composer can shape the space around the instrument after the fact.
