Vienna Symphonic Library (VSL) is a Vienna company, founded in 2000, that records real orchestral musicians and turns those recordings into playable "sample library" virtual instruments used to compose film and game music. Included via path 3 (with a supporting patent) — its documented innovation is a "bidirectional sound-parameter database" architecture, patented by founder Herbert Tucmandl, that automatically finds, time-stretches, and cross-fades the right recorded note-repetition or legato-transition sample for what a composer is playing, instead of forcing every repeated note to reuse one identical, robotic-sounding sample.
