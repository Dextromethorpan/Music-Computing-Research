Herbert Tucmandl is a former cellist (a substitute member of the Vienna Philharmonic) who later worked as a film cameraman and director before founding Vienna Symphonic Library in 2000. Frustrated by how mechanical existing orchestral sample libraries sounded, he personally recorded thousands of samples and is the named inventor on VSL's foundational patent for automatically matching and stretching recorded note-transition samples to fit a composer's tempo.
