Ferruccio Bottoni is a physicist who co-founded USound in Graz in 2014 together with Andrea Rusconi Clerici (CTO), with the goal of replacing traditional speaker technology with silicon-chip-based MEMS loudspeakers. As CEO, he has led the company through raising significant funding to bring MEMS speaker technology into commercial products like earbuds and hearing devices.
