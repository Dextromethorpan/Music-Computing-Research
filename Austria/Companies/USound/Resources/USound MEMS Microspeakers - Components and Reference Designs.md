This trade-press article describes the physical mechanism behind USound's MEMS loudspeakers in more technical detail: instead of a coil pushing a cone (as in a normal speaker), several microscopic "piezoelectric cantilevers" — thin, flexible arms etched onto a silicon chip that bend when an electrical voltage is applied — push a tiny piston back and forth to create sound waves. USound combines this speaker chip with its own audio-processing chip in a single small package, aiming for higher volume and better sound quality than earlier micro-speaker attempts.
