This tech-industry news article explains what makes USound's speakers unusual: instead of the coil-and-magnet mechanism found in virtually every traditional loudspeaker, USound builds "MEMS" speakers — microscopic devices manufactured using the same silicon-chip techniques used for computer processors. This lets a genuine loudspeaker be shrunk down to fit inside earbuds, hearing aids, and other very small devices where a normal speaker driver simply would not fit, while also being manufactured with the precision and consistency of chip fabrication.
