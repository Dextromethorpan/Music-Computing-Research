USound is a Graz company, founded in 2014, that builds MEMS loudspeakers — tiny sound-producing chips manufactured like computer chips rather than assembled from traditional speaker parts. Included via path 2/3 — its documented, patented innovation (500+ patents) replaces the coil-and-magnet mechanism of a normal speaker driver with microscopic piezoelectric "cantilevers" (thin vibrating arms etched onto silicon) that flex to push a tiny piston and produce sound, making it possible to fit a real loudspeaker into space too small for any traditional design, such as earbuds and hearing devices.
