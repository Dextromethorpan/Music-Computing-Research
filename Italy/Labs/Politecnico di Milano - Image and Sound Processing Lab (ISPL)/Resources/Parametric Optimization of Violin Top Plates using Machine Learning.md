Building on the lab's data-driven violin study, this paper builds a "parametric model" — a small set of adjustable numbers that together fully describe a shape — of a violin top plate, and uses machine learning to search for the exact geometry that produces a desired vibration pattern. In effect, it turns violin shape design into a problem a computer can solve through optimization (systematically trying and refining options), potentially letting makers explore new shapes beyond historical models like the Stradivari or Guarneri.
