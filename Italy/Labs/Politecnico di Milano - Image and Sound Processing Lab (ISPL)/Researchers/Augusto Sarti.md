Augusto Sarti founded ISPL in 1993 and is a Full Professor at Politecnico di Milano. His research covers digital signal processing, wave digital filters (a technique for simulating analog circuits), spatial and space-time audio processing, and sound analysis/synthesis. He also heads the lab's Musical Acoustics Lab at the Museo del Violino in Cremona.
