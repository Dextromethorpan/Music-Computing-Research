Alberto Bernardini is a researcher at ISPL working on virtual analog modeling of audio circuits using wave digital filter theory — a mathematical way of turning a real electronic circuit diagram into working simulation software. He maintains the lab's VIOLA toolkit and has written about musical acoustics research on the ISPL blog.
