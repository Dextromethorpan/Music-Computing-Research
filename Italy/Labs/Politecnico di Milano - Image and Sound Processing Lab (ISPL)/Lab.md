ISPL is a research lab within the Department of Electronics, Information and Bioengineering (DEIB) at Politecnico di Milano, founded in 1993 by Augusto Sarti. It works on multimedia signal processing spanning both image/video and audio/acoustic processing, with particular strength in spatial audio, sound source localization, musical acoustics, and virtual analog circuit modeling. It also runs the Musical Acoustics Lab at the Museo del Violino in Cremona.
