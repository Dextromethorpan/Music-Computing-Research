Luca Turchet is an Associate Professor at the University of Trento and head of CIMIL. He works on the Internet of Musical Things, smart musical instruments, extended reality (XR) for music, and human-computer interaction in music. He is the founding president of the Internet of Sounds Research Network and chairs the IEEE Emerging Technology Initiative on the Internet of Sounds, and he co-founded the music-tech company Elk Audio.
