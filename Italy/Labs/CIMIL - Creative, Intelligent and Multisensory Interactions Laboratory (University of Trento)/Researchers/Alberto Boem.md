Alberto Boem is an Assistant Professor at the University of Trento and CIMIL faculty member. He works on multisensory interactions in immersive media, including deformable, shape-changing interfaces and extended-reality (XR) systems for making music. He holds a PhD in Human-Computer Interaction from the University of Tsukuba, Japan.
