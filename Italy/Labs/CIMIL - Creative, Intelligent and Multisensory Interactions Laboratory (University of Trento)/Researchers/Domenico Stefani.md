Domenico Stefani is an Assistant Professor at the University of Trento and CIMIL member, working on embedded audio (audio software running efficiently on small, low-power devices), real-time Music Information Retrieval, and smart musical instruments. He completed his PhD in Computer Science at the University of Trento in 2024.
