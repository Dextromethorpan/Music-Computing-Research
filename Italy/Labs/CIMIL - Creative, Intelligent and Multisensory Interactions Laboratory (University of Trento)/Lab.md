CIMIL is a research group within the Department of Information Engineering and Computer Science (DISI) at the University of Trento, led by [[Luca Turchet]]. It works on the Internet of Musical Things and Internet of Sounds (connecting instruments and audio devices to networks and to each other), smart musical instruments, extended reality (XR) for music, embedded audio AI, and NIME-style new interfaces for musical expression.
