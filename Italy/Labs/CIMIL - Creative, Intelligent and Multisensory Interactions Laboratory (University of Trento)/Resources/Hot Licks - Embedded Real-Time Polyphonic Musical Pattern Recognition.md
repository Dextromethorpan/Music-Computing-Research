This is a VST — a standard plug-in format used inside music production software — that listens to a musician playing in real time and recognizes musical patterns or "licks" as they play them. "Polyphonic" means it can handle several notes played together at once (chords), not just a single melody line. It's "embedded," meaning it's designed to run efficiently on small, low-power hardware rather than needing a full computer. This kind of tool could power adaptive practice apps or interactive performance systems that react to what a musician is actually playing.
