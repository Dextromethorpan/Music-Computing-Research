The "Internet of Things" is the idea of everyday objects — thermostats, lightbulbs, appliances — being connected to the internet so they can share data and be controlled remotely. The "Internet of Musical Things" (IoMusT) extends that same idea to musical instruments and gear: instruments fitted with sensors, wireless connectivity, and a small onboard computer that let them talk to each other and to the cloud during a performance. An "ontology," in computing, is a formal, computer-readable vocabulary that defines the concepts and relationships in a domain (like "instrument," "performer," "gesture," "network") so different software systems can understand each other consistently. This paper defines that shared vocabulary for the IoMusT field.
