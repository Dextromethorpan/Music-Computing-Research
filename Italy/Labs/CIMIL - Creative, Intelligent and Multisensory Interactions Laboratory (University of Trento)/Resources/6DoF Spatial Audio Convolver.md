"6DoF" (six degrees of freedom) means a listener can move and rotate freely through a virtual space — as in virtual reality — rather than only turning their head in place. "Convolution" is a signal-processing technique used to simulate how sound reflects and echoes in a real room, by mathematically combining a dry (untreated) sound with a recorded "impulse response," a short measurement of how a specific room responds to sound. This tool applies convolution so that spatial audio correctly follows a listener as they move through a 6DoF virtual or augmented-reality environment, tied to CIMIL's research on musical "metaverse" experiences.
