A "Smart Musical Instrument" is a traditional instrument — say, a guitar — augmented with sensors, a small onboard computer, and network connectivity, so it can sense how it's being played and share that information live with other devices or musicians. "Ubiquitous" here means music-making that isn't confined to a studio or a stage — enabled anywhere thanks to these connected instruments. This paper defines the concept and explores what it implies for how people could make and share music in the future.
