Antonio Camurri founded InfoMus Lab in 1984 and is the scientific director of Casa Paganini – InfoMus and a Full Professor at the University of Genoa. He works on multimodal interfaces and computational models of non-verbal expressive gesture and emotion, with applications to theatre, dance, museums, and rehabilitation. He was chairman of NIME 2008, an international conference on new interfaces for musical expression.
