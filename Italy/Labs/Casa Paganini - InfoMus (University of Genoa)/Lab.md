Casa Paganini – InfoMus is an international research centre at the University of Genoa (DIBRIS department), founded in 1984 as InfoMus Lab and housed since 2005 in a restored Genoa monastery. It studies computational models of full-body expressive gesture and emotion and builds multimodal (movement + sound + video) interactive systems, applied to performing arts, museums, therapy/rehabilitation, and education.
