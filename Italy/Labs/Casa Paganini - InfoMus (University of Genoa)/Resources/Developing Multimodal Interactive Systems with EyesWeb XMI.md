EyesWeb is InfoMus's own visual-programming software platform — instead of writing lines of code, you connect building blocks on screen like a flowchart. It captures input from a camera or other sensors, such as a dancer's movement, and maps it to sound, music, or video output in real time. This paper (presented at NIME, a conference on new interfaces for musical expression) documents the "XMI" version of the platform. EyesWeb has been used since the late 1990s worldwide in research, art installations, and rehabilitation projects, making it one of Italy's most influential pieces of music-computing software.
