This foundational paper explains how a computer system can sense a performer's or listener's body movements, gestures, and other non-verbal signals, and use them to shape sound, music, or visuals in real time — what the authors call "expressive content processing." "Multimodal" means the system combines several kinds of input at once (video, audio, motion sensors) into one interactive experience. The paper is also notable as a cross-lab collaboration linking Genoa's InfoMus with Padova's CSC, showing the shared research lineage between two of Italy's oldest music-computing labs.
