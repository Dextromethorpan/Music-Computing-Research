Tempo Reale is an independent, nonprofit research, production and education center for electronic music in Florence, founded in 1987 by composer Luciano Berio. Unlike the university labs in this vault, its research output leans toward software tools, essays, and books rather than peer-reviewed papers, but it has real technical output around live sound spatialization, interaction design, and networked performance tools.
