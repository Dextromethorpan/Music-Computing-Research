mEiG is a system for generating and coordinating interactive multimedia events live — audio combined with other media like lighting or visuals — designed for performance and installation settings. It's an example of Tempo Reale's ongoing "Interaction" research theme, which looks at new ways for a human performer to control complex live-electronics setups in real time rather than relying on fixed, pre-programmed cues.
