Max/MSP is a visual programming environment musicians use to build their own custom sound and interaction tools by connecting blocks on screen. An "external" is a custom building-block that adds new functionality not built into the software by default. These two externals — nicknamed "Sound MOVement" control and fade objects — let a composer move a sound around in space during a live performance, making it seem to travel between speakers, based on a simplified control model Tempo Reale developed for its own productions.
