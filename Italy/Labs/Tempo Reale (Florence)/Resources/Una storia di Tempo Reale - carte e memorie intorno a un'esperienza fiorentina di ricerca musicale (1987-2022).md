This book documents Tempo Reale's first 35 years, built directly from the center's own archives — letters, internal memos, and production notes. It's the closest thing to an institutional research monograph on the center, giving useful context for what "research" meant in practice inside an Italian electronic-music studio founded by composer Luciano Berio, and how that work evolved from analogue tape splicing to today's digital tools.
