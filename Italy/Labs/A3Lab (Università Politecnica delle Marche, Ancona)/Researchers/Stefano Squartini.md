Stefano Squartini is an Associate Professor at Università Politecnica delle Marche and a founder of A3Lab. His research covers efficient digital signal processing and computational intelligence, with a special focus on speech/audio processing for real-time, low-power embedded applications; he chairs the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society's Task Force on Computational Audio Processing.
