Leonardo Gabrielli is a researcher at A3Lab, Università Politecnica delle Marche. He works on digital signal processing and machine learning applied to sound synthesis, including physical modeling (simulating instruments' physics in software) and virtual analog modeling (recreating the sound of physical amps and effects units), and is a regular contributor to the DAFx conference.
