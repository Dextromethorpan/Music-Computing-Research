A3Lab is a digital signal processing research lab at the Department of Information Engineering (DII) of Università Politecnica delle Marche (UNIVPM) in Ancona. It works on audio machine learning topics such as virtual analog modeling (using software, often neural networks, to recreate the sound of physical instruments, amplifiers and effects units) and physically-informed sound synthesis. The lab is a regular contributor to and organizer of DAFx (Digital Audio Effects), one of the main international conferences in this field.
