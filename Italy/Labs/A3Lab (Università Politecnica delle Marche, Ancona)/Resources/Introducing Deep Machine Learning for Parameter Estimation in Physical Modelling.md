"Physical modeling" synthesis makes sound by simulating the physics of a real instrument in software (for example, how air vibrates inside an organ pipe) rather than by playing back a recording. The catch is that these simulations have many adjustable numbers (parameters) that need to be tuned correctly to get a specific, musically convincing tone, and doing that by hand is slow and requires deep expertise. This paper trains a neural network to listen to a target sound and automatically work out which parameter settings would recreate it, tested on a real commercial digital pipe-organ model. Notably, the physical model used was developed with Viscount International, an Italian organ manufacturer also in this vault, showing a direct link between this lab's research and Italian industry.
