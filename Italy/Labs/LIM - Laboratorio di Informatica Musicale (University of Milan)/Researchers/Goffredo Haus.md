Goffredo Haus founded LIM in 1985 and is now Professor Emeritus at the University of Milan. He set the lab's long-term research direction toward formal, computational representations of music — the idea that a piece of music can be described in a structured digital format that links its score, audio, and performance data together, which later grew into the IEEE 1599 standard.
