LIM (Laboratorio di Informatica Musicale, "Music Informatics Laboratory") is part of the Department of Computer Science at the University of Milan (Università Statale). Founded in 1985 by Goffredo Haus, it focuses on formal digital representations of music, digitizing and reading sheet music, and automatically synchronizing audio recordings with the written score.
