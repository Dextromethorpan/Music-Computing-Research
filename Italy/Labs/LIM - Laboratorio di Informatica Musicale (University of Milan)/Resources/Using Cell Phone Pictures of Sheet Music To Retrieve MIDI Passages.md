Optical Music Recognition (OMR) is the musical equivalent of the technology (OCR) that lets a computer read printed text from a photo. This paper studies taking an ordinary phone photo of a printed page of sheet music and automatically matching it to the corresponding digital MIDI (a standard format for representing musical notes and timing) version of the piece. It's useful for a musician who sees music on paper — in a library, a friend's house, an old book — and wants to quickly find a playable digital copy or recording of it.
