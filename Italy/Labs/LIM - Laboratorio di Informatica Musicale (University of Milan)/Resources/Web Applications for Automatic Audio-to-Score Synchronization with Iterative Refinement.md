This paper describes a browser-based tool that automatically lines up an audio recording with its written score, refining the match over several passes rather than trying to get it perfect in one shot. "Synchronization" here means marking, for every moment in the recording, which note or measure of the score is being played. It's a practical example of how score-following research gets turned into an actual web app that a musician or musicologist can use directly, without installing special software.
