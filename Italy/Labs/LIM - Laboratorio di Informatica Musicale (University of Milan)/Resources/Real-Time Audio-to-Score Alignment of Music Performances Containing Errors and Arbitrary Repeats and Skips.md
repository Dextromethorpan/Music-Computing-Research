"Score following" is the task of getting a computer to track, in real time, exactly where a live musician currently is within a written score — even if the performer skips a section, repeats a passage, or makes a mistake. This paper tackles a robust way to keep that tracking accurate under those messy, realistic conditions rather than only for a perfect run-through. It's a building block for practical tools like automatic page-turners, interactive accompaniment software that follows a soloist, and rehearsal aids.
