"Photogrammetry" means digitally reconstructing a 3D model of an object from many overlapping photographs, and "multiband/spectroscopic" imaging captures data at wavelengths beyond what the human eye can see (like infrared and ultraviolet) to reveal things such as varnish layers, past repairs, or the wood's composition. This paper combines both techniques into one workflow to document and analyze two historic instruments non-invasively — meaning no physical sampling or damage to a priceless antique instrument is required to study what it's made of and how it was built.
