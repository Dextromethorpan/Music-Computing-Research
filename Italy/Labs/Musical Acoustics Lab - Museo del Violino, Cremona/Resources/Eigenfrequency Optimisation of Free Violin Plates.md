"Eigenfrequencies" are the natural resonant pitches an object rings at when tapped — for a violin's wooden top or back plate, these largely determine its tonal character before it's even assembled into a finished instrument. This paper presents a method for optimizing, or fine-tuning, those frequencies by systematically adjusting a plate's shape and thickness, giving instrument makers a scientific, repeatable way to hit target acoustic properties instead of relying purely on inherited craft tradition and trial-and-error.
