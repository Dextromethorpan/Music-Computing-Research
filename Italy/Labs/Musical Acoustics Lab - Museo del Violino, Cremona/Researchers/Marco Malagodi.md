Marco Malagodi is a Professor at the University of Pavia and heads the Arvedi Laboratory of Non-Invasive Diagnostics, which shares space and equipment with Politecnico di Milano's Musical Acoustics Lab inside the Museo del Violino. He works on non-invasive material and diagnostic analysis of historic musical instruments — for example using spectroscopy and multiband imaging to study varnish, wood, and construction methods without damaging the instrument.
