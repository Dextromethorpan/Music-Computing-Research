The Musical Acoustics Lab is a joint research facility inside the Museo del Violino (Violin Museum) in Cremona, run on-site by Politecnico di Milano's Image and Sound Processing Lab team (led by [[Augusto Sarti]]), alongside the University of Pavia's Arvedi Laboratory of Non-Invasive Diagnostics. Together they study the acoustics, construction, and material composition of historic instruments like Stradivari and Guarneri violins, combining acoustic engineering with non-invasive scientific imaging.
