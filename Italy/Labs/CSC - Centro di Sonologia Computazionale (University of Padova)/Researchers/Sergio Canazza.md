Sergio Canazza is the current Scientific Director of CSC and a Full Professor at the University of Padova; he is also CEO of the university spin-off Audio Innova Srl. His research covers digitizing and restoring historic audio recordings, affective computing in music performance, and using microphone arrays to locate the source of a sound for security applications.
