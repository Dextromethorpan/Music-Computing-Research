Alvise Vidolin is one of CSC's founding figures, having co-created the lab's first computer-music piece ("Consonantico," 1975). As the lab's Artistic Director, he works at the intersection of live electronic music performance and signal processing, helping shape how new digital tools are used in actual musical interpretation rather than only in the lab.
