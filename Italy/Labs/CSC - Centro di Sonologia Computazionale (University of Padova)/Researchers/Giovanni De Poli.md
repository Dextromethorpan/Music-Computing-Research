Giovanni De Poli was CSC's director from 1992 to 2015 and is a Full Professor of Computer Engineering at the University of Padova. His research centers on "affective computing" in music — teaching computers to recognize and generate emotional expression in performance — as well as broader digital tools for musical composition and performance.
