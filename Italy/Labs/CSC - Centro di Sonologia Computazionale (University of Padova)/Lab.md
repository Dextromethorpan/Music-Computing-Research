CSC (Centro di Sonologia Computazionale, "Center for Computational Sonology") is part of the Department of Information Engineering (DEI) at the University of Padova. Founded in 1979 with roots going back to 1960s electronic-music experiments, it is one of Europe's oldest computer-music research centers, working on expressive/affective computing in music performance, digital preservation of historic audio, and computational creativity.
