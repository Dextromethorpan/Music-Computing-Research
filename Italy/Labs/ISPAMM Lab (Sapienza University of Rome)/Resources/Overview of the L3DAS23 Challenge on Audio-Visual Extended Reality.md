L3DAS ("Learning 3D Audio Sources") is a recurring international machine learning competition, and this paper describes its 2023 edition, presented at the major IEEE ICASSP conference. The challenge gives researchers a shared dataset of Ambisonics recordings (audio captured from all directions at once) made in simulated reverberant rooms, paired with pictures of the space. Competitors build machine learning models to do two things: clean up noisy speech in 3D ("3D speech enhancement") and detect where and what sound events are happening in the room ("3D sound event localization and detection"). This kind of shared benchmark is how the audio machine learning research community compares different approaches on equal footing, and it is directly relevant to augmented and virtual reality applications.
