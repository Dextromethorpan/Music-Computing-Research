Aurelio Uncini is a Full Professor at Sapienza University of Rome and one of the founding faculty of the ISPAMM lab. He works on adaptive signal processing and neural networks, with recurring involvement in the lab's spatial-audio and 3D-audio machine learning projects, including co-authoring the Ambisonics and L3DAS papers.
