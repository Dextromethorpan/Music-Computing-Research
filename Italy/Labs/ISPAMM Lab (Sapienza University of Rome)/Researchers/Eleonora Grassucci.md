Eleonora Grassucci is an Assistant Professor at Sapienza University of Rome and a member of ISPAMM. She works on "hypercomplex" deep learning — using extended number systems like quaternions inside neural networks — and is the lead author of the lab's paper on using this approach to represent moving 3D sound fields captured with Ambisonics microphones.
