Danilo Comminiello is an Associate Professor at Sapienza University of Rome who leads the ISPAMM group. His research covers adaptive signal processing and deep learning, with a particular focus on 3D and immersive audio — including neural networks that use quaternion mathematics (a way of jointly representing rotation and position) to process Ambisonics recordings, and organizing the L3DAS 3D-audio machine learning challenge.
