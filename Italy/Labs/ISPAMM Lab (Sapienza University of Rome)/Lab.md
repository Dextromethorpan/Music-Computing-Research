ISPAMM (Intelligent Signal Processing and Multimedia) is a research group in the DIET Department of Sapienza University of Rome, working on deep learning for multimedia signals. Within that broader scope, a significant strand of its work is specifically about 3D/spatial audio: using neural networks (including "hypercomplex" number systems like quaternions, which are a compact way to represent multi-directional sound mathematically) to process Ambisonics recordings — a microphone/format technique that captures sound from all directions around a point, used for immersive and VR audio.
