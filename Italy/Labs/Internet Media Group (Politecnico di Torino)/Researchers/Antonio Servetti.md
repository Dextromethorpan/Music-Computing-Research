Antonio Servetti has been an Assistant Professor at Politecnico di Torino since 2007, working on speech/audio processing and real-time multimedia network protocols. Within the Internet Media Group he focuses on Networked Music Performance — the technical problem of letting musicians in different locations play together live over the internet without the delay or dropouts breaking the timing of the performance.
