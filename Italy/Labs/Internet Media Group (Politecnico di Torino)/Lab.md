The Internet Media Group, part of the Department of Control and Computer Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, researches audio and video streaming over networks. Its music-computing-relevant work focuses on Networked Music Performance (NMP): letting musicians in different physical locations play together in real time over the internet, which requires solving very strict low-latency audio transmission and packet-loss problems (a dropped or delayed network packet can ruin the timing of a live performance).
