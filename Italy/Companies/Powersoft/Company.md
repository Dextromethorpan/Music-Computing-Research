Powersoft (Scandicci, Florence) designs and manufactures professional audio power amplifiers and loudspeaker DSP systems for touring, installed sound, and OEM markets. Its R&D is genuinely patent- and paper-backed, focused on Class-D switching amplification, DSP-based loudspeaker control, and closed-loop transducer feedback systems.
