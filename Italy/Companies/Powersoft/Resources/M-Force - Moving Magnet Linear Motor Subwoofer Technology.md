M-Force is Powersoft's subwoofer driver design that replaces the traditional heavy magnet-and-coil "motor" structure inside a loudspeaker with a "moving magnet" design instead. This reduces the driver's weight while increasing how much power it can handle — a mechanical engineering innovation rather than purely an electronics one, aimed at making very powerful bass speakers lighter and easier to transport and install.
