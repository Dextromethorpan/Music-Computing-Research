This peer-reviewed Audio Engineering Society (AES) convention paper explains Powersoft's Differential Pressure Control (DPC) technology, which measures the actual air pressure on both sides of a loudspeaker's moving cone (its "membrane") in real time and feeds that measurement back into the amplifier — similar to how a thermostat reads the room's temperature and adjusts the heater accordingly. This "closed feedback loop" lets the amplifier automatically correct distortion and push more accurate, powerful sound from the speaker than an open-loop (no-feedback) design could achieve from the same-sized hardware.
