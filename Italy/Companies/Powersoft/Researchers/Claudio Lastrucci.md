Claudio Lastrucci co-founded Powersoft in 1995 with his brother Luca and has led its R&D — audio amplification, DSP, applied mechanics, and transducer design — since the company's founding. He is a named co-author on Powersoft's 2011 AES convention paper describing its Differential Pressure Control technology.
