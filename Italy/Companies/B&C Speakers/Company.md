B&C Speakers (Bagno a Ripoli, Florence) designs and manufactures loudspeaker transducers and drivers — woofers, compression drivers, coaxial components, horns — mostly as a component supplier to other speaker brands. Founded in 1945/1946, it has the strongest verifiable patent portfolio of the Italian loudspeaker companies researched for this vault.
