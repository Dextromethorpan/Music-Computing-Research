A "coaxial" driver puts two speakers — one for lower, one for higher frequencies — on the same axis so sound seems to come from a single point, which helps give even coverage across a room. This patent covers a driver with two vibrating diaphragms sharing one housing, each feeding its own acoustic path, which then merge into a single shared horn opening. It also includes a passive low-pass filter (a circuit that blocks high frequencies) built directly into the acoustic path itself, rather than needing separate external electronics.
