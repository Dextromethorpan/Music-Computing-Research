A "ring radiator" is a diaphragm shaped like a ring rather than a solid dome or cone, which can push more air relative to its size and behave more predictably at high frequencies. Industry press coverage describes this driver as the result of roughly five years of R&D and two patent applications, featuring a "midrange integrator" component that lets two diaphragms work together across a wide frequency range without audible glitches in tone or timing.
