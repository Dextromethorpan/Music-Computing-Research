A "waveguide," or horn, shapes and directs the sound coming from a small speaker driver over a wider area, so it spreads out more evenly instead of just beaming forward narrowly. This patent describes a waveguide with a hollow, flared shape and small internal pins placed directly in the path of the sound, specifically to interfere with and smooth out how sound waves travel through it — reducing unwanted resonances and distortion in the horn's overall response.
