Umberto Nicolao is an Italian acoustics consultant, teacher, and audio journalist (Milan Polytechnic, Electronic Engineering, 1987) who has consulted for Sonus faber, Neumann, and Sennheiser. He is the originator of the patented Sound Field Shaper technology, a rear-firing speaker module used in some Sonus faber loudspeakers to restore a sense of depth to the reproduced sound field, and also assisted with acoustic design on later Sonus faber flagship models.
