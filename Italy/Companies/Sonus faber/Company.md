Sonus faber, founded in 1983 in Arcugnano (Veneto), is a maker of handcrafted high-end audiophile loudspeakers — a different market segment from the touring/pro-audio companies already in this vault (B&C Speakers, K-array, Outline, RCF). It qualifies via the documented product/architectural innovation path: its patented "Sound Field Shaper" (patented in 2000, developed with acoustics consultant Umberto Nicolao) adds a second, rear-facing set of drivers whose level and aim the listener can adjust, specifically to restore the sense of depth and reverberant space that a normal forward-firing speaker pair tends to flatten out — a specific, nameable acoustic mechanism rather than a general voicing or cabinet-quality claim.
