RCF (Reggio Emilia, Italy) is a professional-audio manufacturer founded in 1949, one of the few loudspeaker makers that designs and builds transducers, enclosures, amplifiers, and DSP/control software entirely in-house, with dedicated R&D labs and anechoic (echo-free) test chambers. No RCF-assigned granted patent could be independently verified in this pass, so its R&D is represented here through engineering documentation and an academic partnership rather than patents.
