RCF co-runs a real postgraduate university course with the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia (Unimore), teaching acoustics and sound engineering with RCF's own engineers and lab facilities directly involved. This is a genuine academic-industry link rather than a marketing exercise, showing RCF invests in training the next generation of acoustic engineers and gives students hands-on access to its own labs.
