A "transducer" is the part of a speaker that converts an electrical signal into sound, or vice versa — essentially its motor and diaphragm (vibrating cone or dome) assembly. This technical catalog details RCF's engineering choices: exotic diaphragm materials like titanium and Kevlar, neodymium magnets (a very strong, lightweight magnet type), and a "T-pole" design — a specially shaped metal ring that reduces distortion caused by the magnet's field changing as current flows through the coil. It also covers "Direct Drive" construction, where the wire coil that moves the diaphragm is bonded directly onto a titanium dome for better high-frequency performance.
