A "compression driver" is the small, high-pressure horn-loaded speaker used to reproduce high and mid frequencies in large PA (public address) systems. This is an independent engineering test of an RCF driver, describing how RCF designs the diaphragm's suspension (the flexible ring holding the moving diaphragm in place) using FEM, the Finite Element Method — a computer simulation technique engineers use to predict how a physical part will behave before building it — specifically to reduce distortion and extend low-frequency response. It's evidence RCF does simulation-based engineering design rather than only trial-and-error.
