Fatar, founded in 1956 in Recanati, designs and manufactures the physical "keybeds" (the mechanical key-and-sensor assemblies) used inside a large share of the world's digital pianos, synthesizers, and organs, including instruments from many other brands (OEM/ODM supply). It qualifies via the documented product/architectural innovation path: its Polyphonic Aftertouch keybeds (TP/8S PAFT, TP/90 PAFT) give every single key its own independent pressure sensor for "aftertouch" (extra expression you add by pressing harder after a key is already down), instead of the one shared sensor most electronic keyboards use for the whole keyboard — a specific, nameable mechanical/electronic redesign, not just incremental keyboard-feel tuning.
