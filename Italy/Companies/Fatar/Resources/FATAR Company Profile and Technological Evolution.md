This is Fatar's own account of its keyboard-mechanism engineering, including the specific innovation that qualifies it for this vault: Polyphonic Aftertouch. Ordinary electronic keyboards typically have a single pressure sensor shared across the whole keybed for "aftertouch" (the extra expressive control you get by pushing down harder on a key you're already holding), which means only one note at a time can really benefit from that expression. Fatar's Polyphonic Aftertouch keybeds (TP/8S PAFT from 2020, TP/90 PAFT from 2022) give every individual key its own independent pressure sensor, so a player can apply different amounts of aftertouch to different notes within the same chord — a specific, documented mechanical and electronic redesign of the keybed, not just a marketing term for "better feel."
