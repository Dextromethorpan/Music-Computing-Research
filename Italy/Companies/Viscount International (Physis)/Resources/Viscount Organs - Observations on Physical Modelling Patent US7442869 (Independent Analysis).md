This is a detailed, plain-English walkthrough by an independent expert (not Viscount) explaining what the company's physical-modeling patent actually claims. It explains that unlike most physical models — which tightly link a "generator" and "resonator" in a continuous feedback loop, mimicking exactly how a real pipe works — Viscount's model deliberately decouples the two for computing-efficiency reasons, because a full pipe organ must simulate up to 500+ pipes sounding simultaneously in real time. It's one of the most accessible deep-dives available on how this specific technology actually works.
