Viscount's own explanation of Physis technology describes an organ pipe as a "mechanical multi-modal waveguide resonator": air flow excites the pipe, which "rings" according to its shape and material, and this is calculated in real time by multiple onboard DSP chips performing billions of calculations per second. It's the company's primary-source counterpart to independent analyses of its patent, aimed at a semi-technical audience.
