This granted US patent, the founding patent behind Viscount's "Physis" technology, describes a mathematical method for making an electronic organ sound like a real pipe organ by calculating — rather than recording — the physics of how a pipe generates sound. An oscillating column of air is modeled with a "generator" (mimicking the air jet at the pipe's mouth) and a "resonator" (mimicking the pipe body, using a waveguide, a common technique for digitally simulating tubes and strings). At the time of filing it was the only patented approach in the world for physically modeling organ pipes rather than sampling them.
