Viscount International (Mondaino, Rimini) makes digital and hybrid classical organs and pianos. Its R&D focus is "Physis" physical modeling sound synthesis — mathematically simulating, in real time, how a real organ pipe physically produces sound, rather than playing back recordings of one. It holds a real granted patent for this approach and is unusually well-documented by an independent third-party technical analysis.
