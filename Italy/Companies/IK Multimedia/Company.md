IK Multimedia, founded in Modena in 1996, is best known for AmpliTube guitar/amp simulation software. It qualifies for this vault via the documented product/architectural innovation path: its "AI Machine Modeling" technology (marketed as TONEX) is a specific, nameable mechanism — a deep neural network trained on paired "dry" (unprocessed) and "wet" (processed) guitar recordings that learns to reproduce an entire amp-and-pedal signal chain, rather than relying on hand-tuned circuit equations. IK Multimedia also holds patents on earlier audio-composition technology, giving it some traditional IP grounding as well.
