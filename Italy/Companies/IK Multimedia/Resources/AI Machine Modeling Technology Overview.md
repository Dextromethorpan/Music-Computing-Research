Guitarists often love the exact sound of a specific amplifier, cabinet, and pedal combination ("rig") but can't carry that heavy, expensive hardware everywhere. IK Multimedia's AI Machine Modeling (sold under the TONEX product line) lets someone recreate their own rig in software: you play a specially prepared guitar signal through your real gear, record both the original ("dry") signal and the processed ("wet") output, and feed both into a deep neural network. The network compares the two recordings and learns an algorithm — called a "Tone Model" — that reproduces the rig's exact behavior, including nonlinear distortion from overdrive or fuzz pedals. This is a documented, specific technical mechanism (paired dry/wet neural capture, distinct from older technique-based amp modeling or hardware profiling), which is why it qualifies this company for the vault even without academic papers.
