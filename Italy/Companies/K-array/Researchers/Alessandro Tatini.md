Alessandro Tatini co-founded the company that became K-array in 1990 with his father Carlo Tatini and Massimo Ferrati, initially building broadcast studios before pivoting to compact, slim professional loudspeakers after noticing how difficult touring audio gear was to transport. He now leads both the company's R&D direction and its executive side.
