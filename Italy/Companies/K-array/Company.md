K-array (Scarperia e San Piero, Tuscany) designs slim, lightweight line-array and steerable loudspeaker systems for touring and installed sound, built around proprietary "Slim Array Technology" and "Electronic Beam Steering" (EBS) — software-controlled directional sound. Founded in 1990 (originally as HP Sound Equipment, rebranded K-array in 2005). No granted patent assigned to K-array could be verified; its R&D appears documented mainly through detailed technical product literature rather than patents.
