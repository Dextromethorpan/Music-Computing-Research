"Beam steering" means electronically changing the direction sound travels from a speaker array without physically moving or re-aiming the speakers. A "line array" is a vertical stack of identical speaker units that together focus sound into a narrow, controlled vertical beam aimed at an audience — useful in rooms with a lot of echo. This article explains that once a line array is physically installed, its aiming normally can't be changed; K-array's EBS instead uses digital signal processing to steer that beam electronically after installation, correcting for misalignment or letting the same hardware cover differently shaped audiences.
