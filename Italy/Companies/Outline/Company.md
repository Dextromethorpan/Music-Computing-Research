Outline (Flero, near Brescia) designs and manufactures professional loudspeaker systems, DSP hardware, and precision acoustic test/measurement equipment, with an emphasis on FPGA-based (reconfigurable digital chip) signal processing for concert sound. Founded in 1973 by Guido Noselli, it remains family-owned and holds a real patent portfolio around array/waveguide loudspeaker design.
