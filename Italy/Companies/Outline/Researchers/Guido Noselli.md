Guido Noselli, a musician-turned-engineer, founded Outline in 1973 specifically to improve live-sound audio quality. He is credited as inventor or co-inventor across most of Outline's patent portfolio, including the DPRWG ribbon waveguide technology. He passed away on 1 December 2006.
