A "ribbon driver" uses a very thin, lightweight conductive strip instead of a coil-and-cone to produce high frequencies — normally fragile and inefficient designs. Outline's DPRWG combines a ribbon-style high-frequency section with a "double-parabola" waveguide (two combined curved horn shapes) to reach unusually high output level for its size while staying physically compact, enabling a highly compact three-way (bass/mid/high) speaker cabinet called "Butterfly."
