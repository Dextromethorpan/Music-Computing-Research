This company page states that Outline develops FPGA-based signal processing technology for its speaker systems and test/measurement products — an FPGA is a customizable digital processing chip, as opposed to a fixed-function one — and credits founder Guido Noselli's large number of patents as central to the company's identity. This claim is consistent with Outline's confirmed array and waveguide patents, making the page a useful (if promotional) summary of the company's R&D posture.
