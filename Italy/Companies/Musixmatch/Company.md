Musixmatch (Bologna, Italy) is a lyrics platform and API provider offering crowdsourced, time-synced song lyrics. It has grown an internal machine-learning/AI team whose R&D focus is natural language processing and Music Information Retrieval applied to lyrics — emotion detection, lyrics-audio alignment, and public research datasets.
