Musixmatch released a free public dataset of 237,702 song lyrics in "bag of words" format — meaning it lists which words appear and how many times, rather than the full text, for copyright reasons — tied to the Million Song Dataset, a standard academic benchmark used in Music Information Retrieval (the research field about getting computers to understand and organize music). It went on to become one of the most-cited public lyrics resources in that field, used by researchers studying things like genre classification, mood detection, and text-audio relationships in songs.
