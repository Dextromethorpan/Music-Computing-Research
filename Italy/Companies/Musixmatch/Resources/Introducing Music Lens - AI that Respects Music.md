Music Lens is Musixmatch's newest AI tool, described in this blog post, which automatically scans large music catalogs (collections of songs owned by record labels or publishers) and extracts structured insights from them — such as themes, lyrical content, or metadata — using Musixmatch's internal lyrics and music AI models. It's lighter on technical detail than a research paper, but shows the company has an active, ongoing internal AI research effort beyond its original lyrics-database product.
