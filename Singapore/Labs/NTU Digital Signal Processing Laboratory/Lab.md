The Digital Signal Processing (DSP) Laboratory is based in the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU). Led by Professor Woon-Seng Gan, it is one of Singapore's longest-running audio engineering labs (established 1998) and works across active noise control (canceling unwanted noise electronically), spatial audio, urban soundscape design, and AI-based sound event detection.
