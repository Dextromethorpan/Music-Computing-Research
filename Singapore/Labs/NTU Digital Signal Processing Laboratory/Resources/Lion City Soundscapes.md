Lion City Soundscapes ("Lion City" is a nickname for Singapore) is a dataset of audio and video recordings made at 62 different locations around Singapore, capturing what those places actually sound and look like day to day. The team recorded long sessions (9 to 38 minutes) at each spot and then cropped them into shorter one-minute clips so they could be used in listening studies without tiring out participants. The paper describes a statistical method (a variant of "partitioning around medoids," a way of picking a small set of representative examples from a larger group) used to choose a diverse, representative subset of these recordings for future soundscape research. It builds directly on the same lab's ARAUS work on how people emotionally respond to urban sound environments.
