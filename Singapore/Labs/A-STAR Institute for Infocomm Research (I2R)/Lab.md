I2R is Singapore's largest ICT (information and communications technology) research institute, part of A*STAR (Agency for Science, Technology and Research). Its speech and audio researchers work on singing voice conversion and synthesis, general speech synthesis, and voice biometrics/speaker verification, including a well-known singing-enhancement product (I2R Speech2Singing) and joint projects with Baidu on song-identification and voiceprint technology.
