SINGAN is a system for singing voice conversion: taking a recording of one singer performing a song and making it sound like a different target singer sang it, while keeping the melody and lyrics the same. It uses a Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) — a pair of neural networks where one (the generator) tries to produce convincing converted singing and the other (the discriminator) tries to tell real singing apart from converted singing, with the two improving each other through competition during training. A notable strength of SINGAN is that it works well even with only a small amount of matched ("parallel") training recordings from both singers, and the paper reports it outperforming earlier baseline methods for singing voice conversion quality. The work was done with A*STAR I2R researcher Minghui Dong as a co-author, presented at APSIPA ASC 2019.
