This is a "voiceprint" speaker-verification technology developed by the Baidu-I2R Research Centre (a joint lab between Baidu and A*STAR's Institute for Infocomm Research). Speaker verification means checking whether a voice sample actually belongs to the specific person it claims to be from — similar to a fingerprint but using the unique characteristics of someone's voice instead. This was billed as the first such technology built directly into a smartphone (the Lenovo A586, released in China in late 2012), letting the phone unlock or authenticate a user by having them speak rather than type a password. While closer to voice-security/biometrics than music per se, it's part of I2R's speech-processing research that overlaps heavily with the same voice-modeling techniques used in voice conversion and singing-voice work.
