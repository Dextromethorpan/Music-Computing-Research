"听歌识曲" (Ting Ge Shi Qu, meaning "hear a tune and know the song") is a Shazam-style song-identification feature built by the Baidu-I2R Research Centre (BIRC) — a joint lab set up in 2012 between Baidu (China's largest search engine company) and A*STAR's Institute for Infocomm Research. It listens to music playing in the environment, generates a compact "audio fingerprint" of the waveform, and matches it against a database of several million songs (synced with charts like the Billboard Hits and UK Chart) to return the song title, artist, and album art within seconds. It launched inside the Baidu Music app in 2013 and was one of the first Shazam-style features embedded in a major Chinese consumer music app. This is an applied engineering product built on music-audio-matching research rather than a formal academic paper.
