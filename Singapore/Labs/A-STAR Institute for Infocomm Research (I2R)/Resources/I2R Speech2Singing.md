I2R Speech2Singing is software that turns an ordinary spoken (or off-key sung) recording into a polished singing performance. It works by taking recordings from professional singers as a template, using speech-recognition technology to find exactly where each syllable falls in time, and then stretching or compressing the timing of the input voice — plus correcting its pitch — to match the professional template. Crucially, it keeps the original speaker's own voice character (timbre) and the words unchanged; only the melody and rhythm are corrected. Singaporeans first encountered it via the "Sing for Singapore" mobile app for National Day Parade 2013. The technology won the Best Show & Tell award at Interspeech 2014, a major international speech-research conference.
