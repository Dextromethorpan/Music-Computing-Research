Minghui Dong leads I2R's Speech Generation Team and has spent his career on speech and singing-voice technology, including building text-to-speech systems for English, Chinese, Malay, and Tamil across cloud, PC, and mobile platforms. He led the team behind I2R Speech2Singing, software that turns spoken or off-key recordings into polished singing, and has co-authored later academic work on singing voice conversion (SINGAN) and cross-lingual voice conversion. He holds a PhD from the National University of Singapore and has published over 100 research papers.
