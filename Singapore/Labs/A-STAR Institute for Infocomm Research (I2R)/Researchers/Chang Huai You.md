Chang Huai You is an I2R researcher working on voice conversion and speech synthesis. His recent work (with colleague Minghui Dong) studies how to convert a voice across languages — for example making a Mandarin recording sound like a target speaker whose only available voice samples are in English — by combining methods that need matched training pairs with ones that don't. He has also contributed, as an I2R co-author, to PromptVC, a system that lets people control the speaking style of a converted voice by typing a plain-language description.
