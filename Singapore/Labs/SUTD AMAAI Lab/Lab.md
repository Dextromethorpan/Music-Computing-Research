The Audio, Music, and AI (AMAAI) Lab is based at the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD), led by Associate Professor Dorien Herremans. The lab works on generative AI for music and audio — models that can create or edit music from text descriptions — along with affective computing (getting computers to understand or respond to emotion) and multimodal AI more broadly. It has released widely-used open datasets and models, including the text-to-music model Mustango and the large-scale MIDI captioning dataset MidiCaps.
