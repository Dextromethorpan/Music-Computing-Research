Mustango is an AI model that generates music from a written description, but with much finer control than most text-to-music tools. Instead of just describing a mood or genre, you can specify musical details like the chords, tempo, beat, and key you want, and the model (built around a diffusion model — a type of AI that generates audio by gradually turning noise into a finished sound) will follow them. The team also released a companion dataset called MusicBench with over 52,000 examples of music paired with these kinds of detailed, music-theory-aware descriptions, which helped train the model to actually understand and act on musical instructions rather than just general mood words.
