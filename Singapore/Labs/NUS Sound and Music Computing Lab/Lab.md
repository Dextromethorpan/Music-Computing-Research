The Sound and Music Computing (SMC) Lab is based in the School of Computing at the National University of Singapore (NUS). Led by Associate Professor Ye Wang since the mid-2000s, the lab works on music information retrieval (teaching computers to understand recorded music) with a strong focus on applying it to e-learning and e-health, such as helping people learn instruments, recover from strokes, or manage hearing and speech conditions through music-based tools.
