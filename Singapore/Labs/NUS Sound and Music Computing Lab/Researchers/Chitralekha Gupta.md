Chitralekha Gupta did her PhD in the Sound and Music Computing Lab under Ye Wang, working on audio signal processing problems such as detecting mispronunciation in singing and building tools for singing/language training. She has since continued audio and generative-sound research at NUS, including work on controllable synthesis of environmental sound textures.
