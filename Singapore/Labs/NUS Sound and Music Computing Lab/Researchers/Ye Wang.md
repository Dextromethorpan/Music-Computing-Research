Ye Wang directs the Sound and Music Computing Lab at NUS, which he established after working as a researcher at Nokia Research Center in Finland. His research applies music information retrieval and mobile/wearable computing to e-learning and e-health, including systems that use rhythmic cues to help people with Parkinson's disease walk more steadily and mobile apps that support music-based therapy and language learning.
