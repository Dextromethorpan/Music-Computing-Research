LyricAlly is a system that automatically lines up the words of a song's lyrics with the exact moments they are sung in a recording, without a person having to do it by hand. It works by detecting musical structure such as beats and phrase boundaries in the audio, then using that structure together with the lyric text to estimate when each line and syllable is sung. This kind of automatic timing is the technology behind karaoke displays and "sing-along" apps. Published in IEEE Transactions on Audio, Speech and Language Processing, it was one of the earlier systematic attempts at this problem and combined signal analysis with text processing techniques.
