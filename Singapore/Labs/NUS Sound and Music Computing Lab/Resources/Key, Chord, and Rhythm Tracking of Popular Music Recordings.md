This paper describes a system that listens to a recording of a pop song and automatically works out three things at once: what musical key it's in, what chords are being played over time, and where the beats fall. Instead of solving each problem separately, the system uses knowledge of typical pop-song structure (like verse-chorus patterns and common chord progressions) to make all three estimates more reliable together. This kind of automatic analysis underlies tools that generate guitar tabs, chord charts, or practice aids from an audio recording. It was published in Computer Music Journal, a long-running academic journal on music technology.
