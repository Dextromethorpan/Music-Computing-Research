ByteDance (parent company of TikTok) runs a global AI research organization called "Seed," which explicitly lists Singapore alongside China and the US as one of its lab locations. Within Seed, the "Seed Speech" team (which absorbed the earlier "SAMI" — Speech, Audio & Music Intelligence — research group) works on speech, audio, and music AI, including music generation models, singing voice synthesis, and music source separation. Note: most published papers below list Chinese/US-based ByteDance offices as author affiliations; Singapore's role is confirmed by ByteDance's own statements that Seed research spans Singapore, and by ByteDance job listings for AI research roles based in Singapore, but individual papers below are not all separately confirmed as Singapore-authored — they are included honestly as output of ByteDance's broader Seed Speech/SAMI research org, which has a genuine Singapore research presence.
