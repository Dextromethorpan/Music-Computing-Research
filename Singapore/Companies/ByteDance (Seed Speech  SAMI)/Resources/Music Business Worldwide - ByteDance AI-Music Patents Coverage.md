This trade-press article from Music Business Worldwide reports that ByteDance (TikTok's parent company) had, by 2023, been granted US patents covering AI-generated music (including a "method of generating music data" covering how a piece of music's structure can be automatically produced), while its research division — the SAMI (Speech, Audio & Music Intelligence) team — was simultaneously publishing academic work on a generative music model (MeLoDy) trained on 257,000 hours of music drawn from 6.4 million audio files. The article, along with related follow-up pieces from the same outlet, documents a wider pattern of ByteDance/TikTok filing AI-music-related patents and trademarks (including through its affiliate Lemon Inc.) covering things like automatic background-music generation for videos, showing that the company's academic research and its commercial patent strategy in AI music are closely connected.
