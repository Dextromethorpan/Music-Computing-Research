ByteDance sponsored ISMIR (International Society for Music Information Retrieval) 2020, the leading academic conference for research on analyzing and understanding music with computers, and hosted a virtual booth describing the work of its SAMI (Speech, Audio & Music Intelligence) research team. The page lists several of the team's projects and papers presented that year, including a dataset and tool for musical "loops" (short repeating audio clips used in music production), a system for judging whether two loops fit together musically, a study of human-AI collaborative songwriting, an automatic piano-music transcription system, sound event detection (recognizing what real-world sounds — sirens, footsteps, applause — are happening in an audio clip), and demos of separating a song into its vocal, drum, and bass tracks. It's useful as a single snapshot of the breadth of ByteDance's music/audio research team at that time, spanning offices in London, California, and Beijing/Shanghai.
