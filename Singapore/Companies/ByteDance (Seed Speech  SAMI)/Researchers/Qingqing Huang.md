Qingqing Huang is a researcher at ByteDance working on music and audio understanding and generation, including large language model (LLM) approaches applied to audio. She was a co-author on Seed-Music, ByteDance's unified framework for high-quality, controllable AI music generation, and previously worked on Noise2Music, a text-to-music diffusion model, before joining ByteDance.
