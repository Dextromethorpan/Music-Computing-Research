Yun-Ning (Amy) Hung worked as a research engineer at TikTok/ByteDance, where she built deep learning systems to pull information out of music audio and took research prototypes into production. While there she combined transformer models (a type of neural network good at handling sequences) with music-theory-aware loss functions to analyze the structure of a song, and built a transformer-based system for detecting the beat and downbeat. She has since moved on to Music.AI, continuing to work on music machine learning research.
