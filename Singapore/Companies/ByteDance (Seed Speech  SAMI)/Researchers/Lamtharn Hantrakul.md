Lamtharn "Hanoi" Hantrakul is an audio machine learning research scientist on TikTok/ByteDance's SAMI (Speech, Audio & Music Intelligence) team and a former Google AI resident. He works on AI tools for music creation, including differentiable wavetable synthesis (a way of making classic sound-generation techniques trainable by neural networks). Outside of his research role he is also a composer and sound artist, and won the AI Song Contest 2022 with a piece blending Thai cultural themes with AI-generated music.
