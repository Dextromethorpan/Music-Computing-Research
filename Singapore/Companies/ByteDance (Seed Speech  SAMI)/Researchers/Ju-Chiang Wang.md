Ju-Chiang Wang is a research scientist on ByteDance's Speech, Audio & Music Intelligence (SAMI) team, which is now part of the broader "Seed Speech" research org. He works on music AI: teaching computers to understand the content of a song (music content understanding), building tools that can intelligently edit and remix music, and connecting audio to video (audiovisual cross-modal retrieval). He led the team behind BS-RoFormer, an award-winning system for splitting a song back into its separate instrument/vocal tracks (music source separation).
