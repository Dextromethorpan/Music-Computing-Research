Qiuqiang Kong worked as a research scientist on ByteDance's SAMI (Speech, Audio & Music Intelligence) team from 2019 to 2023, focusing on classifying, detecting, and separating sounds and music. He is known for building pretrained audio neural networks (PANNs) used to automatically tag what's happening in an audio clip, and for creating GiantMIDI-Piano, the largest piano transcription dataset of its kind. He left ByteDance in 2023 to become an assistant professor at the Chinese University of Hong Kong.
