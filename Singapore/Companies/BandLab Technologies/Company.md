BandLab Technologies is a Singapore-headquartered music technology company (a division of Caldecott Music Group) that makes the free social music-making app BandLab, the professional DAW (digital audio workstation — software for recording, editing, and arranging music) Cakewalk, and, since July 2026, the audio-to-audio AI music studio Aiode. Its research focus is applying machine learning to make music creation more accessible: AI songwriting assistants, AI vocal cleanup tools, and licensed generative audio models.
