SongStarter is a free tool inside the BandLab app that helps beginners get past "writer's block" by instantly generating a short musical starting point. A user picks a genre (like Lo-fi or Trap) and optionally types a word or emoji, and within seconds the tool generates chords, a melody, a bassline, drums, and ambience as an editable project. It was built using a language model — a type of AI trained to predict likely sequences of items (here, musical notes and chords instead of words) — trained on millions of songs, built in collaboration with Google using the TensorFlow machine-learning framework. Unlike tools that generate a finished song, SongStarter deliberately produces an unfinished sketch, meant as a royalty-free (free-to-use without paying licensing fees) launchpad for a human to then edit and finish.
