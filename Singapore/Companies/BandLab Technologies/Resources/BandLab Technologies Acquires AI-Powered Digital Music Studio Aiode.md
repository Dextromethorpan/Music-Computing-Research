This official July 2026 press release announces BandLab Technologies' acquisition of Aiode, giving the group a third music-making platform alongside BandLab and Cakewalk. Aiode is described as an "audio-to-audio" AI music studio: instead of typing a text prompt to generate a whole song, a user starts with a blank project or their own recording and picks from a roster of AI models of individual musicians and playing styles to perform sections of the track, generating alternate takes for individual parts without altering the rest of the song. The release emphasizes that 100% of the audio used to train Aiode's models is licensed and traceable to its source, including performances recorded specifically for training in collaboration with the musicians involved — a "fully licensed" approach positioned as an alternative to AI music models trained on scraped or unlicensed audio.
