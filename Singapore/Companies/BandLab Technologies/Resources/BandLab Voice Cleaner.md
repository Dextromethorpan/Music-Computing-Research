Voice Cleaner is an AI-powered vocal repair tool built into BandLab that bundles three fixes into one click: removing background noise, reducing echo/room reverberation ("DeReverb"), and automatically tuning the tonal balance of a voice recording ("AutoEQ"). BandLab's own comparison explains the key technical distinction from its older, simpler "Denoise" feature: Denoise applies a fixed filter that cuts specific frequencies regardless of content, while Voice Cleaner uses a machine-learning model trained to recognize and separate speech from unwanted background sound, so it can remove noise adaptively without also degrading the voice itself. This is useful for podcasters, singers, and anyone recording vocals outside a professional studio.
