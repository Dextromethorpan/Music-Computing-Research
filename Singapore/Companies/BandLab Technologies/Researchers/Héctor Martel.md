Héctor Martel is an ML (machine learning) Research Engineer at BandLab Technologies, based in Singapore, working at the intersection of audio signal processing and machine learning. He led the team's DAFx 2026 paper probing what basic acoustic properties (like reverberation and loudness) are hidden inside general-purpose audio AI embeddings, and has separately published research on audio-visual speech separation (isolating a specific speaker's voice from background sound using both audio and video cues).
