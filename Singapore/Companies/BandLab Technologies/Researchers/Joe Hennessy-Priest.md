Joe Hennessy-Priest is a Machine Learning Engineer at BandLab Technologies, based in Singapore, with a background in audio and music technology (BSc, University of the West of England) and a Master's from Queen Mary University of London — a university with a well-known audio and music research group. Before BandLab he worked as a Senior Machine Learning Researcher at Iris Audio, a company focused on AI-based noise reduction. At BandLab he co-authored the team's DAFx 2026 paper on what audio AI models internally learn about sound properties.
