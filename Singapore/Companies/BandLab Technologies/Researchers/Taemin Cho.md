Taemin Cho is a Principal Engineer at BandLab Technologies with a PhD in Music Technology from New York University, where he researched Music Information Retrieval (MIR) — the field of automatically extracting information like pitch, key, or structure from audio. At BandLab he applies machine learning to music creation and audio generation, source separation (splitting a mixed recording into its individual instrument/voice parts), and real-time audio engines, and is a co-author of the company's DAFx 2026 research paper on audio embeddings.
