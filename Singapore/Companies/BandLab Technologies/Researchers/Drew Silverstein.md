Drew Silverstein joined BandLab Technologies in 2023 as Senior Advisor for AI, Innovation and Strategy, reporting to the group COO. He previously co-founded and led Amper Music, an early AI-driven music generation platform (acquired by Shutterstock in 2020), and worked earlier as a composer and producer for film, TV, and games. At BandLab he guides the company's approach to "human-first" AI music tools — AI that assists musicians rather than replacing them — and was quoted commenting on the Aiode acquisition's approach of building AI models in direct collaboration with the musicians whose playing they are trained on.
