Creative Technology Ltd is a Singapore-founded consumer electronics company, best known for inventing the Sound Blaster PC sound card in the 1990s. More recently its R&D has focused on Super X-Fi, an AI-driven "audio holography" technology that personalizes surround-sound playback on ordinary headphones.
