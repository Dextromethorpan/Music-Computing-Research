This is Creative's own technical explainer for Super X-Fi, a technology built into its headphones and audio dongles that tries to make ordinary headphones sound like a room full of speakers. Normally, headphone audio feels "trapped in your head" because it skips the natural way sound bounces around a room and off your head and ears before reaching your eardrums. Super X-Fi fixes this by taking a photo of your head and ears, extracting hundreds of physical measurements from that photo, and feeding them into an AI model that predicts exactly how sound would sound to you personally if you were sitting in front of real speakers. It then reshapes the audio in real time so headphones recreate that speaker-like experience. The page also introduces the custom "UltraDSP" chip built specifically to run this processing, and states the technology took over 20 years and about US$100 million of R&D.
