This press release announces that Creative Technology won the WIPO IP Enterprise Trophy — the top prize at the 2018 WIPO-IPOS IP Awards, a joint award programme run by the World Intellectual Property Organization (a United Nations agency that oversees patents and trademarks worldwide) and Singapore's own IP office (IPOS). Creative won for Super X-Fi, its headphone "audio holography" technology, which the release credits with over 20 years of engineering R&D. This award is useful evidence that Super X-Fi is genuine, externally validated intellectual property and R&D output, not just marketing — it was judged specifically on innovation and successful commercialization through patents.
