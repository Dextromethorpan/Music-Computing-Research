This is one of Creative's core Super X-Fi patents (US 11,468,663, "Method for generating a customized/personalized head related transfer function"). A Head-Related Transfer Function, or HRTF, is a mathematical description of how a specific person's head, shoulders, and outer ear shape change a sound before it reaches their eardrum — it is why sound seems to come from a specific direction. This patent describes a practical way to build a personal HRTF cheaply: the user takes a photo of their ear with an ordinary phone camera, software automatically scales the image to work out the ear's real physical dimensions, and those measurements are used to generate an HRTF tailored to that individual, rather than relying on one "average" HRTF used for everyone (which is why generic virtual surround headphones often sound wrong). This image-based, camera-only approach is what makes Super X-Fi's personalization step fast enough for everyday consumer use.
