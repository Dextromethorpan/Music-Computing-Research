This is Creative's product-technology hub page listing the various audio processing features built into its Sound Blaster and SXFI product lines. Beyond Super X-Fi, it describes: "Surround Virtualization" (making stereo audio sound like it is coming from many speakers around you), "Crystalizer" (restoring detail lost when music files are compressed), "Scout Mode" (boosting quiet gameplay sounds like footsteps so gamers can react faster), and "NoiseClean" / "CrystalVoice" tools that remove background noise and echo from voice calls using real-time signal processing. While mostly a marketing page, it gives a concrete overview of which specific audio DSP (digital signal processing — techniques for analyzing and modifying sound electronically) problems Creative's engineering teams have targeted over the years.
