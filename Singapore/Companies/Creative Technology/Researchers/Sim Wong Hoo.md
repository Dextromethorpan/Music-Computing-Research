Sim Wong Hoo (1955-2023) founded Creative Technology and led it from the original Sound Blaster sound card through to Super X-Fi. He personally championed and funded the 20-year, roughly US$100 million R&D effort behind Super X-Fi's personalized audio holography, and is frequently quoted in press and on Creative's own technology pages describing the underlying research goal: making headphone sound indistinguishable from listening to real speakers in a room.
