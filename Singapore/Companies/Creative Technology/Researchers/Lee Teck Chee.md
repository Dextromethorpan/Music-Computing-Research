Lee Teck Chee is Creative Technology's Chief Architect and the named inventor behind Super X-Fi, the company's personalized headphone spatial-audio technology. He is listed as lead inventor on Creative's US patent for generating a personalized Head-Related Transfer Function (HRTF) from a photo of a user's ear, the core technical trick that lets Super X-Fi tailor sound to an individual's head and ear shape.
