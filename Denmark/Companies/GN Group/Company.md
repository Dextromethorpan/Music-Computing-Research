GN Group is a Danish audio and hearing-technology company whose hearing-care division (GN Hearing, selling hearing aids under the ReSound and Beltone brands) and audio division (Jabra) both invest in DSP and AI research. Its GN Advanced Science unit and named research scientists publish on noise reduction, own-voice detection, and "neuromorphic" (brain-inspired) sound processing for hearing aids.
