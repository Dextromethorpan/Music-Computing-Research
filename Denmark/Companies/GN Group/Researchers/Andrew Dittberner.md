Andrew Dittberner leads research strategy across GN Group's hearing-care business, with a long-running focus on how hearing aids can better exploit the way the human auditory system and brain already process sound, rather than fighting against it. He has publicly discussed GN's "neuromorphic" (brain-inspired) sound-processing approach for the ReSound hearing-aid brand.
