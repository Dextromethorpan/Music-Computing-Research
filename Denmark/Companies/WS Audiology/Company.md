WS Audiology was formed in 2019 from the merger of Denmark's Widex and Singapore/Germany-based Sivantos, and is headquartered in Lynge, Denmark. Its dedicated research institute, ORCA Labs (successor to Widex's ORCA Europe research group), publishes audiology and hearing-aid-outcomes research from scientists based in Denmark, Germany, the UK, and the US.
