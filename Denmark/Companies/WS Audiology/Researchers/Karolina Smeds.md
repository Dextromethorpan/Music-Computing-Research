Karolina Smeds has worked in hearing research since 1996 and directed Widex's ORCA Europe research group (now part of ORCA Labs) for 15 years. Her research focuses on hearing rehabilitation, people's real-world "auditory ecology" (what sound environments they actually spend time in), and designing outcome measures — tests and questionnaires that predict how a hearing aid will actually perform in someone's everyday life, including group conversations and social participation.
