Dina Lelic joined Widex (now WS Audiology) in 2018 after 15+ years of life-sciences research. She studies the real-life experiences and needs of people with hearing loss, what helps or blocks hearing-aid adoption, and interventions — like coaching people to notice positive listening moments — that measurably improve hearing-aid satisfaction and outcomes.
