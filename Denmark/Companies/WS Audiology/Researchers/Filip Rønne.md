Filip Rønne has worked in hearing science and the hearing-aid business since 2007 and now leads ORCA Labs, WS Audiology's research unit spanning Denmark, Sweden, and Germany. His research spans psychoacoustics, diagnostics, and audiology, with a recent focus on studying group conversations, social participation, and how to motivate people to actually use their hearing aids.
