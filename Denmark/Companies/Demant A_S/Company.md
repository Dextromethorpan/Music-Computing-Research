Demant A/S is a Danish hearing-healthcare group and parent of the Oticon hearing-aid brand. Its Eriksholm Research Centre, founded in 1976 north of Copenhagen, runs long-term basic research — with more than 40 scientists — into personalized audiology, cognitive hearing science, and AI-based hearing-aid signal processing.
