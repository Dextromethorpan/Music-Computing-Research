Jesper Jensen works on acoustic signal processing for hearing aids — the algorithms that clean up noisy speech and compensate for individual hearing loss. He holds a joint position between Oticon's Eriksholm Research Centre and Aalborg University's signal-processing research group, and has co-authored deep-learning approaches to hearing-loss compensation.
