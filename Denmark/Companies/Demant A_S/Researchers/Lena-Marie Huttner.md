Lena-Marie Huttner researches the cognitive side of hearing science — how universal mechanisms of human communication, shared by everyone, interact with the individual traits, preferences, and habits that make each listener's experience of hearing loss different. Her work sits at the intersection of hearing technology and cognitive psychology.
