Paula López Diez works on applying deep learning and AI to hearing-care data, aiming to make hearing-aid personalization more reliable, fair, and explainable to both hearing-care professionals and end users. She combines demographic information and hearing-test results to build models intended to guide better-fitted hearing devices.
