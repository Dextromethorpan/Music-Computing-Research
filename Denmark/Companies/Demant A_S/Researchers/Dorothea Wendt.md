Dorothea Wendt studies listening effort — how mentally tiring it is to follow speech in noisy or difficult situations — using techniques like pupillometry (tracking pupil-size changes) and EEG (brain-wave recording). Her goal is to find measurable, individual signs of listening effort that could one day help hearing aids be fitted or adjusted based on how hard a person's brain is working, not just how loud they can hear.
