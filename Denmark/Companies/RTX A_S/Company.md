RTX A/S is a Danish audio electronics and wireless DSP company (based in Nørresundby, near Aalborg; owner of the Sennheiser consumer audio brand license) that files patents on machine-learning-based audio packet-loss concealment and collaborates with Aarhus University on embedded speech/keyword-detection research for its wireless headset and intercom products.
