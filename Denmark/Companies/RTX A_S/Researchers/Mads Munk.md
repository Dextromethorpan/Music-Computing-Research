Mads Munk is a software engineer at RTX A/S. He is named as an inventor on the company's audio error-concealment patent work, and separately completed an Aarhus University bachelor project done in collaboration with RTX on "Keyword Spotting Using Deep Neural Networks" — building a machine-learning system that lets embedded audio devices detect a specific spoken wake word without needing to send audio to the cloud.
