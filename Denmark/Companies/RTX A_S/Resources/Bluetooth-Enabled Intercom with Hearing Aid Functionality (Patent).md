This patent describes a wireless intercom device (the kind used by motorcyclists or industrial workers) that can also work as a basic hearing aid — boosting and shaping ambient sound picked up by its own microphones for a user with hearing loss, on top of its normal two-way wireless voice-communication function. It shows RTX A/S applying its core wireless-audio and DSP expertise (originally built for cordless phones and headsets) to a new combined use case that blends communication technology with basic hearing assistance.
