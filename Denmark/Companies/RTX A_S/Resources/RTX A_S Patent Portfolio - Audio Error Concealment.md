Wireless headsets and intercoms sometimes drop small chunks of audio data ("packets") due to radio interference, causing brief glitches or dropouts. This Justia listing indexes RTX A/S's patent portfolio, which includes a jointly-held patent (with US company H.M. Electronics) for a method that uses a trained neural network, specifically a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) network — a type of neural network good at learning patterns over time — to predict what the missing chunk of audio probably sounded like and fill in the gap, rather than leaving a silent or garbled dropout. This is a real example of a Danish audio-electronics company applying machine learning to a concrete, everyday wireless-audio reliability problem.
