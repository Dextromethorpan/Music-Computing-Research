IDUN audio is a Copenhagen startup (founded 2018) that builds embedded spatial-audio software for headphones and hearables. Included under the specialized spatial-audio infrastructure path: its dynamic spatial audio (DSA) engine uses time-varying, low-latency head-tracked HRTF processing that runs directly inside a headset's own chip rather than on a connected phone or computer, and this approach is protected by a granted US patent and licensed into chipsets from Qualcomm, Airoha, and BES Technic.
