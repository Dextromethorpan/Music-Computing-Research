Pauli Minnaar founded IDUN audio in 2018 and holds a PhD in Acoustics from Aalborg University. He leads the development of the company's dynamic spatial audio (DSA) software, which uses head-tracked, time-varying HRTF filtering to make headphone sound seem to come from real points in space, and is the named inventor on IDUN's core US patent for the technology.
