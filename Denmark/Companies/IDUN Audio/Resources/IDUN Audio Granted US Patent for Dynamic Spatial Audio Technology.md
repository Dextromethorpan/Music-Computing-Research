This news article covers a US patent granted to IDUN audio for making sound seem like it's coming from a specific point in space around a headphone listener, even as the listener turns their head. The trick is a "time-varying HRTF" — a filter that's constantly recalculated based on live head-tracking data — combined with signal processing light enough to run inside the tiny chip of a headphone or earbud itself, instead of needing a phone or computer to do the heavy work. That low-power, low-latency design is what makes real-time spatial audio practical on battery-powered wireless headphones.
