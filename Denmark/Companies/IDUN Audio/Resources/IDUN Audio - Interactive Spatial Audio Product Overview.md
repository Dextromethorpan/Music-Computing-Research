IDUN audio's own site explains why sound played over ordinary headphones feels like it's stuck "inside your head," instead of coming from the world around you the way real sounds do. Their embedded software simulates a full 3D listening environment — including tracking fast head movements — so that music, calls, games, or augmented-reality audio can seem to come from specific places in a room, at very low computing cost. This low computing cost is what lets the software run directly on a wireless headphone chip rather than requiring a separate phone or PC.
