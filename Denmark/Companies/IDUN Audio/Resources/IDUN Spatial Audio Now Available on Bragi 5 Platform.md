This trade-press item reports IDUN audio's spatial-audio engine being integrated into Bragi's chip reference platform, a hardware/software toolkit that headphone manufacturers use to build wireless earbuds. It's evidence that IDUN's patented processing approach isn't just a lab prototype — it's been packaged so hardware makers can license and ship it directly inside real consumer earbuds and hearables.
