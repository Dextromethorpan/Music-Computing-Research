Thomas Lund spent over a decade at TC Electronic researching audio loudness and true-peak level measurement, publishing widely-cited AES and EBU papers that directly influenced the ITU-R BS.1770 and EBU R128 loudness standards now used across TV broadcast and music streaming. He has been active in international standards bodies (AES, EBU, SMPTE, ITU) and chaired working groups on recreational hearing-loss prevention. He has since moved on from TC Electronic to a research role at Genelec, but the loudness-standardization work described here was done during his TC Electronic tenure.
