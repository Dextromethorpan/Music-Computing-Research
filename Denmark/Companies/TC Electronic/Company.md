TC Electronic is a Danish audio effects and digital signal processing (DSP) company (based in Risskov, near Aarhus; now part of Music Tribe) best known for studio reverb/effects hardware. For over a decade its research work on loudness measurement, most visibly through engineer Thomas Lund, directly shaped the international ITU-R BS.1770 and EBU R128 loudness standards used across broadcast and streaming today.
