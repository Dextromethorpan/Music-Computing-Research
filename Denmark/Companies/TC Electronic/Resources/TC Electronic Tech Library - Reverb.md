Reverb (short for reverberation) is the wash of echoes and reflections a room adds to a sound; artificial reverb units try to simulate that effect digitally so a dry studio recording can sound like it was performed in a concert hall, church, or other real space. This technical library page explains the engineering trade-offs TC Electronic's engineers navigate when designing digital reverb algorithms (like their System 6000 and Reverb 6000 hardware), including how mathematically-generated reflections can be made to sound natural rather than metallic or artificial. TC Electronic has historically been a leading name in high-end studio reverb processors.
