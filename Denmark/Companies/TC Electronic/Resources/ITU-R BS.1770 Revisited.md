ITU-R BS.1770 is the international technical standard that defines how "loudness" (how loud a piece of audio sounds to a human, as opposed to just its raw signal level) is measured for broadcast and streaming. In this paper, TC Electronic's Thomas Lund examines problems and edge cases in the original standard's measurement algorithm and proposes refinements. This work matters to anyone who has noticed that some songs, ads, or streams sound jarringly louder than others when you switch between them — loudness standards like this one are what platforms use to try to keep playback volume consistent.
