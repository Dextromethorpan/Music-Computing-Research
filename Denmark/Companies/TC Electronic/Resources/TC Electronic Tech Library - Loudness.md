This is TC Electronic's own technical explainer hub on loudness measurement, written to help audio engineers understand why simple volume meters (like peak meters) don't match how loud something actually sounds to a human ear, and how standards like ITU-R BS.1770 and EBU R128 fix that with loudness meters. It reflects the company's real, decade-plus involvement in shaping those international loudness standards (led largely by their engineer Thomas Lund) rather than being purely a marketing page.
