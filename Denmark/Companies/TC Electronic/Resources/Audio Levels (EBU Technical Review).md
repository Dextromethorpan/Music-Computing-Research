Published in the European Broadcasting Union's Technical Review, this paper by TC Electronic's Thomas Lund explains why traditional ways of measuring audio "level" in broadcast (like simple peak meters) fail to predict how loud a program will actually sound to viewers, and lays groundwork for the loudness-based measurement approach that broadcasters later adopted worldwide. It's an early, influential piece in the "loudness wars" debate — the well-known problem of TV ads and streamed tracks sounding unpredictably louder or quieter than the content around them.
