This DPA Microphones patent covers a mechanical design for building small, high-quality microphone capsules into a bent, clamped assembly rather than a simple straight tube. In plain terms, it is about how the tiny internal parts of a professional microphone can be shaped and held together so the microphone stays acoustically accurate (doesn't distort or color the sound) while also being small, discreet, and durable enough for use on stage, on a lavalier clip, or in a headset. It reflects DPA's core specialty: engineering the physical structure of a microphone, not just its electronics.
