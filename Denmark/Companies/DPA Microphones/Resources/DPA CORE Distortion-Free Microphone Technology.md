This article describes DPA's patented "CORE" microphone capsule technology, which the company says reduces a specific type of distortion that condenser microphones normally introduce when they pick up very loud sounds close-up (like a drum being hit hard, or a singer belting a note directly into the mic). DPA's engineers redesigned the internal capsule electronics to keep the microphone's response accurate even under those extreme conditions, and patented the resulting design. It is a useful example of DPA turning internal acoustic-engineering research directly into a patented, shipped product feature.
