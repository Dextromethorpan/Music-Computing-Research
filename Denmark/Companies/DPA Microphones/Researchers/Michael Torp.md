Michael Torp is a senior electronics engineer in DPA Microphones' R&D team, working on the electronic design side of the company's microphone products (as distinct from the mechanical/acoustic capsule design). Before DPA he worked on medical device electronics at Novo Nordisk and on embedded downhole electronics at Emerson, giving him a background in precision embedded-electronics design that carries over into building accurate, low-noise microphone circuitry.
