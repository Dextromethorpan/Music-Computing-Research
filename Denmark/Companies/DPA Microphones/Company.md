DPA Microphones is a Danish manufacturer of professional condenser microphones (headquartered in Allerød) with an in-house R&D team that designs and patents its own microphone capsules, acoustic tubes, and assemblies, and publishes technical education material ("Mic University") on microphone acoustics and recording technique.
