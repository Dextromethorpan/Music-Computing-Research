Sonion is a Roskilde-based company, founded in 1974, that designs and manufactures the miniature microphones, balanced-armature receivers (tiny electromagnetic sound drivers), and other micro-acoustic components used inside hearing aids, in-ear monitors, and earbuds — including custom in-ear monitors for touring musicians. Included via traditional R&D: decades of patented micro-acoustic engineering (electret and MEMS microphones, balanced-armature receiver mechanics) underpin components used across the hearing-aid and professional in-ear-audio industry.
