Sonion's product page for its microphone line explains its miniature electret and MEMS (Micro-Electro-Mechanical System — a tiny chip-scale mechanical sensor) microphones, built specifically for hearing aids and professional audio in customizable, extremely small packages. Sonion says it produced the world's first dual-backplate MEMS microphone for hearing aids, a design that uses two sensing surfaces around a vibrating diaphragm instead of one, generally improving how faithfully very quiet or very loud sounds are captured. The page reflects a company built around micro-acoustic component engineering rather than consumer marketing.
