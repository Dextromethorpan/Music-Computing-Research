This technical explainer from Sonion's own "Academy" documentation walks through how a balanced-armature receiver works: a coil around a metal armature, positioned exactly between two magnets, moves in response to electrical current and drives a tiny membrane through a connecting pin to produce sound. It explains why this design is preferred over a conventional small loudspeaker for hearing instruments and in-ear monitors specifically because it's more efficient (better battery life) and can be made extremely small — the two properties that matter most for a device worn inside the ear.
