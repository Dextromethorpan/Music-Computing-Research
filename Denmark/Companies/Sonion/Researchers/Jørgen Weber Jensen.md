Jørgen Weber Jensen co-founded Sonion in 1974 in a small apartment in Roskilde, Denmark, together with Jens-Jørn Stockholm, starting the company that grew into a leading maker of the miniature microphones and receivers used inside hearing aids and in-ear monitors worldwide.
