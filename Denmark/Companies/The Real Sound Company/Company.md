The Real Sound Company is a Copenhagen company that develops Imerz, a patented spatial-audio algorithm founded by composer and sonologist Lars Graugaard. Included under the specialized spatial-audio infrastructure path: instead of the usual stereo pair of point sources, Imerz projects each input channel onto a virtual "sound surface" made from a loudspeaker's own drivers, aiming to remove the single stereo "sweet spot" and let sound propagate through a room more like an acoustic instrument does — a documented, patented signal-processing approach rather than a general product-quality claim.
