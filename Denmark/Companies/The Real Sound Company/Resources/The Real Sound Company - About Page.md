The company's about page tells the origin story of Imerz: founder Lars Graugaard, a composer with over 40 years of experience as a musician and lecturer, grew frustrated with the practical limits of stereo sound (the fixed "sweet spot," the reliance on exactly two speakers) and set out to find something better. He filed the original patent for the Imerz algorithm in 2021. The page frames the company's mission as bringing more natural, room-filling sound reproduction to consumer and professional loudspeakers.
