This page explains Imerz, a patented algorithm that changes how sound is projected from loudspeakers. Normal stereo works by playing slightly different signals from two speakers, which only sounds "right" if you're sitting in one specific "sweet spot" between them. Imerz instead spreads one input channel across a "virtual sound surface" made from a speaker's drivers, similar to how a real acoustic instrument (like a guitar body) radiates sound from many points across its surface rather than one point. The company's claim is that this removes the sweet-spot requirement and lets a listener get a full, natural-sounding mix anywhere in a room, even from a single loudspeaker.
