Lars Graugaard is a Danish composer, laptop performer, and sonologist (a scholar of sound/acoustics) with a PhD in interactive music from Oxford Brookes University and a background in flute performance from the Royal Danish Academy of Music. Drawing on decades of experience with sound in performance, he founded The Real Sound Company and, in 2021, filed the patent for its Imerz spatial-audio algorithm.
