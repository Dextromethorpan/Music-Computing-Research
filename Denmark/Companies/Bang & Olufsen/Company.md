Bang & Olufsen is a Danish luxury audio and consumer electronics maker (headquartered in Struer) with a long-running internal research group that publishes peer-reviewed work on spatial audio perception, sound-zone technology, and loudspeaker acoustics, including a formal research partnership with Aalborg University.
