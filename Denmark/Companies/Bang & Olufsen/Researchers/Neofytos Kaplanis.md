Neofytos Kaplanis works in Bang & Olufsen's acoustics R&D group, studying how people perceive sound fields in real rooms and in virtual/simulated environments. He holds a PhD in the psychoacoustics of room perception from Aalborg University and combines a "Tonmeister" (music/sound recording engineering) background with formal psychology training, and has authored around 20 publications on room acoustics, automotive audio, and spatial audio perception.
