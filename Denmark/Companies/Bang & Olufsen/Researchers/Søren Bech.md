Søren Bech leads a long-running joint research effort between Bang & Olufsen and Aalborg University into how people perceive audio quality and spatial sound. His work develops and refines the listening-test methods used to scientifically measure things like "does this sound spacious" or "does this processing damage sound quality," which are hard to quantify with pure measurements alone. He has authored numerous AES papers spanning decades of this research, several with Bang & Olufsen co-authors.
