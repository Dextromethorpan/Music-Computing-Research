Jon Francombe worked within Bang & Olufsen's research group on the "Perceptually Optimised Sound Zones" project, a collaboration with the University of Surrey where he completed his PhD in perceptual audio quality evaluation. His research focuses on how to test and measure whether spatial audio actually sounds good to real listeners, and on techniques for creating and reproducing spatial audio content. He later moved on to the BBC, but his Bang & Olufsen-era publications remain part of the company's core sound-zones research output.
