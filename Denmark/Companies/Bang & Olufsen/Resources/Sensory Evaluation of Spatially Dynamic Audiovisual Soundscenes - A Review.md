This AES convention paper reviews the scientific methods researchers use to test how people experience sound that moves around them in combination with moving images — for example a film scene where a sound effect pans as the camera moves. "Sensory evaluation" here means structured listening tests where people rate or describe what they hear, similar to how food tasters describe flavors. The authors, from Bang & Olufsen and Aalborg University, survey which testing methods work well for judging spatial audio quality and where the field's methods still fall short. It's a survey/roadmap paper rather than a single new experiment, aimed at improving how the whole field measures "does this spatial audio sound good and realistic."
