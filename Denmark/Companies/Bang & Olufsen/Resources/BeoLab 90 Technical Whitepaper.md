The BeoLab 90 is a high-end active loudspeaker, and this official whitepaper explains the acoustic engineering behind it in technical detail: an "Active Room Compensation" system that uses a built-in microphone to measure how the room around the speaker reflects sound, then automatically adjusts the speaker's output to correct for it; and a "Beam Width Control" / acoustic lens system that lets the speaker widen or narrow the angle over which sound is projected into the room. Both features rely on many individual small drivers and digital signal processing (DSP) working together rather than a single traditional speaker cone. It's a rare case of a consumer audio brand publishing genuine engineering detail about how its flagship product actually works.
