This paper, presented at the Sound and Music Computing (SMC) Conference, describes two experimental digital instruments — the "Bodyharp" and the "Teinophon" — and the new ways of interacting with and controlling sound ("mapping techniques") that were built for them. Mapping is the design choice of how a musician's physical action (like a gesture or touch) gets translated into a sound parameter (like pitch or volume). The paper documents the design process and the creative possibilities these unconventional instruments open up for performers.
