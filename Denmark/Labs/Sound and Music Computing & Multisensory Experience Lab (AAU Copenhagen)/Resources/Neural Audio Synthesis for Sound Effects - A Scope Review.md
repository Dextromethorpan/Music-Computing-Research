"Sound effects" are the artificial or recorded sounds used in games, film, and VR (footsteps, explosions, ambient noise). Traditionally these are recorded or manually programmed, which is slow and inflexible. This review surveys "neural audio synthesis" — using deep-learning models to generate realistic sound effects directly, often letting creators control properties like pitch or intensity. It maps out the current state of the field, comparing different neural techniques and identifying open problems, such as making the generated sounds controllable and fast enough for interactive use (like a live video game).
