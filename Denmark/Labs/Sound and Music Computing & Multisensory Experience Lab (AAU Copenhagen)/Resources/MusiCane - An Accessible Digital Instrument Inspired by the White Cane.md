MusiCane is a new digital musical instrument shaped like a white cane (the mobility tool used by many blind and visually-impaired people), designed to be playable by touch and movement rather than sight. Presented at NIME (New Interfaces for Musical Expression, a research conference on new ways of making music with technology), this paper describes how the instrument was designed and built, and reports on how accessible and enjoyable it was for users. It's an example of designing music technology explicitly around accessibility rather than adding it as an afterthought.
