"Palmas" are the rhythmic hand-clapping patterns used in flamenco music. This dataset captures recordings and data related to palmas performances, intended for researchers studying rhythm — for example, building software that can automatically detect or analyze clapping rhythms, or studying how skilled performers keep time together. Sharing datasets like this lets other researchers test their own rhythm-analysis algorithms on real, agreed-upon material instead of each starting from scratch.
