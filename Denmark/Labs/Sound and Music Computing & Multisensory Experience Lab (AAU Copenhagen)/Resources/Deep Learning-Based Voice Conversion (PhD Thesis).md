This PhD thesis, titled "Deep Learning-Based Voice Conversion: Creating a Real-Time Neural Speech-Modification System for Practical Applications," builds a system that can change how a spoken voice sounds — for example making it sound like a different speaker — while running fast enough to work live, not just after the fact on a recording. The thesis covers the technical design of the neural network system and tests how well it holds up for real, practical uses such as VR avatars or accessibility tools, rather than just laboratory demos.
