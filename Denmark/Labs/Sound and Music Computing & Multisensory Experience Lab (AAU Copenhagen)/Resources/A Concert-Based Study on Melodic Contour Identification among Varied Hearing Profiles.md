"Melodic contour" means the overall up-and-down shape of a melody, regardless of exact notes — whether a tune rises, falls, or stays flat. This study tested how well people with different hearing abilities (including cochlear implant users) can recognize melodic contours during a real live concert setting, rather than a sterile lab test. Comparing people with different hearing profiles helps researchers understand exactly what parts of music perception are lost with certain kinds of hearing loss, which then informs how hearing devices or music technology could better support musical enjoyment for these listeners.
