"Voice conversion" means changing a recording of one person's voice so it sounds like it was spoken by someone else, while keeping the words the same. This paper reviews modern deep-learning methods that can do this without needing matched recordings of both speakers saying the same sentences ("non-parallel" data), which is the realistic, harder version of the problem. It surveys and compares many recent AI approaches, pointing out which techniques work best and what challenges remain, such as keeping the conversion natural-sounding and fast enough for real-time use.
