RASTA is an open-source toolbox that lets developers analyze the frequency content of sound ("spectral analysis" — breaking a sound into the different pitches/frequencies it contains) in real time, inside the Unity3D game engine. This is useful for building interactive VR or game experiences that react live to sound, such as visualizations that move with music or systems that detect specific sound events as they happen. Presented at the Sound and Music Computing Conference, this is a practical tool release rather than a pure research finding.
