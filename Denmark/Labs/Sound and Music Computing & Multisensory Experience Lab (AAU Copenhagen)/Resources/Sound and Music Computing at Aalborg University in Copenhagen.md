This paper is an overview of the Sound and Music Computing group and its Master's program at Aalborg University Copenhagen. "Sound and Music Computing" (SMC) is a field that combines engineering, computer science, and music: it studies how computers can generate, analyze, and let people interact with sound and music. The paper describes the group's mix of research areas — from sound synthesis (making new sounds with software) to building new digital musical instruments and studying how people move and interact with sound — and how these feed into teaching a specialized university program.
