This study looks at virtual reality (VR) avatars — animated characters representing people in a virtual world — and tests whether giving them AI-generated speech (instead of a real recorded human voice) changes how realistic or believable users find them. The researchers ran an experiment comparing different voice types and measured people's perception of the avatar. The findings help VR designers understand when AI voices are "good enough" to use in place of real human speech recordings, which matters for building believable virtual training, therapy, or social VR applications.
