Cumhur Erkut is affiliated with the Sound and Music Computing group at AAU Copenhagen. His recent research focuses on neural and embodied approaches to audio — including real-time neural voice conversion, neural sound-effect synthesis, and sonification (turning data into sound). He remains an active member of the group as of late 2025/2026 publications and conference activity.
