Amalia de Götzen was historically a core member of the Sound and Music Computing group, working on sonic interaction design and product sound design (how the sounds a product makes shape how people perceive it). She has since moved to AAU's Service Systems Design unit, so her current research is no longer centered on sound, but her earlier work remains part of the group's published output and legacy.
