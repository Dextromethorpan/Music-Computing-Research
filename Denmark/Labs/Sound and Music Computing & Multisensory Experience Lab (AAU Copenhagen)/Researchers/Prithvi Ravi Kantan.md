Prithvi Ravi Kantan works on sonification of human movement — converting motion data (like a person's gait) into sound feedback — for rehabilitation applications such as stroke recovery and cerebral palsy. He is now formally based at AAU's Department of Electronic Systems / Center for Neurotechnology and Rehabilitation, but his research remains closely tied to this lab's sonic-interaction and movement work through collaborations with Sofia Dahl and Stefania Serafin.
