Razvan Paisa researches how touch and vibration can supplement hearing for people with hearing loss, especially cochlear implant users, so they can better experience music. His PhD explored vibrotactile ("felt-through-the-skin") augmentation of music, and he has also built open-source audio analysis tools and studied melodic perception across different hearing profiles.
