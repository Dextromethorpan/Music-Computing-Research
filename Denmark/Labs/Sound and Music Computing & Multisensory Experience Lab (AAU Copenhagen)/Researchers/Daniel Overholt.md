Daniel Overholt designs new digital and hybrid acoustic-digital musical instruments, publishing regularly at NIME (New Interfaces for Musical Expression). His work includes accessible instruments like MusiCane and experimental instruments such as the Bodyharp and Teinophon, exploring unconventional ways performers can physically interact with sound.
