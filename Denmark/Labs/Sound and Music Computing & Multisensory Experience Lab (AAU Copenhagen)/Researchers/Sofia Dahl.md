Sofia Dahl studies embodied music cognition — how physical movement and music perception are linked — with a particular focus on movement sonification (turning body movement into sound feedback) for gait training and physical rehabilitation. She co-authored the group's overview paper on Sound and Music Computing at Aalborg.
