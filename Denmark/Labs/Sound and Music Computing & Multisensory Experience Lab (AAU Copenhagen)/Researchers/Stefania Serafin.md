Stefania Serafin leads the Multisensory Experience Lab (ME-Lab/HIVE Lab) and is a central figure in Sound and Music Computing at AAU Copenhagen. She researches "sonic interaction design" — how sound can be used as a real-time feedback channel in virtual reality, rehabilitation devices, and accessible technology — and is also President of the Sound and Music Computing Association. Her recent work spans VR avatar voice realism, cochlear-implant music perception, and accessible digital instruments.
