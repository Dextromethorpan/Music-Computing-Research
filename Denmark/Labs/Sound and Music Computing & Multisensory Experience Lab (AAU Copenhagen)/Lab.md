Part of the Department of Architecture, Design and Media Technology at Aalborg University's Copenhagen campus (AAU CPH). Two closely linked, overlapping sibling units cover music computing here: the **Sound and Music Computing (SMC)** research group (sound synthesis, new digital instruments, machine learning for audio) and the **Multisensory Experience Lab (ME-Lab)** — recently re-listed internally as the **Human Inclusive Versatile Experiences Lab (HIVE Lab)** — which builds VR/AR systems combining sound with touch and other senses for rehabilitation, accessibility, and cultural heritage.
