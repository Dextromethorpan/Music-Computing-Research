The Media, Art & Design (MAD) research group at the IT University of Copenhagen (part of the Play, Culture, and AI section) studies experience design, human-computer interaction, and creative AI. A subset of its work focuses specifically on designing new physical and AI-assisted interfaces that let people with no musical training actively make music, publishing at venues like NIME and ACM DIS.
