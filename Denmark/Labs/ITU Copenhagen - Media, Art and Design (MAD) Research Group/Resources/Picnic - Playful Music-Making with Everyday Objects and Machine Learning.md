"Picnic" is a system that turns everyday objects — like a cup, a spoon, or a piece of fabric — into musical instruments using machine learning. Instead of requiring special sensors built into each object, the system learns to recognize the sounds and touch gestures people make with ordinary items and turns them into musical triggers. The idea is to make music-making playful and low-barrier: anyone can pick up something lying around at a picnic table and start making music with it, without any dedicated hardware or musical skill. This continues the research group's focus on "musicking" — designing everyday, inclusive ways for non-musicians to actively participate in making music rather than just listening to it.
