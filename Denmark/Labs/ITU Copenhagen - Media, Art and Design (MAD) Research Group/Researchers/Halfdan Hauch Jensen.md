Halfdan Hauch Jensen co-designed and built the Tubularium, a large tangible musical instrument for non-musicians. His broader work sits at the intersection of human-computer interaction and generative/AI-driven creative tools, including earlier work exploring digital art collections through drawing interactions with a deep generative model.
