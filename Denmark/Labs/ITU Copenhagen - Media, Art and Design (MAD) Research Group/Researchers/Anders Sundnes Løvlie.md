Anders Sundnes Løvlie leads the MAD research group at ITU Copenhagen. His main research interests are experience design, human-computer interaction, museums, and locative media (technology tied to physical places), and in recent years he has co-supervised a strand of work on designing playful, AI-assisted musical instruments for people with no musical training.
