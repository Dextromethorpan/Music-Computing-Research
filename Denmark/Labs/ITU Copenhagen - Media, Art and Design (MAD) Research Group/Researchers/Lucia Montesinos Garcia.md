Lucia Montesinos Garcia is a PhD student researching "musicking" — designing tangible and embodied instruments (like the Tubularium and Picnic) that let people without musical training actively make music. Her work combines interaction design, machine learning, and creative AI, and she won a Best Paper Award for this research in 2026.
