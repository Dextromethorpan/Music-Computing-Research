Giada Dalla Bontà is a PhD researcher studying experimental sound and art practices from the late Soviet Union to the present, looking at how underground sonic and artistic practices in post-socialist countries relate to politics, memory, and identity. She co-edited the "What Sounds Do" special issue with Holger Schulze and other Sound Studies Lab members.
