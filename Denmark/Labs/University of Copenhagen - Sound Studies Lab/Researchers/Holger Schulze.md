Holger Schulze is a professor of musicology at the University of Copenhagen who studies sound and listening as cultural phenomena — how sound design, sound art, popular music, and even AI voice assistants shape everyday experience and identity. He founded and runs the Sound Studies Lab, has written or edited around 20 books on sound (including "The Sonic Persona" and "The Bloomsbury Handbook of Sound Art"), and is a founding member of the European Sound Studies Association.
