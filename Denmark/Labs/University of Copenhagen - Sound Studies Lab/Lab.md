The Sound Studies Lab is a research environment at the University of Copenhagen's Department of Arts and Cultural Studies, led by musicology professor Holger Schulze. Rather than doing signal-processing or engineering research, it studies sound and listening from a humanities angle — cultural history, anthropology, and sound art — publishing books, journal special issues, and hosting PhD projects on sonic culture.
