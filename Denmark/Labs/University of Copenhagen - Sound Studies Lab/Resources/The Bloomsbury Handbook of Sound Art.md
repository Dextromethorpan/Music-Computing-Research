Sound art is art made primarily out of sound rather than images or objects — think installations you walk into that surround you with recorded or generated sound, rather than a song you'd stream. This 571-page edited handbook brings together scholars and artists to map out what sound art is, its history, and how to think and write about it critically. It's a reference work used by researchers and students trying to understand sound as an artistic medium in its own right, distinct from music composition or pure acoustics research.
