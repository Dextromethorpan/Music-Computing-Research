This is an open-access special issue (anthology) of the journal Seismograf, edited by Sound Studies Lab members, that gathers essays and audio pieces exploring what sound actually "does" in the world — how it affects bodies, politics, memory, and relationships between people, rather than just how it's produced or perceived acoustically. It includes contributions in unconventional formats, like "audio papers," reflecting the lab's broader approach of treating sound research as something that can itself be presented in sound, not only in written text.
