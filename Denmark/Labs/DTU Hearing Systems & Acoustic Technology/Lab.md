Two related research units at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU): the **Hearing Systems Section** (DTU Health Technology) studies how the ear and brain process sound — psychoacoustics, hearing loss, hearing aids, cochlear implants — while the **Acoustic Technology group** (DTU Electrical and Photonics Engineering) studies room, building, and transducer acoustics. Together they underpin much of the science behind spatial audio, VR sound, and hearing-related music technology.
