Simulating exactly how sound waves spread through a room is normally very computationally expensive. This paper introduces "HergNet," a neural network trained to approximate ("surrogate") those expensive physics calculations by representing the sound field as a combination ("superposition") of simple wave patterns. The result is a much faster way to predict how sound will behave in a space, which is useful for real-time applications like game audio or interactive architectural design tools where full physics simulation would be too slow.
