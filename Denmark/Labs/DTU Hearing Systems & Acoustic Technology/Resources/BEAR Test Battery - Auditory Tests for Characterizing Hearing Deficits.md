This paper introduces a "test battery" — a structured set of hearing tests — designed to characterize exactly what kind of hearing difficulty a person has, beyond a simple hearing-loss measurement. It groups listeners into different "auditory profiles" based on how they perform across several tests of sound perception. The goal is to eventually let hearing aids and clinics tailor treatment to a person's specific hearing profile rather than using one-size-fits-all settings.
