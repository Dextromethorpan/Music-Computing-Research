"Fitting" a hearing aid means adjusting its settings for an individual user. This paper proposes a new fitting approach based on grouping people into "auditory profiles" (as introduced by the related BEAR test battery) rather than only using a person's basic hearing-loss measurement. The paper reports on a pilot test of this profile-based fitting method, aiming to make hearing aids work better by matching their settings to the specific type of hearing difficulty someone has, not just how much hearing they've lost.
