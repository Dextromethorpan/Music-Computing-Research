"Room acoustic simulation" means using a computer model to predict how sound will behave in a room — how it echoes, reverberates, or gets absorbed — before the room is even built, or without having to measure it physically. This paper (from the joint German-Danish DAGA acoustics conference) looks at how much detail about a room's shape and materials is actually needed to get accurate simulation results, which is practically important because gathering very detailed 3D room data is expensive and time-consuming.
