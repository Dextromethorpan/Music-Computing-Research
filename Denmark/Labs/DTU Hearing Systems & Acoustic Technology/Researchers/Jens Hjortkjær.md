Jens Hjortkjær studies how the brain encodes and processes natural sounds like speech, using EEG (brain electrical activity) and fMRI (brain imaging). His work includes building systems that decode which sound source a person is paying attention to in real time, aiming toward smarter, brain-controlled hearing instruments.
