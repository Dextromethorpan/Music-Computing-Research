Cheol-Ho Jeong works in the Acoustic Technology group on architectural and room acoustics — how sound behaves inside buildings and rooms. His research includes computer simulation of room acoustics, auditory virtual reality, and how speech and music are perceived in different room designs, including practical applications like hospital wards.
