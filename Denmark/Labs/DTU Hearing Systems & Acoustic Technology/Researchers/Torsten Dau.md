Torsten Dau leads the Hearing Systems Section at DTU and its Computational Auditory Modeling group. He builds quantitative computer models of how the human ear and brain process sound in complex, real-world listening situations, and applies these models to improve hearing-aid algorithms and hearing diagnostics.
