Mads Græsbøll Christensen founded the Audio Analysis Lab and is a leading researcher in multi-pitch estimation (detecting several simultaneous pitches in a recording), microphone-array processing, and spatial audio. He is Editor-in-Chief of the EURASIP Journal on Audio, Speech, and Music Processing, one of the field's key academic journals.
