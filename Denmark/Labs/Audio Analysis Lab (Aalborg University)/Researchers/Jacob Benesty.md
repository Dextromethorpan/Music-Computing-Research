Jacob Benesty is an adjunct professor (based primarily at INRS-EMT, University of Quebec) collaborating with the Audio Analysis Lab. He works on the theory behind microphone-array signal processing and speech/audio enhancement — the mathematical foundations used in many of the lab's beamforming and source-separation methods.
