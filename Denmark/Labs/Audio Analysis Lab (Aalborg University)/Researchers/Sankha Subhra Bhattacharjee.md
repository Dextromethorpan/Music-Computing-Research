Sankha Subhra Bhattacharjee works on adaptive signal processing for hearing aids, personal sound zones, and active noise control (canceling unwanted sound with an opposing sound wave). His PhD research focused on developing fast algorithms suitable for the limited processing power available inside a hearing aid.
