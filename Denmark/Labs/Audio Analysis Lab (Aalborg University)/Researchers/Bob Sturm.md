Bob Sturm created folk-rnn, a widely-discussed project using a recurrent neural network to generate new folk music melodies, while based at the Audio Analysis Lab. The project became an influential early example in the ISMIR/NIME research community of AI-generated music that real musicians could play, and sparked broader discussion about creativity and authorship in AI-assisted composition. He has since moved to Queen Mary University of London and later KTH Royal Institute of Technology, so he is no longer affiliated with this lab.
