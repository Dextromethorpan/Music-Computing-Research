"Pitch tracking" means following how the pitch of a sound (like a voice or instrument) changes moment to moment through a recording. This paper uses "Bayesian" statistical methods — a mathematical framework for reasoning about uncertainty — combined with a model of how musical/vocal sounds are built from harmonics (whole-number multiples of a base frequency) to track pitch reliably even in noisy or difficult recordings. Robust pitch tracking is a building block for applications like auto-tune, singing analysis, and automatic transcription.
