Using several microphones spread across a room (a "microphone array"), it's possible to estimate both the pitch of a sound and the direction it's coming from ("direction of arrival," DOA) — for example, locating and identifying which speaker or instrument is playing where. This paper develops mathematical methods to estimate both properties jointly and more accurately than solving them separately, which is useful for applications like automatically tracking a moving musician or speaker in a room using microphone arrays.
