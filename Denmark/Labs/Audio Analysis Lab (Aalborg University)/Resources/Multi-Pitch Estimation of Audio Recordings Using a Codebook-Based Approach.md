When a recording has several pitched sounds at once — like multiple instruments or singers together — figuring out what pitches are present ("multi-pitch estimation") is a hard signal-processing problem, since the sound waves overlap and mix. This paper proposes a "codebook-based" method: comparing the mixed recording against a pre-built library of typical pitch patterns to quickly find the best match. This kind of technique underlies tools that automatically transcribe music or analyze chords from audio.
