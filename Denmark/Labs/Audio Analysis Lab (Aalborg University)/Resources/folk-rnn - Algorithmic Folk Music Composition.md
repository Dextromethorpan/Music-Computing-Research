folk-rnn is a well-known project using a "recurrent neural network" (RNN — an AI model good at learning patterns in sequences, like notes in a melody) trained on thousands of traditional folk tunes to generate brand-new folk melodies in a similar style. The project was conducted while its creator was based at this lab and became influential in the ISMIR/NIME research community as an early, widely-discussed example of AI-generated music that musicians could actually play, sparking broader discussion about creativity, authorship, and AI in music.
