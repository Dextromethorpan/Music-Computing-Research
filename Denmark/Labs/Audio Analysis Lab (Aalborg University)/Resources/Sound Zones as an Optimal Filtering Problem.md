"Sound zones" is a technology idea where an array of loudspeakers plays audio in such a way that one part of a room hears one piece of audio (say, music) while another part of the same room hears something completely different (or silence) — without headphones. This paper frames the challenge of designing such a system mathematically as an "optimal filtering problem," essentially figuring out precisely how to combine and time the loudspeaker signals for the cleanest separation between the two listening zones.
