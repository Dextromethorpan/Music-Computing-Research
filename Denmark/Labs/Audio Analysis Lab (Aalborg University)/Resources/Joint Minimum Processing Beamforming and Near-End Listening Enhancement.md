"Beamforming" uses multiple microphones together to focus on sound coming from one direction while suppressing sound from other directions, like a directional spotlight for audio. "Near-end listening enhancement" separately means processing sound on the listener's side to make it easier to understand (for example, boosting speech clarity for someone in a noisy room). This paper (from IEEE HSCMA, a hands-free communication and microphone array conference) combines both techniques while trying to keep the amount of processing to a practical minimum, balancing sound quality against computational cost — relevant to headsets, hearing aids, and video-call microphones.
