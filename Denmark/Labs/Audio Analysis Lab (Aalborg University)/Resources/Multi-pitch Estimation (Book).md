This is a textbook-length technical work summarizing the mathematics and algorithms behind "multi-pitch estimation" — detecting multiple simultaneous pitches in an audio signal, such as several notes played together or multiple singers. It brings together statistical signal-processing theory (using probability and optimization methods) into a coherent set of tools, and became a standard reference for researchers building automatic music transcription and polyphonic (multi-note) audio analysis systems.
