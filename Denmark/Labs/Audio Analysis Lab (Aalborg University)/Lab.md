Part of the Section for Artificial Intelligence and Sound, Department of Electronic Systems, on Aalborg University's main Aalborg campus — a distinct group from the AAU Copenhagen Sound and Music Computing/ME-Lab units, focused on statistical and machine-learning-based audio signal processing: pitch estimation, microphone-array source separation, sound zones, and hearing-aid speech enhancement (closely tied to the CASPR centre).
