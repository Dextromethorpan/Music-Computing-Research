## Shortlist
1. **Physically-Modeled Highland Bagpipes VST/AU** — Faust-based waveguide/reed model of chanter + drones, targeting pipers and Celtic/folk producers who find sampled bagpipe libraries unplayable for real phrasing. Build effort: M. Distribution: Bob Dunsire Bagpipe Forums (Software subforum), cross-posted to VI-Control Instruments forum and r/bagpipes. Incumbency risk: Medium — Soundbytes Bagpipes and Studio Piper exist, but a KVR Audio forum thread has a user stating outright they "haven't found one single sample or instrument that let me play natural sounding bagpipe lines" — real, named dissatisfaction with the incumbents, not a saturated market.
2. **Physically-Modeled Hammered Dulcimer / Santur plugin** — Faust waveguide model of struck strings for a specific underserved folk/world instrument, sold to producers on VI-Control and world-music forums, distributed via Gumroad/Patchstorage. Build effort: M. Distribution: VI-Control "Which libraries contain good Hammered Dulcimer/Cimbalom?" thread shows an active but thin niche — not a full community hub, more a recurring ad-hoc question. Incumbency risk: Medium — 8Dio Santur and Cinematique Hammered Dulcimer are established sampled libraries with real (if imperfect) satisfaction; the complaint signal here is weaker ("looking for recommendations," not "these don't work").
3. **Procedural Impact/Footstep Synthesis plugin for indie game devs** — modal-synthesis plugin generating impact/footstep variations from a single reference sample, aimed at indie devs who can't afford/justify Wwise. Build effort: M/L (modal analysis + real-time synthesis is the harder DSP problem here). Distribution: r/GameAudio and indie game-dev Discords — broad but generic, no single specific hub found with a stated "I need this and can't get it" complaint. Incumbency risk: High — Audiokinetic's SoundSeed Impact already solves exactly this problem and ships bundled with Wwise, the dominant middleware; indie devs without Wwise mostly just use free sample packs instead of demanding synthesis, which is weak evidence of real unmet demand.

## Build plan (top pick: Physically-Modeled Highland Bagpipes VST/AU)
v1 scope: single "Highland pipes" preset only (skip Uilleann pipes, Scottish smallpipes, Border pipes variants — different bore/reed physics, save for v2 if v1 sells). Use the Faust `physmodels` library's conical-bore/reed model for the chanter; model the three drones as simpler sustained waveguide tones (steady pitch, less physical complexity than the chanter). Expose a handful of physical parameters as knobs (reed stiffness, bore diameter, drone tuning/beating) rather than building a full fingering-chart or ornament-automation system — grace notes and embellishments (cuttings, doublings, taorluaths) are left to the performer's MIDI input/pitch-bend rather than automated, since modeling authentic piping ornamentation logic is its own multi-week project and not needed for a v1 that's just "more expressive than a sample library."
Tooling: Faust for the DSP core, faust2juce (or faust2vst) to export directly to a VST3/AU plugin shell — this avoids writing a JUCE plugin from scratch. Test/tune by ear against real Highland pipe recordings (readily available on YouTube) rather than building objective validation tooling.
Realistic timeline (solo): ~3 weeks — 1 week for the Faust patch (chanter + drones + tuning), 3–4 days wrapping via faust2juce and getting VST3/AU builds working, 1 week tuning against reference recordings and basic UI, 3–4 days packaging (installer, demo video, Gumroad page).
Cut for v1: multi-instrument variants, ornament automation, convolution-based room/pipe-bag resonance modeling, preset browser beyond 1-2 tone variations.

## Distribution plan (top pick: Physically-Modeled Highland Bagpipes VST/AU)
Post a free beta/demo directly in the Bob Dunsire Bagpipe Forums Software subforum — this is a small, tight-knit, decades-old community of working pipers and pipe-band members who already discuss (and complain about) bagpipe software, so a beta thread inviting feedback puts the product in front of exactly the people who'd buy it, not a cold audience. Lead the demo video with the one thing sample libraries structurally can't do: continuous, expressive control through a phrase (smooth grace-note transitions, dynamic drone beating) rather than round-robin triggered notes — that's the specific, already-articulated pain point (the KVR "haven't found one that lets me play natural sounding bagpipe lines" complaint) the pitch should target verbatim. Cross-post the same demo to the VI-Control Instruments forum (larger sample-library-buying audience who will comparison-shop against Soundbytes/Studio Piper) and to r/bagpipes for secondary reach. Price low and frictionless for an enthusiast/hobbyist market — Gumroad, ~$25-35, no bundling, no subscription — since this is a passion-instrument niche, not an enterprise sale. No paid ads: the entire plan depends on the forum being real and active, which it is (multiple live threads found), but the community is small, so revenue ceiling here is modest — that's the honest tradeoff of chasing a genuinely underserved niche instead of a broad one.
